FAQ

Good questions.

How long is the contract?

All plans start with a 3-month minimum term. After that, the engagement continues month-to-month unless agreed otherwise.

How is the Channel Takeover fee calculated?

The Starter package covers up to 10 SKUs for $999/month, plus a one-time $199 setup fee and 1% of advertising revenue. Every additional 10 SKUs adds $499/month. For more than 50 SKUs, we'll build a custom quote.

What does the Creative Studio add-on include?

For +$699/month on top of your management fee, we produce main images, secondary and infographic images, A+ / Premium A+ content, Brand Store and brand visuals, ad creatives, and product videos.

How is the Brand Accelerator different from your agency plans?

Our agency plans are services for a set fee. The Accelerator is a long-term partnership for selected brands: we align our incentives with your growth through performance-based, revenue-share or hybrid terms, agreed individually after we get to know your brand.

Do you shoot product photos or videos?

We don't offer on-location shoots. Instead, we generate and edit videos and lifestyle visuals with AI tools, using the product photos and footage you provide.

Is ad spend included?

No. Advertising budget is paid directly by you on each platform. Our fee covers strategy and management of those campaigns.

What counts as "net revenue" for Channel Expansion?

Net revenue is your gross sales on the new channel minus returns. Our fee is 5% of that amount.

Can I combine e-commerce and logistics services?

Yes. Many clients use us for both — we'll connect your marketplace operations directly to our shipping services.

My platform isn't listed. Can you still help?

Very likely. Send us a message with the platform name and we'll let you know how we can support it.
