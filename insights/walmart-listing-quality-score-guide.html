Walmart Listing Quality Score: The 5 Components and How to Improve Them

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-02 · 6 min read

On Walmart Marketplace, a listing that is merely "live" isn't enough. Walmart scores every item on Listing Quality, and that score affects how visible your products are and whether you qualify for perks such as the Pro Seller badge. The good news: Walmart tells you exactly what goes into the score. This guide explains each component and how to improve it.

What the Listing Quality score is

According to Walmart's Listing Quality: Overview guide (updated Apr 9, 2026), "Quality listings drive higher conversion rates and boost product visibility, allowing you to unlock growth opportunities such as the Pro Seller badge." You get two scores:

  • Item-level score for each individual listing.
  • Catalog-level score, which is the average of your item-level scores.

Walmart's Listing Quality program page also ties the dashboard to "your Pro Seller Badge status" and "referral fee savings and more perks."

The five components

Price competitiveness"Compares your price against similar products on external marketplaces"Check the Pricing insights dashboard; avoid prices well above the same item elsewhere

In its shipping section, the guide notes that "items that weigh more than 50 pounds or are listed at $10 or less aren't included in your Listing Quality score calculations." Focus shipping fixes on your mid-weight, mid-price items first.

Where to start: a practical order

  1. In stock first. Because availability is measured over the last seven days, stockouts hurt quickly. Sync inventory feeds and set restock alerts.
  2. Content second. Content fixes are within your control and apply immediately. Work through your top-selling items and fill every attribute.
  3. Price third. If your price is far above the same item on other marketplaces, the score and your sales both suffer. Decide whether to match, bundle or differentiate.
  4. Shipping fourth. Faster delivery promises often need operational changes (warehouse location, carrier cutoffs or WFS).
  5. Reviews last, but steadily. Reviews take time. Never offer incentives for positive reviews; see our FTC fake reviews rule guide.

Content checklist for each listing

  • Title: brand + product type + key attribute (size, color, count, material).
  • Key features: at least three concise bullet points focused on benefits and specs.
  • Description: full details, use cases, what's in the box and care instructions.
  • Images: clean main image plus angles, scale and lifestyle shots.
  • Attributes: complete every required and recommended field in Seller Center.

Common mistakes

  • Fixing only the lowest scores. A small item with a terrible score matters less than a best seller with an average one. Prioritize by revenue.
  • Copying Amazon content as-is. Walmart has its own attributes and title rules; fill Walmart's fields rather than pasting another marketplace's listing.
  • Ignoring stock for slow movers. Unpublished or out-of-stock items still drag down your catalog average.
  • Chasing price alone. Matching the lowest price everywhere can wreck margins; sometimes bundles or a better listing are smarter.

How Listing Quality fits with other metrics

Listing Quality is about your items. Walmart also tracks account-level seller performance standards such as on-time delivery and cancellation rate. Both matter: a great listing on an account with poor delivery performance will still struggle.

Weekly Listing Quality routine

  1. Check the catalog-level score and sort items by lowest score.
  2. Fix content issues on the top 20 revenue items first.
  3. Review in-stock rates for the last seven days.
  4. Compare prices on your top items with other marketplaces.
  5. Track whether score changes lead to more traffic and conversions.

Walmart Listing Quality 分数：五大评分维度与提分方法

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-02 · 约 6 分钟阅读

在 Walmart Marketplace 上，商品仅仅"上架了"是不够的。Walmart 会给每个商品打 Listing Quality（商品质量）分，这个分数影响商品曝光，也决定你能否获得 Pro Seller 徽章等权益。好消息是：Walmart 明确公布了评分由哪些部分组成。本文逐项讲解，并告诉你怎么提分。

Listing Quality 分数是什么

据 Walmart Listing Quality 概览指南（2026 年 4 月 9 日更新），"高质量的 Listing 能提升转化率和商品曝光，帮助你获得 Pro Seller 徽章等增长机会。"你会有两个分数：

  • 商品级分数：每个 Listing 单独评分。
  • 目录级分数：所有商品级分数的平均值。

Walmart Listing Quality 项目页面还把该看板与"Pro Seller 徽章状态"以及"佣金优惠和更多权益"联系在一起。

五个评分维度

维度Walmart 衡量什么如何提升
内容质量商品名称、描述、核心卖点和图片的有效性标题写清品牌、品类和关键属性；描述完整；至少 3 条卖点；多张高质量图片
价格竞争力将你的价格与外部平台上的同类商品比较查看 Pricing insights 看板；避免明显高于其他平台的同款价格

评分与评论顾客评论数量和平均评分使用合规的评论项目；解决导致低分的产品问题

来源：Walmart Marketplace Learn，Listing Quality 概览（2026 年 4 月 9 日）。该指南未公布各维度权重。

该指南在配送部分注明：重量超过 50 磅或售价 10 美元及以下的商品，不计入 Listing Quality 分数计算。配送优化可以先从中等重量、中等价位的商品入手。

从哪里入手：建议顺序

  1. 先保有货。可售率按最近七天计算，断货会很快拉低分数。同步库存 Feed，设置补货提醒。
  2. 再改内容。内容完全可控、立即生效。从销量最高的商品开始，把属性填满。
  3. 然后看价格。如果价格远高于其他平台的同款，分数和销量都会受影响。决定是跟价、做组合还是做差异化。
  4. 再优化配送。更快的配送承诺通常需要运营调整（仓库位置、承运商截单时间或 WFS）。
  5. 评论慢慢积累。评论需要时间。绝不要用激励换好评，见我们的FTC 虚假评论规定指南。

单个 Listing 内容清单

  • 标题：品牌 + 品类 + 关键属性（尺寸、颜色、数量、材质）。
  • 核心卖点：至少三条简洁要点，突出功能和规格。
  • 描述：完整细节、使用场景、包装清单和保养说明。
  • 图片：干净的主图，加上多角度、尺寸参照和场景图。
  • 属性：在 Seller Center 中填满所有必填和推荐字段。

常见误区

  • 只修最低分的商品。小商品分数再低，也不如分数一般的爆款重要，应按营收排优先级。
  • 直接照搬亚马逊内容。Walmart 有自己的属性和标题规则，应填写 Walmart 字段，而不是粘贴其他平台的 Listing。
  • 忽视慢销品库存。未上架或断货的商品同样会拉低目录平均分。
  • 只拼价格。处处跟最低价会伤利润，有时做组合装或优化 Listing 更划算。

Listing Quality 与其他指标的关系

Listing Quality 针对商品本身。Walmart 还会考核账户级的卖家绩效标准，如准时送达率和取消率。两者都重要：Listing 再好，账户配送表现差也难以出单。

每周 Listing Quality 例行检查

  1. 查看目录级分数，按商品分数从低到高排序。
  2. 优先修复营收前 20 名商品的内容问题。
  3. 检查最近七天的有货率。
  4. 对比主力商品在其他平台的价格。
  5. 跟踪分数变化是否带来更多流量和转化。
