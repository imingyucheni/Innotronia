FedEx 2027 Rate Increase: 5.9% Headline, Bigger Hikes for Small Parcels

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-30 · 5 min read

FedEx has set its 2027 prices: list rates rise by an average of 5.9% on January 4, 2027. It is the same headline number as the last few years, but analysts say lightweight e-commerce parcels and several surcharges go up by more. If you ship to US customers, now is the time to re-check your 2027 shipping budget and carrier mix.

What FedEx announced

  • Headline increase. According to Logistics Management, FedEx announced on September 22, 2026 that US, export and import list rates will rise by an average of 5.9% from January 4, 2027. Ground Economy, Ground Multiweight, International Premium and International Priority DirectDistribution also change.
  • Fourth year at 5.9%. CEP Research (Sept 22, 2026) and The Loadstar both note this is the fourth consecutive year FedEx has announced a 5.9% average increase.
  • New paper fees. CEP Research reports that from January 18, 2027, FedEx will add a $25 per shipment fee for non-electronic trade documents and a $5 fee for paper air waybills.

Where the increase is bigger than 5.9%

The Loadstar, citing analysis by Paul Yaussy of logistics data platform Loop, reports that lightweight parcels of 1–5 lb face about 6.49%, the steepest of the weight brackets, and that five of seven major services exceed the headline. Logistics Management lists Ground delivery area surcharges up 8.11% and extended delivery area surcharges up 9.09%:

Surcharge2026From Jan 4, 2027
Additional Handling$29.50–$40.75$31.75–$43.75
Oversize (US)$255–$330$270–$355
International Oversize$208$225

Source: Logistics Management, Sept 2026. Surcharge amounts vary by service, zone and package; check FedEx's 2027 service guide for your exact charges.

Minimum charges also rise. Yaussy told The Loadstar that "minimum charges apply regardless of negotiated discounts," which can erode the discounts that lightweight shippers negotiated. Dave Sullivan of ShipScience told Logistics Management: "Budget from your shipping profile and not the 5.9% headline."

What we don't know yet

At the time of writing, the reports we reviewed did not include UPS's 2027 general rate increase or USPS's January 2027 prices. Fuel surcharges are also set separately and change weekly, so they can move your total cost up or down independently of the GRI. Contract terms differ widely: two sellers with the same volume can see very different 2027 increases depending on their negotiated discounts and minimums. We will cover other carriers' 2027 changes once they are confirmed.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Small parcels pay the most. Most marketplace orders are under 5 lb, exactly where the increase is steepest. Expect your real increase to be above 5.9% if you ship lots of small residential parcels.
  • Rural and bulky orders get more expensive. Delivery-area and oversize surcharges rise faster than base rates. Know which share of your orders go to remote ZIP codes or exceed size limits.
  • Discounts are worth less. A higher minimum charge can cancel part of a negotiated discount on light packages.
  • UPS and USPS matter too. Carriers often move in a similar direction. Watch for other 2027 announcements before you commit volume.

Checklist before January 4

  1. Re-rate last year's shipments with 2027 prices, or ask your carrier rep or label platform to do it, so you see your real increase.
  2. Split by weight and zone. Find where the bulk of your cost sits: 1–5 lb, far zones, remote ZIP codes, oversize.
  3. Fix packaging. Smaller boxes can avoid dimensional-weight charges and handling surcharges.
  4. Compare regional carriers and USPS for lightweight residential parcels.
  5. Renegotiate minimum charges and surcharge discounts, not only the base rate discount.
  6. Update shipping prices and free-shipping thresholds on your store and listings for 2027.
Tip: Ask any carrier or label provider for a re-rate using your own shipment file. A headline percentage cannot tell you what your mix of weights and zones will cost.

FedEx 2027 年涨价 5.9%：轻小包裹和附加费涨得更多

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-30 · 约 5 分钟阅读

FedEx 公布了 2027 年价格：2027 年 1 月 4 日起，标准价平均上涨 5.9%。这个数字和前几年一样，但分析人士指出，轻小电商包裹和多项附加费的实际涨幅更高。发美国买家的卖家，现在就该重新核算 2027 年的运费预算和快递组合。

FedEx 公布了什么

  • 平均涨幅：据 Logistics Management 报道，FedEx 于 2026 年 9 月 22 日宣布，美国国内、出口和进口标准价自 2027 年 1 月 4 日起平均上涨 5.9%，Ground Economy、Ground Multiweight、International Premium 和 International Priority DirectDistribution 也在调整范围内。
  • 连续第四年 5.9%：CEP Research（2026 年 9 月 22 日）和 The Loadstar 都指出，这是 FedEx 连续第四年公布 5.9% 的平均涨幅。
  • 新增纸质单据费：据 CEP Research 报道，2027 年 1 月 18 日起，非电子贸易单据每票收费 25 美元，纸质运单每票 5 美元。

哪些地方涨得比 5.9% 更多

The Loadstar 引述物流数据平台 Loop 分析师 Paul Yaussy 的分析称，1–5 磅轻小包裹涨幅约 6.49%，是各重量段中最高的；七项主要服务中有五项超过平均涨幅。Logistics Management 列出的 Ground 偏远地区附加费上涨 8.11%，延伸偏远地区附加费上涨 9.09%：

附加费2026 年2027 年 1 月 4 日起
额外处理费（Additional Handling）$29.50–$40.75$31.75–$43.75
超尺寸费（美国国内）$255–$330$270–$355
国际超尺寸费$208$225

来源：Logistics Management，2026 年 9 月。附加费金额因服务、分区和包裹而异，具体以 FedEx 2027 年服务指南为准。

最低收费同样上调。Yaussy 对 The Loadstar 表示，“最低收费不受协议折扣影响”，这会吃掉轻小件发货人谈下来的部分折扣。ShipScience 的 Dave Sullivan 对 Logistics Management 表示：“要按自己的发货结构做预算，而不是看 5.9% 这个标题数字。”

目前还不确定的

截至发稿，我们查阅的报道中尚未包含 UPS 的 2027 年调价和 USPS 2027 年 1 月的新价格。燃油附加费单独设定、每周调整，会独立于年度调价影响你的总成本。合同条款差异也很大：发货量相同的两个卖家，因为协议折扣和最低收费不同，2027 年的实际涨幅可能完全不一样。其他快递的 2027 年调价确认后，我们会继续跟进。

我们的解读

  • 小包裹涨得最多：平台订单大多在 5 磅以下，恰好是涨幅最高的区间。如果你大量发住宅小包，实际涨幅大概率高于 5.9%。
  • 偏远地区和大件更贵：偏远地区费和超尺寸费涨得比基础运价快，先弄清楚有多少订单发往偏远邮编或超出尺寸限制。
  • 协议折扣“缩水”：最低收费上调，会抵消轻小件的一部分协议折扣。
  • 也要关注 UPS 和 USPS：几家快递的调价方向往往接近，确定货量分配之前，先看看其他家的 2027 年公告。

1 月 4 日前的行动清单

  1. 用 2027 年价格重算去年的发货数据，或请快递客户经理、面单平台帮你重算，看清真实涨幅。
  2. 按重量和分区拆分：找出成本主要集中在哪里，1–5 磅、远区、偏远邮编还是超尺寸。
  3. 优化包装：更小的箱子可以避开体积重和额外处理费。
  4. 比较区域快递和 USPS：轻小住宅件尤其值得比价。
  5. 重新谈判：不只谈基础运价折扣，也要谈最低收费和附加费折扣。
  6. 更新运费设置：调整店铺和 Listing 的运费和包邮门槛。
小提示：让任何快递或面单服务商用你自己的发货数据做一次重算。一个平均涨幅数字，说明不了你自己的重量和分区结构要多花多少钱。
