TikTok Shop LIVE GMV Max: How to Run Ads for Your Livestreams

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-03 · 6 min read

Livestreams are one of the strongest sales drivers on TikTok Shop, especially around holiday campaigns. LIVE GMV Max is TikTok's automated ad campaign for pushing traffic into your live room. Set it up well and it can fill a livestream with buyers; set it up badly and it burns budget on a quiet room. Here is how it works, based on TikTok's own help center.

What LIVE GMV Max is

According to TikTok's About LIVE GMV Max article (updated Sept 2026), "LIVE GMV Max is an automated Shop Ads campaign that optimizes liveroom traffic to get the highest gross revenue for your LIVE event."

  • How ROI is measured: "LIVE ROI measures your total liveroom GMV divided by your total ad spend."
  • Two creative types: video-to-LIVE (short videos that send viewers into the live room) and LIVE-to-LIVE (showing the livestream itself). TikTok allocates between them automatically.
  • Existing ads pause. When a shop's primary ad account has an active LIVE GMV Max campaign, existing LIVE Shopping Ads for the shop's official TikTok account are automatically paused, and they "won't restart automatically" after the campaign ends.

LIVE GMV Max is separate from Product GMV Max, which optimizes non-LIVE sales; see our Product GMV Max guide.

TikTok's recommended setup

From TikTok's Best practices for LIVE GMV Max (updated Sept 2026):

SettingTikTok's guidance
Campaign lengthAt least three days (doesn't need to run continuously)
Session lengthSessions of 3+ hours generally grow GMV faster; more than 8 hours a day for a more significant increase
ROI targetBased on historical LIVE performance
BudgetSystem recommendation, or 2–3× your historical LIVE ad budget
ProfileChoose a profile with strong LIVE GMV; about $1,000 gross revenue in the last 7 days is considered strong
Videos for video-to-LIVE50–70 videos for creative exploration, or 5–10 new videos daily; up to 10 Preferred videos
ScheduleKeep livestream timing and duration consistent across campaign days

Source: TikTok Business Help Center (Sept 2026). Guidance may change; check the current help articles.

Adjusting after day one

TikTok suggests checking delivery one day after launch:

  • If spending is slower than expected, consider lowering the ROI target.
  • If spending is faster than expected, consider raising the budget or the ROI target.
Know your break-even ROI before you start: 1 ÷ your margin before ad costs. With a 30% margin, break-even ROI is about 3.3. Don't set a target far below that just to make the campaign spend.

Planning a livestream that converts

  1. Pick a hero offer. One or two products with a clear, time-limited deal work better than a long catalog.
  2. Prepare stock. Make sure inventory covers a spike; selling out mid-stream wastes ad spend.
  3. Script the first minutes. New viewers arrive constantly; repeat the offer and price regularly.
  4. Use LIVE screens. TikTok recommends sharing key information on screen to connect with the audience.
  5. Feed the video pipeline. Post short videos before and during the campaign to drive video-to-LIVE traffic.
  6. Keep a schedule. Go live at the same times each day so the system and viewers learn your pattern.

Common mistakes

  • Running a one-day campaign when TikTok recommends at least three days.
  • Short, irregular sessions that never build momentum.
  • Forgetting to restart regular LIVE Shopping Ads after the campaign, since they don't resume automatically.
  • Using a profile with little recent LIVE revenue.

Holiday timing matters too; plan streams around the TikTok Shop Black Friday campaign (Nov 11–30). Treat each campaign as a test: record session length, viewers, orders and ROI so you can compare the next one.

LIVE GMV Max checklist

  1. Calculate break-even ROI and set a target above it.
  2. Plan a campaign of three days or more with consistent session times.
  3. Aim for sessions of 3 hours or longer.
  4. Prepare 50–70 videos or post 5–10 new ones daily.
  5. Review spend and ROI after day one and adjust.
  6. After the campaign, restart any paused LIVE Shopping Ads you still need.

TikTok Shop LIVE GMV Max：直播间广告怎么投

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-03 · 约 6 分钟阅读

直播是 TikTok Shop 上最强的出单方式之一，大促期间尤其如此。LIVE GMV Max 是 TikTok 为直播间引流的自动化广告。设置得好，直播间能挤满买家；设置不好，就是在冷清的直播间里烧钱。下面根据 TikTok 官方帮助中心，讲清楚它怎么运作。

LIVE GMV Max 是什么

据 TikTok About LIVE GMV Max 帮助文档（2026 年 9 月更新），"LIVE GMV Max 是一种自动化商城广告，通过优化直播间流量，为直播带来最高的总销售额。"

  • ROI 怎么算："LIVE ROI = 直播间总 GMV ÷ 广告总花费。"
  • 两种素材：视频引流直播（用短视频把观众带进直播间）和直播引流直播（展示直播画面本身），系统自动分配。
  • 原有广告会暂停。店铺主广告账户有进行中的 LIVE GMV Max 计划时，店铺官方账号已有的 LIVE 购物广告会被自动暂停，计划结束后"不会自动恢复"。

LIVE GMV Max 与优化非直播销售的 Product GMV Max 是两回事，后者见我们的Product GMV Max 投放指南。

TikTok 推荐的设置

摘自 TikTok LIVE GMV Max 最佳实践（2026 年 9 月更新）：

设置TikTok 建议
计划时长至少三天（不必连续投放）
单场时长3 小时以上的场次 GMV 增长通常更快；每天直播超过 8 小时效果更明显
ROI 目标参考历史直播表现
预算按系统推荐，或设为历史直播广告预算的 2–3 倍
账号选择直播 GMV 强的账号；最近 7 天总收入约 1,000 美元即视为强
引流视频每次准备 50–70 条视频用于素材探索，或每天新增 5–10 条；最多选 10 条优选视频
排期计划期内直播时间和时长保持一致

来源：TikTok Business 帮助中心（2026 年 9 月）。规则可能调整，请以最新帮助文档为准。

第一天之后怎么调

TikTok 建议在开播一天后检查投放情况：

  • 消耗慢于预期，可考虑降低 ROI 目标。
  • 消耗快于预期，可考虑提高预算或 ROI 目标。
开播前先算保本 ROI：1 ÷ 广告前利润率。利润率 30% 时，保本 ROI 约为 3.3。不要为了让计划花出去，把目标设得远低于保本线。

策划一场能出单的直播

  1. 选好主推款。一两款带限时优惠的爆品，比长长的货盘更有效。
  2. 备足库存。确保库存能承接峰值，直播中断货等于浪费广告费。
  3. 设计开场话术。新观众不断进来，要反复讲清优惠和价格。
  4. 用好直播贴片。TikTok 建议在画面上展示关键信息，拉近与观众的距离。
  5. 持续发短视频。计划前和计划中都要发短视频，为视频引流直播提供素材。
  6. 固定排期。每天固定时间开播，让系统和观众熟悉你的节奏。

常见误区

  • 只投一天，而 TikTok 建议至少三天。
  • 场次短且不规律，始终积累不起势能。
  • 计划结束后忘了重启原有的 LIVE 购物广告——它们不会自动恢复。
  • 用近期直播收入很低的账号投放。

假日节点也很重要，可围绕TikTok Shop 黑五大促（11 月 11–30 日）规划直播。把每次计划当作一次测试，记录场次时长、观看人数、订单和 ROI，方便下一次对比。

LIVE GMV Max 清单

  1. 算出保本 ROI，目标设在保本线以上。
  2. 规划至少三天的计划，场次时间保持一致。
  3. 单场尽量 3 小时以上。
  4. 准备 50–70 条视频，或每天新发 5–10 条。
  5. 第一天后复盘消耗和 ROI 并调整。
  6. 计划结束后，重启仍需要的 LIVE 购物广告。
