Amazon Insurance Changes Nov 2: Safety Categories and the Insurance Accelerator

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-20 · 6 min read

From November 2, 2026, Amazon's insurance rule changes in two important ways. Sellers with products in "enhanced safety" categories will need $1 million of commercial liability coverage from the first sale, not only after passing $10,000 a month. And sellers based in Mainland China will have to get newly submitted policies through the Amazon Insurance Accelerator. This guide explains who is affected and how to get compliant before the deadline.

For the general insurance rules, see our earlier Amazon seller liability insurance guide; this article covers what changes on November 2.

What changes on November 2

Amazon posted the notice in its US Seller Forums on September 3, 2026, giving 60 days' notice, according to PPC Land (Sept 21, 2026) and Novadata (Sept 7, 2026):

  • Category trigger. Sellers with products in enhanced safety categories must hold a commercial liability policy covering those products regardless of monthly sales. It applies to both new and existing listings.
  • The old trigger stays for everyone else. Other sellers still need a policy within 30 days after gross proceeds exceed $10,000 in a month.
  • Mainland China sellers. PPC Land quotes Amazon: "Starting November 2, 2026, we will reject newly submitted insurance policies that are not obtained through AIA." Valid third-party policies submitted before that date can be used until they expire.

Which categories count

Amazon's notice names children's products, cosmetic and ingestible products and lithium battery products, and says the categories "include, but are not limited to" these. PPC Land, citing SellerEssentials, lists 11 enhanced safety categories:

#Enhanced safety categoryExamples
1Children's productsToys, baby gear
2Consumable and ingestible productsSupplements, cosmetics
3Fire-related productsFire extinguishers
4General household devicesSmall kitchen appliances
5Home medical devices—
6Lithium battery productsPower banks, battery-powered devices
7Outdoor power and heating equipment—
8Personal safety equipment—
9Sleep productsMattresses
10Transport-related productsTires
11Water and marine safety products—

Examples beyond those Amazon names are illustrative. Check the category assigned to each ASIN in Seller Central; Amazon's list is "not limited to" these.

What the policy must include

PPC Land and Novadata summarize Amazon's published requirements:

  • Commercial general, umbrella or excess liability, generally on an occurrence basis.
  • At least $1,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate.
  • Deductible no higher than $10,000, shown on the certificate.
  • Covers all products sold on Amazon.com.
  • Amazon.com Services LLC, its affiliates and assignees named as additional insureds.
  • Insured name matching the legal entity on the selling account.

What is the Amazon Insurance Accelerator?

The Amazon Insurance Accelerator (AIA) is a network of vetted insurers set up with broker Marsh. Sellers reach it in Seller Central under Settings › Account Info › Business Insurance. Marsh's program page says sellers can get quotes from multiple carriers online. For sellers outside Mainland China, using AIA remains optional; for Mainland China sellers, new policies from November 2 must come through it.

Premiums vary by category, revenue and claims history. PPC Land cites earlier AIA quotes reported by EcomCrew of $837 to $2,200 a year for sellers with $500,000 to $1 million in revenue; treat these as examples, not a price list.

What happens if you're not covered?

Amazon's notice does not state the consequence. PPC Land reports that SellerEssentials describes a 45-day window to submit proof before enhanced-safety listings can be deactivated, while a forum email gives November 2 as the deadline. Plan for the stricter reading.

Compliance checklist

  1. List all ASINs in enhanced safety categories, including low-volume ones.
  2. Check your current policy: limit, deductible, occurrence basis, additional insureds, insured name.
  3. If you are based in Mainland China and need a new policy, get quotes through AIA in Seller Central before November 2.
  4. If your existing third-party policy is valid, upload it before November 2 and note its expiry date.
  5. Diarize renewal at least 30 days ahead, and keep certificates on file.

亚马逊 11 月 2 日保险新规：加强安全类目与保险加速器

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-20 · 约 6 分钟阅读

自 2026 年 11 月 2 日起，亚马逊的保险规定有两处重要变化。一是“加强安全”类目的卖家从第一单起就需要 100 万美元的商业责任险，而不再是月销超过 1 万美元后才需要；二是中国大陆卖家新提交的保单必须通过亚马逊保险加速器（Amazon Insurance Accelerator，AIA）购买。本文讲清哪些卖家受影响，以及如何在截止前完成合规。

保险的一般规定请参阅我们此前的《亚马逊卖家责任险指南》；本文聚焦 11 月 2 日的变化。

11 月 2 日有什么变化

据 PPC Land（2026 年 9 月 21 日）和 Novadata（2026 年 9 月 7 日），亚马逊于 2026 年 9 月 3 日在美国卖家论坛发布通知，提前 60 天告知：

  • 按类目触发。在“加强安全”类目有商品的卖家，无论月销多少，都须持有覆盖这些商品的商业责任险，新老 Listing 均适用。
  • 其他卖家沿用原门槛。其他卖家仍是在单月销售总额超过 1 万美元后 30 天内投保。
  • 中国大陆卖家。PPC Land 引用亚马逊原文：“自 2026 年 11 月 2 日起，我们将拒绝非通过 AIA 获得的新提交保单。”在此之前提交的有效第三方保单可用至到期。

哪些类目属于“加强安全”

亚马逊通知点名了儿童产品、化妆品与可食用产品、锂电池产品，并说明类目“包括但不限于”这些。PPC Land 援引 SellerEssentials 列出了 11 个加强安全类目：

#加强安全类目示例
1儿童产品玩具、婴童用品
2消耗品与可食用产品保健品、化妆品
3消防相关产品灭火器
4一般家用设备小型厨房电器
5家用医疗器械—
6锂电池产品充电宝、电池供电设备
7户外动力与取暖设备—
8个人防护装备—
9睡眠产品床垫
10交通相关产品轮胎
11水上与船舶安全产品—

除亚马逊点名的之外，示例仅供参考。请在卖家后台核对每个 ASIN 所属类目；亚马逊的清单“不限于”这些。

保单必须满足的条件

PPC Land 和 Novadata 汇总的亚马逊要求：

  • 商业综合责任险、伞式或超额责任险，一般为“事故发生制”。
  • 每次事故及累计限额至少 100 万美元。
  • 免赔额不超过 1 万美元，并在保险凭证上注明。
  • 覆盖在 Amazon.com 销售的所有商品。
  • 将 Amazon.com Services LLC 及其关联公司和受让人列为附加被保险人。
  • 被保险人名称与销售账户的法律主体一致。

什么是亚马逊保险加速器？

亚马逊保险加速器（AIA）是与保险经纪 Marsh 合作搭建的认证保险公司网络，入口在卖家后台“设置 › 账户信息 › 企业保险”。Marsh 项目页面称卖家可在线获取多家保险公司报价。对中国大陆以外的卖家，AIA 仍是可选项；对中国大陆卖家，11 月 2 日起的新保单必须通过它购买。

保费因类目、营收和理赔记录而异。PPC Land 引用 EcomCrew 早前报道的 AIA 报价：营收 50 万–100 万美元的卖家每年约 837–2,200 美元，仅作参考，并非报价表。

没有保险会怎样？

亚马逊通知未说明后果。PPC Land 称，SellerEssentials 描述有 45 天的提交期，逾期后加强安全类目的 Listing 可能被下架；而一封论坛邮件给出的截止日期是 11 月 2 日。请按更严格的理解准备。

合规清单

  1. 列出所有加强安全类目的 ASIN，包括销量很低的。
  2. 检查现有保单：限额、免赔额、事故发生制、附加被保险人、被保险人名称。
  3. 中国大陆卖家如需新保单，请在 11 月 2 日前通过卖家后台的 AIA 获取报价。
  4. 现有第三方保单有效的，在 11 月 2 日前上传并记录到期日。
  5. 至少提前 30 天安排续保，并留存保险凭证。
