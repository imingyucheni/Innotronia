Amazon Brand Registry Growth Tools: What Amazon's Data Says

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-10 · 6 min read

Many brands register their trademark, enroll in Amazon Brand Registry for protection, and then stop there. That leaves the growth tools that come with Brand Registry unused. This guide walks through the tools that Amazon itself says can lift sales, what the official numbers are, and the order we would set them up in.

What Brand Registry unlocks

Brand Registry is Amazon's program for brand owners, usually with a registered or pending trademark (see our US trademark guide). Besides brand protection, it unlocks a set of listing, analytics and marketing tools. On its Brand Registry page, Amazon publishes figures from its internal data for several of them:

ToolWhat Amazon reports
Basic A+ ContentCan increase sales by up to 8%
Premium A+ ContentCan increase sales by up to 20%
Manage Your ExperimentsActing on optimized content can increase sales by up to 25%
Brand StoresStore visitors purchase 53.9% more frequently and have a 71.3% higher average order value than non-visitors (Aug 2024 data)
Subscribe & SaveA 10%–15% discount can drive up to a 1.8x increase in conversion on average
TransparencyMore than 2.7 billion product units verified as genuine (May 2026)

Two cautions: these are "up to" figures and Amazon's own data, and the Brand Store comparison shows correlation (shoppers who visit a Store are often already keen on the brand), not proof that a Store caused the extra spending. Still, they show where Amazon sees the biggest gains.

The tools, in the order we would set them up

1. A+ Content on every parent ASIN

A+ Content replaces the plain product description with images, comparison charts and brand modules. Start with your best sellers. Use a comparison chart to cross-sell your other products, and design every module for mobile first, because most shoppers will see it on a phone.

2. Manage Your Experiments (A/B testing)

Manage Your Experiments lets you test two versions of a main image, title, bullet points or A+ Content on eligible ASINs that get enough traffic. Test one element at a time, let the test run to completion, and then apply the winner. Amazon's "up to 25%" figure is for acting on the optimized version, so the work only pays off if you actually publish the winner.

3. A Brand Store

A Brand Store is a free multi-page store on Amazon with its own URL. Use it as the landing page for Sponsored Brands ads and for external traffic. Keep the navigation simple: best sellers, categories and a few product collections. The Store's own analytics show visits, sources and sales.

4. Sponsored Brands and video ads

Sponsored Brands ads, which can show your logo, a headline and several products or a video, require Brand Registry. They work well for branded and category keywords and can send shoppers to your Store. Keep Sponsored Products as the base of your ad plan and add Sponsored Brands on proven keywords (see our bidding strategies guide).

5. Subscribe & Save for repeat products

If you sell consumables such as supplements, pet supplies or cleaning products, Subscribe & Save turns one-time buyers into repeat orders. Amazon's figure for a 10%–15% discount (up to 1.8x conversion on average) gives a useful starting point for testing. Make sure you can keep the product in stock, since subscriptions depend on it.

6. Amazon Attribution and the Brand Referral Bonus

If you send traffic from TikTok, Instagram, Google or email to Amazon, set up Amazon Attribution. Amazon describes it as "a free measurement solution" for professional sellers in Brand Registry, vendors and agencies, measuring clicks, detail page views, add-to-carts and purchases across search, social, display, video and email, with a 14-day attribution window. Brands using Attribution can also enroll in the Brand Referral Bonus, which Amazon describes as earning "credits from your non-Amazon marketing efforts." Bonus rates and rules are listed in Seller Central and may vary by category, so check them there before you build a budget around them.

7. Brand Analytics and Transparency

Brand Analytics shows search term, market basket and repeat-purchase data that is not visible to non-brand sellers; use it to find keywords and bundle ideas. If counterfeits or commingling are a problem, look at Transparency, Amazon's product serialization service.

Brand Registry growth checklist

  1. Confirm every ASIN is listed under your registered brand name.
  2. Publish A+ Content on your top 10 parent ASINs, mobile-first.
  3. Run one Manage Your Experiments test per month on a top seller.
  4. Build a simple Brand Store and point Sponsored Brands ads to it.
  5. Turn on Subscribe & Save for repeat-purchase products.
  6. Tag all external traffic with Amazon Attribution and enroll in the Brand Referral Bonus.
  7. Review Brand Analytics search terms every month.

亚马逊品牌备案增长工具全解：官方数据与上手顺序

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-10 · 约 6 分钟阅读

很多品牌注册了商标、完成亚马逊品牌备案（Brand Registry），拿到品牌保护后就止步了，备案附带的一整套增长工具却一直闲置。本文梳理亚马逊官方称能提升销量的几款工具、官方公布的数据，以及我们建议的上手顺序。

品牌备案能解锁什么

品牌备案是亚马逊面向品牌所有者的项目，通常需要已注册或申请中的商标（参见我们的《美国商标注册指南》）。除了品牌保护，它还解锁一系列 Listing、数据分析和营销工具。亚马逊在其品牌备案官方页面上公布了其中几项工具的内部数据：

工具亚马逊公布的数据
基础版 A+销量最高可提升 8%
高级版 A+（Premium A+）销量最高可提升 20%
管理实验（Manage Your Experiments）按优化后的内容调整，销量最高可提升 25%
品牌旗舰店（Brand Store）访问过旗舰店的买家购买频次高 53.9%，客单价高 71.3%（2024 年 8 月数据）
订购省（Subscribe & Save）10%–15% 的折扣平均可带来最高 1.8 倍的转化提升
透明计划（Transparency）已验证超过 27 亿件商品为正品（2026 年 5 月）

两点提醒：这些都是亚马逊自己公布的“最高”数据；旗舰店的对比只说明相关性（逛旗舰店的买家本来就更认可品牌），不能证明是旗舰店带来了额外消费。但它们清楚地显示了亚马逊认为收益最大的方向。

按顺序把工具用起来

1. 每个父 ASIN 都做 A+

A+ 页面用图片、对比表和品牌模块替代纯文字描述。先从销量最好的产品开始；用对比表带动店内其他产品；所有模块都按手机端优先设计，因为多数买家是在手机上看的。

2. 管理实验（A/B 测试）

“管理实验”可以对流量足够的合格 ASIN 测试两版主图、标题、五点或 A+。每次只测一个元素，让测试跑完再采用胜出版本。亚马逊“最高 25%”的数据针对的是采用优化版本之后，所以测完一定要真正上线胜出方案。

3. 品牌旗舰店

品牌旗舰店是亚马逊上免费的多页面店铺，有独立网址。把它作为品牌推广广告和站外引流的落地页。导航保持简单：热销、品类、几个产品合集即可。旗舰店自带的数据可查看访问量、流量来源和销售额。

4. 品牌推广（Sponsored Brands）与视频广告

品牌推广广告可展示品牌 Logo、标语和多个商品或视频，需要品牌备案才能投放。适合品牌词和品类词，还能把买家引到旗舰店。广告体系仍以商品推广（Sponsored Products）为基础，在已验证的关键词上叠加品牌推广（参见我们的《竞价策略指南》）。

5. 复购型产品开通订购省

保健品、宠物用品、清洁用品等消耗品，开通订购省可以把一次性买家变成定期复购。亚马逊“10%–15% 折扣平均最高 1.8 倍转化”的数据可作为测试起点。前提是库存稳定，否则订阅会断。

6. 亚马逊归因（Amazon Attribution）与品牌引流奖励

如果你从 TikTok、Instagram、Google 或邮件给亚马逊引流，一定要设置亚马逊归因。亚马逊称其为“免费的衡量工具”，面向已品牌备案的专业卖家、供应商和代理商，可衡量搜索、社交、展示、视频和邮件等渠道带来的点击、详情页浏览、加购和购买，归因窗口为 14 天。使用归因的品牌还可加入品牌引流奖励（Brand Referral Bonus），亚马逊称可“从站外营销中赚取积分/抵扣”。具体奖励比例和规则以卖家后台为准，可能因品类而异，做预算前请先确认。

7. 品牌分析与透明计划

品牌分析（Brand Analytics）提供搜索词、购物篮、复购等非品牌卖家看不到的数据，可用来挖关键词和组合装思路。如果遇到假货或混仓问题，可以了解亚马逊的商品防伪编码服务“透明计划”。

品牌备案增长清单

  1. 确认所有 ASIN 都挂在已备案的品牌名下。
  2. 为前 10 个父 ASIN 上线 A+，手机端优先。
  3. 每月对一个主力款跑一次“管理实验”。
  4. 搭建简洁的品牌旗舰店，并把品牌推广广告指向它。
  5. 为复购型产品开通订购省。
  6. 站外流量全部加上亚马逊归因标签，并加入品牌引流奖励。
  7. 每月复盘品牌分析中的搜索词数据。
