Walmart's October Deals Event (Oct 5–11, 2026): What Sellers Should Know

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-28 · 5 min read

Walmart will run its October "Deals" event from October 5 to 11, 2026, a full week that wraps around Amazon's two-day Prime Big Deal Days. For Walmart Marketplace sellers it is the first big traffic test of the holiday season, and it comes as Walmart's online business is growing fast. Here is what was announced, the numbers behind it and how to prepare.

What Walmart announced

  • Dates. According to Fox Business (Sept 16, 2026), the "Deals" sale starts at 12 a.m. ET on October 5 and runs through October 11, overlapping Amazon's October 6–7 event.
  • No membership required. Unlike Amazon's members-only event, the sale is open to all shoppers. NBC News Select reports that Walmart+ members ($12.95 a month or $98 a year) get early access to Walmart sales.
  • Advertised discounts. Fox Business lists up to 50% off home, 40% off tech, 30% off toys, food and Halloween, 25% off tools and 20% off wellness items.
  • Walmart's reasoning. A Walmart spokesperson told Fox Business: "We plan our events around how and when our customers want to shop. We know many customers are starting to think about the holidays earlier."

Why the event matters more this year

In its Q2 FY27 earnings release (Aug 20, 2026), Walmart reported that Walmart U.S. eCommerce grew 24% year over year, "with strength in store-fulfilled delivery, advertising, and marketplace," while Walmart Connect advertising rose 43% (excluding VIZIO). CNBC also covered the results on the day of release.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • A week, not a day. A seven-day event spreads traffic out. Budgets and stock need to last the whole week, not just the first morning.
  • Price comparison across channels. The overlap with Amazon means many shoppers will compare prices on both sites. If you sell on both, decide deliberately where each deal runs and keep prices consistent with each platform's pricing rules.
  • Ads are getting more competitive. Walmart Connect's growth means more brands are bidding. Expect higher costs per click during the event and plan budgets accordingly.
  • Fulfillment speed wins placement. Walmart's growth is led by fast delivery. Items with two-day or faster delivery promises are more likely to convert.

Checklist for Walmart sellers this week

  1. Check promotions in Seller Center. Review which reduced-price or promotional options are available to your account for the event window, and set start and end dates for October 5–11.
  2. Protect your margin. Recalculate each deal price after Walmart's referral fee, fulfillment cost and ad spend.
  3. Top up inventory. Confirm WFS or warehouse stock covers a full week of higher sales, plus a buffer for the start of holiday shopping.
  4. Raise Sponsored Products budgets for the week and watch pacing daily, especially on October 5 and on Amazon's event days.
  5. Update listings. Refresh titles, images and attributes for gifting, and fix any content quality issues flagged in Seller Center.
  6. Keep performance clean. Upload valid tracking for every order and answer customer messages quickly; a busy week can hurt metrics fast.
Tip: Treat this week as a rehearsal for Black Friday. Record units, conversion and ad costs per day so you can plan November with real numbers.

沃尔玛 10 月大促（10 月 5–11 日）：卖家需要知道的事

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-28 · 约 5 分钟阅读

沃尔玛将于 2026 年 10 月 5 日至 11 日举办 10 月“Deals”大促，整整一周，把亚马逊两天的秋季 Prime 大促包在中间。对沃尔玛美国站卖家来说，这是节日季第一次大流量考验，而且恰逢沃尔玛线上业务高速增长。下面整理官方信息、背后的数据和备战要点。

沃尔玛公布了什么

  • 时间：据 Fox Business（2026 年 9 月 16 日）报道，“Deals”大促于美东时间 10 月 5 日 0 点开始，持续到 10 月 11 日，与亚马逊 10 月 6–7 日的活动重叠。
  • 无需会员：与亚马逊的会员专享活动不同，沃尔玛大促面向所有顾客。据 NBC News Select 报道，Walmart+ 会员（每月 12.95 美元或每年 98 美元）可以提前抢购。
  • 宣传折扣：Fox Business 列出的折扣为：家居最高 5 折，科技产品 6 折，玩具、食品和万圣节用品 7 折，工具 75 折，健康类 8 折。
  • 官方说法：沃尔玛发言人对 Fox Business 表示：“我们根据顾客想在何时、以何种方式购物来安排活动。我们知道很多顾客开始更早考虑节日购物。”

为什么今年这场大促更重要

沃尔玛在 2027 财年第二季度财报（2026 年 8 月 20 日）中披露，沃尔玛美国电商同比增长 24%，增长动力来自“门店履约配送、广告和第三方平台（Marketplace）”；Walmart Connect 广告收入增长 43%（不含 VIZIO）。CNBC 在财报当天也做了报道。

10 月大促一览

日期（2026 年）活动
10 月 5–11 日沃尔玛“Deals”大促（所有顾客可参与）
10 月 6–7 日亚马逊秋季 Prime 大促（仅限 Prime 会员）

如果你同时在亚马逊销售，可以参考我们的亚马逊秋季大促备战清单，把两场活动放在一起统筹，而不是当成两个独立项目。

我们的解读

  • 是一周，不是一天：七天的活动会把流量摊开，广告预算和库存要撑满整周，而不只是第一天上午。
  • 跨平台比价：与亚马逊活动重叠，意味着很多买家会两边比价。如果你两个平台都在做，要想清楚每个优惠放在哪里，同时遵守各平台的价格规则。
  • 广告竞争更激烈：Walmart Connect 高速增长说明投广告的品牌越来越多，大促期间点击成本大概率上涨，预算要提前规划。
  • 配送时效决定曝光：沃尔玛的增长主要来自快速配送，承诺两日达或更快的商品更容易转化。

沃尔玛卖家本周行动清单

  1. 检查后台促销工具：确认你的账户在活动期间可以使用哪些降价或促销方式，并把起止时间设为 10 月 5–11 日。
  2. 守住利润：扣除佣金、履约成本和广告费后，重新核算每个优惠价。
  3. 补足库存：确认 WFS 或自有仓的库存能覆盖一整周的高销量，并为节日季开局留出余量。
  4. 提高 Sponsored Products 预算：活动期间每天盯预算消耗，尤其是 10 月 5 日和亚马逊大促的两天。
  5. 优化 Listing：更新标题、图片和属性，突出送礼场景，修复后台提示的内容质量问题。
  6. 守住绩效：每单上传有效物流单号，及时回复买家消息。订单高峰期绩效很容易掉。
小提示：把这一周当作黑五的彩排。每天记录销量、转化率和广告费，11 月就能用真实数据做计划。
