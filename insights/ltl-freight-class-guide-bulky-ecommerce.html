LTL Freight Class Explained: How Density Sets Your Shipping Cost for Bulky Items

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-05 · 6 min read

If you sell furniture, fitness equipment, appliances or anything shipped on a pallet, your freight bill depends heavily on one number: the freight class. Since July 2025, that class depends more on density than ever before, and a few inches of packaging can move your shipment into a more expensive class. Here is how LTL freight classes work and how to keep them, and your costs, down.

What LTL and freight class mean

Less-than-truckload (LTL) freight is shipping that fills only part of a truck, typically one or a few pallets. LTL carriers price shipments using the National Motor Freight Classification (NMFC), managed by the National Motor Freight Traffic Association (NMFTA). Classes run from 50 to 500; the lower the class, the cheaper the freight usually is per pound, because dense freight uses truck space efficiently.

When should you use LTL instead of parcel? Very heavy or oversized items, and multiple boxes going to the same address, are often cheaper as a single palletized LTL shipment. Get both quotes for your most common order types before deciding.

The 2025 density scale

According to Cole International and carrier Old Dominion, NMFC Docket 2025-1 took effect on July 19, 2025. It moved many products to density-based classification and expanded the density scale from 11 to 13 tiers, adding classes 55 and 50 for very dense freight:

How to calculate density

  1. Measure the shipment as it will travel, including the pallet: length × width × height in inches.
  2. Divide by 1,728 to get cubic feet.
  3. Divide the total weight in pounds by the cubic feet. The result is pounds per cubic foot (pcf).
  4. Find the class for that density in the table above.

Ways to lower your freight class

  • Cut empty space. Oversized cartons and pallets with overhang increase volume without adding weight, lowering density.
  • Build full, square pallets. Stack evenly and avoid pyramid shapes; carriers measure the outer dimensions.
  • Ship knocked-down or flat-packed where possible; furniture that ships flat is much denser.
  • Combine SKUs on one pallet when they go to the same destination.
  • Use the right pallet height. Many carriers charge by the space a pallet takes, so very tall, light pallets are expensive.

Watch the accessorial fees

For e-commerce, the base rate is only part of the cost. Delivering to homes often needs extra services that carriers charge separately, such as liftgate, residential delivery, delivery appointments and inside delivery. Ask for these to be listed in every quote. Amazon now sells LTL labels through Buy Shipping; see our news on Amazon and Walmart bulky shipping.

Avoid reclassification charges

Carriers inspect and re-weigh shipments. If your declared class or weight is wrong, they can reclassify the shipment and bill the difference, often with an inspection fee. Keep accurate dimensions and weights per SKU and per pallet configuration, and update them when packaging changes.

Tip: Build a simple sheet per pallet configuration: dimensions, weight, density and class. Recalculate it whenever you change cartons or pallet patterns.

零担运费等级详解：密度如何决定大件商品运费

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-05 · 约 6 分钟阅读

如果你卖家具、健身器材、家电或任何需要打托发货的产品，你的货运账单很大程度上取决于一个数字：运费等级（freight class）。自 2025 年 7 月起，这个等级比以往更依赖密度，包装多出几英寸，就可能让货物跳到更贵的等级。下面讲清楚零担运费等级如何运作，以及如何把等级和成本降下来。

什么是零担和运费等级

零担（LTL）货运指只占用卡车部分空间的运输，通常是一托或几托货。零担承运商使用由美国国家汽车货运协会（NMFTA）管理的《国家汽车货运分类》（NMFC）来定价。等级从 50 到 500，等级越低，每磅运费通常越便宜，因为高密度货物能更高效地利用车厢空间。

什么时候该用零担而不是快递？特别重或超大的商品，以及发往同一地址的多箱货物，打托走零担往往更便宜。针对最常见的订单类型，两种报价都拿一下再做决定。

如何计算密度

  1. 按实际运输状态测量货物尺寸（含托盘）：长 × 宽 × 高（英寸）。
  2. 除以 1,728，得到立方英尺。
  3. 用总重量（磅）除以立方英尺，得到密度（磅/立方英尺，pcf）。
  4. 在上表中查找对应等级。

降低运费等级的方法

  • 减少空隙：纸箱过大、托盘外沿超出，会增加体积却不增加重量，从而降低密度。
  • 打方正满托：码放整齐，避免金字塔形，承运商按外部尺寸测量。
  • 尽量拆装或扁平包装：扁平包装的家具密度高得多。
  • 合并 SKU：发往同一目的地的货物尽量拼在一托。
  • 控制托盘高度：很多承运商按占用空间计费，又高又轻的托盘很贵。

注意附加费

对电商来说，基础运价只是成本的一部分。送货到住宅往往需要尾板、住宅派送、预约派送、室内送货等额外服务，承运商会单独收费。每次报价都要让对方逐项列出。亚马逊现已通过 Buy Shipping 销售零担面单，详见我们关于亚马逊和沃尔玛大件物流新动向的文章。

避免重新分级费用

承运商会抽查并复称货物。如果申报的等级或重量不对，承运商可以重新分级并补收差价，通常还会加收检验费。按 SKU 和打托方式记录准确的尺寸和重量，包装变化时及时更新。

小提示：为每种打托方式建一张表：尺寸、重量、密度和等级。每次更换纸箱或码垛方式都重新计算一遍。
