USTR Proposes Keeping China Ship Fees on Hold Until Jan 9, 2027

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-31 · 5 min read

A set of US port fees on Chinese-built and Chinese-operated ships has been on hold for a year, and the pause was due to end on November 9. Now the US Trade Representative wants to keep it on hold a little longer. On October 26, 2026, USTR proposed extending the suspension through January 9, 2027. For importers who ship from China by ocean, this affects freight cost risk going into early 2027.

What USTR proposed

  • Two more months. USTR is "proposing to modify the action ... to further suspend that action for two months, through January 9, 2027," according to the Federal Register notice (Oct 26, 2026). The extra suspension would run from November 10, 2026 to January 9, 2027.
  • What is covered. No one would owe the service fees on maritime transport in Annexes I, II and III of the April 2025 action, or the Annex V.A duties on cargo-handling equipment such as ship-to-shore cranes, during that period.
  • Why. The stated reason is "to allow for further negotiation with China." A USTR statement quoted by gCaptain (Oct 23, 2026) said the US is "continuing to engage with China with the goal of obtaining the elimination of the acts, policies, and practices at issue."
  • Comments. Written comments were due by 5 p.m. ET on October 30, 2026 (docket USTR-2026-0694), so a final decision could come before November 9.

The fees that are on hold

Under the April 23, 2025 notice, Chinese vessel owners and operators would pay $50 per net ton from October 14, 2025, rising to $80 in April 2026, $110 in 2027 and $140 in 2028. Chinese-built vessels would pay the higher of a per-net-ton fee ($18 rising to $33) or a fee per container discharged, which was set to climb from $120 to $250 by 2028. Fees could be charged up to five times a year per vessel.

Supply Chain Dive (Oct 26, 2026) reports that trade groups wrote to USTR on September 28 urging an extension, warning that new or resumed fees "would create immediate and far-reaching consequences." gCaptain notes that China also suspended its retaliatory fees on US-linked vessels, and that the US notice does not say whether China will extend its own pause.

What it means for importers (our analysis)

No new fee shock before January. If finalized, the extension removes the risk that carriers add new port-fee surcharges on China–US lanes in late 2026, during the final weeks of peak season.

But the risk is only postponed. A two-month extension is short. If talks stall, fees could return on January 10, 2027, just as importers plan spring and pre-Chinese New Year shipments. Carriers could pass costs on through surcharges.

Watch your freight contracts. Check whether your ocean contracts or forwarder quotes let carriers add new regulatory surcharges, and how much notice they must give.

Keep the bigger tariff picture in view. Port fees are separate from product tariffs. Also watch the Section 301 exclusions expiring November 9 and any changes to duty rates on your products.

Checklist for China-to-US importers

  1. Watch for USTR's final decision before November 9.
  2. Ask your forwarder how carriers would pass on port fees if they return in January.
  3. Review surcharge clauses in your ocean contracts.
  4. Plan Q1 2027 shipments with a cost buffer in case fees resume.
  5. Track China's response on its own port-fee suspension.

USTR 拟将中国船舶港口费暂停期延至 2027 年 1 月 9 日

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-31 · 约 5 分钟阅读

美国对中国建造和中国运营船舶征收的港口费已经暂停了一年，原定 11 月 9 日到期。现在美国贸易代表办公室（USTR）打算再延长一段时间：2026 年 10 月 26 日，USTR 提议将暂停期延长至 2027 年 1 月 9 日。对走海运从中国进口的卖家来说，这关系到 2027 年初的运费风险。

USTR 提议了什么

  • 再延两个月。据 联邦公报公告（2026 年 10 月 26 日），USTR"拟修改该措施，再暂停两个月，至 2027 年 1 月 9 日"。新增暂停期为 2026 年 11 月 10 日至 2027 年 1 月 9 日。
  • 覆盖范围。暂停期间，任何一方都无需缴纳 2025 年 4 月措施附件 I、II、III 中的海运服务费，以及附件 V.A 中针对岸桥等港口装卸设备的关税。
  • 原因。官方理由是"以便与中国进一步谈判"。据 gCaptain（2026 年 10 月 23 日）引用的 USTR 声明，美方"继续与中国接触，目标是消除调查涉及的相关做法和政策"。
  • 意见征集。书面意见截止时间为 2026 年 10 月 30 日美东 17 点（案卷号 USTR-2026-0694），最终决定可能在 11 月 9 日前出炉。

被暂停的是哪些费用

根据 2025 年 4 月 23 日公告，中国船东和运营商须自 2025 年 10 月 14 日起按每净吨 50 美元缴费，2026 年 4 月升至 80 美元，2027 年 110 美元，2028 年 140 美元。中国建造的船舶按每净吨费用（18 美元逐年升至 33 美元）或每卸一个集装箱的费用中较高者缴纳，后者原定从 120 美元逐步升至 2028 年的 250 美元。每艘船每年最多收取五次。

据 Supply Chain Dive（2026 年 10 月 26 日），多家行业协会 9 月 28 日致信 USTR 要求延长暂停，警告新征或恢复收费"将带来立即且深远的影响"。gCaptain 指出，中国也暂停了对美国相关船舶的反制收费，而美方公告并未说明中方是否会延长其暂停。

我们的解读

1 月前不会有新的费用冲击。如果最终落地，延期意味着 2026 年底旺季最后几周，船公司不会在中美航线上加征新的港口费附加费。

但风险只是推迟。两个月很短。如果谈判卡住，费用可能在 2027 年 1 月 10 日恢复，正好赶上进口商规划春季和春节前出货的时候，船公司可能通过附加费转嫁成本。

看好你的海运合同。检查海运合同或货代报价是否允许船公司新增监管类附加费，以及需要提前多久通知。

别忽视更大的关税图景。港口费和商品关税是两回事，同时关注 11 月 9 日到期的 301 关税排除以及你产品税率的任何变化。

中国出口美国卖家清单

  1. 关注 USTR 在 11 月 9 日前的最终决定。
  2. 询问货代：如果 1 月恢复收费，船公司将如何转嫁。
  3. 检查海运合同中的附加费条款。
  4. 2027 年第一季度出货计划预留成本缓冲。
  5. 跟踪中方是否延长其港口费暂停。
