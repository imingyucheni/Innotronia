Amazon Display Ads (Formerly Sponsored Display): A Guide for Sellers

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-09 · 6 min read

If you have been looking for "Sponsored Display" in your Amazon ad console and can't find it, you're not imagining things. Amazon has folded it into a broader product simply called display ads, run through its Amazon Ads Agent platform. This guide explains what changed, how display ads work for marketplace sellers, what they cost, and when they are worth using alongside Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands.

What changed

Amazon's Sponsored Display page now reads: "Sponsored Display is now known as display ads." Amazon says it is introducing "a single ad platform that connects your display campaigns with customers across Amazon properties including Twitch, Fire TV, and Echo Show, as well as premium placements across the open internet."

  • "Existing Sponsored Display campaigns will continue to run uninterrupted," and can be found under Display, Video, and Audio campaigns in Amazon Ads Agent.
  • To create a new one: go to Campaigns, click Create Campaign, then under "Display, Video, and Audio" click "Display."

How display ads work

According to Amazon's display ads page and display advertising guide:

TopicWhat Amazon says
Who can use it"Available for any business to get started in minutes"
Where ads appearAcross the Amazon store (e.g. homepage and product detail pages), Amazon-owned sites like Twitch and IMDb, and "thousands of third-party apps and websites"
TargetingAudiences built from "demographics and shopping signals," plus remarketing to shoppers "who have already viewed your products"
Pricing (self-service)Cost-per-click (CPC) or cost-per-thousand viewable impressions (vCPM), "with no minimum campaign spend requirements"
Managed serviceDisplay, Video, and Audio managed by Amazon "typically requires a minimum spend of $50,000 (minimum may vary per country)"
FormatsImages and video; standard sizes include 300×250, 728×90, 160×600 and 300×600
Landing pageAn Amazon product detail page or a custom external landing page
Retail rulesAds "will not serve if the advertised ASIN is out of stock or not the Featured Offer"

Source: Amazon Ads (accessed Nov 9, 2026). Features and minimums can change and vary by country.

What you pay per click

Amazon's CPC guide defines cost per click as "the amount paid when a shopper clicks on your ad," noting that "the final CPC is usually determined by an auction and is based on your adjusted bid plus additional factors." Maximum CPC is "the highest amount an advertiser could be charged for a click," and average CPC is "the average amount spent on each click." Amazon also says display ads use "automated bidding to adjust your bid based on conversion." With vCPM you pay for viewable impressions instead, which suits awareness goals.

Because display ads stop serving when your ASIN is out of stock or loses the Featured Offer, check inventory and pricing before blaming the campaign for low delivery.

When display ads make sense for sellers

  • Remarketing. Reaching shoppers who viewed your product but didn't buy is often the most efficient starting point.
  • Defending your listings. Showing ads on your own detail pages and related products can keep shoppers in your catalog.
  • New launches. Display builds awareness when search volume for a new product is still low.
  • Off-Amazon reach. Larger brands can reach Amazon audiences on third-party sites and send them back to Amazon.

For most small sellers, display ads complement rather than replace Sponsored Products bidding and Sponsored Brands. Search ads capture existing demand; display helps create and recapture it.

Setting up a first campaign

  1. Pick a clear goal: conversions for remarketing, reach for awareness.
  2. Start with a small group of best-selling ASINs that are in stock and win the Featured Offer.
  3. Use CPC for performance goals; consider vCPM for awareness.
  4. Upload clean images and, if you have it, short video.
  5. Set a modest daily budget and review results after a few weeks.
  6. Judge results by ACOS and by total sales impact; see our guide to ACOS and TACoS.

亚马逊展示广告（原 Sponsored Display）卖家指南

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-09 · 约 6 分钟阅读

如果你在亚马逊广告后台找不到"Sponsored Display"（展示型推广）了，不是你的错觉。亚马逊已把它并入一个更大的产品，直接叫"display ads"（展示广告），在 Amazon Ads Agent 平台上运行。本文讲清楚改了什么、展示广告对平台卖家怎么用、费用怎么算，以及什么时候值得和 Sponsored Products、Sponsored Brands 搭配使用。

改了什么

亚马逊Sponsored Display 页面现在写着："Sponsored Display 现更名为 display ads。"亚马逊表示正在推出"一个统一的广告平台，把你的展示广告与亚马逊旗下 Twitch、Fire TV、Echo Show 等场景的顾客，以及开放互联网上的优质广告位连接起来"。

  • "现有的 Sponsored Display 广告活动将继续正常投放"，可在 Amazon Ads Agent 的"Display, Video, and Audio"广告活动中找到。
  • 新建方法：进入"Campaigns"，点"Create Campaign"，在"Display, Video, and Audio"下点"Display"。

展示广告怎么运作

据亚马逊展示广告页面和展示广告指南：

项目亚马逊的说法
谁能用"任何企业都可以在几分钟内开始使用"
投放位置亚马逊站内（如首页、商品详情页）、Twitch 和 IMDb 等亚马逊自有网站，以及"数千个第三方应用和网站"
定向基于"人口属性和购物信号"建立受众，并可再营销"已浏览过你商品"的顾客
计费（自助）按点击（CPC）或按千次可见展示（vCPM），"无最低花费要求"
托管服务由亚马逊托管的 Display、Video、Audio"通常最低花费 50,000 美元（各国可能不同）"
素材图片和视频；标准尺寸包括 300×250、728×90、160×600、300×600
落地页亚马逊商品详情页或外部自定义落地页
零售规则"如果推广的 ASIN 缺货或不是推荐报价，广告将不会展示"

来源：Amazon Ads（2026 年 11 月 9 日访问）。功能和最低花费可能调整，各国不同。

每次点击付多少钱

亚马逊 CPC 指南将单次点击费用定义为"顾客点击你的广告时支付的金额"，并指出"最终 CPC 通常由竞价决定，基于调整后的出价及其他因素"。最高 CPC 是"广告主一次点击可能被收取的最高金额"，平均 CPC 是"每次点击的平均花费"。亚马逊还表示，展示广告"使用自动出价，根据转化调整出价"。选择 vCPM 则按可见展示付费，更适合品牌曝光目标。

ASIN 缺货或失去推荐报价时展示广告会停止投放。投放量低时，先检查库存和价格，再怀疑广告设置。

卖家什么时候适合用展示广告

  • 再营销。触达看过你商品但没下单的顾客，通常是最划算的起点。
  • 守住自己的 Listing。在自家详情页和相关商品上展示广告，把顾客留在你的产品线里。
  • 新品上市。新品搜索量还低时，展示广告能帮助建立认知。
  • 站外触达。规模较大的品牌可以在第三方网站触达亚马逊受众，再把他们带回亚马逊。

对大多数中小卖家来说，展示广告是Sponsored Products 出价策略和Sponsored Brands的补充，而非替代。搜索广告承接已有需求，展示广告负责创造和召回需求。

搭建第一个广告活动

  1. 明确目标：再营销看转化，品牌曝光看触达。
  2. 先选少量有货且赢得推荐报价的畅销 ASIN。
  3. 效果类目标用 CPC，曝光类目标可考虑 vCPM。
  4. 上传干净的图片，有条件的话加上短视频。
  5. 设置适中的日预算，几周后复盘。
  6. 同时用 ACOS 和整体销售影响评估效果，参见我们的ACOS 和 TACoS指南。
