Choosing an agency

How to Give an Agency Access to Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop Safely

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-25 · 6 min read

The first practical step with any marketplace agency is giving them access to your accounts, and it is also where many brands take unnecessary risks. Sharing your main login and password can expose bank details, tax information and two-step verification codes, and makes it hard to see who did what. Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop all have built-in ways to invite users with limited permissions. Here is how each one works and what access an agency actually needs.

Why you should never share your password

  • Control. A user invitation can be limited, changed or removed in seconds. A shared password means changing it, and updating two-step verification, every time someone leaves.
  • Accountability. Separate users let you see which person or company made a change.
  • Responsibility. Walmart says plainly that sellers are responsible for their users' actions, according to Walmart Marketplace Learn (updated Jul 2, 2025). The same logic applies on every platform: whatever happens in your account is on you.
  • Sensitive areas stay private. On TikTok Shop, some areas are only visible to the shop owner's account "for safety reasons," per TikTok's Seller Center roles guide (updated Aug 2025).

Amazon

Only the primary account holder of a Professional selling account can grant access. In an Amazon Seller Forums reply, an Amazon staff member explained that you open Settings, choose User permissions, enter the new user's details and send an invite. Once the user accepts the email link, you set their access level for each area of Seller Central. The staff member also noted that "Amazon cannot grant permissions," so this always has to come from you.

Walmart

Walmart offers three roles, according to Walmart Marketplace Learn. Admin has full access, including generating API credentials, requesting special category approvals, creating users and accepting binding legal agreements. Read and Write covers day-to-day work like setting up items and viewing reports. Read Only can view data and download reports but cannot change anything. Only Admins can add users.

TikTok Shop

TikTok Shop members are added through TikTok Business Center, where you can assign a member or a partner to your shop and pick their permissions, per TikTok's Business Center help article. Seller Center has eight roles. Even the Main Administrator role cannot see user management, account information, bank accounts, tax information or linked TikTok accounts; those stay with the shop owner.

What access an agency actually needs

  • Full channel management: listings, inventory, pricing, orders, ads and reports. On Walmart that usually means Read and Write; on TikTok Shop, roles such as Product Management, Advertising and Order Fulfillment, or Main Administrator if they run everything.
  • Ads only: advertising and reporting access, without finance or account settings.
  • Reporting or audits: view-only access.
  • Almost never: bank account, tax settings, user management, or the ability to accept legal agreements. Keep these with an owner or an officer of your company.
Grant the smallest role that lets the agency do the job, then expand it if a task really requires more. It is much easier to add permissions than to undo a mistake.

Access checklist

  1. Turn on two-step verification for your own owner account first.
  2. Invite each agency team member with their own work email, never a shared login.
  3. Choose the lowest role that covers the agreed scope of work.
  4. Keep bank, tax and user-management settings with your company.
  5. Write the access scope into your agency agreement.
  6. Review users every quarter and remove anyone who no longer needs access.
  7. When a contract ends, remove all agency users on the same day.

如何安全地给代运营开通 Amazon、Walmart、TikTok Shop 权限

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-25 · 约 6 分钟阅读

和代运营公司合作的第一步，是给对方开通店铺权限——这也是很多品牌最容易埋雷的环节。直接共享主账号密码，可能让对方接触到银行卡、税务信息和两步验证码，出了问题也查不清是谁操作的。其实 Amazon、Walmart、TikTok Shop 都自带"邀请子账号、按需授权"的功能。下面讲清楚三个平台各自怎么设置，以及代运营真正需要哪些权限。

为什么绝不能共享密码

  • 可控。子账号权限可以随时调整或一键移除；共享密码则意味着每次有人离职都要改密码、重设两步验证。
  • 可追溯。每人一个账号，谁改了什么一清二楚。
  • 责任在你。据 Walmart Marketplace Learn（2025 年 7 月 2 日更新），卖家要对其用户的操作负责。其他平台同理：店铺里发生的一切，最终都算在店主头上。
  • 敏感信息留在店主手里。据 TikTok Seller Center 角色权限说明（2025 年 8 月更新），部分页面"出于安全考虑"只对店主账号可见。

Amazon

只有专业卖家账户的主账号持有人才能授权。在 Amazon 卖家论坛的一则回复中，亚马逊工作人员说明：进入"设置"→"用户权限"，填写新用户信息并发送邀请；对方点击邮件链接接受后，再逐个模块设置访问级别。工作人员也明确表示"亚马逊无法代为授予权限"，必须由你自己操作。

Walmart

据 Walmart Marketplace Learn，Walmart 有三种角色：Admin 拥有全部权限，包括生成 API 凭证、申请特殊类目审批、创建用户、接受有法律约束力的协议；Read and Write 负责上架商品、查看报表等日常操作；Read Only 只能查看数据和下载报表，不能修改。只有 Admin 能添加用户。

TikTok Shop

据 TikTok Business Center 帮助文章，TikTok Shop 的成员通过 Business Center 添加，可以给店铺分配成员或合作伙伴并选择权限。Seller Center 共有 8 种角色，即使是主管理员也看不到用户管理、账户信息、银行账户、税务信息和绑定的 TikTok 账号——这些只留在店主账号。

代运营到底需要哪些权限

  • 全店托管：Listing、库存、定价、订单、广告和报表。Walmart 一般给 Read and Write；TikTok Shop 可给商品管理、广告、订单履约等角色，全托管时可给主管理员。
  • 只做广告：广告和报表权限即可，不需要财务和账户设置。
  • 数据分析或店铺诊断：只读权限。
  • 几乎不需要：银行账户、税务设置、用户管理、签署法律协议的权限。这些应留在公司负责人手里。
先给能完成工作的最小权限，确有需要再追加。加权限容易，撤销误操作难。

权限管理清单

  1. 先给自己的店主账号开启两步验证。
  2. 每位代运营成员用自己的工作邮箱单独邀请，绝不共用登录。
  3. 选择能覆盖约定工作范围的最低角色。
  4. 银行、税务和用户管理设置留在自己公司。
  5. 把权限范围写进代运营合同。
  6. 每季度检查一次用户列表，移除不再需要的人。
  7. 合作结束当天移除所有代运营账号。
