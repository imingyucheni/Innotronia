Prime Big Deal Days 2026 (Oct 6–7): Dates, Data and a Seller Prep Plan

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-27 · 6 min read

Amazon has confirmed that Prime Big Deal Days, its October sale for Prime members, runs on October 6–7, 2026. That leaves sellers about a week to lock in pricing, ads and stock, and one week later, on October 15, Amazon's holiday peak fulfillment fees begin. Here is what is confirmed, what last year's event tells us, and a short prep plan.

What Amazon announced

  • Dates and length. According to Amazon's announcement, the event starts at 12:01 a.m. PDT on October 6 and offers "48 hours of Prime member-exclusive deals across more than 35 categories." NBC News (Sept 23, 2026) reported the same dates and said some early deals are already live.
  • Deal drops. Amazon says new "Today's Big Deals" go live three times a day, at midnight, 8 a.m. and 1 p.m. PDT.
  • 22 countries. The event runs in 22 countries including the US, Canada, the UK, Japan and Australia; Amazon notes that dates may vary by country.
  • Alexa for Shopping. Amazon highlights deal alerts, price tracking and automatic purchase at a target price, so shoppers can be notified when your deal goes live.

What last year's event tells us

Adobe reported that US consumers spent $9.1 billion online across all retailers during the 2025 event (October 7–8), up 7.3% year over year, with discounts peaking at 18% off list price. Numerator's early read (Oct 9, 2025) found an average order of $45.42, with 44% of orders at $20 or less and 90% under $100. Everyday categories led:

Numerator also reported that 23% of shoppers bought holiday gifts during the event and 56% compared prices with other retailers.

Supply Chain Dive (Jul 13, 2026) reported that peak fees add an average of $0.32 per unit, the same as last year, with the 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge applied on top. Check exact dates and amounts in Seller Central, since they are Amazon's to change.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Low-price, repeat-purchase items win. With 44% of orders under $20, consumables, accessories and add-on items are where Prime members stock up. Higher-ticket products need a very clear discount to stand out.
  • Price comparison is the norm. More than half of shoppers compared prices elsewhere. A deal that is not the lowest price across channels may convert poorly.
  • October sales ship at off-peak fees. Orders fulfilled before October 15 avoid the peak fee, which slightly improves event margins compared with November.
  • Stock that sells now is not there in November. Plan inventory so a strong event does not leave you out of stock before Black Friday.

Checklist for the next 7 days

  1. Confirm your deals. Deal submission for this event has closed, but you can still set Prime Exclusive Discounts or coupons where eligible. Check your promotions dashboard for status.
  2. Check price floors. Make sure your deal price leaves margin after referral fees, FBA fees and ad costs.
  3. Raise ad budgets in advance. Increase daily budgets and use budget rules for October 6–7 so campaigns don't run out by midday.
  4. Refresh listings. Update main images, titles and A+ content for the gifting season and make sure every variation is in stock.
  5. Watch stock daily. Set restock alerts and plan the next inbound for the Oct 21/28 arrival dates.
  6. Review results by Oct 10. Compare units, ACoS and conversion against a normal week, then adjust for Black Friday.
Tip: Keep a separate campaign for your deal ASINs during the event. It makes it much easier to see what the discount really delivered.

2026 亚马逊秋季 Prime 大促 10 月 6–7 日：关键数据与卖家备战清单

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-27 · 约 6 分钟阅读

亚马逊已确认，2026 年 Prime 会员秋季大促（Prime Big Deal Days，俗称“秋季 Prime Day”）将于 10 月 6 日至 7 日举行。留给卖家锁定价格、广告和库存的时间只剩一周左右；紧接着 10 月 15 日，亚马逊旺季配送费开始执行。下面把已确认的信息、去年大促的数据和一份备战清单整理给你。

亚马逊公布了什么

  • 时间：根据亚马逊官方公告，活动于太平洋时间 10 月 6 日 0:01 开始，为会员提供“覆盖 35 个以上类目、为期 48 小时的专属优惠”。NBC News（2026 年 9 月 23 日）报道了相同日期，并称部分提前优惠已经上线。
  • 分时段上新：官方表示“Today's Big Deals”每天分三次上新，分别在太平洋时间 0 点、8 点和 13 点。
  • 22 个国家：包括美国、加拿大、英国、日本、澳大利亚等 22 个国家同步开展，官方提示各国日期可能不同。
  • Alexa 购物功能：支持降价提醒、价格追踪和到价自动下单，买家可以在你的优惠上线时收到通知。

去年的数据说明了什么

据 Adobe 统计，2025 年秋季大促（10 月 7 日至 8 日）期间，美国消费者在全网零售商的线上消费达 91 亿美元，同比增长 7.3%，折扣最高达标价的 18%。Numerator 2025 年 10 月 9 日发布的早期数据显示，平均客单价为 45.42 美元，44% 的订单不超过 20 美元，90% 低于 100 美元。日常消费类目领跑：

Numerator 还发现，23% 的买家在活动期间购买了节日礼物，56% 的买家会到其他零售商比价。

据 Supply Chain Dive（2026 年 7 月 13 日）报道，旺季配送费平均每件增加 0.32 美元，与去年相同，另外还要叠加 3.5% 的燃油及物流附加费。具体日期和金额以卖家后台为准，亚马逊有权调整。

我们的解读

  • 低客单、复购型产品更吃香：44% 的订单不超过 20 美元，消耗品、配件和凑单类产品是会员囤货的重点；高客单产品需要非常明显的折扣才能突围。
  • 比价是常态：超过一半的买家会去别处比价。如果你的优惠价不是全渠道最低，转化可能不理想。
  • 10 月订单按平季配送费结算：10 月 15 日之前完成配送的订单不收旺季费，大促利润比 11 月略好。
  • 现在卖掉的货，11 月就没有了：规划好库存，别让秋促卖爆之后黑五前断货。

未来 7 天行动清单

  1. 确认活动状态：本次大促的秒杀提报已截止，但符合条件的产品仍可设置 Prime 专享折扣或优惠券。到促销后台确认状态。
  2. 核对最低价：确保优惠价在扣除佣金、FBA 费用和广告费后仍有利润。
  3. 提前加广告预算：10 月 6–7 日提高日预算并设置预算规则，避免中午就花完。
  4. 优化 Listing：更新主图、标题和 A+ 页面，突出送礼场景，确保所有变体有货。
  5. 每天盯库存：设置补货提醒，按 10 月 21 日 / 28 日的入仓节点安排下一批头程。
  6. 10 月 10 日前复盘：对比平常一周的销量、ACoS 和转化率，再调整黑五策略。
小提示：大促期间把参加活动的 ASIN 单独建一个广告活动，才能看清这次折扣到底带来了多少增量。
