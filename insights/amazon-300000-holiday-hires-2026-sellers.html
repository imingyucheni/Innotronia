Amazon to Hire 300,000+ for the Holidays: What It Signals for Sellers

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-23 · 5 min read

Amazon is hiring more people for the holidays than at any time in the past three years. On October 20, 2026, Amazon said it plans to hire more than 300,000 workers across its US operations network for the holiday season, up from a 250,000 target in each of the previous three years. For sellers, more warehouse and delivery staff usually means Amazon expects higher order volumes. Here is what was announced and what it means for your Q4.

What Amazon announced

  • Over 300,000 roles. Seasonal, part-time and full-time roles across Amazon's US core operations network, with new openings posted weekly from October through December, according to FOX 9 / FOX Business (Oct 20, 2026).
  • First increase in three years. FOX Business, citing Reuters, called it Amazon's first increase in holiday recruitment in three years. PPC Land (Oct 21, 2026) notes the 250,000 target applied to each of the previous three seasons.
  • Pay. Seasonal workers average about $20 an hour; full-time operations roles start at a minimum of $20; average hourly pay across core operations is nearly $24.
  • Healthcare from day one. From January 1, 2027, seasonal employees working at least 30 hours a week become eligible for healthcare from their first day, with plans starting at $5 a week.

PPC Land points out that the 2025 target was described as "up to" 250,000 and the 2026 one as "over" 300,000, and that scope descriptions may differ, so the roughly 20% increase is an approximation.

The wider holiday picture

Amazon's plan sits alongside retail-wide expectations. The National Retail Federation expects retailers to hire 325,000 to 375,000 temporary workers this season, compared with an estimated 356,000 in 2025, and forecasts November–December sales up 4.2% to $1.013 trillion, according to Chain Drug Review (Oct 15, 2026). Note that NRF's figure covers retailers broadly and is not directly comparable with Amazon's operations hiring. For more on the forecast, see our NRF holiday forecast article.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Amazon is planning for more volume. Companies don't add 50,000 roles unless they expect to need them. Expect strong traffic around Black Friday (November 27) and Cyber Monday (November 30), and plan inventory accordingly.

Capacity helps, but deadlines still matter. More staff can speed up receiving and delivery, but peak-season FBA check-in can still slow down. Ship inventory early rather than counting on faster processing, and watch your inbound shipment status closely.

Faster delivery raises the bar. If Amazon keeps Prime delivery fast through December, seller-fulfilled offers will be compared against that. Check your handling times and carrier cutoffs; see our 2026 holiday shipping deadlines guide.

Costs are rising too. Higher wages are part of why fulfillment fees and peak surcharges keep climbing. Recheck your Q4 margins with current FBA fees and peak surcharges included.

Plan for returns as well. Higher December volume means more January returns, especially with Amazon's extended holiday return window. Keep budget and storage space for returned stock; see our Amazon holiday returns guide.

Checklist for Q4

  1. Confirm all FBA inventory for Black Friday week is shipped or in transit.
  2. Track inbound shipments daily and resolve problems quickly.
  3. Set realistic handling times for seller-fulfilled orders.
  4. Recalculate margins with peak fees for every promoted SKU.
  5. Plan a restock order for December based on November sell-through.

亚马逊假日季招聘超 30 万人：对卖家意味着什么

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-23 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊今年假日季的招聘规模是三年来最大的。2026 年 10 月 20 日，亚马逊宣布计划为假日季在美国运营网络招聘超过 30 万人，而此前三年的目标均为 25 万人。对卖家来说，仓储和配送人手增加，通常意味着亚马逊预期订单量会更高。下面梳理公告内容以及对你 Q4 的影响。

亚马逊公布了什么

  • 超过 30 万个岗位。据 FOX 9 / FOX Business（2026 年 10 月 20 日），岗位涵盖亚马逊美国核心运营网络的季节性、兼职和全职职位，10 月至 12 月每周发布新岗位。
  • 三年来首次增加。FOX Business 援引路透社称，这是亚马逊三年来首次扩大假日季招聘。PPC Land（2026 年 10 月 21 日）指出，此前三个假日季的目标均为 25 万人。
  • 薪资。季节性员工平均时薪约 20 美元；全职运营岗位起薪至少 20 美元；核心运营员工平均时薪近 24 美元。
  • 入职即享医保。自 2027 年 1 月 1 日起，每周工作至少 30 小时的季节性员工入职第一天即可享受医保，计划每周 5 美元起。

PPC Land 指出，2025 年目标表述为“最多”25 万人，2026 年为“超过”30 万人，口径可能不同，约 20% 的增幅只是近似值。

更大的假日季背景

亚马逊的计划与零售行业整体预期相呼应。据 Chain Drug Review（2026 年 10 月 15 日）报道，美国零售联合会（NRF）预计今年零售商将招聘 32.5 万–37.5 万名临时工（2025 年约 35.6 万），并预测 11–12 月销售增长 4.2% 至 1.013 万亿美元。注意 NRF 的数字覆盖整个零售业，与亚马逊的运营招聘不能直接比较。预测详情见我们的《NRF 假日季预测》。

我们的解读

亚马逊在为更大的单量做准备。企业不会无缘无故多招 5 万人。预计黑五（11 月 27 日）和网一（11 月 30 日）前后流量强劲，请据此规划库存。

产能提升，但截止时间依然关键。人手增加有助于加快入仓和配送，但旺季 FBA 入库仍可能变慢。尽早发货入仓，不要指望处理速度更快，并密切跟踪入库状态。

配送更快，门槛更高。如果亚马逊在 12 月保持 Prime 快速配送，自发货商品会被拿来比较。检查备货时间和承运商截单时间，参见我们的《2026 假日季发货截止日期指南》。

成本也在上升。工资上涨是配送费和旺季附加费持续上涨的原因之一。用当前 FBA 费用和旺季附加费重新核算 Q4 毛利。

同时为退货做准备。12 月单量越大，1 月退货越多，尤其亚马逊还有假日延长退货期。为退回库存预留预算和仓位，参见我们的《亚马逊假日延长退货指南》。

Q4 行动清单

  1. 确认黑五周所需的 FBA 库存已发出或在途。
  2. 每天跟踪入库货件，及时处理问题。
  3. 为自发货订单设置合理的备货时间。
  4. 为每个促销 SKU 计入旺季费用后重新核算毛利。
  5. 根据 11 月售罄情况规划 12 月补货。
