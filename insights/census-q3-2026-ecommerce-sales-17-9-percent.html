Census Q3 2026: US E-Commerce Hits $347.3B and 17.9% of Retail

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-20 · 5 min read

Online shopping took a bigger slice of US retail again in the third quarter. On November 19, 2026, the US Census Bureau reported that retail e-commerce sales reached $347.3 billion in Q3 2026, up 10.4% from a year earlier and equal to 17.9% of all retail sales. Growth slowed from the second quarter, but online is still growing almost twice as fast as retail overall.

The headline numbers

According to the Census Bureau's Q3 2026 Quarterly Retail E-Commerce Sales report (seasonally adjusted, not adjusted for price changes):

  • E-commerce sales: $347.3 billion, up 1.6% (±0.4%) from Q2 2026 and up 10.4% (±0.9%) from Q3 2025.
  • Total retail sales: $1,941.9 billion, up 1.1% from Q2 and 5.6% from a year earlier.
  • E-commerce share: 17.9% of total retail sales, up from 17.8% in Q2 and 17.1% a year earlier.
  • Revisions: Census revised earlier quarters using its annual survey. Q2 2026 e-commerce is now $341.7 billion, with year-over-year growth of 11.2%.

Breitbart (Nov 19) reported that the 17.9% share is the highest since Census began tracking it in 1999, and that the 1.6% quarterly gain was the smallest since Q2 2025. PYMNTS (Nov 20) noted that e-commerce took "nearly 18 cents of every U.S. retail dollar" and accounted for almost a third of the increase in retail spending from a year earlier.

Our check of the "almost a third" figure from Table 1: retail sales grew by $102.4 billion year over year, and e-commerce grew by $32.8 billion, or about 32% of the increase (Calculation in millions: 347,322 − 314,500 = 32,822; 1,941,858 − 1,839,422 = 102,436). Retail outside e-commerce grew about 4.6%, in line with PYMNTS's figure.

The holiday pattern in unadjusted data

The seasonally adjusted series smooths out the holidays. The not-adjusted figures in the same report show how strong Q4 is for online sellers: in Q4 2025, unadjusted e-commerce sales were $365.4 billion, or 18.9% of retail, up 20.1% from Q3 2025. In Q1 2026 they fell 17.3% quarter over quarter to $302.1 billion. For Q3 2026, unadjusted e-commerce sales were $335.7 billion, or 17.2% of retail. If the usual pattern holds, Q4 is when your annual share of sales is decided, and January is when demand drops sharply, so plan cash and inventory for both.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Online keeps gaining share, slowly. The share rose 0.8 points in a year. That is steady, not explosive, so growth will come more from winning share within channels than from the market lifting everyone.
  • Double-digit growth heading into Q4. A 10.4% year-over-year pace going into the holidays supports a solid peak, in line with the NRF holiday forecast. Plan inventory on your own sell-through data, not the headline.
  • Use the official series for planning. Census numbers are preliminary and get revised. Use them for direction, and see our guide on how to size your US market with official data.
  • Monthly data comes first. For a faster read, the monthly nonstore retailers figure (see our September retail sales report) arrives weeks before the quarterly report.

What to do with this

  1. Compare your own Q3 year-over-year growth with the 10.4% market rate. Below it means you are losing share.
  2. Check which channels (Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop, your own site) drove your growth.
  3. Set Q1 2027 budgets assuming continued online share gains, but stress-test for slower growth.
  4. Mark February 19, 2027, when Census releases Q4 2026 data.

美国人口普查局：2026 年三季度电商销售 3,473 亿美元，占零售 17.9%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-20 · 约 5 分钟阅读

三季度，网购在美国零售中的份额再次提升。美国人口普查局 2026 年 11 月 19 日公布，2026 年第三季度零售电商销售额达 3,473 亿美元，同比增长 10.4%，占零售总额的 17.9%。增速较二季度放缓，但线上增速仍接近零售整体的两倍。

核心数据

根据 人口普查局《2026 年第三季度零售电商销售》报告（经季节调整，未经价格调整）：

  • 电商销售额：3,473 亿美元，环比增长 1.6%（±0.4%），同比增长 10.4%（±0.9%）。
  • 零售总额：19,419 亿美元，环比增长 1.1%，同比增长 5.6%。
  • 电商占比：17.9%，二季度为 17.8%，去年同期为 17.1%。
  • 数据修订：人口普查局根据年度调查修订了以往季度，2026 年二季度电商销售额修订为 3,417 亿美元，同比增长 11.2%。

据 Breitbart（11 月 19 日） 报道，17.9% 是人口普查局自 1999 年开始统计以来的最高占比，1.6% 的环比增幅则是 2025 年二季度以来最小。PYMNTS（11 月 20 日） 指出，美国每 1 美元零售消费中电商拿走了近 18 美分，并贡献了零售同比增量的近三分之一。

未经季节调整数据里的旺季规律

季节调整后的数据会抹平节日波动。同一份报告中未经调整的数据显示了四季度对线上卖家有多重要：2025 年四季度，未调整电商销售额为 3,654 亿美元，占零售 18.9%，环比增长 20.1%；2026 年一季度则环比下降 17.3%，至 3,021 亿美元。2026 年三季度未调整电商销售额为 3,357 亿美元，占零售 17.2%。如果延续往年规律，四季度决定全年份额，1 月需求会明显回落，现金流和库存都要提前规划。

我们的解读

  • 线上份额稳步提升。一年内占比提高 0.8 个百分点，属于稳步而非爆发式增长；卖家的增长更多要靠在渠道内抢份额，而不是等市场整体上涨。
  • 带着两位数增速进入四季度。10.4% 的同比增速为旺季提供了支撑，与 NRF 假日季预测方向一致。但备货要以自己的动销数据为准，不要只看大盘。
  • 规划时用官方数据。人口普查局数据是初值，之后还会修订，适合判断方向。可参考我们的如何用官方数据测算美国市场规模。
  • 月度数据更早出。想更快掌握趋势，可看月度无店铺零售数据（见我们的9 月零售数据解读），比季度报告早几周发布。

卖家可以怎么用

  1. 把自己三季度的同比增速与 10.4% 的市场增速对比，低于大盘就意味着在丢份额。
  2. 拆分增长来源：Amazon、Walmart、TikTok Shop、独立站分别贡献多少。
  3. 制定 2027 年一季度预算时，假设线上份额继续提升，同时做好增速放缓的压力测试。
  4. 记下 2027 年 2 月 19 日，人口普查局届时发布 2026 年四季度数据。
