How to Sell on Amazon Business: B2B Pricing, Quantity Discounts and Quotes

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-30 · 6 min read

Most Amazon sellers think only about consumers, but businesses, schools, hospitals and government offices buy on Amazon too, often in larger quantities. Amazon Business gives sellers tools to reach them: business-only prices, quantity discounts and quote requests. For factories and brands with case packs or office, industrial and supply products, it can be an easy extra channel. Here is how it works.

Amazon Business in numbers

Amazon's Amazon Business page for sellers gives these figures:

MetricFigure (per Amazon)
Annualized sales driven by Amazon Business$35 billion
Organizations served globally (excluding emerging markets)8 million
Fortune 100 companies shopping on Amazon Business97

Source: sell.amazon.com Amazon Business page (accessed Oct 30, 2026). The page shows no date, so figures may not be the latest; check Amazon's current materials.

Amazon also says business customers tend to buy "in larger quantities and return items less," which is good for margins and account health.

Who can sell to business customers

You need a Professional selling account. Amazon's FAQ says, "To start using Amazon Business selling tools, sign up for a Professional selling account." Per Amazon's pricing page, the Professional plan costs $39.99 a month plus selling fees, and it includes "Sell B2B with Amazon Business." Amazon doesn't list a separate fee for the business features; its FAQ mentions lower referral fees on larger purchases but gives no figures.

The main tools

  • Business Price. A price shown only to verified business customers, set in the "Business Price" column on Manage Inventory or with Automate Pricing rules.
  • Quantity discounts. Tiered prices that reward larger orders, for example a lower unit price at 10 or 50 units.
  • Business-only offers. Offers and discounts visible only to business customers.
  • Quote requests. Review and respond to requests for quotes using Manage quotes in Seller Central.
  • B2B Central. A hub in Seller Central with tools such as B2B Product Opportunities, Case Pack Product Opportunities and Business Discounts Insights.
  • Package hierarchies. List single units, case packs and pallets so buyers can order the size they need.
  • Tax exemption. Amazon's Tax-Exemption program automates exemptions on qualified purchases.
  • Certifications. Add diversity and quality certifications to your seller profile, which some procurement teams look for.
  • APIs. Amazon Business APIs in the Selling Partner API can automate business prices and quantity discounts.

Which products do well

Think about what offices, warehouses, clinics, schools, restaurants and contractors reorder: cleaning and janitorial supplies, packaging and shipping materials, office and breakroom items, tools and safety gear, lab and medical consumables, and spare parts. Products that come naturally in case packs are a strong fit. Amazon's own Case Pack Product Opportunities tool in B2B Central can show demand you're missing.

Start with your existing best sellers. For example, add a business price a few percent below your consumer price and two quantity tiers, then watch Business Discounts Insights before expanding.

Setting quantity discounts that make sense

  1. Work out your margin per unit at each order size, including FBA or shipping costs.
  2. Pick tiers that match how businesses buy (a case, several cases, a pallet).
  3. Make sure each discount still covers fees and ad costs.
  4. If you offer case packs, list them as separate offers with the right package hierarchy.
  5. Review results monthly and adjust tiers that don't get used.

Common mistakes

  • Discounts that ignore fees. A deep quantity discount on an FBA item can turn a profitable SKU into a loss once referral and fulfillment fees are counted.
  • Slow quote replies. Procurement teams compare suppliers; a quote answered days later is often too late.
  • Thin listings. Business buyers want specs, pack counts and compliance details in the listing, not just lifestyle images.

Getting started checklist

  1. Confirm you have a Professional selling account.
  2. Open B2B Central from the B2B menu in Seller Central.
  3. Set business prices and quantity discounts on top SKUs.
  4. Add case packs or pallets if you have them.
  5. Turn on quote notifications and respond quickly.
  6. Add any certifications your business holds to your profile.
  7. Check the Amazon Tax-Exemption program settings.

如何在 Amazon Business 上做 B2B：企业价、数量折扣与询价

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-30 · 约 6 分钟阅读

大多数亚马逊卖家只盯着个人消费者，其实企业、学校、医院和政府机构也在亚马逊上采购，而且单量往往更大。Amazon Business（亚马逊企业购）为卖家提供触达这些客户的工具：企业专享价、数量折扣和询价。对于有整箱装或办公、工业、耗材类产品的工厂和品牌来说，这是一个很容易上手的增量渠道。下面讲清楚它怎么运作。

Amazon Business 数据

亚马逊面向卖家的 Amazon Business 页面给出以下数据：

指标数据（据亚马逊）
Amazon Business 带动的年化销售额350 亿美元
全球服务的机构数（不含新兴市场）800 万
在 Amazon Business 采购的财富 100 强企业97 家

来源：sell.amazon.com Amazon Business 页面（2026 年 10 月 30 日访问）。页面未标注日期，数据可能不是最新，请以亚马逊最新资料为准。

亚马逊还表示，企业客户往往"采购量更大、退货更少"，这对利润和账户健康都有好处。

谁能向企业客户销售

需要专业卖家账户。亚马逊 FAQ 写道："要使用 Amazon Business 销售工具，请注册专业卖家账户。"据亚马逊费用页面，专业计划月费 39.99 美元另加销售佣金，并包含"通过 Amazon Business 进行 B2B 销售"。亚马逊没有列出企业功能的单独费用；FAQ 提到大额采购可享更低佣金，但未给出具体数字。

主要工具

  • 企业价（Business Price）。只对经过验证的企业客户显示的价格，可在"管理库存"的 Business Price 列设置，或通过自动定价规则设置。
  • 数量折扣。按采购量分档定价，比如买 10 件或 50 件单价更低。
  • 企业专享报价。只对企业客户可见的报价和折扣。
  • 询价。在卖家后台的 Manage quotes 中查看并回复询价。
  • B2B Central。卖家后台的 B2B 工具中心，包括 B2B 选品机会、整箱装选品机会和企业折扣洞察等工具。
  • 包装层级。可分别上架单件、整箱和整托，方便买家按需下单。
  • 免税。亚马逊免税计划可对符合条件的采购自动免税。
  • 资质认证。可在卖家资料中添加多元化和质量认证，部分企业采购部门会看重这些。
  • API。SP-API 中的 Amazon Business API 可自动管理企业价和数量折扣。

哪些产品适合

想想办公室、仓库、诊所、学校、餐厅和施工方经常复购什么：清洁用品、包装和物流耗材、办公和茶水间用品、工具和劳保用品、实验室和医疗耗材、备品备件。天然以整箱销售的产品尤其合适。B2B Central 里的整箱装选品机会工具，可以帮你发现被忽略的需求。

先从现有爆款开始：例如设置比零售价略低几个百分点的企业价和两档数量折扣，观察企业折扣洞察的数据后再扩大范围。

如何设置合理的数量折扣

  1. 计算每个订单量下的单件利润，包括 FBA 或运费成本。
  2. 按企业采购习惯设档（一箱、多箱、一托）。
  3. 确保每档折扣仍能覆盖平台费用和广告成本。
  4. 有整箱装的，用正确的包装层级单独上架。
  5. 每月复盘，调整没人用的折扣档。

常见误区

  • 折扣忽略费用。FBA 商品的数量折扣定得太深，算上佣金和配送费后，赚钱的 SKU 可能变成亏钱。
  • 询价回复太慢。采购部门会同时比较多家供应商，隔几天才回复往往已经错过。
  • Listing 信息太少。企业买家需要规格、每箱数量和合规信息，而不只是场景图。

上手清单

  1. 确认是专业卖家账户。
  2. 从卖家后台 B2B 菜单进入 B2B Central。
  3. 为主力 SKU 设置企业价和数量折扣。
  4. 有整箱或整托的一并上架。
  5. 开启询价通知并快速回复。
  6. 把公司已有的资质认证添加到卖家资料。
  7. 检查亚马逊免税计划设置。
