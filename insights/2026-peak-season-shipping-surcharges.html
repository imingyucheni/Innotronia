2026 Peak Season Surcharges Start This Weekend: UPS, FedEx and USPS

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-26 · 6 min read

Holiday shipping costs start climbing this weekend. UPS's first 2026 peak charges begin on September 27, FedEx's on September 28, and USPS's temporary holiday prices on October 4, and every carrier is charging more per package than last year. If you ship to US homes, your Q4 margin math needs updating now, not in December.

What the carriers announced

USPS: temporary holiday prices from Oct 4

On August 25, 2026, the U.S. Postal Service announced a temporary price change for Priority Mail Express, Priority Mail, USPS Ground Advantage and Parcel Select, in effect from October 4, 2026 to January 17, 2027. Retail Dive reported the increase averages about 6%. According to FP, a postage-meter provider, the Postal Regulatory Commission approved the change on September 9, 2026. First-Class Mail and international products are not included.

UPS: phases from Sept 27

According to Supply Chain Dive (Aug 27, 2026), UPS's demand surcharges on Additional Handling, Large Package and Over Maximum Limits start on September 27; surcharges on Ground Residential, Air and Ground Saver start on October 25; the highest rates apply November 22 to December 26; and the program ends January 16, 2027. Value Added Resource and Reveel list the Ground Residential surcharge at $0.50 in the shoulder periods and $0.75 at the peak, up from $0.60 at last year's peak.

FedEx: phases from Sept 28

Supply Chain Dive (Jul 23, 2026) reported that FedEx's Ground Residential peak surcharge rises to $0.80 per package from $0.65 in 2025, about 23% higher. Charges on Additional Handling, Oversize and unauthorized packages start September 28; most residential surcharges start October 26; the highest rates apply November 23 to December 27; and the program runs to January 17, 2027. Reveel lists Ground Economy at $2.55 in the shoulder periods and $4.05 at the peak.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Residential is where it hurts. Most marketplace orders go to homes. A $0.75–$0.80 surcharge on a $10 label is a 7–8% cost increase on that shipment, before any oversize or handling charge.
  • Big and awkward boxes cost the most. The oversize and additional-handling charges start first, in late September, and are much larger per package than the residential fee. Review your packaging for bulky SKUs now.
  • USPS is no longer a free pass. Lightweight Ground Advantage parcels still see the smallest increases, but heavier parcels to far zones rise by several dollars.
  • Your rates may differ. Contract shippers, marketplace-purchased labels and third-party label platforms can have different surcharge terms. Check your own agreement.

Checklist: what to do this week

  1. Pull your last Q4 shipping data by carrier, service, zone and weight, and apply this year's surcharges to estimate the new cost per order.
  2. Re-price or set a free-shipping threshold for low-price SKUs where the surcharge wipes out the margin.
  3. Right-size boxes for anything close to the oversize or additional-handling limits, and recheck dimensional weight.
  4. Move inventory closer to customers (FBA, WFS or a regional 3PL) before mid-November, so more orders ship to near zones.
  5. Compare carriers per lane. Regional last-mile carriers may not add the same peak charges; ask for their Q4 terms in writing.
  6. Set customer expectations with clear holiday cut-off dates on your listings and store.
Tip: Surcharges are charged per package. Combining multi-unit orders into one box, or offering bundles, reduces how many times you pay them.

2026 旺季附加费本周末起生效：UPS、FedEx、USPS 涨了多少

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-26 · 约 6 分钟阅读

从这个周末开始，美国快递的旺季附加费陆续生效：UPS 9 月 27 日起、FedEx 9 月 28 日起、USPS 临时节日价格 10 月 4 日起，而且三家的单件收费都比去年更高。只要你发美国住宅地址，Q4 的利润测算现在就要更新，别等到 12 月对账才发现。

三大快递公司公布了什么

USPS：10 月 4 日起执行临时节日价格

2026 年 8 月 25 日，美国邮政（USPS）宣布对 Priority Mail Express、Priority Mail、USPS Ground Advantage 和 Parcel Select 实行临时调价，执行期为 2026 年 10 月 4 日至 2027 年 1 月 17 日。据 Retail Dive 报道，平均涨幅约 6%。据邮资机服务商 FP 的消息，美国邮政监管委员会（PRC）已于 2026 年 9 月 9 日批准。平信（First-Class Mail）和国际件不在调价范围内。

UPS：9 月 27 日起分阶段收取

据 Supply Chain Dive（2026 年 8 月 27 日）报道，UPS 的超长超重类附加费（Additional Handling、Large Package、Over Maximum Limits）9 月 27 日起收取；Ground 住宅件、Air 和 Ground Saver 的附加费 10 月 25 日起收取；最高峰为 11 月 22 日至 12 月 26 日；2027 年 1 月 16 日结束。Value Added Resource 和 Reveel 的汇总显示，Ground 住宅件附加费平峰期每件 $0.50，最高峰 $0.75，去年最高峰为 $0.60。

FedEx：9 月 28 日起分阶段收取

据 Supply Chain Dive（2026 年 7 月 23 日）报道，FedEx Ground 住宅件最高峰附加费从 2025 年的 $0.65 升至 $0.80，涨幅约 23%。超尺寸、额外处理等附加费 9 月 28 日起收取；多数住宅件附加费 10 月 26 日起收取；最高峰为 11 月 23 日至 12 月 27 日；持续到 2027 年 1 月 17 日。Reveel 数据显示，Ground Economy 平峰期每件 $2.55，最高峰 $4.05。

我们的解读

  • 住宅件最受影响：平台订单大多送到住宅地址。一张 $10 的面单加上 $0.75–$0.80 的附加费，运费就上涨 7%–8%，这还没算超尺寸或额外处理费。
  • 大件、异形件最贵：超尺寸和额外处理类附加费 9 月底就开始收，单件金额远高于住宅件附加费。体积大的 SKU 现在就要检查包装。
  • USPS 也不再“便宜无忧”：轻小件 Ground Advantage 涨幅最小，但重件发远区，每件要多花好几美元。
  • 实际费率因人而异：签有合同价的发件人、平台购买的面单和第三方面单平台，附加费条款可能不同，请以自己的协议为准。

本周行动清单

  1. 拉出去年 Q4 的发货数据，按快递、服务、分区和重量拆分，套用今年的附加费，算出新的单均运费。
  2. 低价 SKU 调价或设包邮门槛，避免附加费把利润吃光。
  3. 优化箱规：接近超尺寸或额外处理标准的产品重新选箱，同时复核体积重。
  4. 11 月中旬前把库存前置到 FBA、WFS 或区域海外仓，让更多订单发近区。
  5. 按线路比价：区域尾程快递不一定收同样的旺季附加费，让对方书面确认 Q4 条款。
  6. 提前告知买家节日最晚下单日期，写在 Listing 和店铺页面上。
小提示：附加费按包裹收。多件订单合箱发货、推出组合装，可以减少被收费的次数。
