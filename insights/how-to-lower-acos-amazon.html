How to Lower ACoS on Amazon Without Killing Your Sales

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-25 · 6 min read

A high ACoS is one of the most common complaints we hear from Amazon sellers, and the usual reaction, cutting bids across the board, often kills sales along with the waste. Lowering ACoS well means knowing what number you can actually afford, then removing spend that does not convert while protecting what does. Here is the process we use.

Start with the right numbers

ACoS (advertising cost of sales) is ad spend divided by ad-attributed sales. TACoS is ad spend divided by your total sales, including organic. Both matter: ACoS tells you how efficient the ads are, TACoS tells you how much of the whole business the ads are carrying.

Before changing anything, calculate your break-even ACoS: your profit margin per unit before advertising, as a percentage of the selling price. A simple example with made-up numbers:

ItemPer unit
Selling price$30.00
Product cost + inbound freight$8.00
Amazon referral + FBA fees (example)$10.50
Profit before ads$11.50
Break-even ACoSabout 38%

In this example, an ACoS of 30% is profitable and 45% loses money on every ad sale. Use your own fees from Seller Central, because referral and fulfillment fees vary by category, size and price. Then set a target ACoS by goal: below break-even for a mature product, possibly above it for a launch where you are deliberately buying ranking and reviews for a limited time.

1. Cut wasted search terms first

Open the search term report for the last 30 to 60 days and sort by spend. Look for terms with meaningful clicks and no orders, or with orders at an ACoS far above target. Add clearly irrelevant ones as negative exact or negative phrase keywords. Typical waste includes other brands you do not want to target, wrong product types, wrong sizes or compatible models you do not fit, and "free" or "used" searches. This step alone often gives the fastest improvement without hurting sales, because it removes spend that was never going to convert.

2. Structure campaigns so you can control them

  • Separate research from performance. Use automatic and broad-match campaigns to discover terms, then move proven converting terms into exact-match campaigns with their own bids and budgets.
  • Negate harvested terms in the discovery campaigns so the same search is not bid on twice.
  • Group by margin. Products with very different margins should not share one campaign and one target.
  • Branded vs non-branded. Keep searches for your own brand in their own campaign. They usually have a low ACoS and can make a weak non-branded campaign look better than it is.

3. Adjust bids with a rule, not by feel

For keywords with enough data, a simple approach is: new bid = current bid × (target ACoS ÷ actual ACoS), applied in small steps. Change bids once or twice a week, not every day, and wait for data before judging. Also review placement performance (top of search, rest of search, product pages) and use placement bid adjustments only where the numbers support it. Amazon's bidding strategies (dynamic down only, up and down, fixed) change how aggressively your bids move; if ACoS is the priority, dynamic down only is usually the more conservative choice.

4. Fix the listing, not just the ads

ACoS is also a conversion metric. If shoppers click but do not buy, cheaper clicks only hide the problem. Check the basics on your advertised ASINs:

  • Main image that reads clearly at thumbnail size, plus images that answer common questions.
  • A title and bullets that match the search terms you pay for.
  • Competitive price and delivery promise, and a visible coupon or deal where it makes sense.
  • Rating and review count relative to the products shown next to you.
  • A+ Content (for brand-registered sellers) and no stock-outs on top variations.

A better conversion rate lowers ACoS at the same bid, and it helps organic ranking too.

5. Stop advertising what should not be advertised

Pause or limit ads on SKUs with very thin margin, low stock, weak ratings, or poor conversion history. Put budget behind the products that convert, then use them to support the rest of the catalog, for example through Sponsored Brands for brand-registered sellers.

6. Watch TACoS while you optimize

If ACoS falls but total sales drop sharply, you may have cut spend that was driving organic ranking. Track TACoS and organic sales weekly. The goal is a healthy business, not the lowest possible ACoS.

Tip: Change one thing at a time and write it down with the date. When results move, you will know why.

A weekly checklist

  1. Review the search term report and add negatives.
  2. Move converting search terms into exact-match campaigns.
  3. Adjust bids on keywords with enough clicks, toward target ACoS.
  4. Check placement performance and budgets that run out early.
  5. Check stock, price, ratings and listing issues on advertised ASINs.
  6. Record ACoS, TACoS, total sales and organic sales.

亚马逊 ACoS 太高怎么办？不掉销量的降 ACoS 方法

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-25 · 约 6 分钟阅读

“ACoS 太高”是亚马逊卖家最常见的烦恼之一，而最常见的做法——全部关键词一刀切降竞价——往往把浪费和销量一起砍掉。真正有效地降低 ACoS，要先算清楚自己能承受多少，再砍掉不出单的花费，同时保护能出单的部分。下面是我们实际使用的方法。

先把账算对

ACoS（广告销售成本比）= 广告花费 ÷ 广告带来的销售额。TACoS = 广告花费 ÷ 总销售额（含自然单）。两个都要看：ACoS 反映广告效率，TACoS 反映整个店铺对广告的依赖程度。

动手之前，先算出盈亏平衡 ACoS：扣除广告前的单件利润占售价的百分比。下面用一组假设数字举例：

项目单件
售价$30.00
产品成本 + 头程运费$8.00
亚马逊佣金 + FBA 费用（示例）$10.50
广告前利润$11.50
盈亏平衡 ACoS约 38%

在这个例子里，ACoS 30% 是赚钱的，45% 则每出一单广告单都在亏。佣金和配送费会因类目、尺寸和售价不同而变化，请以 Seller Central 后台的实际费用计算。然后按阶段设定目标 ACoS：成熟产品要低于盈亏线；新品期为了冲排名和评价，可以在有限时间内有意高于盈亏线。

1. 先砍无效搜索词

打开最近 30–60 天的搜索词报告，按花费排序，找出点击不少却零出单、或者 ACoS 远高于目标的词。明显不相关的，添加为否定精准或否定词组。常见的浪费包括：你不想打的其他品牌词、品类不对、尺寸或适配型号不对，以及带 “free”“used” 的搜索。这一步通常见效最快，而且基本不影响销量，因为这些花费本来就不会转化。

2. 把广告结构搭清楚

  • 拓词和收割分开：用自动广告和广泛匹配拓词，把验证过能出单的词移到精准匹配活动里，单独设置竞价和预算。
  • 在拓词活动里否定已收割的词，避免同一个搜索被自己的两个活动重复竞价。
  • 按毛利分组：毛利差别很大的产品，不要放在同一个活动、用同一个目标。
  • 品牌词单独拆开：自家品牌词 ACoS 通常很低，混在一起会掩盖非品牌词的真实表现。

3. 按规则调竞价，而不是凭感觉

对于数据量足够的关键词，可以用一个简单公式：新竞价 = 当前竞价 ×（目标 ACoS ÷ 实际 ACoS），每次小幅调整。每周调一到两次即可，不要天天改，给数据留出时间。同时查看广告位表现（搜索结果顶部、搜索结果其余位置、商品详情页），只在数据支持的位置加溢价。竞价策略（仅降低、提高和降低、固定竞价）决定了系统调价的激进程度，如果当前重点是控 ACoS，“动态竞价-仅降低”通常更稳妥。

4. 优化 Listing，而不只是广告

ACoS 本质上也是转化率指标。买家点了不买，点击再便宜也只是掩盖问题。重点检查投放中的 ASIN：

  • 主图在缩略图尺寸下是否一眼看懂，辅图是否回答了买家常见疑问；
  • 标题和五点是否覆盖了你正在付费的搜索词；
  • 价格和配送时效是否有竞争力，必要时设置优惠券或促销；
  • 评分和评论数量与同屏竞品相比如何；
  • 已品牌备案的卖家是否做了 A+ 页面，主力变体是否断货。

转化率提升后，同样的竞价下 ACoS 自然下降，还能带动自然排名。

5. 不该投的就别投

毛利太薄、库存紧张、评分偏低、历史转化差的 SKU，暂停或限制广告。把预算集中在能转化的产品上，再用它们带动其他产品，例如品牌备案卖家可以通过品牌推广（Sponsored Brands）做关联引流。

6. 优化时盯住 TACoS

如果 ACoS 降了，但总销售额大幅下滑，说明你可能砍掉了支撑自然排名的广告。每周记录 TACoS 和自然销售额。目标是店铺整体健康，而不是把 ACoS 压到最低。

小提示：每次只改一个变量，并记录日期。数据变化时，你才知道原因是什么。

每周检查清单

  1. 查看搜索词报告，添加否定词；
  2. 把出单搜索词移入精准匹配活动；
  3. 对点击量足够的关键词，按目标 ACoS 调整竞价；
  4. 检查广告位表现，以及提前花完的预算；
  5. 检查投放 ASIN 的库存、价格、评分和 Listing 状态；
  6. 记录 ACoS、TACoS、总销售额和自然销售额。
