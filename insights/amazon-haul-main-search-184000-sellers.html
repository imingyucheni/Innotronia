Amazon Haul Joins Main Search, and 184,000 Sellers Are Already In It

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-15 · 5 min read

Amazon Haul, the low-price store Amazon launched in November 2024, is no longer a separate corner of the app. On November 9, 2026, Amazon said relevant Haul products "now surface within Amazon search results." Three days later, Marketplace Pulse counted about 184,000 sellers with Haul listings, most of whom never signed up. If you sell on Amazon.com, there is a good chance some of your products are already in Haul.

What Amazon announced

In its November 9, 2026 Haul-iday announcement, Amazon said Haul's selection "has expanded by more than 25 times to more than 10 million products" in two years, with "the number of customers making purchases growing nearly 350% year over year." The early Haul-iday sale ran November 9–14 with $1 flash deals and $0.11 deals on November 10–11, and Amazon says more deal drops will run through November and December. PYMNTS reported the same figures the same day. PPC Land noted that Amazon gave no base for the 350% figure and no sales totals.

For comparison, Amazon's first-anniversary post (Nov 2025) cited "over a million items" under $10 and said selection had grown by nearly 400%.

How 184,000 sellers ended up on Haul

According to Marketplace Pulse (Nov 12, 2026), Haul launched with strict rules: "Generic" branding, shipping from an Amazon warehouse in Dongguan, price caps of up to $20, and no presence in regular search. Most of those rules have gone. Marketplace Pulse cites Amazon seller documentation for a program called Haul Distributed Fulfillment, which cross-lists "select" products already in Amazon fulfillment centers into Haul while they stay on sale in the main store. It reports that sellers pay the same fees, Amazon funds Haul's promotions, and sellers cannot remove individual products, only opt their whole account out. The original direct-from-China route remains invitation-only.

That explains the jump from the 3,287 Haul-only sellers that Marketplace Pulse counted in March (a different method) to 184,000 today. Chinese sellers still dominate:

Marketplace Pulse adds that 16% of Haul sellers are US-based, and that nearly half of all active China-based sellers on Amazon.com now have Haul listings, compared with 22% of US sellers.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Check whether you're in Haul. Marketplace Pulse says sellers can review cross-listed offers in Seller Central. Know which SKUs are there before Black Friday Week (Nov 19–30).
  • More low-price competition in search. Sub-$10 Haul items can now appear next to your listings, which matters most in home, accessories and small-ticket categories. Make your value clear in title, images and reviews rather than only cutting price.
  • Opting out is all or nothing. If the reports are right, leaving Haul removes all your offers, including Amazon-funded discounts. Weigh the extra sales against brand and price perception.
  • Do the unit math. At around $10 a unit, referral and FBA fees take a large share; see our FBA cost breakdown.

Checklist this week

  1. Review which of your offers appear in Haul in Seller Central.
  2. Calculate margin on each cross-listed SKU after fees and any discounts.
  3. Search your main keywords on the Amazon app and note Haul items that now appear.
  4. Strengthen listings for keywords where cheap Haul items compete: bundles, quality claims, A+ content.
  5. Decide on Haul participation at the account level before peak deals start.

Amazon Haul 进入主站搜索：已有 18.4 万卖家被纳入

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-15 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊 2024 年 11 月推出的低价商城 Amazon Haul，已不再是 App 里一个独立的角落。2026 年 11 月 9 日，亚马逊宣布相关 Haul 商品"现在会出现在亚马逊搜索结果中"。三天后，Marketplace Pulse 统计约有 18.4 万卖家有 Haul listing，其中大多数并没有主动报名。如果你在 Amazon.com 卖货，你的部分商品很可能已经在 Haul 里了。

亚马逊宣布了什么

在 2026 年 11 月 9 日的 Haul-iday 公告 中，亚马逊表示两年内 Haul 商品数增长超过 25 倍，达到 1,000 万件以上，下单顾客数同比增长近 350%。早鸟 Haul-iday 活动于 11 月 9–14 日举行，有 1 美元闪购和 11 月 10–11 日的 0.11 美元特价，亚马逊称 11–12 月还会持续上新优惠。PYMNTS 当天报道了相同数据；PPC Land 指出，亚马逊没有给出 350% 的基数，也没有披露销售额。

作为对比，亚马逊 2025 年 11 月一周年文章 提到，低于 10 美元的商品"超过 100 万件"，商品数增长近 400%。

18.4 万卖家是怎么进入 Haul 的

据 Marketplace Pulse（2026 年 11 月 12 日），Haul 上线时规则很严：品牌须为"Generic"、从亚马逊东莞仓发货、价格上限最高 20 美元、不进入普通搜索。如今这些规则大多已取消。Marketplace Pulse 引用亚马逊卖家文档中名为"Haul Distributed Fulfillment"的项目：亚马逊把已在其运营中心的"部分"商品交叉上架到 Haul，同时继续在主站销售。报道称卖家费用不变，Haul 促销由亚马逊出资，卖家不能单独下架某个商品，只能整个账户退出。最初的中国直发模式仍为邀请制。

这也解释了为什么卖家数从 Marketplace Pulse 3 月统计 的 3,287 家（仅在 Haul 销售的 listing，统计口径不同）跃升到现在的 18.4 万。中国卖家仍占主导：

Marketplace Pulse 还指出，Haul 卖家中 16% 位于美国；Amazon.com 上近一半活跃的中国卖家已有 Haul listing，而美国卖家这一比例为 22%。

我们的解读

  • 先确认自己是否在 Haul。据 Marketplace Pulse，卖家可在 Seller Central 查看哪些商品被交叉上架。在 黑五周（11 月 19–30 日） 之前搞清楚哪些 SKU 在里面。
  • 搜索页低价竞争加剧。10 美元以下的 Haul 商品可能出现在你的 listing 旁边，家居、配件和小件类目影响最大。与其一味降价，不如在标题、图片和评价上把价值讲清楚。
  • 退出是"全有或全无"。如报道属实，退出 Haul 会移除你全部的 Haul 商品，包括亚马逊出资的折扣。要在增量销量与品牌、价格形象之间权衡。
  • 算清单件利润。10 美元左右的客单价，佣金和 FBA 费用占比很高，参考我们的 FBA 成本拆解。

本周行动清单

  1. 在 Seller Central 查看哪些商品出现在 Haul。
  2. 逐个计算被交叉上架 SKU 扣除费用和折扣后的利润。
  3. 在亚马逊 App 搜索核心关键词，记录新出现的 Haul 商品。
  4. 对有低价 Haul 竞品的关键词，强化 listing：组合装、品质卖点、A+ 内容。
  5. 在旺季大促开始前，决定账户层面是否参与 Haul。
