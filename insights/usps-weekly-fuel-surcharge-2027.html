USPS Adds a Weekly Fuel Surcharge in 2027: What Sellers Need to Know

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-28 · 5 min read

USPS has been the cheapest major option for many small parcels partly because it never had a FedEx- or UPS-style fuel surcharge. That is about to change. On October 26, 2026, the Postal Service said it will add a fuel surcharge to its main package services from January 2027, recalculated every week. For sellers who rely on Ground Advantage, 2027 shipping budgets just got harder to predict.

What USPS announced

  • Start and scope. From January 2027, a fuel surcharge will apply to USPS Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express and Parcel Select domestic shipments, according to Supply Chain Dive (Oct 26, 2026), citing USPS's PostalPro fuel surcharge FAQs.
  • Weekly, as a percentage. The surcharge is a percentage of base postage, updated weekly based on diesel and jet fuel prices published by the US Energy Information Administration (EIA).
  • Which fuel applies. Ground Advantage and Parcel Select follow diesel prices, while Priority Mail and Priority Mail Express follow jet fuel, according to Value Added Resource (Oct 26, 2026), which quotes the FAQ. It applies to retail and commercial shipments, including applicable return services.
  • Notice. Diesel-based percentages are published 3–5 days before they take effect and jet-fuel-based ones 3–4 days before. USPS says shippers will receive updates electronically, including through API, and historical data will be posted publicly.
  • Contracts. Negotiated contracts "may have customized language" on whether the surcharge applies.
  • Not yet known. USPS has not published the starting percentages, the formula, or any caps, Value Added Resource notes.

How we got here

This replaces a temporary approach. In March 2026, USPS announced a time-limited 8% price increase on the same four services as fuel costs rose, CNBC reported (Mar 25, 2026). That increase took effect April 26 and is scheduled to end January 17, 2027. USPS described it as less than one-third of what competitors charge for fuel alone. For comparison, UPS and FedEx fuel surcharges ran roughly 21% to more than 30% depending on service, according to a Retail Industry Leaders Association analysis (Apr 1, 2026).

USPS's finances explain the push. Shipping and packages revenue rose 7.7% year over year in fiscal Q3 2026 while volume fell 3.4%, and the Postal Service again reported a net loss, Supply Chain Dive and Value Added Resource report. Postmaster General David Steiner told the Board of Governors in August that USPS has "more price to take in the marketplace."

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

USPS costs will move every week. Until now, you could set USPS shipping prices once a year. From January, the cost of the same label can change weekly with fuel prices, just like FedEx and UPS.

The gap with FedEx and UPS may narrow. We won't know until USPS publishes its percentages, but if they approach private-carrier levels, USPS's price advantage on light parcels will shrink. Combined with the UPS 2027 rate increase and FedEx's 5.9% increase, many sellers will need to re-quote carriers for 2027.

Watch your marketplace label costs. Labels bought through marketplaces and shipping software usually pass through carrier surcharges. Check how your tools show fuel surcharges once January rates are out.

Free shipping thresholds may need a buffer. If you offer free shipping, consider building in a small fuel buffer rather than repricing every week.

Checklist for January

  1. List your USPS volume by service (Ground Advantage vs Priority Mail) to see whether diesel or jet fuel matters more to you.
  2. Watch for USPS's first published percentages and start date.
  3. Ask your shipping software or label provider how the surcharge will appear on labels and invoices.
  4. Recalculate 2027 shipping costs with a fuel line item for every carrier.
  5. Compare USPS against regional carriers for your top zones. See our USPS Ground Advantage guide for service basics.
  6. Review free-shipping thresholds and shipping charges on each marketplace.

USPS 2027 年起按周收燃油附加费：卖家须知

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-28 · 约 5 分钟阅读

USPS 之所以长期是轻小件最便宜的主流选择，一个原因是它从来没有像 FedEx、UPS 那样收燃油附加费。这一点即将改变。2026 年 10 月 26 日，美国邮政宣布从 2027 年 1 月起对主要包裹服务加收燃油附加费，并且每周调整。对依赖 Ground Advantage 的卖家来说，2027 年运费预算更难做了。

USPS 公布了什么

  • 时间和范围。据 Supply Chain Dive（2026 年 10 月 26 日）援引 USPS PostalPro 燃油附加费 FAQ，2027 年 1 月起，USPS Ground Advantage、Priority Mail、Priority Mail Express 和 Parcel Select 的国内包裹将加收燃油附加费。
  • 按周调整、按比例收取。附加费为基础邮资的一定百分比，根据美国能源信息署（EIA）公布的柴油和航空燃油价格每周更新。
  • 对应哪种燃油。据 Value Added Resource（2026 年 10 月 26 日）引用的 FAQ，Ground Advantage 和 Parcel Select 挂钩柴油价格，Priority Mail 和 Priority Mail Express 挂钩航空燃油。零售和商业寄件均适用，包括相应的退货服务。
  • 提前通知。柴油类费率提前 3–5 天公布，航空燃油类提前 3–4 天。USPS 表示会通过 API 等电子方式通知寄件人，历史数据也会公开。
  • 协议客户。议价合同"可能有定制条款"决定是否适用附加费。
  • 尚不清楚的部分。据 Value Added Resource，USPS 尚未公布起始比例、计算公式或任何上限。

来龙去脉

这将取代此前的临时方案。据CNBC 2026 年 3 月 25 日报道，随着燃油成本上涨，USPS 宣布对同样四项服务临时加价 8%，4 月 26 日生效，计划于 2027 年 1 月 17 日结束。USPS 称这不到竞争对手单项燃油附加费的三分之一。作为对比，据美国零售业领袖协会（RILA）2026 年 4 月 1 日的分析，UPS 和 FedEx 的燃油附加费视服务不同约在 21% 到 30% 以上。

USPS 的财务状况解释了这一举措。据 Supply Chain Dive 和 Value Added Resource，2026 财年第三季度包裹收入同比增长 7.7%，但包裹量下降 3.4%，USPS 再次亏损。邮政总局局长 David Steiner 8 月对理事会表示，USPS"在市场上还有提价空间"。

我们的解读

USPS 成本将每周变动。过去 USPS 运费一年定一次；从 1 月起，同一张面单的成本可能随油价每周变化，和 FedEx、UPS 一样。

与 FedEx、UPS 的价差可能缩小。在 USPS 公布比例之前无法下定论，但如果接近私营快递水平，USPS 在轻小件上的价格优势会收窄。再加上 UPS 2027 年涨价和 FedEx 的 5.9% 涨价，很多卖家需要为 2027 年重新比价。

关注平台面单成本。通过平台和物流软件购买的面单通常会转嫁承运商附加费。1 月费率出来后，检查你的工具如何显示燃油附加费。

包邮门槛要留余量。如果你提供包邮，建议预留一点燃油缓冲，而不是每周调价。

1 月前的行动清单

  1. 按服务统计 USPS 发货量（Ground Advantage 与 Priority Mail），判断柴油还是航空燃油对你影响更大。
  2. 关注 USPS 首次公布的比例和生效日期。
  3. 询问物流软件或面单服务商，附加费将如何体现在面单和账单上。
  4. 为每家承运商的 2027 年运费加入燃油附加费一项重新测算。
  5. 主要分区对比 USPS 与区域承运商，服务基础知识见我们的USPS Ground Advantage 指南。
  6. 检查各平台的包邮门槛和运费设置。
