Amazon Black Friday Week 2026 Runs Nov 19–30: What Sellers Need to Know

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-10 · 5 min read

Amazon has finally set its holiday sale calendar. On November 9, 2026, Amazon announced that Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday will run from November 19 to November 30, with "millions of deals." With Walmart's and TikTok Shop's events already announced, sellers now have the full picture for the busiest two weeks of the year.

What Amazon announced

According to Amazon's press release (Nov 9, 2026) and its first-look deals article:

  • Dates: Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday run November 19–30, kicking off at 12:01 a.m. PST on November 19.
  • Today's Big Deals: "12 days of daily themed deal drops" across categories like Toys, Tech, Fashion, Beauty, Premium Gifts and Stocking Stuffers, with limited-time deals of up to 60% off.
  • Double drops: for the first time, deals drop twice on Black Friday and Cyber Monday, at midnight and 8 a.m. PT.
  • Deal claims: up to 50% off brands such as Levi's and Beats, up to 45% off Keurig and toy brands such as NERF and Mattel, and up to 35% off Amazon devices.
  • Independent sellers: "More than 60% of sales in Amazon's store come from independent sellers, most of which are small and medium-sized businesses," Amazon says, with small-business deals highlighted.
  • AI shopping: "Alexa for Shopping" offers deal alerts, auto-buy and price history up to 365 days.

Doug Herrington, CEO of Worldwide Amazon Stores, said: "Customers count on Amazon for low prices and great selection every day, not just during the holidays." WBAL-TV (Nov 10, 2026) also reported the Nov 19–30 window and "millions of deals."

Events already under way

Amazon isn't waiting for November 19 entirely. Its press release lists an early Amazon Haul event from November 9 to 14, with $1 flash deals all week and $0.11 deals on November 10–11, and an Amazon Autos offer of a $1,500 Amazon gift card for eligible Prime members buying or leasing a new car from November 12 through November 30. Prime Video will also stream the fourth annual Black Friday Football game, which Amazon uses to drive traffic on the day itself.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Your Amazon peak is 12 days, not one. Plan inventory and ad budgets to last from November 19 through November 30, with extra headroom for the double drops on November 27 and 30.

Timing overlaps across channels. TikTok Shop starts November 11 and Walmart November 12, before Amazon. If you sell on all three, early sales elsewhere can drain stock you planned for Amazon. Allocate inventory by channel.

Price consistency matters. Because Amazon's Featured Offer can be affected by lower prices elsewhere, coordinate promotional prices across channels rather than discounting one channel first.

Inventory deadlines are close. With the event starting November 19, FBA inventory needs to be checked in beforehand. Seller-fulfilled orders depend on carrier cutoffs; see our holiday shipping deadlines guide.

Small sellers get visibility, but competition is intense. Amazon highlights small businesses, yet "millions of deals" means discounts alone won't stand out. Strong images, reviews and clear value messaging matter.

Seller checklist for Nov 19–30

  1. Confirm FBA inventory for hero SKUs is received and available.
  2. Set deal and coupon prices that beat recent prices clearly.
  3. Raise daily ad budgets and bid caps for Nov 27 and Nov 30.
  4. Align promotional prices across Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop.
  5. Monitor stock daily; pause ads before you sell out.
  6. Prepare a restock plan for December based on event sell-through.

亚马逊 2026 黑五周定档 11 月 19–30 日：卖家须知

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-10 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊终于公布了假日大促日程。2026 年 11 月 9 日，亚马逊宣布黑五周和网络星期一将于 11 月 19 日至 30 日举行，提供"数百万个优惠"。加上此前已公布的 Walmart 和 TikTok Shop 活动，卖家现在可以看清一年中最忙两周的全貌。

亚马逊公布了什么

据亚马逊新闻稿（2026 年 11 月 9 日）和其抢先看优惠文章：

  • 日期：黑五周和网络星期一为 11 月 19 日至 30 日，11 月 19 日太平洋时间 0:01 开始。
  • Today's Big Deals："连续 12 天每日主题优惠"，覆盖玩具、科技、时尚、美妆、高端礼品和圣诞袜小礼物等，限时优惠最高 60% 折扣。
  • 双波上新：首次在黑五和网一当天两次放出优惠，分别在太平洋时间午夜和上午 8 点。
  • 折扣宣传：Levi's、Beats 等品牌最高 5 折，Keurig 及 NERF、Mattel 等玩具品牌最高 55 折，亚马逊自有设备最高 65 折。
  • 独立卖家：亚马逊称"亚马逊商店超过 60% 的销售来自独立卖家，其中大多数是中小企业"，并重点推荐了小企业优惠。
  • AI 购物："Alexa for Shopping"提供降价提醒、自动下单和最长 365 天的价格历史。

亚马逊全球商店 CEO Doug Herrington 表示："顾客每天都依赖亚马逊的低价和丰富选品，而不只是在假日季。"WBAL-TV（2026 年 11 月 10 日）也报道了 11 月 19–30 日的活动窗口和"数百万个优惠"。

已经开跑的活动

亚马逊并不是全部等到 11 月 19 日。新闻稿列出：Amazon Haul 预热活动 11 月 9–14 日，全周 1 美元秒杀，11 月 10–11 日还有 0.11 美元特价；Amazon Autos 自 11 月 12 日至 30 日，符合条件的 Prime 会员购买或租赁新车可获 1,500 美元亚马逊礼品卡。Prime Video 还将直播第四届黑五橄榄球赛，为当天引流。

我们的解读

亚马逊旺季是 12 天，不是 1 天。库存和广告预算要撑过 11 月 19 日到 30 日，并为 27 日和 30 日的双波上新留足余量。

各渠道时间重叠。TikTok Shop 11 月 11 日、Walmart 11 月 12 日就开始了，都早于亚马逊。多平台卖家要防止其他渠道提前卖空原本给亚马逊的库存，按渠道分配库存。

价格要协调。其他渠道更低的价格可能影响亚马逊推荐报价，所以各渠道促销价要统一规划，不要某一渠道先降价。

库存截止日临近。活动 11 月 19 日开始，FBA 库存需在此前完成入库。自发货订单取决于承运商截单时间，参见我们的假日季寄件截止日期指南。

小卖家有曝光，但竞争激烈。亚马逊重点推荐小企业，但"数百万个优惠"意味着光靠折扣很难突围，好的图片、评价和清晰的性价比卖点同样重要。

11 月 19–30 日卖家清单

  1. 确认主推 SKU 的 FBA 库存已入库可售。
  2. 设置明显低于近期价格的秒杀和优惠券价格。
  3. 为 27 日和 30 日提高日预算和出价上限。
  4. 统一 Amazon、Walmart、TikTok Shop 的促销价格。
  5. 每天盯库存，断货前暂停广告。
  6. 根据活动动销制定 12 月补货计划。
