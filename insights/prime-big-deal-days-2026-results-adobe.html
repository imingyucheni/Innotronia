Prime Big Deal Days 2026 Results: $9.86B Online, Deeper Discounts

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-09 · 5 min read

Amazon's October Prime Big Deal Days ran on October 6–7, 2026, and the first full numbers are in. According to Adobe Digital Insights, US shoppers spent $9.86 billion online across all retailers over the two days, up 8.5% from last year and just short of Adobe's own forecast. For sellers, the more useful story is underneath the headline: which categories moved, how deep the discounts went, and what this says about the next eight weeks.

This is a follow-up to our pre-event article, Prime Big Deal Days 2026: dates, data and a seller prep plan, now with the results.

The headline numbers

Adobe's figures cover online spending at US retailers in general, not only on Amazon, and are based on direct online transactions. Two trade publications reported the same final totals: Digital Commerce 360 on October 8 and Retail Dive on October 9.

Total online spend$9.09 billion (Oct 7–8)$9.86 billion (Oct 6–7), +8.5%
Adobe's pre-event forecast—$9.9 billion, +9.2%

Mobile share of online sales—52.0% ($5.1 billion, +12.8% YoY)

The forecast figures come from Adobe's preview, reported by MediaPost on October 5, 2026. The 2025 baseline and the 18% peak discount were reported by Adobe after last year's event. In short: the event grew a little faster than last year (8.5% vs 7.3%), but discounts went deeper than Adobe expected to get there.

Which categories moved most

Adobe compared sales during the two days with average September 2026 levels. Toys more than doubled, and electronics, video games and personal care all saw large lifts. Within electronics, Adobe reported that headphones and speakers rose 300% and smartwatches 255% versus September, according to Digital Commerce 360.

Everyday goods also did well. Retail Dive, citing Adobe, reported increases of 55% in household cleaning and paper products, 45% in personal hygiene, 45% in pet food and 25% in apparel basics. Adobe also said the share of the most expensive goods sold in tracked categories rose 21% compared with the year-to-date average, led by electronics (+45%) and personal care (+34%). Shoppers were willing to trade up when the discount was right.

The deepest discounts, per Adobe, were in computers, televisions, electronics, toys and apparel. That fits the category lifts: the biggest jumps came where the markdowns were biggest.

Shopper behavior: mobile, BNPL and price comparison

  • Mobile is the main checkout. Adobe called mobile "the dominant transaction channel," with 52% of online sales. Product pages, images and A+ content need to work on a small screen first.
  • BNPL grew slowly. BNPL accounted for 6% of orders and $692.3 million in sales, up 4% year over year. Adobe expects BNPL to drive more than $1 billion in spending on Cyber Monday (Nov 30, 2026).
  • Shoppers compared prices. Retail Dive reported Numerator survey findings that half of shoppers compared prices at other retailers such as Walmart, which ran its own competing sale, and that 74% cited inflation as a reason to shop the event. Numerator's figures come from its own panel and differ from Adobe's methodology, so treat them as directional.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Discounts are doing the work. Growth was solid but not spectacular, and it came with deeper markdowns than forecast. If your margins could not support a 20%-type discount in October, expect the same pressure around Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Decide now which SKUs you will discount, by how much, and which you will protect.

Gift categories have started early. Toys, video games and electronics jumped well above September levels. If you sell giftable products, holiday demand has already begun; check your FBA inventory against the recommended arrival dates for Black Friday week rather than waiting for November.

Essentials sellers should plan for repeat buying. Strong lifts in cleaning, hygiene and pet food suggest shoppers used the event to stock up. Subscribe & Save, bundles and multi-packs are worth testing before the next sale.

Watch your post-event data. Pull your own Business Reports for October 6–7 and compare conversion rate and ad cost per order with a normal week. Your account's numbers matter more than any national average.

Next steps checklist

  1. Compare your event sales, conversion and TACoS with the prior two weeks and with last October.
  2. List the SKUs that sold out or ran low and reorder for Black Friday–Cyber Monday now.
  3. Set a discount floor per SKU based on margin after fees, including Amazon's holiday peak fulfillment fees that start October 15.
  4. Check your listings on a phone: main image, title and first bullets.
  5. Plan your Black Friday and Cyber Monday ad budgets using the cost per click you actually saw on October 6–7.

2026 秋季 Prime 大促复盘：全网线上 98.6 亿美元，折扣更深

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-09 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊秋季 Prime 大促（Prime Big Deal Days）已于 2026 年 10 月 6–7 日结束，第一份完整数据也出来了。据 Adobe Digital Insights 统计，两天内美国消费者在全网线上消费 98.6 亿美元，同比增长 8.5%，略低于 Adobe 自己的预测。对卖家来说，更有价值的是总数背后的细节：哪些品类涨得最多、折扣有多深，以及接下来 8 周该怎么打。

本文是我们大促前文章 《2026 亚马逊秋季 Prime 大促：关键数据与卖家备战清单》的后续复盘。

核心数据

需要注意，Adobe 的数据统计的是美国全渠道线上零售（不只是亚马逊站内），基于实际线上交易。Digital Commerce 360（10 月 8 日）和Retail Dive（10 月 9 日）报道了相同的最终数字。

指标（美国全网线上）2025 年 10 月大促2026 年 10 月大促
线上总消费90.9 亿美元（10 月 7–8 日）98.6 亿美元（10 月 6–7 日），+8.5%
Adobe 赛前预测—99 亿美元，+9.2%
最高折扣（较标价）18%20%（预测为 19%）
移动端销售占比—52.0%（51 亿美元，同比 +12.8%）
先买后付（BNPL）—6.923 亿美元，占订单 6.0%，同比 +4.0%

预测数据来自 Adobe 赛前报告，据 MediaPost 2026 年 10 月 5 日报道；2025 年基数和 18% 折扣峰值为 Adobe 去年活动后公布的数据。简单说：今年增速（8.5%）略高于去年（7.3%），但靠的是比预期更深的折扣。

哪些品类涨得最多

Adobe 将两天大促的销售额与 2026 年 9 月日均水平对比：玩具翻了一倍多，电子产品、电子游戏、个护也都大幅增长。据 Digital Commerce 360 报道，电子产品中耳机音箱较 9 月增长 300%，智能手表增长 255%。

日用品同样表现不错。Retail Dive 援引 Adobe 数据称，家庭清洁与纸品增长 55%，个人卫生用品 45%，宠物食品 45%，基础服饰 25%。Adobe 还提到，在其追踪的品类中，高价商品的销售占比较年初至今平均水平提升了 21%，其中电子产品 +45%、个护 +34%——只要折扣到位，消费者愿意买更贵的。

Adobe 表示折扣最深的品类是电脑、电视、电子产品、玩具和服装，这与增幅排行基本吻合：折扣越大，涨得越多。

消费者行为：移动端、BNPL 与比价

  • 手机是主要下单渠道。Adobe 称移动端是“主导交易渠道”，占线上销售 52%。主图、标题、A+ 页面都要先保证在手机上好看、好读。
  • BNPL 增长放缓。先买后付占订单 6%、销售额 6.923 亿美元，同比仅增 4%。Adobe 预计网络星期一（11 月 30 日）BNPL 带动的消费将超过 10 亿美元。
  • 消费者在比价。Retail Dive 引用 Numerator 的调研称，一半购物者会去沃尔玛等平台比价（沃尔玛同期也在做促销），74% 的人把通胀列为参加大促的原因。Numerator 基于自有样本，方法与 Adobe 不同，仅作趋势参考。

我们的解读

增长靠折扣撑起来。增速稳健但不算亮眼，而且折扣比预期更深。如果你的毛利扛不住 10 月这种 20% 左右的折扣，黑五网一只会压力更大。现在就要确定哪些 SKU 打折、打多少、哪些守价。

礼品类需求已经启动。玩具、游戏、电子产品远超 9 月水平，说明节日采购已经开始。做礼品类的卖家，请对照黑五周的建议入仓时间检查 FBA 库存，别等到 11 月。

日用品卖家要做复购。清洁、个护、宠物食品大涨，说明不少人借大促囤货。订购省（Subscribe & Save）、组合装、多件装都值得在下次大促前测试。

以自己的数据为准。拉出 10 月 6–7 日的业务报告，对比平常一周的转化率和单均广告成本——你账户的数据比全国平均值更重要。

行动清单

  1. 对比大促期间与前两周、去年 10 月的销售额、转化率和 TACoS。
  2. 列出断货或库存偏低的 SKU，立即为黑五网一补货。
  3. 按扣除费用后的毛利为每个 SKU 设定折扣底线，别忘了 10 月 15 日起生效的亚马逊旺季配送附加费。
  4. 用手机检查 Listing：主图、标题和前几条五点。
  5. 参考 10 月 6–7 日实际 CPC，规划黑五和网一的广告预算。
