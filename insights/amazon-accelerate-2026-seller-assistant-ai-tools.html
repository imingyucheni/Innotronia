Amazon Accelerate 2026: Seller Assistant Upgrade and New AI Plugin

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-25 · 5 min read

Amazon used its Accelerate 2026 seller conference in Seattle (September 22–24, 2026) to announce what it calls the biggest upgrade to its seller tools in a decade. The headline: Seller Assistant now works across your whole business, and a new plugin lets sellers run parts of their Amazon account from outside AI tools such as Amazon Quick and Anthropic's Claude. Here is what was announced, what is still in beta, and what we think sellers should do now.

What Amazon announced

On September 23, 2026, Amazon published details of the following changes. These are the facts as Amazon and independent press reported them:

  • A broader Seller Assistant. The AI assistant inside Seller Central is now described as connecting sales, inventory, advertising and compliance in one place, rather than answering isolated questions.
  • Memory across sessions. Amazon says Seller Assistant now remembers a seller's pricing patterns, inventory cycles and growth goals, and that this context carries across Seller Central, Amazon Quick and Claude.
  • Background automations. Sellers can set up monitoring that runs even when they are not logged in, with an audit trail. Amazon's example is an alert if any of your top 10 products drops below a 4-star rating.
  • Canvas. A visual workspace built on the fly from your live data, for example to compare pricing strategies or diagnose an ad campaign, which updates as you ask follow-up questions.
  • A selling partner plugin for outside AI tools. The plugin connects listings, performance metrics, inventory and sales data to Amazon Quick and to Anthropic's Claude, and lets those tools act on the data (for example checking inventory, adjusting prices or updating listings) with the seller approving actions. Amazon says it launches in Amazon Quick and in beta with Claude, for sellers in Amazon's U.S. stores first, with international expansion planned. Sellers choose which data the plugin can access.
  • Free Amazon Quick Plus for a year. Amazon is offering every primary selling account holder worldwide a free 12-month Amazon Quick Plus subscription, plus two co-workers at no cost. Amazon says the offer is available through December 31, 2026.

GeekWire, reporting from the event, quoted Amazon VP Mary Beth Westmoreland describing the goal that sellers "would never have to log into Seller Central." Exact feature availability can differ by account and marketplace, so check the announcements in your own Seller Central before planning around any single feature.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

1. Routine account work is getting cheaper

Daily checks such as stock levels, rating drops, suppressed listings and budget pacing are exactly what background automations are designed for. Small teams that spend the first hour of each day clicking through reports should be able to take some of that time back. The value is not in the AI itself but in catching problems earlier.

2. Judgment still matters more than ever

Amazon says sellers approve actions before they run, and that is important. An assistant can propose a price cut or a bid change; it does not know your landed cost, your cash position, your supplier's next production slot or your brand's positioning unless you give it that context. Treat recommendations as a well-informed first draft, not as a decision.

3. Access control becomes a real business risk

A plugin that can change prices and listings is powerful, and anyone with access to that connected AI tool may be able to use it. The same discipline you apply to Seller Central user permissions should apply to every connected tool.

4. Cross-border sellers should watch the rollout

The plugin starts with U.S. stores. Many Chinese sellers run their U.S. store from teams in China and use several tools at once, so test with one store and one small, low-risk workflow before connecting everything.

What to do now: a short checklist

  1. Claim the offer if it fits. If you are a primary account holder and want to try Amazon Quick Plus, review the terms in Seller Central and note the December 31, 2026 date.
  2. Start with read-only questions. Ask Seller Assistant to explain last week's sales change or rank your SKUs by inventory risk, and check its answers against your own reports.
  3. Set up two or three alerts. Good first candidates: rating drops on top ASINs, low days of cover before peak season, and listing suppressions.
  4. Keep humans on money decisions. Require manual approval for price changes, budget increases and bulk listing edits, especially in Q4.
  5. Review permissions. List who can access Seller Central and every connected AI tool. Remove anyone who no longer needs it, and never share the primary account password.
  6. Document your context. Write down margins, price floors and stock targets so both people and AI tools work from the same numbers.
Tip: Peak season is not the time to hand an untested automation your pricing. Pilot new workflows on a few SKUs now, and scale only what you have checked.

亚马逊 Accelerate 2026：卖家助手大升级，新插件接入外部 AI

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-25 · 约 5 分钟阅读

2026 年 9 月 22 日至 24 日，亚马逊在西雅图举办年度卖家大会 Accelerate 2026，并发布了官方口中“十年来最大的一次卖家工具升级”。重点有两个：Seller Assistant（卖家助手）开始覆盖整个店铺的经营，另外新推出的插件让卖家可以在 Amazon Quick、Anthropic 的 Claude 等外部 AI 工具里直接处理亚马逊店铺的部分工作。下面我们把已公布的事实、仍在测试的功能和我们的建议分开讲清楚。

亚马逊公布了什么

2026 年 9 月 23 日，亚马逊公开了以下内容（以下为亚马逊官方及独立媒体报道的事实）：

  • Seller Assistant 能力扩展：后台 AI 助手不再只是回答单个问题，而是把销售、库存、广告和合规打通。
  • 跨会话记忆：官方称助手会记住卖家的定价习惯、库存周期和增长目标，并且这些信息在 Seller Central、Amazon Quick 和 Claude 之间通用。
  • 后台自动化：即使卖家没有登录，也可以持续监控并留有操作记录。官方举的例子是：“如果我排名前 10 的产品评分跌破 4 星，就提醒我。”
  • Canvas 可视化工作区：根据实时数据即时生成，可以用来对比定价方案、诊断广告活动，并随着追问动态更新。
  • 面向外部 AI 工具的卖家插件：可以把 Listing、绩效指标、库存和销售数据接入 Amazon Quick 和 Claude，并允许这些工具执行操作（如查库存、调价、改 Listing），由卖家确认后才执行。官方表示：Amazon Quick 正式上线，Claude 为测试版（beta），目前先面向亚马逊美国站卖家，之后计划扩展到其他站点；卖家可以自行选择开放哪些数据。
  • 免费一年 Amazon Quick Plus：全球所有主账号持有人可获得 12 个月免费订阅，并可额外为两名同事免费开通，官方称该优惠截至 2026 年 12 月 31 日。

据 GeekWire 现场报道，亚马逊副总裁 Mary Beth Westmoreland 表示，愿景是让卖家“再也不用登录 Seller Central”。各功能的具体开放情况可能因账号和站点而不同，请以自己后台的通知为准。

我们的解读

1. 日常巡店的成本会明显下降

查库存、看评分、找被下架的 Listing、盯广告预算，这些每天重复的工作正是后台自动化最擅长的。很多小团队每天早上要花一个小时翻报表，这部分时间有望省下来。真正的价值不在 AI 本身，而在于更早发现问题。

2. 运营判断反而更重要

官方强调操作需要卖家确认，这一点很关键。AI 可以建议降价或调竞价，但它不知道你的到岸成本、现金流、工厂下一批排产时间和品牌定位，除非你把这些告诉它。把 AI 建议当作一份“有依据的初稿”，而不是最终决定。

3. 权限管理变成真正的经营风险

一个能改价格、改 Listing 的插件功能很强，谁能用那个 AI 工具，谁就可能动你的店铺。对待外部 AI 工具，要像管理 Seller Central 子账号权限一样严格。

4. 跨境卖家要关注开放节奏

插件先从美国站开始。很多国内团队运营美国站，同时使用多款 ERP 和工具，建议先用一个店铺、一个低风险流程试点，不要一上来就全部接入。

现在可以做什么：行动清单

  1. 按需领取优惠：如果你是主账号持有人并想试用 Amazon Quick Plus，先在后台看清条款，留意 2026 年 12 月 31 日这个时间点。
  2. 先从“只读”问题开始：让助手解释上周销量变化、按库存风险给 SKU 排序，再用自己的报表核对。
  3. 设置 2–3 个提醒：比如核心 ASIN 评分下降、旺季前可售天数不足、Listing 被抑制。
  4. 涉及钱的操作保留人工审批：调价、加预算、批量改 Listing 都要人工确认，Q4 尤其如此。
  5. 清理权限：列出所有能访问 Seller Central 和已接入 AI 工具的人员，不再需要的及时移除，主账号密码绝不外传。
  6. 把经营参数写下来：毛利、最低价、库存目标写成文档，让团队和 AI 工具用同一套数字。
小提示：旺季不是把定价交给未经测试的自动化的时候。现在先在少量 SKU 上试点，验证没问题再逐步放开。
