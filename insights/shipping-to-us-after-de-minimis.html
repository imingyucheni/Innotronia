Shipping to US Customers After De Minimis: Rules and 3 Fulfillment Models

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-29 · 6 min read

For years, many Chinese sellers shipped small orders straight to US customers and paid no US duty thanks to the $800 "de minimis" exemption. That model is over. Every low-value parcel now needs a proper customs entry and pays duties, so the way you get products to US buyers directly affects your costs, speed and risk. This guide explains the rules and compares the three main fulfillment models.

What changed

According to CBP's fact sheet (updated Aug 18, 2025), from August 29, 2025 imported goods from all countries valued at or below $800 are no longer eligible for de minimis treatment and are subject to all applicable duties, taxes and fees. Key points:

  • Non-postal shipments (express couriers, freight) must be filed using an appropriate entry type in CBP's ACE system by a party qualified to make entry.
  • Postal shipments are subject to duties; carriers bringing international postal packages to the US need an international carrier bond, and qualified parties can file and pay duties in place of carriers.
  • In June 2026, CBP published a rule titled Indefinite Suspension of the De Minimis Exemption for Mail Shipments and New Postal Informal Entry Process in the Federal Register (June 24, 2026), so sellers should not plan around de minimis returning.

Under 19 CFR 143.21, shipments valued at $2,500 or less may generally use an informal entry; higher-value shipments need a formal entry, which normally requires a customs bond. Duty rates depend on the product's tariff classification and country of origin and have changed often, so confirm current rates with a licensed customs broker.

Who should be the importer of record?

The importer of record (IOR) is legally responsible for correct classification, value and payment of duties. Options include your own US company, a customs broker acting for a foreign IOR, or a logistics provider offering delivered-duty-paid (DDP) service. Whatever you choose:

  • Declare the real transaction value. Undervaluing to cut duties can lead to penalties and seizures.
  • Use the correct HTS code and country of origin for every SKU; keep supplier invoices and packing lists.
  • For DDP offers, ask in writing who is the IOR and whose name appears on customs filings. A very low all-in price can mean the declaration is not accurate, and the goods are still yours if they are held.
Tip: Ask your broker to build a landed-cost sheet per SKU: product cost, freight, duty, broker fees, US last-mile and marketplace fees. Price from that number, not from the factory price.

Checklist: moving from parcel shipping to bulk import

  1. Classify your top SKUs with a customs broker and estimate duty per unit.
  2. Compare landed cost per unit for direct parcels vs bulk import, including US last-mile.
  3. Decide the IOR and, for formal entries, arrange a customs bond through your broker.
  4. Pick a US warehouse or marketplace program that fits your channels.
  5. Start with best sellers. Move steady sellers to bulk import first; keep slow or test SKUs on direct parcel shipping.
  6. Update prices and delivery promises on your listings once stock is in the US.

小额豁免取消后如何发货到美国：规则与三种履约模式

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-29 · 约 6 分钟阅读

过去很多国内卖家把小包直接发给美国买家，靠着 800 美元的“小额豁免”（de minimis）免缴美国关税。这种模式已经结束了。现在每个低价值包裹都要正式报关并缴纳关税，你把货送到美国买家手里的方式，直接决定了成本、时效和风险。本文讲清楚现行规则，并对比三种主要的履约模式。

规则变了什么

根据美国海关（CBP）事实说明（2025 年 8 月 18 日更新），自 2025 年 8 月 29 日起，来自所有国家、价值不超过 800 美元的进口商品不再享受小额豁免，需缴纳所有适用的关税、税费。要点如下：

  • 非邮政渠道（快递、货运）必须由有资格报关的一方，在 CBP 的 ACE 系统中以适当的报关类型申报。
  • 邮政渠道同样需要缴纳关税；运输国际邮包入境的承运人需要有国际承运人保证金，符合资格的一方可以代替承运人申报并缴税。
  • 2026 年 6 月 24 日，CBP 在联邦公报上发布了题为《无限期暂停邮件小额豁免及新的邮政非正式报关流程》的规则，卖家不应再指望小额豁免恢复。

根据 19 CFR 143.21，价值 2,500 美元及以下的货物一般可以走非正式报关（informal entry）；超过的需要正式报关（formal entry），通常需要海关保证金（customs bond）。具体税率取决于商品的 HTS 编码和原产国，而且近年调整频繁，请以持牌报关行确认的最新税率为准。

谁来做进口商（IOR）？

进口商（Importer of Record）对商品归类、申报价值和缴税承担法律责任。可以由你自己的美国公司担任，也可以由报关行为境外进口商代理，或者使用物流商的“双清包税”（DDP）服务。无论选哪种：

  • 按真实成交价申报。低报价格逃税可能导致罚款和扣货。
  • 每个 SKU 使用正确的 HTS 编码和原产地，保存好供应商发票和装箱单。
  • 对于 DDP 报价，书面确认谁是进口商、报关文件上是谁的名字。全包价格低得离谱，可能意味着申报不实，货被扣了损失还是你的。
小提示：请报关行按 SKU 做一张到岸成本表：产品成本、头程运费、关税、报关费、美国尾程和平台费用。用这个数字定价，而不是用出厂价。

从直发小包转向整批进口的清单

  1. 给主力 SKU 做归类：找报关行确认 HTS 编码，估算每件关税。
  2. 对比单件到岸成本：直发小包 vs 整批进口，记得算上美国尾程。
  3. 确定进口商：正式报关的，通过报关行办理海关保证金。
  4. 选择美国仓或平台仓：根据你的销售渠道决定。
  5. 先从爆款开始：稳定出单的产品先转整批进口，慢销和测款产品继续直发。
  6. 更新价格和配送承诺：货到美国后，及时调整 Listing 的价格和时效。
