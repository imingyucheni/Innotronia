Walmart Opens Its Store to AI Shopping Agents: What Sellers Should Do

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-25 · 5 min read

AI assistants are starting to shop on behalf of customers, and Walmart has just set the rules for how they can do it on its site. On October 21, 2026, Walmart announced Agent Profiles, a new Walmart Agent Gateway for AI assistants, and terms that agents must follow. For Marketplace sellers, this changes who (or what) reads your listings before a customer buys.

What Walmart announced

  • Agent Profiles. Customers can give each AI agent its own profile in their Walmart account, track its purchases separately, and either approve every purchase or let it buy up to a limit. Walmart gave $50, $100 and $200 as example limits, according to Digital Commerce 360 (Oct 23, 2026). Profiles roll out "next month" and can be deleted at any time.
  • Walmart Agent Gateway. A direct connection that lets assistants such as OpenAI's ChatGPT, Google's Gemini and Meta's Muse search products and complete purchases at Walmart and Sam's Club, Reuters reported (Oct 21, 2026). It is built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and starts as a limited beta for companies and developers.
  • Rules for agents. Agents see the same prices as people, cannot access payment card data, cannot act beyond the shopper's instructions, and are blocked if they break the rules. Walmart's normal returns process applies, including when an agent doesn't do what was intended, per TechCrunch (Oct 21, 2026).
  • No membership needed. Agent support is open to anyone with a Walmart account; Walmart+ is not required (Reuters).

The contrast with Amazon

Amazon has sought to limit third-party AI shopping agents' access to its platform, Reuters notes, and TechCrunch reports that Amazon has begun blocking AI agents from making purchases for shoppers. Walmart is taking the opposite approach and inviting approved agents in. Mizuho analyst David Bellinger told Reuters that agentic purchasing is still "experimental," but that "the momentum and buy-in from top management teams seems to be making this into a real thing."

How ready are shoppers?

Early, but growing. In an NMI survey of 1,000 US adults in August 2026, cited by Digital Commerce 360, 11% said they had used AI to complete a transaction, 70% wanted to review or override an AI purchase before checkout, and only 10% would give AI full control.

Walmart has also said vitamin and protein supplements were the top two product types bought from Walmart through ChatGPT, according to comments by Walmart executive Daniel Danker reported by Digital Commerce 360.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Your catalog data is now your storefront. An AI agent doesn't look at your lifestyle image. It reads titles, attributes, prices, stock and delivery promises. Manish Joneja, who leads Walmart Global Marketplace, said accurate product information, competitive prices, availability and reliable delivery promises will matter more, and that Walmart is working with sellers on catalog quality so products are easier for AI to understand and recommend.

Spending limits favor clear value. With limits like $100, agents will handle many routine, repeat purchases first. Consumables, household basics and replenishment items are likely early winners; make sure your pack sizes and unit prices are easy to compare.

Returns still land on you. If an agent buys the wrong item, Walmart's normal returns process applies, so clear variant data (size, count, compatibility) protects your return rate.

Don't overreact yet. Profiles aren't live and the gateway is a beta. This is a signal to clean up data now, not a reason to change your whole strategy. Google is moving in a similar direction with Google Universal Cart.

Checklist for Walmart sellers

  1. Fill every required and recommended attribute for your top SKUs.
  2. Write titles that state brand, product type, size or count plainly.
  3. Keep inventory and prices synced so agents never see stale offers.
  4. Set accurate shipping templates and delivery promises.
  5. Check variant groups so agents can tell sizes and colors apart.
  6. Monitor returns for "wrong item" reasons after Agent Profiles launch.

Walmart 向 AI 购物代理开放：卖家该做什么

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-25 · 约 5 分钟阅读

AI 助手开始替消费者下单了，而 Walmart 刚刚为它们在自家平台购物立下规矩。2026 年 10 月 21 日，Walmart 宣布推出代理档案（Agent Profiles）、面向 AI 助手的 Walmart Agent Gateway，以及 AI 代理必须遵守的条款。对 Marketplace 卖家来说，这意味着在顾客下单前读你 Listing 的，可能不再是人。

Walmart 公布了什么

  • 代理档案。顾客可在 Walmart 账户里为每个 AI 代理单独建档，分开追踪其购买记录，并选择逐单确认或设置消费上限。据 Digital Commerce 360（2026 年 10 月 23 日）报道，Walmart 举例的上限为 50、100、200 美元。该功能将于"下个月"上线，可随时删除。
  • Walmart Agent Gateway。据路透社 10 月 21 日报道，这一通道让 OpenAI 的 ChatGPT、Google 的 Gemini、Meta 的 Muse 等助手可直接在 Walmart 和 Sam's Club 搜索商品并完成购买。它基于模型上下文协议（MCP），初期面向企业和开发者有限内测。
  • 代理规则。AI 代理看到的价格与真人相同，不能获取支付卡信息，不能超出顾客指令行事，违规将被封禁。据 TechCrunch（2026 年 10 月 21 日），即使代理买错，也适用 Walmart 的常规退货流程。
  • 无需会员。所有 Walmart 账户均可使用，不需要 Walmart+ 会员（路透社）。

与亚马逊的对比

路透社指出，亚马逊一直在限制第三方 AI 购物代理访问其平台；TechCrunch 报道亚马逊已开始阻止 AI 代理替消费者下单。Walmart 则反其道而行，欢迎经过授权的代理进入。瑞穗分析师 David Bellinger 对路透社表示，代理式购物仍是"实验性的"，但"高管层的势头和投入似乎正让它成为现实"。

消费者准备好了吗？

还处于早期，但在增长。据 Digital Commerce 360 引用的 NMI 调查（2026 年 8 月，1,000 名美国成年人），11% 的人曾用 AI 完成交易，70% 希望在结账前能审核或否决 AI 的购买决定，只有 10% 愿意让 AI 全权做主。

据 Digital Commerce 360 报道，Walmart 高管 Daniel Danker 曾表示，通过 ChatGPT 在 Walmart 购买最多的两类商品是维生素和蛋白补充剂。

我们的解读

商品数据就是你的门面。AI 代理不看场景图，它读的是标题、属性、价格、库存和配送承诺。Walmart 全球 Marketplace 负责人 Manish Joneja 表示，准确的商品信息、有竞争力的价格、库存和可靠的配送承诺会更重要，Walmart 也在和卖家一起提升目录质量，让商品更容易被 AI 理解和推荐。

消费上限利好"一眼看懂"的商品。在 100 美元这类上限下，代理会先接手日常复购。消耗品、家居日用和补货类商品可能最先受益——确保你的规格和单价便于比较。

退货还是落到你头上。代理买错同样走常规退货，所以清晰的变体数据（尺码、数量、兼容型号）能保护你的退货率。

不必过度反应。代理档案尚未上线，Gateway 仍在内测。现在该做的是整理数据，而不是推翻整体策略。Google 也在走类似方向，见我们关于 Google Universal Cart 的文章。

Walmart 卖家行动清单

  1. 为主力 SKU 补全所有必填和推荐属性。
  2. 标题直接写清品牌、品类、规格或数量。
  3. 库存和价格保持同步，避免代理看到过期报价。
  4. 设置准确的运费模板和配送承诺。
  5. 检查变体组，让代理分得清尺码和颜色。
  6. 代理档案上线后，关注"买错商品"类退货原因。
