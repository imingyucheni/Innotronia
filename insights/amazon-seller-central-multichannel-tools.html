Amazon Seller Central Now Connects Walmart, eBay, Shopify and TikTok

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-11 · 5 min read

Amazon wants Seller Central to run your whole e-commerce business, not just your Amazon store. On September 24, 2026, Amazon announced free multichannel selling tools that let US sellers connect their eBay, Shopify, TikTok and Walmart accounts, import those listings and see off-Amazon orders inside Seller Central. The rollout is gradual, so many sellers are getting access now, in the middle of Q4 planning. Here is what is live, what is still coming, and how to think about it.

What Amazon announced

According to Amazon's announcement (Sept 24, 2026), sellers can connect other channels from a new multichannel settings page in Seller Central. PPC Land (Sept 27, 2026) reported the same details and separated what is available now from what Amazon described as coming later:

FeatureStatus
Connect eBay, Shopify, TikTok and Walmart accountsRolling out now (US store)
Import and link off-Amazon listingsRolling out now
View multichannel sales and ordersRolling out now
Fulfill off-Amazon orders through AmazonRolling out now
Edit a listing once and update every channelDescribed as coming
AI-generated channel-ready listing contentDescribed as coming
Cross-channel profitability view (ad spend, traffic, sales)Described as coming

Amazon says the tools come at no additional cost, and that the rollout starts with sellers in the US store "over the coming months," with more channels to follow. Fulfilling orders through Amazon still uses Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF), which has its own fees; see our article on MCF and Prime delivery on your own website.

The numbers behind the move

Amazon backed the launch with its own figures: more than 95% of independent sellers in its store sell across multiple channels, independent sellers drive more than 60% of sales in Amazon's store, and they created more than 12 million listings with generative AI in 2025. PPC Land noted that Amazon gives no source or definition for the 95% figure.

Amazon's position is also clear from independent estimates. Marketplace Pulse (Mar 19, 2026) estimates Amazon's US third-party sales at about $300 billion, more than seven times eBay's, with Temu, TikTok Shop and Walmart much smaller:

In other words, the channels Amazon now invites into Seller Central are much smaller than Amazon, and many are growing. Becoming the place where sellers manage all of them keeps Amazon at the center of the seller's day, and its fulfillment network busy.

Open questions

  • Timing. No end date was given for the rollout, so not every seller will have the tools before Black Friday (November 27).
  • Channel rules. Walmart and TikTok Shop have their own rules on which fulfillment providers and carriers sellers may use. PPC Land pointed out that Amazon's post does not address them, so check each platform's policy before routing orders through Amazon.
  • Data use. Amazon's post says seller data is not shared with other sellers or used to inform Amazon's retail decisions. PPC Land noted it does not address advertising systems.
  • Listing control. It is not yet clear whether sellers will review channel-specific versions before automatic updates go live.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

A free hub is worth testing. Many sellers pay for separate tools to sync listings, orders and reports across channels. If Amazon's tools do the basics well, they could replace part of that stack. Start with one secondary channel and compare order sync and inventory accuracy with your current setup.

Don't put all your eggs in one basket. Running every channel through one Amazon login is convenient, but it ties your other channels to an account that can be suspended. Keep direct access to each platform and your own records of orders and customer data.

Fulfillment rules come first. Before you ship Walmart or TikTok Shop orders with MCF, confirm that the platform allows it for your account, including rules on branded packaging and delivery speed.

Profitability by channel is the real prize. If the profit dashboard arrives as described, it will make it easier to see whether a channel actually makes money after ads and fees. Until then, keep your own channel P&L. For more on Amazon's AI tools from the same event, see our Accelerate 2026 article.

Checklist

  1. Look for the multichannel settings page in Seller Central (Settings menu).
  2. Connect one secondary channel first and check order and inventory sync for two weeks.
  3. Review each platform's fulfillment and packaging rules before using MCF for its orders.
  4. Keep direct logins and order exports for every channel.
  5. Track profit per channel, including ads, fees and returns.

亚马逊卖家后台接入沃尔玛、eBay、Shopify 和 TikTok：多渠道工具解读

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-11 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊希望卖家后台（Seller Central）管理的不只是亚马逊店铺，而是你整个电商生意。2026 年 9 月 24 日，亚马逊宣布推出免费的多渠道销售工具：美国卖家可以把 eBay、Shopify、TikTok 和沃尔玛账号接入卖家后台，导入这些平台的 Listing，并在后台查看站外订单。功能分批开放，不少卖家正是在 Q4 备战期间陆续拿到权限。下面梳理哪些已上线、哪些还在路上，以及该怎么看。

亚马逊公布了什么

据亚马逊官方公告（2026 年 9 月 24 日），卖家可以在卖家后台新的多渠道设置页面连接其他渠道。PPC Land（2026 年 9 月 27 日）报道了相同内容，并区分了已上线和“即将推出”的功能：

功能状态
连接 eBay、Shopify、TikTok、沃尔玛账号正在开放（美国站）
导入并关联站外 Listing正在开放
查看多渠道销售和订单正在开放
用亚马逊履约站外订单正在开放
改一次 Listing，同步更新所有渠道官方称即将推出
AI 生成各渠道适配的 Listing 内容官方称即将推出
跨渠道利润看板（广告花费、流量、销售）官方称即将推出

亚马逊表示工具不额外收费，先向美国站卖家“在未来几个月内”逐步开放，之后会加入更多渠道。用亚马逊履约站外订单仍然走多渠道配送（MCF），有单独的费用，可参考我们的《亚马逊 MCF 支持独立站 Prime 配送》。

背后的数字

亚马逊用自己的数据为这次发布背书：亚马逊上超过 95% 的独立卖家在多个渠道销售；独立卖家贡献了亚马逊商城 60% 以上的销售额；2025 年独立卖家用生成式 AI 创建了超过 1,200 万条 Listing。PPC Land 指出，亚马逊并未说明 95% 这一数字的来源和口径。

第三方估算也说明了亚马逊的地位。Marketplace Pulse（2026 年 3 月 19 日）估计亚马逊美国第三方卖家销售额约 3,000 亿美元，是 eBay 的 7 倍多，Temu、TikTok Shop 和沃尔玛的体量则小得多：

也就是说，亚马逊这次“请进”卖家后台的渠道体量都远小于它自己，而且不少还在增长。成为卖家管理所有渠道的入口，能让亚马逊继续处在卖家日常工作的中心，也让它的物流网络更忙。

尚待明确的问题

  • 开放时间。官方没有给出全部开放的截止日期，并非所有卖家都能在黑五（11 月 27 日）前用上。
  • 各平台规则。沃尔玛和 TikTok Shop 对卖家可用的履约服务商和承运商有各自规定。PPC Land 指出亚马逊的公告没有提及这些，用亚马逊履约前请先核对各平台政策。
  • 数据使用。亚马逊称卖家数据不会分享给其他卖家，也不会用于亚马逊自营决策；PPC Land 指出公告未提及广告系统。
  • Listing 控制权。目前还不清楚自动同步前，卖家能否先审核各渠道版本。

我们的解读

免费的中台值得一试。很多卖家要花钱买第三方工具同步各渠道的 Listing、订单和报表。如果亚马逊的工具能把基础功能做好，就能替代其中一部分。建议先接入一个次要渠道，与现有工具对比订单同步和库存准确度。

别把鸡蛋放在一个篮子里。所有渠道都通过一个亚马逊账号管理固然方便，但也意味着其他渠道和一个可能被封的账号绑在一起。保留各平台的直接登录权限，以及自己的订单和客户数据记录。

先看履约规则。用 MCF 发沃尔玛或 TikTok Shop 订单之前，确认平台允许你的账号这样做，包括包装品牌和配送时效要求。

按渠道算利润才是关键。如果利润看板如期上线，就能更直观地看出某个渠道扣除广告和费用后到底赚不赚钱。在此之前，请继续自己做分渠道损益表。同场大会发布的 AI 工具，可参考我们的《亚马逊 Accelerate 2026 解读》。

行动清单

  1. 在卖家后台“设置”菜单中查看是否已有多渠道设置页面。
  2. 先接入一个次要渠道，观察两周订单和库存同步情况。
  3. 用 MCF 履约前，核对各平台的履约和包装规则。
  4. 保留每个渠道的直接登录和订单导出。
  5. 按渠道核算利润，计入广告、费用和退货。
