Amazon Transparency and Project Zero: How Brands Stop Counterfeits

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-16 · 6 min read

Counterfeits and look-alike listings can take sales and reviews from a brand in weeks. Amazon offers two programs to fight back: Transparency, which puts a unique code on every unit, and Project Zero, which lets brands remove counterfeit listings themselves. This guide explains how each works, what it costs and who qualifies, based on Amazon's official Transparency and Project Zero pages.

How Transparency works

Amazon describes Transparency as "a product serialization service and anti-counterfeiting program." Every unit of an enrolled product carries a unique code. Once protections are active, "a valid Transparency code must accompany every unit listed or shipped" in Transparency-supported stores, whether the order is fulfilled by Amazon or by the seller. Amazon says codes can also stop "materially different" versions (different materials, compatibility, color or language) and products made for other countries' stores from passing listing checks.

You can use Amazon-issued codes (a small square barcode that customers can scan with the Amazon Shopping or Transparency app) or connect your existing serial codes. App scanning by customers is only available with Amazon-issued codes. For China-based sellers, Amazon lists "regional integration partners (China only)" that connect Transparency to ERP systems.

Protections are not active until your product passes an operational performance review. For FBA, codes are scanned when units arrive at the fulfillment center; for self-fulfilled orders, you provide codes at order confirmation in Seller Central.

What Transparency costs

There is no enrollment fee, subscription or minimum; you pay only for codes. Amazon's published price per code:

Example (calculation): 50,000 units in a year at the first tier cost 50,000 × $0.05 = $2,500 in codes, before labeling or printing costs. Tiers reset each year on your account creation date.

How Project Zero works

Project Zero gives eligible brands a self-service tool to "search for counterfeit listings and immediately remove them." Each removal also strengthens Brand Registry's automated protections. Enrollment requires a 10-minute training. Amazon recommends a test buy to confirm a violation, and it sends a notice of complaint to each seller affected by a removal.

The tool is only for counterfeits, meaning unlawful use of a registered trademark. It is not for patent, copyright or pricing disputes. Brands must keep an accuracy rate of at least 99% to keep access, and eligibility requires at least a 90% acceptance rate on Report a Violation submissions in the last six months.

Which one should you use?

  • Frequent counterfeits or hijacked listings: Transparency gives the strongest protection because units without valid codes are blocked.
  • Occasional counterfeit offers: start with Report a Violation, build a clean record, and enroll in Project Zero when invited.
  • High-value or regulated products: combine both; Amazon says brands using both "see the best results."

Checklist

  1. Secure a US trademark registration and enroll in Brand Registry (see our Brand Registry growth tools).
  2. Decide which SKUs to serialize first: bestsellers and products with counterfeit history.
  3. Choose a labeling method: self-print, pre-printed labels, co-packer, or print on packaging.
  4. Coordinate codes with your factory so every unit is labeled before shipping; keep GS1 barcodes and Transparency labels separate and scannable.
  5. Run the operational performance review before relying on protections.
  6. Report violations accurately to qualify for Project Zero, and test-buy before removals.

亚马逊 Transparency 与 Project Zero：品牌如何防假货、防跟卖

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-16 · 约 6 分钟阅读

假货和跟卖可能在几周内抢走品牌的销量和评价。亚马逊提供两个反假货项目：Transparency（透明计划）给每一件商品一个唯一码；Project Zero（零计划）让品牌自己移除假货 listing。本文根据亚马逊官方 Transparency 和 Project Zero 页面，讲清两者怎么运作、费用多少、谁有资格。

Transparency 怎么运作

亚马逊把 Transparency 定义为"商品序列化服务和反假货项目"。已登记商品的每一件都带有唯一码。保护生效后，在支持 Transparency 的站点，每件上架或发货的商品都必须附带有效的 Transparency 码，无论是 FBA 还是自发货。亚马逊表示，该码还能拦截材质、兼容性、颜色或语言不同的"实质性不同"版本，以及为其他国家站点生产的商品。

你可以使用亚马逊签发的码（小方形条码，买家可用 Amazon Shopping 或 Transparency App 扫码验真），也可以接入自有序列码；只有亚马逊签发的码支持买家扫码验真。针对中国卖家，亚马逊列出了"区域集成合作伙伴（仅限中国）"，可与 ERP 系统打通。

商品通过运营表现审核后，保护才会生效。FBA 商品在入仓时扫码；自发货订单需在 Seller Central 确认发货时提供码。

Project Zero 怎么运作

Project Zero 为符合资格的品牌提供自助工具，可"搜索假货 listing 并立即移除"，每次移除还会强化品牌注册的自动保护。报名需完成 10 分钟培训。亚马逊建议先做测试购买确认侵权；移除后，系统会向每个相关卖家发送投诉通知。

该工具只能用于假货，即非法使用已注册商标的情况，不能用于专利、版权或价格纠纷。品牌须保持至少 99% 的准确率才能继续使用；报名资格要求近 6 个月内通过 Report a Violation 提交的举报受理率不低于 90%。

该选哪个？

  • 假货、跟卖频繁：Transparency 保护最强，没有有效码的商品直接被拦截。
  • 偶尔出现假货：先用 Report a Violation，积累良好记录，收到邀请后再报名 Project Zero。
  • 高价值或受监管产品：两者结合使用；亚马逊表示同时使用的品牌"效果最好"。

操作清单

  1. 完成美国商标注册并报名品牌注册（参考我们的品牌注册工具指南）。
  2. 确定优先序列化的 SKU：爆款和曾被仿冒的商品。
  3. 选择贴标方式：自行打印、预印标签、代包装商或直接印在包装上。
  4. 与工厂协调，确保每件商品出厂前贴好码；GS1 条码与 Transparency 码分开且都可扫描。
  5. 在依赖保护之前，先完成运营表现审核。
  6. 准确举报侵权以达到 Project Zero 资格，移除前先做测试购买。
