Walmart Seller Performance Standards: The 8 Metrics and Thresholds

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-22 · 6 min read

On Walmart Marketplace, selling well is not enough; you also have to ship, track and serve customers to Walmart's standards. Miss them and your listings can be suppressed or your account suspended. Walmart's Seller Performance Standards set eight metrics, each with a clear threshold. This guide lists them, explains what drives each one, and gives a weekly routine to stay in the green.

The eight metrics and thresholds

From Walmart's Seller performance standards (last updated July 28, 2026):

MetricWhat it measuresThresholdWindow
Cancellation RateOrders canceled by the seller after receiving them from Walmart2% or below30 or 60 days
On-Time Delivery RateOrders delivered on or before the Expected Delivery Date90% or above30 or 60 days
Valid Tracking RateOrders with valid tracking information99% or above30 or 60 days
Seller Response RateCustomer inquiries answered within 48 hours95% or above30 or 60 days
Negative Feedback RateOrders with a one- or two-star customer rating2% or below60 days
Return RateDelivered orders returned by the customer6% or below (9% for Resold inventory)60 days
Item Not Received RateOrders not delivered to the customer2% or below60 days
Late Shipment RateOrders shipped after the Expected Ship Date5% or below30 or 60 days

The "rate limits" in one view:

Walmart says that if your account misses any standard you must act immediately, and that continued failure may lead to suppression, suspension or termination. Notifications come by email and in Seller Center, and you cannot opt out. Walmart also notes that for sellers using Walmart Fulfillment Services, Negative Feedback Rate is the only metric the seller still owns.

What drives each metric, and how to fix it

Cancellations and late shipments

Both come down to inventory accuracy and handling time. Sync inventory at least several times a day across channels, set a realistic lag time, and avoid overselling during promotions. Walmart's advice: meet your Estimated Ship Date to avoid auto-cancellations.

On-time delivery and item not received

These depend on your carrier and service choice. Walmart recommends choosing the right lag time, shipping method, carrier and service level. Only use carriers on Walmart's approved parcel carrier list, and avoid promising delivery dates your carrier can't meet in peak season. See our article on Walmart's newly approved carriers.

Valid tracking

Walmart says to provide accurate tracking numbers only after the package is handed to the carrier. Upload tracking promptly, make sure your shipping software maps the carrier name correctly, and avoid placeholder or re-used numbers.

Response rate

Answer every customer message within 48 hours, including weekends during peak season. Templates for common questions (shipping time, returns, sizing) help.

Negative feedback and returns

Both point to listing accuracy and product quality. Walmart suggests clear descriptions, accurate inventory and reviewing carrier choices. Read return reasons weekly and fix the top causes in titles, images and size information.

A weekly performance routine

  1. Open the Performance dashboard in Seller Center every Monday and note any metric near its limit.
  2. Check inventory sync and lag times before promotions.
  3. Spot-check tracking uploads for each carrier you use.
  4. Clear the customer inbox daily; aim for under 24 hours.
  5. Review returns and 1–2 star ratings; fix one listing issue per week.
  6. Before peak season, add buffer to lag time rather than risk late shipments.
Metrics with a 60-day window recover slowly. A bad week in November can still affect your account in January, so prevention is far cheaper than recovery.

Peak season watch-outs

November and December put every metric under pressure: order volumes jump, carriers slow down and customers ask more questions. Three habits help most: add a day of lag time on items that are hard to restock, keep a backup carrier ready if your main one falls behind, and staff customer service for weekends. Then check the dashboard daily rather than weekly until mid-January.

沃尔玛卖家绩效标准：8 项指标与门槛

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-22 · 约 6 分钟阅读

在沃尔玛 Marketplace，卖得好还不够，发货、追踪和客服也必须达到沃尔玛的标准。达不到，商品可能被屏蔽，账号可能被暂停。沃尔玛《卖家绩效标准》设定了 8 项指标，每项都有明确门槛。本文逐一列出，解释各项指标受什么影响，并给出一套每周巡检流程。

8 项指标与门槛

来自沃尔玛《卖家绩效标准》（2026 年 7 月 28 日更新）：

指标衡量内容门槛统计周期
取消率卖家接单后取消的订单2% 及以下30 或 60 天
准时送达率在预计送达日当天或之前送达的订单90% 及以上30 或 60 天
有效追踪率提供有效追踪信息的订单99% 及以上30 或 60 天
卖家回复率48 小时内回复的客户咨询95% 及以上30 或 60 天
差评率获得一星或两星评价的订单2% 及以下60 天
退货率已送达后被退回的订单6% 及以下（转售库存 9%）60 天
未收到货率未送达客户的订单2% 及以下60 天
延迟发货率晚于预计发货日发出的订单5% 及以下30 或 60 天

各项“上限”一览：

沃尔玛表示，账户任何一项未达标都须立即采取行动，持续不达标可能导致商品屏蔽、账号暂停或终止。通知通过邮件和卖家后台发送，且不能退订。沃尔玛还说明，使用沃尔玛官方仓（WFS）的卖家，唯一仍需自己负责的指标是差评率。

各指标的影响因素与改善方法

取消率与延迟发货率

两者都取决于库存准确性和处理时间。各渠道库存每天多次同步，设置合理的备货时间（lag time），促销期间避免超卖。沃尔玛的建议：按预计发货日发货，避免订单被自动取消。

准时送达率与未收到货率

取决于承运商和服务等级的选择。沃尔玛建议选择合适的备货时间、配送方式、承运商和服务等级。只使用沃尔玛认可承运商名单中的承运商，旺季不要承诺承运商做不到的时效。参见我们的《沃尔玛新增认可承运商》。

有效追踪率

沃尔玛要求包裹交给承运商后再上传准确的追踪号。及时上传，确认发货软件正确映射承运商名称，不要使用占位号或重复使用的单号。

回复率

48 小时内回复每一条客户消息，旺季周末也不例外。为常见问题（时效、退货、尺码）准备模板会很有帮助。

差评率与退货率

两者都指向 Listing 准确性和产品质量。沃尔玛建议描述清晰、库存准确，并复核承运商选择。每周查看退货原因，在标题、图片和尺码信息中修复排名靠前的问题。

每周绩效巡检流程

  1. 每周一打开卖家后台的绩效看板，记录接近门槛的指标。
  2. 促销前检查库存同步和备货时间。
  3. 抽查每家承运商的追踪号上传情况。
  4. 每天清理客户消息，争取 24 小时内回复。
  5. 查看退货和 1–2 星评价，每周至少修复一个 Listing 问题。
  6. 旺季前适当增加备货时间，而不是冒延迟发货的风险。
按 60 天计算的指标恢复很慢。11 月表现差的一周，到 1 月仍会影响账户，所以预防远比补救便宜。

旺季注意事项

11 月和 12 月每项指标都承压：订单量激增、承运商变慢、客户咨询增多。最有用的三个习惯：难补货的商品多加一天备货时间；主力承运商跟不上时有备用承运商可切换；周末也安排客服值班。旺季到次年 1 月中旬，把绩效看板从每周检查改为每天检查。
