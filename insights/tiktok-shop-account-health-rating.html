TikTok Shop Account Health Rating (AHR): Thresholds, Penalties and Recovery

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-01 · 6 min read

On TikTok Shop US, your Account Health Rating (AHR) decides whether you can launch new products and join the big sales campaigns. Let it slip and you can lose weeks of listings right before peak season; let it hit zero and you can lose the shop. Here is how the score works, what the thresholds mean, and how to protect it.

How the Account Health Rating works

According to TikTok Shop's Seller University page on AHR requirements (updated June 29, 2026):

  • AHR is a color-coded score from 0 to 1,000.
  • All sellers start at 200 points.
  • Points are deducted for policy violations, based on severity and frequency.
  • Deductions expire automatically after 180 days.
  • You can earn points back: 4 points per 200 completed orders (up to 20 points a week), or by passing policy quizzes.

AdBeacon reports that AHR fully replaced the older violation-points system in July 2026, and that the 180-day window means a violation affects your account for twice as long as under the old 90-day reset.

What happens at each threshold

When your score drops to certain levels, TikTok Shop restricts your account:

AHR scoreRestriction (per TikTok Shop)
150Cannot enroll in mega campaigns or create new listings for 7 days
100Same restrictions for 14 days
50Same restrictions for 28 days
0Account may be permanently deactivated

Because every seller starts at 200, a single serious violation can take you to the first threshold. Losing mega-campaign access in October or November can mean missing Black Friday and Cyber Monday promotions entirely.

Common causes of point deductions

TikTok Shop deducts points for violations of its policies. In practice, sellers most often run into trouble with:

  • Fulfillment: late dispatch, seller-fault cancellations, invalid tracking.
  • Listings: restricted or prohibited products, misleading titles or images, missing certifications.
  • Content and claims: exaggerated or medical claims by you or your creators.
  • Intellectual property: selling or showing products that infringe trademarks or copyrights.
  • Customer service: slow responses, steering buyers off-platform.

The exact deduction for each violation is set by TikTok Shop and shown in Seller Center; check the enforcement policy there for current values.

A worked example: how fast can you recover?

Using TikTok Shop's published recovery rule of 4 points per 200 completed orders, capped at 20 points a week:

  • A shop completing 400 orders a week earns 8 points a week from orders.
  • A shop completing 1,000 or more orders a week reaches the 20-point weekly cap.
  • A shop completing fewer than 200 orders a week earns nothing from orders that week, so quizzes and the 180-day expiry matter most.

Calculation: weekly orders ÷ 200 × 4, up to a maximum of 20. Smaller shops recover slowly, which is why preventing violations is far cheaper than fixing them.

How to protect and rebuild your score

  1. Check AHR weekly in Seller Center, and read every violation notice the day it arrives.
  2. Appeal quickly when a violation is wrong, with clear evidence (invoices, authorization letters, test reports).
  3. Take the policy quizzes. They are one of the two official ways to earn points back.
  4. Keep orders flowing. Every 200 completed orders earns 4 points, up to 20 a week, so steady, clean volume rebuilds the score.
  5. Set realistic handling times and use carriers with reliable tracking, especially before peak.
  6. Brief your creators in writing on what claims they may and may not make about your product.
  7. Audit listings before launch for restricted keywords, certifications and image rules.
Tip: Treat 150 as your floor, not 0. Plan big launches and campaign enrollment only when your score has a comfortable buffer above the first threshold.

Peak-season checklist

  • Confirm your AHR is well above 150 before campaign registration deadlines.
  • Resolve open violations and appeals now, not in November.
  • Stock up so you don't cancel orders for being out of stock.
  • Re-check creator scripts and product claims for holiday content.

TikTok Shop 店铺健康分（AHR）详解：扣分档位、限制与恢复方法

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-01 · 约 6 分钟阅读

在 TikTok Shop 美国站，店铺健康分（Account Health Rating，AHR）决定了你能不能上新品、能不能报名大促。分数一掉，旺季前可能好几周都不能上新；掉到零，店铺可能就没了。下面讲清楚分数怎么算、各档位意味着什么，以及如何守住它。

店铺健康分怎么算

根据 TikTok Shop 商家大学的 AHR 要求页面（2026 年 6 月 29 日更新）：

  • AHR 是 0–1,000 分的分色评分；
  • 所有卖家初始 200 分；
  • 违反政策会扣分，扣分多少取决于严重程度和频率；
  • 扣分180 天后自动失效；
  • 可以挣回分数：每完成 200 单加 4 分（每周最多 20 分），或通过政策测验。

据 AdBeacon 报道，AHR 已于 2026 年 7 月全面取代旧的违规积分制度；180 天的滚动周期意味着一次违规的影响时间是旧制度 90 天清零的两倍。

各档位会受到什么限制

分数降到特定档位时，TikTok Shop 会限制店铺：

健康分限制措施（TikTok Shop 官方）
1507 天内不能报名大促（mega campaign），不能新建商品
100同样限制 14 天
50同样限制 28 天
0账户可能被永久停用

因为所有卖家都从 200 分起步，一次严重违规就可能掉到第一个档位。10 月或 11 月失去大促报名资格，可能直接错过黑五网一的活动。

常见扣分原因

TikTok Shop 会对违反政策的行为扣分。实际操作中，卖家最常踩的坑包括：

  • 履约：发货超时、卖家原因取消订单、物流单号无效；
  • 商品：受限或禁售商品、标题图片误导、缺少资质证书；
  • 内容与宣传：你或合作达人做出夸大或医疗功效宣传；
  • 知识产权：销售或展示侵犯商标、版权的商品；
  • 客服：回复慢、引导买家站外交易。

每类违规具体扣多少分，以 TikTok Shop 卖家后台的执行政策为准。

示例：分数能恢复多快？

按 TikTok Shop 公布的恢复规则（每完成 200 单加 4 分，每周最多 20 分）计算：

  • 每周完成 400 单的店铺，每周可通过订单挣回 8 分；
  • 每周完成 1,000 单及以上的店铺，可达到每周 20 分的上限；
  • 每周完成不足 200 单的店铺，当周无法通过订单加分，只能靠政策测验和 180 天自动失效。

计算方法：每周订单数 ÷ 200 × 4，最多 20 分。小店铺恢复得很慢，所以预防违规远比事后补救划算。

如何守住和恢复分数

  1. 每周查看 AHR，违规通知当天就处理。
  2. 误判及时申诉，附上清晰证据（发票、授权书、检测报告）。
  3. 参加政策测验：这是官方挣回分数的两种方式之一。
  4. 保持稳定出单：每完成 200 单加 4 分，每周最多 20 分，健康的订单量能逐步把分数拉回来。
  5. 处理时效设置要实际，选择物流追踪可靠的承运商，旺季前尤其重要。
  6. 书面告知达人哪些宣传话术能说、哪些不能说。
  7. 上新前自查 Listing：违禁词、资质证书和图片规则。
小提示：把 150 分当作底线，而不是 0 分。只有在分数离第一个档位有足够余量时，才安排大规模上新和大促报名。

旺季检查清单

  • 大促报名截止前确认 AHR 明显高于 150 分；
  • 现在就处理未结的违规和申诉，别拖到 11 月；
  • 备足库存，避免因断货取消订单；
  • 重新检查节日内容中的达人脚本和产品宣传。
