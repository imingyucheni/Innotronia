Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) Guide: Fees, Weight Rules and When to Use It

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-15 · 6 min read

Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) is TikTok Shop's own warehousing and delivery service in the US: you send inventory to TikTok's warehouses, and TikTok picks, packs and ships each order. For sellers growing on TikTok Shop, FBT can mean free shipping badges and less operational work, but the fee rules change often. This guide summarizes the current rules from TikTok Shop's official FBT Rate Card & FAQ (updated November 10, 2026).

What FBT covers and how you pay

According to TikTok Shop, FBT fulfillment fees "include pick, pack, packaging, and last-mile shipping to the shopper" and are charged per unit. On top of that come storage fees, optional hub placement fees if you route inventory through TikTok's East or West hubs, and incident fees if inbound shipments don't follow the plan. Shipping directly to an FBT fulfillment center incurs no hub placement fee, provided you follow TikTok's routing and allocation requirements.

The rate card itself is published as tables in Seller Center. Fees vary by weight tier and units per order, so always check the live table before pricing.

How chargeable weight works

Since May 1, 2026, TikTok Shop charges small, light units on actual weight only: if a unit weighs 2 lb or less and its volume is 332 cubic inches or less. If either limit is exceeded, the chargeable weight is the greater of the actual weight or dimensional weight, calculated as length × width × height ÷ 166 (inches). Free shipping: keep it or opt out?

By default, all FBT units ship free to shoppers, which TikTok Shop says improves visibility on the Shop tab and For You page and gives access to free-shipping campaigns. Sellers with low-margin items can opt out: orders under $30 then pay a fixed $5.99 shipping fee (which TikTok receives), and the seller receives a $0.75 per-unit reimbursement on those orders. TikTok warns that opted-out products "may see reduced sales performance." For most items under $30, we suggest testing before opting out.

When FBT makes sense

  • Good fit: fast-moving SKUs, products driven by creators and GMV Max campaigns, and sellers who want free-shipping badges without running their own warehouse.
  • Think twice: slow movers (storage after 60 days), products with high return rates (per-unit return fees), and SKUs with bulky packaging.
  • Alternatives: ship from your own 3PL or use Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment; TikTok's academy includes an Amazon MCF setup guide.

Checklist before you send inventory

  1. Measure each SKU's packaged dimensions and weight; calculate chargeable weight with the ÷166 rule.
  2. Check the live FBT rate card for your weight tiers, single- and multi-unit.
  3. Plan inbound quantities for 60 days of sales to stay in the free storage window.
  4. Book inbound appointments and follow the routing plan to avoid incident fees.
  5. Decide on SIOC certification if your products ship in their own sturdy boxes.
  6. Set return disposition (return to seller or disposal) to control return fees.
  7. Monitor delivery and account health after switching.

TikTok Shop FBT 官方仓配指南：费用、计费重量规则与适用场景

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-15 · 约 6 分钟阅读

Fulfilled by TikTok（FBT）是 TikTok Shop 在美国的官方仓配服务：卖家把库存送进 TikTok 仓库，由 TikTok 负责拣货、打包和配送。对正在 TikTok Shop 起量的卖家来说，FBT 能带来包邮标识、减少运营工作，但费用规则变化很频繁。本文根据 TikTok Shop 官方 《FBT Rate Card & FAQ》（2026 年 11 月 10 日更新）整理现行规则。

FBT 包含什么、怎么收费

TikTok Shop 表示，FBT 配送费包含拣货、打包、包材和到买家的尾程配送，按件收取。此外还有仓储费；如选择经 TikTok 东部或西部中转仓入库，会产生可选的中转费；入库不按计划执行则会产生异常费用。直接送往 FBT 运营中心且符合路线与分仓要求的，不收中转费。

具体费率表在 Seller Center 以表格形式公布，按重量档和每单件数不同而变化，定价前请以最新费率表为准。

计费重量怎么算

自 2026 年 5 月 1 日起，小而轻的商品只按实重计费：单件实重 ≤2 磅且体积 ≤332 立方英寸。只要超过任一条件，计费重量就取实重与体积重中较大者，体积重 = 长 × 宽 × 高 ÷ 166（英寸）。下面是四个示例箱规的计算结果：

包邮：保留还是退出？

默认情况下所有 FBT 商品对买家包邮，TikTok Shop 表示这能提升商品在商城页和推荐页的曝光，并可参加包邮活动。低毛利商品可以选择退出：不满 30 美元的订单由买家支付固定 5.99 美元运费（这笔运费归 TikTok），卖家在这类订单上每件获得 0.75 美元补贴。TikTok 提醒，退出后商品"销量可能下降"。对 30 美元以下的商品，建议先测试再决定是否退出。

什么情况适合用 FBT

  • 适合：动销快的 SKU、依靠达人带货和 GMV Max 投放的商品，以及想拿包邮标识又不想自建仓的卖家。
  • 要慎重：动销慢的商品（60 天后开始收仓储费）、退货率高的商品（按件收退货费），以及包装体积大的 SKU。
  • 其他选择：用自己的海外仓发货，或使用 亚马逊多渠道配送（MCF）；TikTok 学习中心也有亚马逊 MCF 设置指南。

发货入仓前清单

  1. 测量每个 SKU 的包装尺寸和重量，按 ÷166 规则算出计费重量。
  2. 对照最新 FBT 费率表，查看单件和多件订单的重量档费用。
  3. 按约 60 天销量规划入库数量，尽量留在免费仓储期内。
  4. 预约入库并严格按路线计划送货，避免异常费用。
  5. 如果商品自带结实外箱，考虑申请 SIOC 认证。
  6. 设置退货处理方式（退回卖家或销毁），控制退货费用。
  7. 切换后持续关注配送表现和店铺健康分。
