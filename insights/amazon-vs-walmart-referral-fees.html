Amazon vs Walmart Referral Fees: A Category-by-Category Comparison

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-16 · 6 min read

If you sell on both Amazon and Walmart, you might assume their marketplace commissions are roughly the same. For many categories they are, but there are real differences in a few categories, in minimum fees and in fixed costs. This guide compares the two official fee schedules side by side so you can price each channel correctly.

The basics on each platform

ItemAmazonWalmart Marketplace
Fixed selling feeProfessional plan $39.99/month, or Individual plan $0.99 per item soldNo setup or monthly fees
Referral fee basisTotal price, including shipping and gift-wrap chargesTotal sales price, including shipping, handling, gift wrap and other charges
Minimum referral feeHigher of the percentage or a per-item minimum, $0.30 in most categoriesNo minimum stated on Walmart's page
Media closing fee$1.80 per media item (books, DVD, music, software, video)None listed

Sources: Amazon selling plans and pricing and Walmart Marketplace referral fees (accessed October 15–16, 2026).

Category names differ slightly between the platforms, and so do the exact product types inside each category. Always check the category your specific product falls into on each site.

What the differences look like in practice

Calculation for tiered categories (both platforms apply the lower rate to the portion above the threshold): furniture at $500 = 15% × $200 + 10% × $300 = $60 (12%); electronics accessories at $150 = 15% × $100 + 8% × $50 = $19 (12.7%); jewelry at $400 = 20% × $250 + 5% × $150 = $57.50 (14.4%).

The takeaways:

  • Most categories match. Home, toys, pet, beauty, grocery, furniture, jewelry and electronics accessories carry the same headline rates.
  • Some categories differ. Computers (8% vs 6%), watches (16% vs 15%) and clothing above $20 (17% vs 15%) cost more on Amazon.
  • Fixed costs differ. Amazon's Professional plan costs $39.99 a month; Walmart has no monthly fee. Amazon's $0.30 minimum also matters for very cheap items: on a $2 item, 15% would be $0.30 anyway, but on a $1.50 item the minimum is higher than the percentage.

Referral fees are only part of the cost. Fulfillment (FBA or WFS), storage, advertising and returns usually decide which channel is more profitable for a given SKU. For Walmart's side in detail, see our Walmart referral fees guide and WFS fees guide.

How to use this when pricing across channels

  1. Build one margin sheet per channel. Price, referral fee, fulfillment, ads, returns and fixed fees per unit.
  2. Check band thresholds in clothing, beauty, baby and grocery. A product that crosses $10, $15 or $20 may pay a higher rate on its whole price.
  3. Price channel by channel if fees differ. In categories like computers or clothing above $20, the same retail price leaves different margins on each site.
  4. Watch new-seller offers. Both platforms run incentive programs for new sellers or brands; Amazon's pricing page mentions referral-fee waivers for a new-to-Amazon brand's first year. Read the terms before counting on them.
  5. Re-check rates every year. Fee schedules change; confirm before peak season and before price changes.

亚马逊 vs 沃尔玛佣金：分类目对比

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-16 · 约 6 分钟阅读

同时在亚马逊和沃尔玛开店的卖家，可能以为两边佣金差不多。多数类目确实如此，但在少数类目、最低佣金和固定费用上存在实打实的差别。本文把两个平台的官方费率表放在一起对比，帮你给每个渠道定准价。

两个平台的基本规则

项目亚马逊沃尔玛 Marketplace
固定销售费用专业版 39.99 美元/月，或个人版每售出一件 0.99 美元无开店费、无月费
佣金计算基础总价，含运费和礼品包装费总售价，含运费、手续费、礼品包装及其他费用
最低佣金取百分比和每件最低额中较高者，多数类目最低 0.30 美元官方页面未列最低佣金
媒体类成交费每件媒体商品 1.80 美元（图书、DVD、音乐、软件、视频）未列出

来源：亚马逊销售计划与费用页面和沃尔玛 Marketplace 佣金页面（2026 年 10 月 15–16 日访问）。

差别在实际中的样子

分段类目计算方法（两个平台都对超过门槛的部分适用较低费率）：家具 500 美元 = 15% × 200 + 10% × 300 = 60 美元（12%）；电子配件 150 美元 = 15% × 100 + 8% × 50 = 19 美元（12.7%）；珠宝 400 美元 = 20% × 250 + 5% × 150 = 57.50 美元（14.4%）。

结论：

  • 多数类目一致。家居、玩具、宠物、美妆、食品杂货、家具、珠宝和电子配件的标准费率相同。
  • 部分类目有差别。电脑（8% 对 6%）、手表（16% 对 15%）和 20 美元以上的服装（17% 对 15%）在亚马逊更贵。
  • 固定成本不同。亚马逊专业版每月 39.99 美元，沃尔玛不收月费。亚马逊 0.30 美元的最低佣金对极低价商品也有影响：2 美元的商品按 15% 正好是 0.30 美元，但 1.50 美元的商品，最低佣金就高于按比例计算的金额。

佣金只是成本的一部分。履约（FBA 或 WFS）、仓储、广告和退货，往往才决定某个 SKU 在哪个渠道更赚钱。沃尔玛方面的详细规则，见我们的《沃尔玛佣金详解》和《WFS 费用详解》。

跨渠道定价怎么用

  1. 每个渠道一张毛利表。每件的售价、佣金、履约、广告、退货和固定费用分摊。
  2. 服装、美妆、母婴、食品杂货要盯价格档。售价跨过 10、15 或 20 美元，可能整单适用更高费率。
  3. 费率不同就分渠道定价。在电脑或 20 美元以上服装等类目，同样的零售价在两边留下的毛利不同。
  4. 留意新卖家优惠。两个平台都有新卖家或新品牌激励计划；亚马逊费用页面提到，新入驻亚马逊的品牌首年可享佣金减免。使用前先看清条款。
  5. 每年复核费率。费率表会调整，旺季前和调价前都要再确认一次。
