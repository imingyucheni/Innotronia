Walmart Pro Seller Program: Tiers, Thresholds and Benefits Explained

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-20 · 6 min read

Walmart rewards sellers who deliver on time, answer customers quickly and price competitively. Its Pro Seller program has three tiers (Rising Seller, Advanced Seller and Pro Seller) that unlock badges, referral fee discounts and shipping savings automatically when you meet the criteria. This guide explains the thresholds, the benefits and how to reach the top tier, based on Walmart's Pro Seller overview (updated Oct 16, 2026).

How the program works

There is no application. According to Walmart, "Once you meet the eligibility criteria, you'll earn a Rising Seller, Advanced Seller or Pro Seller status automatically." Metrics in the Pro Seller dashboard are calculated daily, and status refreshes on the 5th and 20th of every month. You receive the highest tier you qualify for at the next refresh, and you may lose status if you fall below the criteria for two consecutive refresh cycles.

The thresholds for each tier

Every tier also requires more than 100 completed orders in a rolling 90 days, at least 90 days active and compliance with all Marketplace policies. The Pro Seller tier adds a seller rating of 4.0 or higher. Cancellation rate must be at most 2.5% (Rising), 2.0% (Advanced) or 1.5% (Pro) over 90 days.

Definitions matter: on-time delivery is measured against the Expected Delivery Date over 90 days; seller response counts customer inquiries answered within 48 hours over 30 days; shipping speed score is the share of your published catalog offering free ThreeDay or faster delivery; content quality is the average score across your catalog; and price competitiveness compares your prices with similar products over 30 days.

Pro Listing Savings: the fee discount explained

The referral fee discount does not apply to every order. According to Walmart's Pro Listing Savings guide (updated Nov 12, 2026), you need Advanced or Pro Seller status, and each offer must have competitive pricing that meets or beats the market and fast, free shipping in two days or less. Qualified offers then earn a 5% (Advanced) or 10% (Pro) discount on referral fees for up to 90 days. Discounts from other programs can't be combined; Walmart applies the higher one. An order may miss the discount if the price wasn't competitive at the time or the gap between order date and deliver-by date exceeded two days.

How to reach Pro Seller

  1. Shipping speed first. Reaching 50% of your catalog on free ThreeDay-or-faster delivery is often the hardest step. Enroll in TwoDay delivery or Simplified Shipping Settings, or use Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS); expand TwoDay ZIP code coverage.
  2. Price competitively. Use Walmart's Repricer and submit external price matches; offers without a price match can't meet the competitive price requirement for Pro Listings.
  3. Lift content quality to 75%+. Fix images, attributes and descriptions using your Listing Quality score.
  4. Protect delivery and cancellations. Keep inventory accurate to stay under 1.5% cancellations and above 95% on-time delivery.
  5. Answer within 48 hours. Set daily inbox checks, including weekends.
  6. Use the benefits. Apply the SEM credit, use Ship with Walmart discounts, and review Pro Listings in the dashboard.

Pro Seller criteria sit on top of Walmart's baseline seller performance standards; missing those can cost you more than a badge.

沃尔玛 Pro Seller 项目详解：三个等级的门槛与权益

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-20 · 约 6 分钟阅读

沃尔玛会奖励准时送达、及时回复买家、价格有竞争力的卖家。它的 Pro Seller 项目分三个等级：Rising Seller、Advanced Seller 和 Pro Seller。达到标准后自动获得徽章、佣金折扣和运费优惠。本文根据沃尔玛官方 《Pro Seller 概览》（2026 年 10 月 16 日更新），讲清楚各级门槛、权益以及如何冲到最高等级。

项目怎么运作

无需申请。沃尔玛表示，达到资格标准后会"自动"获得 Rising Seller、Advanced Seller 或 Pro Seller 等级。Pro Seller 看板中的指标每天计算，等级在每月 5 日和 20 日刷新；达标后在下一次刷新时获得可达到的最高等级，连续两个刷新周期低于标准则可能失去等级。

各等级门槛

所有等级还要求：滚动 90 天内完成订单超过 100 单、活跃满 90 天、遵守所有平台政策；Pro Seller 另需卖家评分不低于 4.0。90 天取消率分别不得高于 2.5%（Rising）、2.0%（Advanced）和 1.5%（Pro）。

指标口径：准时送达率按 90 天内是否在预计送达日（EDD）前送达计算；卖家回复率为 30 天内 48 小时内回复买家咨询的比例；配送速度分为在售商品中提供免费 3 日达或更快配送的比例；内容质量分为全店商品的平均分；价格竞争力为 30 天内与同类商品的价格对比。

Pro Listing Savings：佣金折扣怎么拿

佣金折扣并非所有订单都有。根据沃尔玛 《Pro Listing Savings》指南（2026 年 11 月 12 日更新），你需要达到 Advanced 或 Pro Seller 等级，且每个商品要满足：价格达到或优于市场水平，并提供 2 日内免费送达。符合条件的商品可享最长 90 天的佣金折扣：Advanced 为 5%，Pro 为 10%。与其他项目的佣金折扣不能叠加，沃尔玛取较高者。如果下单时价格不具竞争力，或下单日到送达日超过两天，该订单可能拿不到折扣。

如何冲到 Pro Seller

  1. 先解决配送速度。让 50% 的商品提供免费 3 日达或更快配送，往往是最难的一步。可开通 TwoDay、简化运费设置（Simplified Shipping Settings），或使用沃尔玛仓配（WFS），并扩大 2 日达覆盖的邮编范围。
  2. 保持价格竞争力。使用沃尔玛 Repricer 自动调价，并提交外部比价（external price match）；没有比价的商品无法满足 Pro Listings 的价格要求。
  3. 内容质量提升到 75% 以上。参照 Listing Quality 分数优化图片、属性和描述。
  4. 控制送达和取消。库存准确，取消率低于 1.5%，准时送达率高于 95%。
  5. 48 小时内回复。每天检查消息，包括周末。
  6. 用好权益。用掉 SEM 广告额度，使用 Ship with Walmart 面单折扣，并定期查看 Pro Listings 看板。

Pro Seller 标准建立在沃尔玛基础的卖家绩效标准之上，后者不达标的代价远不止失去徽章。
