NRF Port Tracker: US Imports Peaked in September, Slower Months Ahead

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-11 · 5 min read

US retail imports had their biggest month of the year in September, but the extended 2026 import peak is now winding down. The latest Global Port Tracker from the National Retail Federation (NRF) and Hackett Associates, released on November 9, 2026, forecasts lower monthly volumes from November through March. For sellers who stock US warehouses and FBA, this tells you a lot about container space, rates and timing for spring inventory.

What the November Port Tracker says

According to Retail Insight Network's November 11 report on the NRF/Hackett data, US ports covered by the tracker handled 2.37 million TEU (twenty-foot equivalent units) of loaded imports in September 2026. That was up 3.4% from August and up 12.6% year over year. It was the busiest month of 2026 and the third-busiest month on record, behind only May 2022 (2.4 million TEU) and July 2025 (2.39 million TEU).

The forecast for the following months:

  • October 2026: 2.26 million TEU, up 9.1% year over year.
  • November and December 2026: 2.05 million and 2.03 million TEU, both slightly above 2025. FashionUnited puts the year-over-year gains at 1.2% and 1.3%.
  • January 2027: 2.04 million TEU (down 3%); February: 1.89 million (down 0.5%); March: 1.92 million (down 11.2%).
  • Full year 2026: about 26 million TEU, up 2% from 25.4 million in 2025. The first half of 2026 totaled 12.7 million TEU, up 1.1%.

Both reports attribute the shape of the year to tariffs. Hackett Associates founder Ben Hackett said the Supreme Court decision striking down the "Liberation Day" tariffs eased pressure early in 2026, and that new tariffs announced in the second quarter then drove a surge in imports from May to mid-October. NRF Vice President for Supply Chain and Customs Policy Jonathan Gold said, as quoted by FashionUnited: "Nonetheless, most holiday merchandise is already here, and retailers are working closely with their supply chain partners."

How the forecast changed in a month

NRF's October 8 release had already said the extended peak was "wrapping up." Compared with that edition, the new forecast raises November and the full-year total, and trims January and February slightly:

Month / periodOctober 8 forecastNovember forecast
November 20262.00M TEU2.05M TEU
December 20262.02M TEU2.03M TEU
January 20272.07M TEU2.04M TEU
February 20271.92M TEU1.89M TEU
Full year 202625.8M TEU26.0M TEU

In October, Gold said "we're likely past the busiest part of the year," and Hackett noted that "core economic indicators are broadly flat or weakening slightly month over month." The November numbers confirm that direction: the peak lasted longer than usual, but volumes are stepping down.

Labor watch at Los Angeles and Long Beach

Retail Insight Network also reports that ILWU Local 26, which represents watchmen at the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach, has agreed to a third short-term contract extension, to November 20, 2026. Its previous contract expired in July 2025. No disruption has been reported, but it is worth following if your containers are scheduled to arrive at San Pedro Bay in late November.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Holiday stock is mostly landed. If your Q4 inventory is not already in the US, plan for domestic replenishment, not ocean. Check carrier holiday shipping deadlines for last-mile cut-offs.
  • Softer early-2027 volumes can mean more negotiating room. A March forecast down 11.2% year over year suggests less competition for space around and after Lunar New Year. This is our reading, not a rate forecast; quote with several forwarders before you book.
  • Policy still drives volume. 2026 showed how quickly tariff news pulls imports forward. Keep watching the Section 301 exclusions and port-fee suspension that run to January 2027.
  • Don't over-order on a strong September. Much of that volume was tariff-driven front-loading, not a sign of stronger consumer demand.

Checklist for the next 60 days

  1. Reconcile what is in transit against Q4 sell-through forecasts; move slow stock to promotions rather than storing it into peak storage fees.
  2. Book spring (pre- and post-Lunar New Year) shipments early, while asking forwarders for quotes on softer lanes.
  3. Confirm your HTS codes, country-of-origin marking and ISF filings are clean, so cargo is not held when it does arrive.
  4. If you use LA/Long Beach, ask your forwarder for an update on the ILWU Local 26 extension after November 20.

NRF 港口追踪：美国进口 9 月见顶，未来数月逐步回落

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-11 · 约 5 分钟阅读

美国零售进口量在 9 月创下年内单月新高，但 2026 年被拉长的进口旺季正在收尾。美国零售联合会（NRF）与 Hackett Associates 于 2026 年 11 月 9 日发布的最新《全球港口追踪》（Global Port Tracker）预测，11 月至明年 3 月的月度进口量将逐步回落。对备货美国仓和 FBA 的卖家来说，这直接关系到舱位、运价和春季备货节奏。

11 月港口追踪报告说了什么

据 Retail Insight Network 11 月 11 日报道，追踪覆盖的美国港口 2026 年 9 月处理满载进口集装箱 237 万 TEU（20 英尺标准箱），环比增长 3.4%，同比增长 12.6%。这是 2026 年最繁忙的月份，也是有记录以来第三高的单月，仅次于 2022 年 5 月（240 万 TEU）和 2025 年 7 月（239 万 TEU）。

后续月份预测：

  • 2026 年 10 月：226 万 TEU，同比增长 9.1%。
  • 2026 年 11 月、12 月：分别为 205 万和 203 万 TEU，均略高于 2025 年同期；FashionUnited 给出的同比增幅为 1.2% 和 1.3%。
  • 2027 年 1 月：204 万 TEU（同比下降 3%）；2 月：189 万（下降 0.5%）；3 月：192 万（下降 11.2%）。
  • 2026 全年：约 2,600 万 TEU，比 2025 年的 2,540 万增长 2%；上半年合计 1,270 万 TEU，增长 1.1%。

两篇报道都把全年走势归因于关税。Hackett Associates 创始人 Ben Hackett 表示，最高法院推翻"解放日"关税，缓解了 2026 年初的压力；随后二季度宣布的新关税，推动了 5 月至 10 月中旬的进口高峰。据 FashionUnited 引述，NRF 供应链与海关政策副总裁 Jonathan Gold 表示，大部分假日季商品已经到港，零售商正与供应链伙伴密切配合。

一个月内预测有何变化

NRF 在 10 月 8 日的报告 中就已表示，被拉长的旺季"正在收尾"。与那一版相比，新预测上调了 11 月和全年总量，小幅下调了 1 月和 2 月：

月份 / 期间10 月 8 日版预测11 月版预测
2026 年 11 月200 万 TEU205 万 TEU
2026 年 12 月202 万 TEU203 万 TEU
2027 年 1 月207 万 TEU204 万 TEU
2027 年 2 月192 万 TEU189 万 TEU
2026 全年2,580 万 TEU2,600 万 TEU

10 月时 Gold 曾表示"我们很可能已经过了一年中最忙的阶段"，Hackett 也指出核心经济指标环比"大体持平或略有走弱"。11 月的数据印证了这一方向：旺季比往年持续更久，但量正在逐步下台阶。

洛杉矶 / 长滩港劳资动态

Retail Insight Network 还报道，代表洛杉矶港和长滩港守望员的 ILWU 第 26 分会已第三次同意短期延长合同，延至 2026 年 11 月 20 日；其上一份合同已于 2025 年 7 月到期。目前没有出现中断的报道，但如果你的集装箱计划 11 月下旬到达圣佩德罗湾港区，建议持续关注。

我们的解读

  • 假日季货基本已到港。如果 Q4 库存还没到美国，应改为美国境内补货，而不是指望海运。尾程截单时间可参考 假日季寄件截止日期。
  • 2027 年初进口量走软，议价空间可能变大。3 月预测同比下降 11.2%，意味着春节前后舱位竞争可能缓和。这是我们的判断，不是运价预测，订舱前请多找几家货代比价。
  • 政策依然决定货量。2026 年证明关税消息能迅速推动提前进口。请继续关注持续到 2027 年 1 月的 301 关税排除延期与港口费暂停。
  • 别被 9 月的高峰误导而过量下单。其中相当一部分是关税驱动的提前出货，并不代表终端需求变强。

未来 60 天行动清单

  1. 核对在途库存与 Q4 销售预测，滞销款尽早做促销，避免进入旺季高仓储费阶段。
  2. 春节前后的春季货尽早订舱，同时让货代就走软的航线报价。
  3. 确认 HTS 编码、原产地标识和 ISF 申报准确无误，避免货到后被扣。
  4. 走洛杉矶 / 长滩港的卖家，11 月 20 日后向货代确认 ILWU 第 26 分会合同进展。
