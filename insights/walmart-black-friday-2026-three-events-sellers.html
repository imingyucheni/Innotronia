Walmart Black Friday 2026: Three Events, 14 Deal Days From Nov 12

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-27 · 5 min read

Walmart's Black Friday season will be longer this year. On October 26, 2026, Walmart announced three holiday shopping events running from November 12 through December 2, adding up to 14 days of deals compared with 10 last year. For Marketplace sellers, that means more selling days, but also earlier inventory and pricing deadlines.

The 2026 schedule

According to Walmart's Oct 26, 2026 announcement:

EventDatesStart timesWalmart+ early access
Early Black Friday DealsNov 12–1512:01 a.m. ET online; 6 a.m. local in storesNov 12 (open to all Nov 13)
Black Friday DealsNov 23–2912:01 a.m. ET online Nov 23; stores from 6 a.m. local on Nov 27Nov 23 (open to all Nov 24)
Cyber Monday DealsNov 30 – Dec 212:01 a.m. ET online—

Walmart says the events cover thousands of items, with thousands of gifts under $20 and 25 toys from its Top Toys list under $25. During the two Black Friday events, select online-only deals are reserved for Walmart+ members for the first 24 hours.

Longer than 2025

Retail Dive (Oct 26, 2026) notes that the three events total 14 days this season, up from 10 days in 2025. Last year's events ran November 14–16, November 25–30 and December 1 for Cyber Monday, per Walmart's 2025 announcement. This year, Cyber Monday alone grows from one day to three.

Other numbers worth knowing

  • Shopper sentiment. In Walmart's own research, 60% of shoppers said gift and holiday prices affect how they feel about the season, and 70% said lower prices would make holiday shopping easier.
  • Membership push. Walmart+ and Sam's Club Plus can be bought together for $120 a year, about $100 less than buying both separately, according to Retail Dive.
  • Analyst view. Telsey Advisory Group wrote that "Walmart should be another winner this holiday season," Retail Dive reports.
  • Coverage. TODAY (Oct 26, 2026) lists deals of up to 77% off in its early roundup (the article is sponsored by Walmart).

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Your first deadline is earlier. The first event starts November 12, two days earlier than last year. If you sell through Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS), inventory needs to be received and live before then. Seller-fulfilled items need stock on hand and realistic shipping promises.

Three Cyber Monday days change the plan. With Cyber Monday running November 30 to December 2, plan stock and ad budgets to last through December 2 rather than spending everything on one day.

Value matters. Walmart's messaging focuses on low prices and gifts under $20. Bundles, multipacks and giftable price points fit that story.

Watch overlap with other channels. Walmart's events overlap with TikTok Shop's Nov 11–30 campaign. If you sell on both, make sure inventory and pricing rules don't conflict.

Online starts before stores. The main Black Friday event opens online on November 23, but stores join only on November 27. Expect online traffic to build through Thanksgiving week rather than spike on Friday alone, so keep ads running and inventory visible from November 23.

Note on delivery perks. Walmart's release says the Walmart+ free delivery benefit excludes most Marketplace items, so your own shipping speed and price matter.

Seller checklist

  1. Confirm WFS inbound shipments will be received before November 12.
  2. Set promotional prices for Nov 12–15, Nov 23–29 and Nov 30–Dec 2.
  3. Check Walmart's pricing rules so promo prices aren't flagged as unreasonable.
  4. Plan Walmart Connect budgets for all three windows, with extra for Black Friday week.
  5. Check carrier cutoffs and our holiday shipping deadlines guide for seller-fulfilled orders.
  6. Review stock daily during each event and pause ads before you sell out.

Walmart 2026 黑五：三场大促共 14 天，11 月 12 日开跑

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-27 · 约 5 分钟阅读

今年 Walmart 的黑五季更长。2026 年 10 月 26 日，Walmart 宣布 11 月 12 日至 12 月 2 日举办三场假日大促，促销天数合计 14 天，去年是 10 天。对 Marketplace 卖家来说，卖货的日子多了，但备货和定价的截止时间也更早了。

2026 年活动日程

据 Walmart 2026 年 10 月 26 日公告：

活动日期开始时间Walmart+ 会员抢先
早鸟黑五（Early Black Friday Deals）11 月 12–15 日线上美东 0:01；门店当地 6:0011 月 12 日（13 日对所有人开放）
黑五（Black Friday Deals）11 月 23–29 日线上 11 月 23 日美东 0:01；门店 11 月 27 日当地 6:00 起11 月 23 日（24 日对所有人开放）
网络星期一（Cyber Monday Deals）11 月 30 日–12 月 2 日线上美东 0:01—

Walmart 表示活动覆盖数千款商品，其中数千款礼品低于 20 美元，Top Toys 榜单中有 25 款玩具低于 25 美元。两场黑五活动期间，部分线上专享优惠前 24 小时仅限 Walmart+ 会员。

比 2025 年更长

据 Retail Dive（2026 年 10 月 26 日），今年三场活动合计 14 天，2025 年为 10 天。据 Walmart 2025 年公告，去年活动为 11 月 14–16 日、11 月 25–30 日，以及 12 月 1 日的网络星期一。今年仅网络星期一就从 1 天变成 3 天。

其他值得关注的数字

  • 消费者情绪。Walmart 自己的调研显示，60% 的购物者表示礼品和节日商品价格会影响他们对节日的感受，70% 表示更低的价格会让节日购物更轻松。
  • 会员捆绑。据 Retail Dive，Walmart+ 与 Sam's Club Plus 可合买，每年 120 美元，比分开买约省 100 美元。
  • 分析师观点。据 Retail Dive，Telsey Advisory Group 认为"Walmart 应该会再次成为本假日季的赢家"。
  • 媒体报道。TODAY（2026 年 10 月 26 日）的早期汇总中列出最高 77% 的折扣（该文为 Walmart 赞助内容）。

我们的解读

第一个截止日更早。首场活动 11 月 12 日开始，比去年早两天。用 WFS（Walmart 仓配）的卖家，库存必须在此前入仓上架；自发货卖家要备足库存，设置合理的配送承诺。

网络星期一变成三天。11 月 30 日至 12 月 2 日都是网一，库存和广告预算要撑到 12 月 2 日，别在一天内花光。

主打性价比。Walmart 的宣传重点是低价和 20 美元以下礼品，组合装、多件装和适合送礼的价位更契合。

注意与其他渠道重叠。Walmart 活动与 TikTok Shop 11 月 11–30 日大促时间重叠，多平台卖家要确保库存和价格规则不冲突。

线上先于门店开跑。主场黑五 11 月 23 日线上开始，门店要到 11 月 27 日才加入。预计线上流量会在感恩节这一周逐步升温，而不是只在周五爆发，所以从 23 日起就要保持广告在线、库存可见。

配送权益提醒。Walmart 公告注明，Walmart+ 免运费权益不包括大部分 Marketplace 商品，所以你自己的配送时效和运费更关键。

卖家行动清单

  1. 确认 WFS 入仓货件能在 11 月 12 日前签收上架。
  2. 为 11 月 12–15 日、23–29 日和 11 月 30 日–12 月 2 日分别设置促销价。
  3. 检查 Walmart 定价规则，避免促销价被判定为不合理价格。
  4. 为三个窗口规划 Walmart Connect 广告预算，黑五周多预留一些。
  5. 自发货订单核对承运商截止时间，参考我们的假日季寄件截止日期。
  6. 每场活动期间每天盯库存，断货前先暂停广告。
