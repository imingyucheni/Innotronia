Amazon Adds LTL Freight Labels, Walmart Builds Bulky Hub: What It Means for Big-Item Sellers

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-05 · 5 min read

Heavy and bulky products, such as furniture, fitness equipment and large appliances, are some of the hardest items to sell online profitably. In the past week, both Amazon and Walmart announced moves aimed at exactly this segment. For sellers of big items, especially those who ship from their own US warehouse, this changes the options for Q4 and 2027.

What Amazon announced

According to Supply Chain Dive's report of September 30, 2026 (via Yahoo Finance), Amazon announced at its Accelerate conference:

  • LTL labels in Buy Shipping (October 2026). Merchant-fulfilled sellers can buy less-than-truckload (LTL) freight labels through Amazon Buy Shipping in Seller Central and third-party shipping software. Amazon VP Ripley MacDonald said this means "lower label costs, with fewer disputes eating into your margins."
  • Regional delivery pricing (early 2027). Sellers will be able to set delivery fees by region for heavy and bulky items.
  • Local carriers (early 2027). Amazon will connect sellers with local carriers offering same-day or next-day delivery within 100 miles of their warehouse.
  • Seller Flex for heavy and bulky. Through this invite-only program, Amazon handles warehouse pickup, in-home delivery, installation and returns. Amazon says sellers using it see "more than 2.5 times the sales" of those shipping independently.

EcomCrew (Oct 1, 2026) adds a caution: LTL quotes may not include accessorials such as liftgate, residential delivery, appointments or inside delivery.

What Walmart announced

On September 28, 2026, Walmart announced plans to invest more than $300 million in a fulfillment center in Turtlecreek Township, Ohio, near Cincinnati, creating more than 300 jobs, according to JobsOhio and Supply Chain Dive. The site will specialize in larger, non-sortable items such as televisions and furniture, supporting Walmart's next-day delivery network.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Freight becomes a marketplace feature. Buying LTL inside Seller Central simplifies bulky FBM orders and may reduce delivery disputes, but you should compare total landed cost, including accessorials, with your current freight broker.
  • Regional pricing helps far-away orders. Bulky items shipped across the country often lose money. Charging by region could make more ZIP codes profitable from 2027.
  • Delivery speed for big items is the next battleground. Walmart's investment and Amazon's local-carrier plan show that faster delivery of furniture and TVs is a priority. Sellers with inventory in the right region will be favored.
  • Know your freight class. LTL pricing depends on freight class, which since July 2025 depends more heavily on density. See our LTL freight class guide.

What to do now

  1. List your heavy and bulky SKUs with dimensions, weight and current shipping cost per order.
  2. Test Amazon Buy Shipping LTL on 5–10 representative orders and compare full costs, including accessorials.
  3. Map where your bulky orders go; identify regions where shipping wipes out margin.
  4. Ask your Amazon account manager about Seller Flex eligibility if you ship heavy items in volume.
  5. Review packaging to reduce dimensions or improve density before peak season.
Tip: For bulky items, always ask a freight quote to list liftgate, residential and appointment fees separately. These extras often decide whether an order is profitable.

亚马逊上线零担面单、沃尔玛建大件仓：大件卖家要知道的事

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-05 · 约 5 分钟阅读

家具、健身器材、大家电等大件重货，是线上最难赚钱的品类之一。过去一周，亚马逊和沃尔玛都宣布了针对这一领域的新举措。对于卖大件、尤其是从自有美国仓发货的卖家来说，Q4 和 2027 年的选择变了。

亚马逊公布了什么

据 Supply Chain Dive 2026 年 9 月 30 日报道（经 Yahoo Finance 转载），亚马逊在 Accelerate 大会上宣布：

  • Buy Shipping 开放零担（LTL）面单（2026 年 10 月）：自发货卖家可在卖家后台和第三方发货软件中，通过亚马逊 Buy Shipping 购买零担货运面单。亚马逊副总裁 Ripley MacDonald 表示，这意味着“更低的面单成本，更少吃掉利润的纠纷”。
  • 按区域定价（2027 年初）：卖家可以按区域设置大件商品的配送费。
  • 本地承运商（2027 年初）：亚马逊将为卖家对接仓库 100 英里范围内可提供当日或次日达的本地承运商。
  • 大件 Seller Flex：在这个邀请制项目中，亚马逊负责上门提货、入户配送、安装和退货。亚马逊称参与卖家的销售额是自行发货卖家的“2.5 倍以上”。

EcomCrew（2026 年 10 月 1 日）提醒：零担报价可能不含尾板、住宅派送、预约、室内送货等附加费。

沃尔玛公布了什么

据 JobsOhio 和 Supply Chain Dive 报道，沃尔玛于 2026 年 9 月 28 日宣布，计划投资超过 3 亿美元，在俄亥俄州辛辛那提附近的 Turtlecreek 镇建设履约中心，创造 300 多个就业岗位。该中心专门处理电视、家具等无法上分拣线的大件商品，支持沃尔玛的次日达网络。

我们的解读

  • 货运变成平台功能：在卖家后台直接买零担面单，能简化大件自发货流程、减少配送纠纷，但要和现有货代对比含附加费的总成本。
  • 按区域定价有利于远距离订单：大件跨州配送常常亏钱，2027 年起按区域收费，可能让更多邮编变得有利可图。
  • 大件配送时效是下一个战场：沃尔玛的投资和亚马逊的本地承运商计划都说明，家具和电视的快速配送是重点，库存放在合适区域的卖家会更有优势。
  • 了解你的运费等级：零担价格取决于运费等级，而 2025 年 7 月起等级更多由密度决定。详见我们的零担运费等级指南。

现在可以做什么

  1. 列出所有大件 SKU 的尺寸、重量和当前单均运费。
  2. 挑 5–10 个有代表性的订单试用亚马逊 Buy Shipping 零担，对比含附加费的完整成本。
  3. 统计大件订单的目的地分布，找出运费吃掉利润的区域。
  4. 大件发货量大的卖家，向亚马逊客户经理咨询 Seller Flex 资格。
  5. 旺季前优化包装，缩小尺寸或提高密度。
小提示：大件货运报价一定要让对方把尾板、住宅派送、预约等费用单独列出，这些额外费用往往决定一单是否赚钱。
