Amazon Sponsored Brands: Formats, Pricing and Setup Guide

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-21 · 6 min read

Sponsored Products ads sell individual items. Sponsored Brands ads sell your brand: a logo, a headline and several products or a video at the top of search results. For brand-registered sellers, they are often the next step after a solid Sponsored Products setup. This guide explains what Sponsored Brands offers according to Amazon Ads, the pricing options, and a practical way to set up and manage campaigns.

What Sponsored Brands is

Amazon Ads describes Sponsored Brands as "an advertising solution built to help customers discover your brand within the Amazon store" (Amazon Ads, accessed Oct 21, 2026). Ads can send shoppers to your Brand Store or to a product detail page.

ItemWhat Amazon Ads says
Who can use itVendors, book vendors, KDP authors, agencies, and professional sellers enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry
Not eligibleAdult, used, refurbished and closed-category products
FormatsVideo ads; image ads (including AI-generated custom visuals at no cost); collections showing multiple products
PlacementsAbove, alongside or within shopping results on desktop and mobile; top of search; product pages; top of mobile search results and the mobile home page
Pricing optionsCost per click (CPC); cost per 1,000 viewable impressions (vCPM); fixed, upfront reserved share of voice for top-of-search on branded keywords (minimum spend commitment)
MinimumsNo minimum spend for CPC and vCPM campaigns; bids can be adjusted or campaigns paused any time

Features vary by marketplace. If you are not yet in Brand Registry, start with our US trademark guide.

Results Amazon highlights

Amazon's page includes case studies, with sources noted:

  • Loftie: return on ad spend of $5.66 and ACOS of 17.68% (agency-provided data, US, 2023).
  • NF Imports: 80% of sales from new customers between March 2018 and December 2022 (advertiser-provided data, Japan).
  • HP: impressions up 224% and clicks up 142% year over year, including a 42% increase in clicks for Sponsored Brands video placements (no footnote given).

These are selected examples supplied by advertisers or agencies, not averages. Use them as an idea of what is possible, not as a forecast.

Which format to use when

  • Collection (multiple products): good for category keywords, where you want to show a range, for example "yoga mats" or "travel adapters." Pick three products that sell well together.
  • Video: good for products that need a demo. Keep it short, show the product in the first seconds, and make it work without sound.
  • Brand Store landing page: good for brand keywords and broader catalogs, sending shoppers to a curated Store.

A simple setup that works

  1. Start with proven keywords. Use search terms that already convert in your Sponsored Products campaigns. Sponsored Brands rarely works as a discovery tool for brand-new products.
  2. Separate brand and non-brand campaigns. Protect your brand name with one campaign and grow category traffic with another, so you can judge each fairly.
  3. Choose the pricing model by goal. CPC for traffic and sales; vCPM if your goal is impression share; reserved share of voice only when owning top-of-search on your brand terms is worth a fixed commitment.
  4. Use a dedicated landing page. Send collections to a Store page with the same products, not a generic home page.
  5. Measure new-to-brand customers. Sponsored Brands is about building the brand; look at new-to-brand orders as well as ACOS.
  6. Review weekly. Add negative keywords, pause poor creatives, and move budget to the best performers.
Keep Sponsored Products as the base of your Amazon ads. Add Sponsored Brands on keywords where you already convert, then expand. See our Sponsored Products bidding guide.

Common mistakes

  • Judging it only on ACOS. Sponsored Brands often costs more per sale than Sponsored Products but brings in shoppers who have not bought from you before. Track new-to-brand orders alongside ACOS.
  • Weak creative. A blurry logo, a generic headline or a video that starts slowly wastes the most visible spot on the page.
  • Mismatched products. Showing unrelated items in one collection confuses shoppers; keep each ad to one need.
  • Bidding on everything. Broad, unproven keywords burn budget quickly at top-of-search prices.

亚马逊品牌推广（Sponsored Brands）指南：形式、计费与搭建

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-21 · 约 6 分钟阅读

商品推广（Sponsored Products）卖的是单个商品，品牌推广（Sponsored Brands）卖的是品牌：在搜索结果顶部展示品牌 Logo、标语以及多个商品或一段视频。对已品牌备案的卖家来说，在商品推广跑顺之后，品牌推广往往是下一步。本文依据亚马逊广告官方说明，讲清品牌推广提供什么、有哪些计费方式，以及一套实用的搭建和管理方法。

品牌推广是什么

亚马逊广告称品牌推广是“帮助顾客在亚马逊商城发现你的品牌的广告方案”（亚马逊广告，2026 年 10 月 21 日访问）。广告可以把买家引到你的品牌旗舰店或商品详情页。

项目亚马逊广告的说明
谁可以使用供应商、图书供应商、KDP 作者、代理商，以及已加入品牌备案的专业卖家
不符合条件成人、二手、翻新及受限类目商品
广告形式视频广告；图片广告（含免费的 AI 生成定制图片）；展示多个商品的商品集
展示位置桌面和移动端搜索结果上方、旁边或之中；搜索顶部；商品详情页；移动端搜索顶部和移动首页
计费方式按点击（CPC）；按千次可见展示（vCPM）；固定预付的品牌词搜索顶部预留份额（需最低花费承诺）
最低花费CPC 和 vCPM 活动无最低花费，可随时调整出价或暂停

各站点功能可能不同。如果还没有品牌备案，可先看我们的《美国商标注册指南》。

亚马逊展示的案例

亚马逊页面列出了以下案例，并注明数据来源：

  • Loftie：广告支出回报 5.66 美元，ACOS 17.68%（代理商提供数据，美国，2023 年）。
  • NF Imports：2018 年 3 月至 2022 年 12 月 80% 的销售来自新客（广告主提供数据，日本）。
  • HP：展示量同比增长 224%，点击同比增长 142%，其中品牌推广视频位点击增长 42%（未注明来源）。

这些是广告主或代理商提供的精选案例，不是平均水平。可作为参考上限，不要当作预测。

不同形式怎么选

  • 商品集（多商品）：适合品类词，想展示产品线时使用，例如“瑜伽垫”“转换插头”。选三款能搭配销售的畅销品。
  • 视频：适合需要演示的产品。时长要短，前几秒就展示产品，静音也能看懂。
  • 旗舰店落地页：适合品牌词和产品线较宽的品牌，把买家引到精心布置的旗舰店。

一套好用的搭建方法

  1. 从已验证的关键词开始。用商品推广里已经出单的搜索词。品牌推广很少适合用来给全新产品“开荒”。
  2. 品牌词与非品牌词分开。一个活动守品牌词，另一个拓展品类流量，便于分别评估。
  3. 按目标选计费方式。要流量和销量用 CPC；要展示份额用 vCPM；只有当占据品牌词搜索顶部值得固定投入时，才考虑预留份额。
  4. 使用专属落地页。商品集广告指向包含相同商品的旗舰店页面，而不是笼统的首页。
  5. 关注品牌新客。品牌推广重在建立品牌，除了 ACOS，还要看品牌新客订单。
  6. 每周复盘。添加否定词、暂停表现差的素材，把预算转向表现最好的组合。
亚马逊广告仍以商品推广为基础。在已经出单的关键词上叠加品牌推广，再逐步扩展。参见我们的《商品推广竞价策略》。

常见误区

  • 只看 ACOS。品牌推广每单成本往往高于商品推广，但能带来从未买过你产品的新客。在看 ACOS 的同时跟踪品牌新客订单。
  • 素材质量差。模糊的 Logo、空洞的标语或开头拖沓的视频，会浪费页面上最显眼的位置。
  • 商品搭配混乱。同一商品集里放不相关的商品会让买家困惑，每条广告只解决一个需求。
  • 什么词都投。宽泛、未经验证的关键词在搜索顶部的出价下会迅速烧掉预算。
