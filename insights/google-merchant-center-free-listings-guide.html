Google Free Listings Guide: Show Your Products on Google for Free

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-29 · 6 min read

If your brand has its own website, Google can show your products to shoppers for free. Through Google Merchant Center, products can appear in the Shopping tab, search results, Google Images and more without paying for clicks. Many marketplace sellers never set it up. Here is how free listings work, what product data Google requires, and how to avoid the most common disapprovals.

What free listings are and where they appear

According to Google Merchant Center Help — Free listings, free listings let your products appear on Google at no cost, though showing is not guaranteed because Google matches products to searches using your data. Surfaces include:

  • Shopping tab on mobile and desktop.
  • Search rich results with price, availability and reviews.
  • Popular Products carousels for apparel and accessories (US, mobile).
  • Shopping Knowledge Panel, where strong identifiers like GTIN or MPN plus brand help.
  • Google Images and Lens product labels.
  • YouTube shoppable listings in some countries, plus Maps, Gemini and Business Profile modules.

New Merchant Center accounts are usually opted in automatically. You can check under Marketing methods, on the Free listings card; changing the setting requires Admin access.

The product data Google requires

Your product feed is everything. These are the core attributes from Google's product data specification:

Source: Google Merchant Center Help, product data specification (accessed Oct 29, 2026). Apparel also needs color, size, age group and gender. Google recommends adding up to 10 additional images per product.

Shipping and returns are not optional

Google requires shipping settings (in Merchant Center or via the shipping attribute) in 30 countries, including the US, UK, Canada, Japan and Germany, and asks you to add return policy information, ideally in Merchant Center. Wrong shipping costs are a common reason for problems, so make sure your settings match your checkout.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Create a Google Merchant Center account for your business.
  2. Verify your website domain, since product links must use it.
  3. Set up shipping rates and delivery times that match your checkout.
  4. Add your return policy.
  5. Upload products by feed file, spreadsheet, API or an ecommerce platform integration.
  6. Fix any disapprovals in the Products section (missing GTIN, price mismatch, small images are typical).
  7. Confirm free listings are on under Marketing methods.
Price and availability on your site must match the feed. If you run promotions on your site, update the feed at the same time, or products can be disapproved during exactly the weeks you most want traffic.

Tips to get more visibility

  • Write titles for search. Put brand, product type and key attributes (size, color, material) in the first part of the title.
  • Use real GTINs. They help Google match your product in comparison surfaces such as the Knowledge Panel.
  • Add more images. Lifestyle and detail shots, up to 10 additional images.
  • Keep the feed fresh. Sync stock and prices daily, especially in Q4.
  • Collect reviews on your site, since rich results can show ratings.

Google keeps adding shopping features, such as Google Universal Cart, and they all start with a clean Merchant Center feed.

Free listings checklist

  1. Merchant Center account created and domain verified.
  2. Shipping and returns set up.
  3. All required attributes filled; GTINs added where available.
  4. Images at least 500 × 500 px before January 31, 2027.
  5. Free listings switched on; disapprovals reviewed weekly.

Google 免费商品展示指南：让商品免费出现在 Google

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-29 · 约 6 分钟阅读

如果你的品牌有独立站，Google 可以免费展示你的商品。通过 Google Merchant Center，商品可以出现在购物标签页、搜索结果、Google 图片等位置，无需按点击付费。很多平台卖家从来没有设置过。本文讲清楚免费商品展示（free listings）怎么运作、Google 要求哪些商品数据，以及如何避开最常见的拒登问题。

免费展示是什么、出现在哪里

据 Google Merchant Center 帮助中心 — 免费商品展示，免费展示让你的商品零成本出现在 Google 上，但不保证展示，因为 Google 依据你的数据把商品匹配到搜索。展示位置包括：

  • 购物标签页（移动端和桌面端）。
  • 搜索富媒体结果，显示价格、库存和评价。
  • 热门商品轮播（服装配饰，美国移动端）。
  • 购物知识面板，有 GTIN 或 MPN 加品牌等强标识更有利。
  • Google 图片和 Lens商品标签。
  • 部分国家的 YouTube 可购物列表，以及地图、Gemini 和商家资料模块。

新 Merchant Center 账户通常会自动开启。可在"营销方式"中的"免费商品展示"卡片查看；修改设置需要管理员权限。

Google 要求的商品数据

商品 Feed 决定一切。以下是 Google 商品数据规范中的核心属性：

属性要求限制 / 格式
id必填最多 50 个字符，建议用 SKU
title（标题）必填最多 150 个字符
description（描述）必填最多 5,000 个字符
link（链接）必填必须使用已验证的域名
image_link（图片）必填至少 500 × 500 像素（2027 年 1 月 31 日起执行）
price（价格）必填数字加币种，如 15.00 USD
availability（库存）必填in_stock、out_of_stock、preorder 或 backorder
brand（品牌）新品必填（多数类目）最多 70 个字符
gtin / mpn强烈建议提供 GTIN；没有 GTIN 时填 MPNGTIN 每个值最多 14 位数字
condition（新旧）二手或翻新商品必填new、refurbished、used

来源：Google Merchant Center 帮助中心商品数据规范（2026 年 10 月 29 日访问）。服装类还需填写颜色、尺码、年龄段和性别。Google 建议每个商品最多再补充 10 张附加图片。

运费和退货必须设置

Google 在包括美国、英国、加拿大、日本、德国在内的 30 个国家要求设置运费（在 Merchant Center 中设置或用 shipping 属性提交），并要求填写退货政策，最好直接在 Merchant Center 中设置。运费不准是常见问题，务必与网站结账页一致。

设置步骤

  1. 为公司创建 Google Merchant Center 账户。
  2. 验证网站域名，商品链接必须使用该域名。
  3. 设置与结账页一致的运费和配送时效。
  4. 添加退货政策。
  5. 通过 Feed 文件、表格、API 或电商平台插件上传商品。
  6. 在"商品"页面处理拒登问题（常见原因：缺 GTIN、价格不一致、图片太小）。
  7. 在"营销方式"中确认免费展示已开启。
网站上的价格和库存必须与 Feed 一致。独立站做促销时要同步更新 Feed，否则商品可能恰好在你最需要流量的那几周被拒登。

提升曝光的技巧

  • 按搜索习惯写标题。把品牌、品类和关键属性（尺寸、颜色、材质）放在标题前部。
  • 使用真实 GTIN。有助于 Google 在知识面板等比价场景中匹配你的商品。
  • 多放图片。场景图、细节图，最多 10 张附加图片。
  • 保持 Feed 更新。每天同步库存和价格，Q4 尤其重要。
  • 积累站内评价，富媒体结果可以显示星级。

Google 在不断推出新的购物功能，比如 Google Universal Cart，而这些都以一份干净的 Merchant Center Feed 为前提。

免费展示清单

  1. 已创建 Merchant Center 账户并验证域名。
  2. 已设置运费和退货政策。
  3. 必填属性齐全，有 GTIN 的都已填写。
  4. 2027 年 1 月 31 日前所有图片达到 500 × 500 像素以上。
  5. 已开启免费展示，每周检查拒登。
