TikTok Shop Plus: A $9.99 Membership Pilot for 1 Million US Shoppers

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-16 · 5 min read

TikTok Shop is testing a paid membership in the US. According to Retail Dive (Nov 12, 2026), TikTok rolled out "TikTok Shop Plus" to 1 million shoppers on a Saturday shortly before the report, according to a company spokesperson. It costs $9.99 a month after a 30-day free trial. For sellers, a membership program changes which products get the free-shipping badge and how often loyal buyers come back.

What TikTok Shop Plus includes

Retail Dive reports members get free shipping and exclusive discounts. WERSM (Nov 11) and Chinesellers (Nov 16), citing Chinese trade outlet Ebrun, give more detail:

Member benefitDetails reported
Free shippingOn eligible products with a "Plus" badge
Extra discountAdditional 10% off selected items
CouponsThree per month
Instant refundsUp to two per month on eligible products, issued as TikTok Shop credit before the return is dropped off
HolidayMember-exclusive deals during Black Friday and Cyber Monday

The pilot is invitation-based. WERSM describes it as offered to "more than one million of its most loyal U.S. customers," and TikTok has not said when, or whether, it will open to all US users.

At $9.99, TikTok Shop Plus is $5 a month cheaper than Prime's monthly plan, which Amazon's own Prime page lists at $14.99. Unlike Prime, the reported benefits focus only on shopping; there is no video or delivery-speed promise in the reports.

The bigger picture

Retail Dive cites Emarketer estimates that TikTok Shop made up nearly 20% of US social commerce by the end of 2025, that its US sales are expected to exceed $20 billion this year and surpass $30 billion in 2028, and that it will account for nearly a quarter of US social commerce sales by 2027. The membership arrives in TikTok Shop's first holiday season with retailers such as Best Buy, Ulta Beauty and Sephora on the platform, according to WERSM, and after the fall Happy Returns integration.

What we don't know yet

  • Seller costs. None of the reports say whether sellers fund any part of the extra 10% discount, the coupons or member free shipping. Until TikTok publishes seller terms, assume nothing and check every promotion invitation carefully.
  • Which products get the Plus badge. The reports describe a "Plus" label on eligible products but not the criteria, for example whether FBT or fast handling time is required.
  • Member numbers and results. TikTok has not shared how many shoppers accepted the trial or how members shop compared with non-members.
  • Timing of a wider launch. Expansion beyond the pilot group has not been announced.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • The "Plus" badge will matter. If members get free shipping only on Plus-badged items, those listings should convert better with members. Watch Seller Center for how products qualify; we have not seen official seller-side criteria yet.
  • Free shipping costs someone. FBT units already ship free by default (see our Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) guide). Self-fulfilled sellers should check their shipping cost per order before opting into any member program.
  • Instant refunds raise return risk. Refunds issued before the item is dropped off speed up the customer experience but put more pressure on sellers' return processes and fraud checks.
  • Loyalty shifts the playbook. TikTok Shop has relied on impulse discovery. A membership means more repeat buyers, so reviews, bundles and replenishable products become more valuable.

What to do now

  1. Check Seller Center and seller emails for any TikTok Shop Plus enrollment or badge criteria.
  2. Review shipping cost and margin on your top SKUs at 10% extra discount.
  3. Tighten return policies and inspection steps ahead of the Black Friday and Cyber Monday campaign.
  4. Plan repeat-purchase offers (bundles, subscriptions to restock) for loyal buyers.

TikTok Shop Plus：9.99 美元付费会员向 100 万美国用户试点

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-16 · 约 5 分钟阅读

TikTok Shop 正在美国测试付费会员。据 Retail Dive（2026 年 11 月 12 日） 报道，TikTok 发言人称公司已在报道前的一个周六向 100 万名购物者推出"TikTok Shop Plus"，30 天免费试用后每月 9.99 美元。对卖家来说，会员体系会影响哪些商品能拿到包邮标识，以及忠实买家的复购频率。

TikTok Shop Plus 包含什么

Retail Dive 报道称，会员可享包邮和专属折扣。WERSM（11 月 11 日） 和引述亿邦动力的 Chinesellers（11 月 16 日） 给出了更多细节：

会员权益报道内容
包邮带"Plus"标识的合格商品免运费
额外折扣指定商品再享 9 折
优惠券每月 3 张
即时退款合格商品每月最多 2 次，在退货寄出前以 TikTok Shop 余额形式退款
假日季黑五网一期间会员专属优惠

这次试点采用邀请制。WERSM 称会员资格面向"100 多万名最忠实的美国用户"，TikTok 尚未说明何时、或是否会向所有美国用户开放。

价格对比

会员月费免费试用来源
TikTok Shop Plus（试点）9.99 美元30 天Retail Dive，2026 年 11 月 12 日
Walmart+12.95 美元—亿邦动力（经 Chinesellers），2026 年 11 月 16 日
Amazon Prime14.99 美元（或 139 美元/年）30 天亚马逊 Prime 开通页面，2026 年 11 月 16 日查阅

9.99 美元的月费比 Prime 月度会员便宜 5 美元——亚马逊 Prime 官方页面 标价 14.99 美元/月。与 Prime 不同，目前报道的权益只集中在购物本身，没有视频服务或配送时效承诺。

更大的背景

Retail Dive 引述 Emarketer 估计：截至 2025 年底，TikTok Shop 约占美国社交电商的近 20%；今年美国销售额预计超过 200 亿美元，2028 年将超过 300 亿美元；到 2027 年将占美国社交电商销售额的近四分之一。据 WERSM，这是 Best Buy、Ulta Beauty、Sephora 等零售商入驻后 TikTok Shop 的第一个假日季，此前秋季平台还接入了 Happy Returns 免箱退货服务。

目前尚不清楚的问题

  • 卖家成本。目前的报道都没有说明额外 9 折、优惠券和会员包邮是否需要卖家分摊。在 TikTok 公布卖家条款前，不要预设任何结论，认真核对每一份活动邀请。
  • 哪些商品能拿到 Plus 标识。报道提到合格商品带有"Plus"标识，但没有说明标准，例如是否要求使用 FBT 或更短的备货时间。
  • 会员数量与效果。TikTok 没有披露有多少用户开通了试用，也没有披露会员与非会员的购物差异。
  • 何时全面推出。试点人群之外的扩展计划尚未公布。

我们的解读

  • "Plus"标识会越来越重要。如果会员只在带 Plus 标识的商品上享受包邮，这些 listing 对会员的转化会更高。请关注 Seller Center 中商品如何获得该标识——目前我们还没看到官方面向卖家的标准。
  • 包邮总有人买单。使用 FBT 的商品默认已经包邮（参考我们的 FBT 官方仓配 指南）。自发货卖家在参加任何会员项目前，先算清每单运费。
  • 即时退款会放大退货风险。在商品寄回前就退款，买家体验更好，但对卖家的退货流程和防欺诈检查压力更大。
  • 从冲动消费转向会员复购。TikTok Shop 过去依赖内容种草带来的冲动购买；会员制意味着复购买家更多，评价、组合装和可复购商品的价值会更高。

现在可以做什么

  1. 留意 Seller Center 和平台邮件中关于 TikTok Shop Plus 报名或标识的规则。
  2. 按额外 9 折测算主力 SKU 的运费和毛利。
  3. 在 黑五网一大促 前收紧退货政策和验货流程。
  4. 为忠实买家规划复购方案（组合装、补货提醒等）。
