Amazon Negative Keywords: How to Cut Wasted Ad Spend Without Losing Sales

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-13 · 6 min read

Negative keywords are the cheapest way to cut wasted Amazon ad spend: they stop your ads from showing on searches that click but don't buy. Used carelessly, they can also block searches that would have sold. This guide covers how negatives work, Amazon's own rules of thumb, and a simple weekly routine, based on Amazon Ads' published guidance.

What negative targeting does

According to Amazon Ads' guide to targeting with Sponsored Products, negative keywords "help prevent ads appearing on shopping results pages that do not meet your campaign objectives," which can help improve click-through rate (CTR), return on ad spend (ROAS) and cost per click (CPC). Amazon's keyword targeting guide says you can add negative keywords to Sponsored Products automatic, product and keyword targeting campaigns, and to Sponsored Brands keyword targeting campaigns, at the campaign or ad group level.

There are also negative product targets (ASINs and brands). Amazon's example: if a camera lens ad appears next to a camera it doesn't fit, adding that camera as a negative ASIN stops the ad from showing there.

Negative phrase vs negative exact

  • Use negative exact when one specific search wastes money but related searches work. Example: you sell an iPhone 16 case and the search "iphone 15 case" gets clicks but no orders. Negating it exactly blocks just that search.
  • Use negative phrase when a word signals the wrong shopper in any search. Amazon's own example: for a luxury product, terms like "cheap" or "inexpensive" are likely not relevant.
  • Be careful with short phrases. A negative phrase like "case" would block almost everything for a case seller. The shorter the phrase, the more it blocks.

How to decide what to negate

Start from the Search Term Report. Amazon recommends adding terms "from your Search Term Report that aren't driving results," and evaluating a keyword "after it has received at least 20 clicks" before negating it. That threshold protects you from cutting a search after a few unlucky clicks.

A simple way to see the cost (illustration, not real data): if a search term has 20 clicks at an average CPC of $1.20 and zero orders, it has cost $24 with nothing to show. Calculation: 20 × $1.20 = $24. Use your own CPC and conversion rate; if your product usually converts at 10%, 20 clicks with no sale is a stronger signal than for a product that converts at 3%.

Good candidates for negatives, per Amazon's guide:

  • Low-performing keywords and shopping terms you don't want to appear against.
  • Low-performing ASINs, ASINs you don't want to appear on, and non-complementary ASINs.
  • Searches for products you don't sell: wrong model, size, material or use case.

A weekly routine

  1. Download the Search Term Report for the last 14–30 days.
  2. Filter for terms with 20+ clicks and zero orders, or ACoS far above your target.
  3. Check each term against your product: irrelevant → negate; relevant but expensive → lower the bid instead (see our bidding strategies).
  4. Choose exact or phrase match deliberately; prefer exact when unsure.
  5. When a manual campaign converts well on a search term, add it as exact there and consider negating it in the auto campaign so the two don't compete.
  6. Log every negative with the date, and wait two weeks before judging the change.
  7. Review the list monthly. Amazon notes negatives are "not a permanent action, you can always remove them."

Common mistakes

  • Negating your own brand name or core category terms because of one bad week.
  • Adding broad, short negative phrases that silently kill traffic.
  • Negating at campaign level when only one ad group has the problem.
  • Never revisiting old negatives after a listing, price or season changes.

Negatives are one part of efficiency; see also how to lower ACoS.

亚马逊否定关键词指南：减少广告浪费又不丢订单

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-13 · 约 6 分钟阅读

否定关键词是降低亚马逊广告浪费最省钱的办法：让广告不再出现在"有点击、没转化"的搜索词上。但用得不好，也会把本来能出单的流量挡在门外。本文根据 Amazon Ads 官方指南，讲清楚否定词的原理、官方给出的经验规则，以及一套每周可执行的优化流程。

否定定位有什么用

根据 Amazon Ads 的 Sponsored Products 定位指南，否定关键词可以防止广告出现在不符合广告目标的搜索结果页上，有助于提升点击率（CTR）、广告支出回报（ROAS）并控制单次点击成本（CPC）。官方 关键词定位指南 说明：否定关键词可用于 SP 的自动、商品定位和关键词定位广告，以及 SB 的关键词定位广告，可在广告活动或广告组层级设置。

除了关键词，还可以否定商品（ASIN）和品牌。官方举例：镜头广告出现在不兼容的相机页面上，把这款相机设为否定 ASIN，广告就不会再在那里展示。

否定词组 vs 否定精准

  • 用否定精准：某一个搜索词烧钱，但相关词效果不错。例如你卖 iPhone 16 手机壳，"iphone 15 case"有点击没订单，精准否定只会挡住这一个词。
  • 用否定词组：某个词出现在任何搜索里都说明不是你的目标买家。官方举例：卖高端产品时，"cheap""inexpensive"这类词通常不相关。
  • 短词组要慎用。手机壳卖家如果否定词组"case"，几乎会挡掉全部流量。词组越短，挡掉的越多。

如何判断该否定什么

从搜索词报告（Search Term Report）入手。官方建议把报告中"没有带来效果"的词加入否定，并且在否定前，先让一个词积累"至少 20 次点击"再评估。这个门槛能避免因为几次偶然点击就误杀搜索词。

算一笔账（示意，非真实数据）：某搜索词 20 次点击、平均 CPC 1.2 美元、0 订单，就是花了 24 美元没有回报。计算：20 × 1.2 = 24 美元。请用自己的 CPC 和转化率判断：平时转化率 10% 的产品，20 次点击 0 单是比转化率 3% 的产品更强的信号。

官方指南建议优先考虑否定的对象：

  • 表现差的关键词，以及你不希望广告出现的搜索词。
  • 表现差的 ASIN、不希望出现的 ASIN，以及广告组中不互补的 ASIN。
  • 你不卖的产品的搜索：型号、尺寸、材质或用途不对的词。

每周优化流程

  1. 下载最近 14–30 天的搜索词报告。
  2. 筛选点击 20 次以上且 0 订单、或 ACoS 远高于目标的词。
  3. 逐个对照产品判断：不相关 → 否定；相关但太贵 → 降低出价（参考我们的 竞价策略）。
  4. 有意识地选择精准还是词组，拿不准时优先用否定精准。
  5. 手动广告里某个搜索词转化好，就在手动活动中以精准匹配投放，并考虑在自动广告中否定它，避免两边互相抢量。
  6. 每次加否定都记录日期，至少观察两周再评估效果。
  7. 每月复查否定列表。官方提醒：否定"不是永久操作，随时可以移除"。

常见错误

  • 因为一周数据不好，就否定了自己的品牌词或核心类目词。
  • 加了宽泛的短词组否定，流量悄悄被砍掉。
  • 只有一个广告组有问题，却在整个广告活动层级否定。
  • listing、价格或季节变化后，从不回头检查旧的否定词。
