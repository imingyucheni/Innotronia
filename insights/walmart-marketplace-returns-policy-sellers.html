Walmart Marketplace Returns Policy: A Seller Guide for 2026

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-24 · 6 min read

Returns are where many new Walmart Marketplace sellers get surprised. Walmart sets the rules, not you: the return window, who pays shipping, whether you can charge a restocking fee, and even whether a Walmart store takes the item back on your behalf. With holiday orders arriving now, here is how Walmart's returns policy for seller-fulfilled orders works, based on the official policy last updated August 31, 2026.

The core rules at a glance

According to Walmart Marketplace Learn — Returns policy (updated Aug 31, 2026), sellers must accept eligible returns regardless of the reason or the item's condition, except for items set up with an approved exemption (for example Final Sale items).

RuleWhat Walmart requires
Minimum return window30 days, starting 7 days after the ship date
Holiday returnsMost items bought Oct 1 – Dec 31 returnable until Jan 31 (exceptions include wireless phones, protection plans, major appliances, food and beverages, select seasonal and luxury goods)
Return shipping charged to customerNot allowed
Restocking feeNot allowed, except up to 20% of item price for exempt items not returned in original or sellable condition
Return label for exempt itemsSeller must provide it within 48 hours
Refund timing (Walmart.com / Customer Care)When the carrier first scans the return
Refund timing (in store)When the store receives the item

Source: Walmart Marketplace Learn returns policy. Rules can change; always check the current page before setting up your account.

The holiday window matters right now

Because most items bought from October 1 to December 31 can be returned until January 31, an order shipped on October 2 may come back almost four months later. Plan for a longer tail of returns in January, and don't count Q4 revenue as final until February.

Return labels: RSS or your own carrier account

By default, Walmart generates return labels through its Returns Shipping Service (RSS), which offers discounted return rates. According to the Walmart RSS policy (updated Dec 11, 2025), your business is the shipper of record, Walmart picks the carrier (the policy names FedEx and USPS), and Walmart is not liable for loss or damage to RSS packages. If a return is lost, you file the claim with the carrier yourself; see our guide on how to file a USPS claim. You can also use your own FedEx or UPS account for return labels.

Either way, you need a valid US return address (not a PO box, and not in Hawaii, Alaska or US territories) and at least one active return center. If the address is invalid, packages may be rerouted, and you pay the resulting charges.

In-store returns

Walmart stores accept eligible Marketplace items on your behalf, which customers like and which you can't opt out of for normal items. Items that need a return exemption are not eligible for in-store returns, including odd-sized, freight-shipped, oversized, heavy and luxury items, major appliances and fully regulated hazardous materials. If one of these is returned in store anyway, the policy says the seller bears the costs.

Keep It and returnless refunds

For low-value or bulky items that aren't worth shipping back, you can set up Keep It rules so the customer is refunded and keeps the item. This saves return shipping but you still lose the sale, so use it for items where the label costs more than the item is worth to you.

What you must not do

  • Negotiate with customers, or discourage or delay an eligible return.
  • Offer alternative solutions (partial refunds, discounts to keep the item) unless Walmart explicitly authorizes them.
  • Charge return shipping or unapproved restocking fees.

If you don't comply, Walmart may refund the customer on your behalf and charge you, and can suspend or terminate sellers. Returns also feed into your performance: Walmart's seller performance standards include a return rate target, covered in our Walmart seller performance standards guide.

Returns checklist for Walmart sellers

  1. Set a valid US return address and at least one active return center in Seller Center.
  2. Decide between RSS and your own FedEx or UPS account for return labels.
  3. Apply for exemptions for freight, multi-box or luxury items, and be ready to send labels within 48 hours.
  4. Set up Keep It rules for low-value items where return shipping isn't worth it.
  5. Inspect returns quickly and restock sellable units.
  6. Budget for holiday returns arriving through January 31.
  7. Track your return rate weekly and fix listing, sizing or quality problems behind it.
Most high return rates start with the listing. Clear size charts, accurate photos and honest specs prevent more returns than any policy setting.

Walmart 平台退货政策详解：2026 卖家指南

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-24 · 约 6 分钟阅读

很多新入驻 Walmart Marketplace 的卖家，都是在退货上吃了亏才发现：规则由 Walmart 定，不由你定——退货期多长、运费谁出、能不能收补货费，甚至 Walmart 门店会不会替你收货。假日季订单正在涌入，下面根据 2026 年 8 月 31 日更新的官方政策，讲清楚 Walmart 自发货订单的退货规则。

核心规则一览

根据 Walmart Marketplace Learn 退货政策（2026 年 8 月 31 日更新），卖家必须接受符合条件的退货，无论退货原因或商品状况，已获批豁免的商品（如 Final Sale 不退不换商品）除外。

规则Walmart 要求
最短退货期30 天，从发货日后第 7 天起算
假日季退货10 月 1 日至 12 月 31 日购买的大部分商品可退至 1 月 31 日（例外包括手机、保障计划、大家电、食品饮料、部分季节性和奢侈品）
向买家收取退货运费不允许
补货费（Restocking fee）不允许；仅豁免商品退回时非原包装或不可再售，可收最多商品价格的 20%
豁免商品的退货面单卖家须在 48 小时内提供
退款时间（Walmart.com / 客服）退货包裹首次被承运商扫描时
退款时间（门店）门店收到商品时

来源：Walmart Marketplace Learn 退货政策。规则可能调整，设置店铺前请以官方页面为准。

假日退货期现在就要考虑

10 月 1 日至 12 月 31 日购买的大部分商品可退到 1 月 31 日，意味着 10 月 2 日发出的订单，可能将近四个月后才被退回。1 月份要预留更长的退货尾巴，Q4 的收入到 2 月再算定。

退货面单：RSS 还是自有承运商账户

Walmart 默认通过退货运输服务（RSS）生成退货面单，享受折扣运费。根据 Walmart RSS 政策（2025 年 12 月 11 日更新），你的公司是发货人（shipper of record），承运商由 Walmart 选择（政策中提到 FedEx 和 USPS），且 Walmart 不对 RSS 包裹的丢失或损坏负责——包裹丢了需要你自己向承运商索赔，可参考我们的USPS 理赔指南。你也可以用自己的 FedEx 或 UPS 账户生成退货面单。

无论哪种方式，都需要一个有效的美国退货地址（不能是邮政信箱，也不能在夏威夷、阿拉斯加或美国海外领地），并至少有一个启用中的退货中心。地址无效的话，包裹可能被改派，由此产生的费用由卖家承担。

门店退货

Walmart 门店会代你接收符合条件的 Marketplace 商品退货，买家很喜欢，普通商品你也无法选择退出。需要退货豁免的商品不支持门店退货，包括异形、走卡车货运、超大、超重和奢侈品、大家电以及完全受管制的危险品。如果这类商品仍被退到门店，政策规定相关费用由卖家承担。

Keep It 与无需退货的退款

对于不值得寄回的低价或大件商品，可以设置 Keep It 规则：直接退款、买家保留商品。这能省下退货运费，但这单销售同样没了，所以适合退货运费高于商品残值的品。

卖家不能做的事

  • 和买家讨价还价，或劝阻、拖延符合条件的退货。
  • 未经 Walmart 明确授权，提出部分退款、折扣留货等替代方案。
  • 收取退货运费或未经批准的补货费。

违规的话，Walmart 可能直接代你给买家退款并向你扣款，严重的可暂停或终止店铺。退货还会影响绩效：Walmart 卖家绩效标准里有退货率指标，详见我们的Walmart 卖家绩效标准指南。

Walmart 卖家退货设置清单

  1. 在 Seller Center 设置有效的美国退货地址，并至少启用一个退货中心。
  2. 决定用 RSS 还是自有 FedEx / UPS 账户生成退货面单。
  3. 卡车货运、多箱、奢侈品等申请退货豁免，并确保 48 小时内能发出面单。
  4. 为低价商品设置 Keep It 规则，省掉不划算的退货运费。
  5. 退货到仓尽快质检，可售的及时重新上架。
  6. 为持续到 1 月 31 日的假日退货预留预算。
  7. 每周跟踪退货率，从 Listing、尺码、质量上找原因。
高退货率大多源于 Listing。清晰的尺码表、真实的图片和准确的参数，比任何政策设置都更能减少退货。
