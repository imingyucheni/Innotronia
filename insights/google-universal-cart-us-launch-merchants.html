Google Launches Universal Cart in the US: What Merchants Need to Know

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-20 · 5 min read

Google now has a shopping cart. On October 15, 2026, Google launched Universal Cart in the US, letting shoppers add products from different retailers into one cart inside Google Search and the Gemini app, get price-drop alerts and check out with Google Pay. It arrives just ahead of the holiday season, and it matters for brands that sell on their own websites. Here is what launched, how merchants get in, and what is still unknown.

What Google launched

  • One cart across stores. According to Search Engine Roundtable (Oct 15, 2026), searchers can add products from multiple merchants to one cart and check out once, with Google Pay. Google describes it as an "intelligent shopping cart and your new hub for shopping on Google."
  • Where it lives. Google Search (including AI Mode) and the Gemini app, US only. PPC Land (Oct 15, 2026) notes that YouTube and Gmail, mentioned at the May preview, are not part of this launch.
  • Alerts and promo codes. The cart alerts users to price drops and restocks and applies available promo codes at checkout.
  • Named retailers. Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart, Wayfair and "many Shopify merchants," per both outlets. Amazon is not on the list.

How merchants get in

Universal Cart runs on the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), the open standard Google uses for agent-driven shopping. According to Stellagent (Oct 16, 2026) and PPC Land:

  • Build the UCP capabilities. Merchants publish a UCP profile at /.well-known/ucp (version 2026-04-08 or later) and implement the cart capability; discount codes are passed only if the merchant declares the discount capability.
  • Choose in Merchant Center. A Merchant Center integration hub launched in October offers cart transfer, native checkout on Google, or identity linking.
  • Wait for selection. Google selects merchants; selection criteria, counts and timelines are not published.
  • You stay the seller. The merchant remains seller of record and handles order confirmation and customer support.

What we still don't know

PPC Land lists several open questions: whether merchants pay Google fees on in-cart Google Pay transactions, how offers are ranked when several stores sell the same product, whether ads or paid placements will appear in the cart, what cart data merchants receive, and how referrals are attributed in analytics. Native checkout fees have not been published.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Shopping is spreading beyond marketplaces. Within weeks, TikTok announced in-feed checkout (see our TikTok Buy Direct article) and Google launched a cross-store cart. For brands with their own websites, discovery and checkout increasingly happen on platforms you don't control.

Shopify brands may already be in. If you sell on Shopify, check Merchant Center and your Google channel settings to see whether your store was switched on, and review what is enabled.

Product data quality decides visibility. Price alerts, restock alerts and promo codes all depend on accurate, up-to-date feeds. Wrong prices or stock levels in Merchant Center will now show up directly in shoppers' carts.

Marketplace sellers: watch, don't rush. If you sell mainly on Amazon, Universal Cart doesn't change much today; Amazon isn't a listed partner. If you also run a website, it's worth testing.

Checklist

  1. Check Google Merchant Center for the new integration hub and your store's status.
  2. Audit your product feed: prices, availability, shipping and promo codes.
  3. Ask your platform or developer about UCP support and the cart capability.
  4. Set up analytics to track Google-referred carts and orders separately.
  5. Review your returns and customer-service flow for orders started on Google.

谷歌在美国上线 Universal Cart 通用购物车：商家需要知道什么

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-20 · 约 5 分钟阅读

谷歌也有购物车了。2026 年 10 月 15 日，谷歌在美国上线 Universal Cart（通用购物车）：用户可以在谷歌搜索和 Gemini App 中把不同零售商的商品放进同一个购物车，接收降价提醒，并用 Google Pay 结账。它赶在假日季前上线，对拥有独立站的品牌尤其重要。下面梳理上线内容、商家如何接入，以及仍未明确的问题。

谷歌上线了什么

  • 跨店铺一个购物车。据 Search Engine Roundtable（2026 年 10 月 15 日）报道，用户可以把多个商家的商品加入同一购物车并一次结账，使用 Google Pay。谷歌称其为“智能购物车，也是你在谷歌购物的新中心”。
  • 入口。谷歌搜索（含 AI 模式）和 Gemini App，仅限美国。PPC Land（2026 年 10 月 15 日）指出，5 月预览时提到的 YouTube 和 Gmail 不在此次上线范围内。
  • 提醒与优惠码。购物车会提醒降价和补货，并在结账时自动使用可用优惠码。
  • 公布的零售商。两家媒体均报道：Nike、Sephora、Target、Ulta Beauty、沃尔玛、Wayfair 以及“众多 Shopify 商家”。亚马逊不在名单中。

商家如何接入

Universal Cart 基于通用商务协议（UCP），这是谷歌用于 AI 代理购物的开放标准。据 Stellagent（2026 年 10 月 16 日）和 PPC Land：

  • 实现 UCP 能力。商家需在 /.well-known/ucp 发布 UCP 配置（2026-04-08 或更新版本）并实现购物车能力；只有声明了折扣能力，谷歌才会传递优惠码。
  • 在 Merchant Center 选择。10 月上线的 Merchant Center 集成中心提供三种方式：购物车转移、在谷歌原生结账、账号关联。
  • 等待入选。由谷歌挑选商家，标准、名额和时间表均未公布。
  • 你仍是卖方。商家仍是交易主体，负责订单确认和客户服务。

仍未明确的问题

PPC Land 列出了几个待解问题：商家是否需为购物车内的 Google Pay 交易向谷歌付费；多家店铺卖同一商品时如何排序；购物车里是否会出现广告或付费位；商家能拿到哪些购物车数据；以及分析工具中如何归因。原生结账的费用尚未公布。

我们的解读

购物正在溢出平台之外。短短几周内，TikTok 推出了信息流内结账（见我们的《TikTok Buy Direct》一文），谷歌又上线了跨店铺购物车。对独立站品牌来说，发现和结账越来越多地发生在你无法掌控的平台上。

Shopify 品牌可能已被接入。如果你用 Shopify，请检查 Merchant Center 和谷歌销售渠道设置，确认店铺是否已被开启以及开通了哪些功能。

商品数据质量决定曝光。降价提醒、补货提醒和优惠码都依赖准确、及时的商品数据。Merchant Center 中价格或库存出错，会直接体现在消费者的购物车里。

平台卖家：先观察，不必急。如果你主要在亚马逊销售，Universal Cart 目前影响不大，亚马逊并非公布的合作方。如果你也有独立站，值得测试。

行动清单

  1. 在谷歌 Merchant Center 查看新的集成中心和店铺状态。
  2. 检查商品数据：价格、库存、配送和优惠码。
  3. 询问建站平台或开发者是否支持 UCP 及购物车能力。
  4. 在分析工具中单独追踪来自谷歌的购物车和订单。
  5. 检查从谷歌发起的订单的退货和客服流程。
