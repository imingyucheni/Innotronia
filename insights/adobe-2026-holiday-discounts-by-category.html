When Holiday Discounts Peak by Category: What Adobe's 2026 Data Shows

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-03 · 6 min read

Adobe's headline holiday forecast, $275.1 billion in US online sales, got most of the attention last week; we covered it in our comparison of 2026 holiday forecasts. The full Adobe Digital Insights forecast deck goes further, with category-level data on when discounts peak, which products surge, and where traffic comes from. Here is the part sellers can actually plan with.

October is now the first act

Adobe expects US shoppers to spend $9.9 billion online during Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days (October 6–7, 2026), up 9.2% year over year, and $95.8 billion online across October, up 8%. It says October's spending lead over September has widened every year since 2022, from 9.4% to a projected 16.4% this year.

When each category's discounts peak

According to Adobe, discounts build from about 14% off in early November to 21% just before Thanksgiving and a season-high 30% during Cyber Week. Black Friday (November 27) is forecast to be the best day for five categories, while electronics reach the deepest single-category discount on Cyber Monday (November 30):

December discounts are expected to stay in the 10%–15% range.

What shoppers will stock up on

Adobe expects holiday sales to drive spending on everyday goods, not just gifts. Compared with normal levels, it forecasts these boosts:

CategoryOctober Prime eventCyber Week
Clothing basics+29%+210%
Personal hygiene+51%+150%
Household cleaning+54%+49%

Source: Adobe Digital Insights, Sept 2026. During the Cyber 5 period, Adobe forecasts sales of top subcategories rising more than 600% versus the January–August daily average, including vacuum cleaners (+930%), cookware (+860%), small kitchen appliances (+840%), headphones and speakers (+805%) and kids' toys (+760%).

Where the traffic comes from

  • Social and affiliates grow, organic search shrinks. Adobe forecasts revenue share from social networks up 17% and affiliates/partners up 12% this season, paid search up 4%, and natural search down 13%.
  • Creators matter inside affiliate. Influencer, creator and shoppable media drove 22% of affiliate visits from January to August 2026, with monthly share up 28% since January.
  • AI shoppers convert. Adobe says AI-referred retail visitors converted 48% better than other traffic in August 2026, and forecasts AI-driven traffic up 130% over the season.

Adobe's press release and coverage by Forbes and Chain Store Age on September 28 confirm the headline figures; the category detail comes from the deck.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Match your deal calendar to your category. If you sell toys, apparel or appliances, Black Friday is the day shoppers expect the best price. Electronics sellers should plan their deepest offer for Cyber Monday.
  • Essentials sellers have two peaks. Cleaning and hygiene products get a strong lift in October, not just in Cyber Week. Make sure stock is in place for both.
  • Small appliances and cookware are crowded but huge. Expect heavy competition and higher ad costs in Cyber 5; lock in budgets and inventory early.
  • Diversify traffic. With organic search share falling, creator programs and affiliate partnerships are worth testing before peak, not during it.

Checklist for the next 8 weeks

  1. Map each SKU's category to Adobe's peak-discount day and plan your deepest offer for that date.
  2. Set a discount ladder: modest early-November deals, bigger before Thanksgiving, best price in Cyber Week.
  3. Check inventory for October essentials demand and Cyber 5 surges.
  4. Line up creators and affiliate partners in October.
  5. Make product data complete so AI shopping assistants can surface your listings.
Tip: Forecast discount levels are market averages. Price from your own margin first, then decide how close to the category peak you can afford to go.

2026 节日季各类目折扣何时最深？Adobe 数据告诉你

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-03 · 约 6 分钟阅读

上周大家关注的是 Adobe 的总预测：美国节日季线上销售额 2,751 亿美元，我们已在2026 节日季预测对比中做过解读。完整的 Adobe Digital Insights 预测报告还有更细的类目数据：各类目折扣什么时候最深、哪些产品会暴涨、流量从哪里来。下面挑出卖家真正能用来做计划的部分。

10 月已成为节日季的“第一幕”

Adobe 预计，亚马逊秋季 Prime 大促（2026 年 10 月 6–7 日）期间美国线上消费将达 99 亿美元，同比增长 9.2%；整个 10 月线上消费 958 亿美元，增长 8%。报告指出，自 2022 年以来，10 月相对 9 月的消费增幅逐年扩大，从 9.4% 升至今年预计的 16.4%。

各类目折扣什么时候最深

据 Adobe 预测，折扣会从 11 月初的约 14% 逐步加深，到感恩节前达到 21%，网络周期间达到全季最高的 30%。黑五（11 月 27 日）预计是五个类目的最佳购买日，电子产品则在网一（11 月 30 日）达到单一类目最大折扣：

12 月折扣预计维持在 10%–15% 之间。

消费者会囤什么

Adobe 预计，节日大促带动的不只是礼品，还有日常用品。与平时相比，预测增幅如下：

类目10 月 Prime 大促网络周
基础服装+29%+210%
个人护理+51%+150%
家居清洁+54%+49%

来源：Adobe Digital Insights，2026 年 9 月。在“黑五网一五天”（Cyber 5）期间，Adobe 预测热门子类目销售额较 1–8 月日均水平增长超过 600%，包括吸尘器（+930%）、锅具（+860%）、厨房小家电（+840%）、耳机音箱（+805%）和儿童玩具（+760%）。

流量从哪里来

  • 社交和联盟增长，自然搜索萎缩：Adobe 预测本季社交网络带来的收入占比增长 17%，联盟/合作伙伴增长 12%，付费搜索增长 4%，自然搜索下降 13%。
  • 达人在联盟流量中越来越重要：2026 年 1–8 月，网红/达人/可购物内容占联盟访问量的 22%，月度占比自 1 月以来增长 28%。
  • AI 导购转化更高：Adobe 称 2026 年 8 月 AI 推荐的零售访客转化率比其他流量高 48%，并预测本季 AI 流量增长 130%。

Adobe 新闻稿以及 福布斯、Chain Store Age 9 月 28 日的报道印证了核心数字，类目细节来自完整报告。

我们的解读

  • 按类目安排促销日历：卖玩具、服装或家电，买家期待黑五拿到最低价；卖电子产品，最大力度的优惠应放在网一。
  • 日用品卖家有两个高峰：清洁和个护产品在 10 月就有明显增长，不只是网络周，两个节点都要备好货。
  • 小家电和锅具竞争激烈但体量巨大：黑五网一期间竞争和广告成本都会很高，预算和库存要提前锁定。
  • 流量要多元化：自然搜索份额下滑，达人合作和联盟营销值得在旺季前测试，而不是旺季中才开始。

未来 8 周行动清单

  1. 把每个 SKU 对应到 Adobe 预测的类目折扣高峰日，把最大优惠放在那一天。
  2. 设计折扣阶梯：11 月初小幅优惠，感恩节前加大，网络周给出最低价。
  3. 检查库存，覆盖 10 月日用品需求和黑五网一的暴涨。
  4. 10 月就把达人和联盟合作伙伴谈好。
  5. 补全商品数据，让 AI 购物助手能推荐你的 Listing。
小提示：预测折扣是市场平均水平。先按自己的利润定价，再决定能在多大程度上接近类目最高折扣。
