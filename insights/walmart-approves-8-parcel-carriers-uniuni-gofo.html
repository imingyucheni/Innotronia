Walmart Approves 8 More Parcel Carriers, Including UniUni and GOFO

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-22 · 5 min read

Walmart Marketplace sellers who ship their own orders now have more carrier options. In a Marketplace release note dated October 13, 2026, Walmart added eight parcel carriers to its approved list for seller-fulfilled shipping, including regional last-mile networks such as UniUni, GOFO and SwiftX, plus DoorDash. For sellers looking to cut shipping costs before peak season, this opens new options, as long as tracking and delivery performance hold up.

What Walmart announced

According to Supply Chain Dive (Oct 19, 2026) and EcomCrew (Oct 20, 2026), citing Walmart's October 13 release note, the eight new approved parcel carriers are:

CarrierAdded for
UniUniSeller-fulfilled parcel shipping
SpeedXSeller-fulfilled parcel shipping
ExelotSeller-fulfilled parcel shipping
SwiftXSeller-fulfilled parcel shipping
TForce Final MileSeller-fulfilled parcel shipping
GOFOSeller-fulfilled parcel shipping
OSM WorldwideSeller-fulfilled parcel shipping
DoorDashSeller-fulfilled parcel shipping
Metropolitan Warehouse & DeliveryFreight shipments only

USPS, FedEx and UPS remain Walmart's top preferred carriers. All eight now appear on Walmart's approved parcel carrier list, which includes more than 50 carriers in total.

The rules that come with it

  • Tracking must validate. Walmart's carrier guide says valid tracking must be provided for more than 99% of Marketplace orders under its Seller Performance Standards.
  • Non-approved carriers are risky. Supply Chain Dive quotes Walmart that using a non-approved carrier is not recommended because tracking cannot be validated, and may result in suppression, suspension or termination.
  • Approval isn't protection. EcomCrew notes that a carrier being approved does not mean labels bought through your own carrier account qualify for Walmart's label protection programs; check each program's terms.
  • WFS is unaffected. The change applies to seller-fulfilled orders, not Walmart Fulfillment Services.

Why Walmart is doing this

Walmart's marketplace is growing fast. Supply Chain Dive reports that marketplace net sales rose more than 50% in the second quarter, that e-commerce is now over 23% of Walmart's US business mix (double its level five years ago, per CFO John David Rainey), and that nearly 50% of marketplace business flowed through WFS in Q2. More approved carriers give the other half, seller-fulfilled orders, more ways to ship affordably.

Which orders suit a regional carrier?

Regional and last-mile carriers usually make the most sense for small, light parcels going to large metro areas they serve directly, where national carriers charge residential and delivery-area surcharges. Heavier boxes, rural addresses and shipments that need guaranteed delivery dates are usually better kept with USPS, UPS or FedEx. Map your order history by ZIP code and weight before deciding.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Regional carriers can lower costs. For lightweight parcels into dense metro areas, regional last-mile carriers often price below national carriers. Now they can be used on Walmart orders without breaking the approved-carrier rule.

Test before you switch everything. EcomCrew's worked example makes the point: saving $1 per label on 2,000 shipments is $2,000, but if extra losses and pickup costs reach $1,700, the real saving is only $300. Compare total cost and delivery outcomes, not just label prices.

Protect your metrics. Walmart's on-time delivery and valid tracking thresholds still apply. Check that your shipping software maps each new carrier correctly so tracking numbers validate. Our Walmart Seller Performance Standards guide lists every threshold.

Coverage varies. Regional carriers serve some ZIP codes, not all. Use rules that route only covered ZIPs and fall back to USPS, UPS or FedEx elsewhere.

Checklist

  1. Confirm your shipping software lists the new carriers for Walmart orders.
  2. Pick one regional carrier and test it on a limited set of ZIP codes.
  3. Track on-time delivery, valid tracking and lost-package rates against your current carrier.
  4. Check whether your labels qualify for any Walmart protection program.
  5. Expand only when total cost per delivered order is lower.

沃尔玛新增 8 家认可承运商，含 UniUni、GOFO 和 SwiftX

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-22 · 约 5 分钟阅读

沃尔玛 Marketplace 自发货卖家的承运商选择变多了。在 2026 年 10 月 13 日的平台更新说明中，沃尔玛为自发货订单新增了 8 家认可的包裹承运商，包括 UniUni、GOFO、SwiftX 等区域尾程网络以及 DoorDash。对希望在旺季前降低运费的卖家来说，这打开了新的选择，前提是追踪和配送表现跟得上。

沃尔玛公布了什么

据 Supply Chain Dive（2026 年 10 月 19 日）和 EcomCrew（2026 年 10 月 20 日）援引沃尔玛 10 月 13 日的更新说明，新增的 8 家认可包裹承运商为：

承运商新增用途
UniUni自发货包裹配送
SpeedX自发货包裹配送
Exelot自发货包裹配送
SwiftX自发货包裹配送
TForce Final Mile自发货包裹配送
GOFO自发货包裹配送
OSM Worldwide自发货包裹配送
DoorDash自发货包裹配送
Metropolitan Warehouse & Delivery仅限大件货运

USPS、FedEx 和 UPS 仍是沃尔玛的首选承运商。这 8 家现已出现在沃尔玛的认可包裹承运商名单中，名单共有 50 多家承运商。

随之而来的规则

  • 追踪必须可验证。沃尔玛承运商指南规定，根据卖家绩效标准，超过 99% 的订单须提供有效追踪信息。
  • 使用非认可承运商有风险。Supply Chain Dive 引用沃尔玛的说法：不建议使用非认可承运商，因为追踪信息无法验证，可能导致商品被屏蔽、账号暂停或终止。
  • 认可不等于保障。EcomCrew 指出，承运商获认可并不意味着用你自己的承运商账号买的面单能享受沃尔玛的面单保障计划，需逐一核对条款。
  • WFS 不受影响。此次调整只涉及自发货订单，不涉及沃尔玛官方仓（WFS）。

沃尔玛为什么这么做

沃尔玛平台增长很快。Supply Chain Dive 报道，第二季度平台净销售额增长超过 50%；据 CFO John David Rainey，电商已占沃尔玛美国业务的 23% 以上，是五年前的两倍；第二季度近 50% 的平台业务通过 WFS 完成。更多认可承运商，能让另一半自发货订单有更多低成本的发货方式。

哪些订单适合区域承运商？

区域和尾程承运商通常最适合寄往其直接覆盖的大城市的小件轻货，因为全国性承运商在这些地区会收取住宅和偏远地区附加费。较重的箱子、乡村地址以及需要保证送达时间的货件，通常还是走 USPS、UPS 或 FedEx 更稳妥。决定前先按邮编和重量梳理历史订单。

我们的解读

区域承运商能降成本。寄往大城市的轻小件，区域尾程承运商的价格往往低于全国性承运商。现在它们可以用于沃尔玛订单，而不违反认可承运商规定。

先测试，再全面切换。EcomCrew 的计算示例很能说明问题：2,000 单每单省 1 美元就是 2,000 美元，但如果额外丢件和揽收成本达到 1,700 美元，实际只省了 300 美元。要比较总成本和配送结果，而不只是面单价格。

守住绩效指标。沃尔玛的准时送达和有效追踪门槛仍然适用。确认发货软件为每家新承运商正确映射，追踪号才能通过验证。各项门槛见我们的《沃尔玛卖家绩效标准指南》。

覆盖范围各不相同。区域承运商只覆盖部分邮编。设置规则：覆盖区域走区域承运商，其他区域回落到 USPS、UPS 或 FedEx。

行动清单

  1. 确认发货软件已为沃尔玛订单列出新承运商。
  2. 选一家区域承运商，在部分邮编小范围测试。
  3. 与现有承运商对比准时送达率、有效追踪率和丢件率。
  4. 核对你的面单是否适用沃尔玛的保障计划。
  5. 只有当每单送达总成本更低时再扩大使用。
