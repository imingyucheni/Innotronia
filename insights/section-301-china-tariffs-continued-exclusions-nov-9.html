China Section 301 Tariffs Continue; 178 Exclusions Expire Nov 9

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-18 · 5 min read

The China Section 301 tariffs are staying. On October 7, 2026, the Office of the US Trade Representative (USTR) published a notice confirming that the tariffs first imposed in 2018 did not terminate at their four-year mark and remain in effect. At the same time, the 178 product exclusions that still shield some goods from these duties are set to expire on November 9, 2026, unless USTR extends them. Here is what was confirmed, what is still open, and what importers should check now.

What USTR confirmed

According to the Federal Register notice (91 FR 64212, Oct 7, 2026), USTR began the second statutory four-year review of the two China Section 301 actions on May 6, 2026, and notified domestic industries of the possible termination. Because it "received requests for continuation of both actions," the actions "have not terminated and will remain in effect, subject to possible further modifications, including any modifications resulting from the statutory four-year review."

  • Requests received. Gateway Lines (Oct 6, 2026) reports 68 producer and 18 association requests to continue the July 6, 2018 action, and 57 producer and 19 association requests for the August 23, 2018 action.
  • No rate changes. The tariffs range from 7.5% to 25% depending on the list and cover about $370 billion of imports from China across Lists 1 to 4A, according to Sandler, Travis & Rosenberg (Oct 8, 2026).
  • A review is coming. ST&R says a separate notice will describe the review process, with a comment period on the tariffs' effectiveness and economic effects, including on consumers.

The November 9 exclusion deadline

Gateway Lines reports that 178 product exclusions remain and expire at 11:59 p.m. Eastern on November 9, 2026, unless extended; as of October 6, no extension had been published. These are what is left of a much longer list:

"Unless USTR extends them again, excluded goods entered on or after Nov. 10 pay the Section 301 duty of their list," Gateway Lines writes. Its worked example: on a $100,000 shipment of a List 3 product, a lapsed exclusion adds 25%, or $25,000; on a List 4A product, 7.5%, or $7,500.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Plan on Section 301 staying through 2027. The review could modify the tariffs, but nothing in the notice suggests a quick removal. Price and margin plans for China-made goods should assume these duties continue.

Check whether any of your products use an exclusion. Only 178 product types are excluded, so many sellers will not be affected, but if your broker has been claiming one, the duty on your next shipments could jump after November 9. Ask your broker now.

Time shipments carefully. If you rely on an exclusion, goods entered on or before November 9 still qualify. Ocean transit takes weeks, so check arrival and entry dates, not ship dates.

Watch for the review notice. The comment period is a chance for importers and industry groups to explain how the tariffs affect them. Keep an eye on USTR and Federal Register announcements.

Remember that Section 301 is only one layer of US duties on Chinese goods. See our guides on finding your HTS code and duty rate and the US–China truce and 30-for-30 list. This article is general information, not legal advice.

Checklist for importers

  1. Confirm the Section 301 list and rate for each of your HTS codes.
  2. Ask your broker whether any entries use a Section 301 exclusion.
  3. If yes, map shipments that will enter after November 9 and recalculate landed cost.
  4. Update 2027 pricing assumptions to include Section 301 duties.
  5. Follow USTR for the four-year review notice and comment deadline.

对华 301 关税继续有效，178 项排除 11 月 9 日到期

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-18 · 约 5 分钟阅读

对华 301 关税不会取消。2026 年 10 月 7 日，美国贸易代表办公室（USTR）发布公告，确认 2018 年起征收的 301 关税没有在四年期满时终止，继续有效。与此同时，目前仍让部分商品免缴 301 关税的 178 项产品排除，若 USTR 不延期，将于 2026 年 11 月 9 日到期。下面梳理已确认的事项、尚待明确的问题，以及进口商现在该检查什么。

USTR 确认了什么

根据《联邦公报》公告（91 FR 64212，2026 年 10 月 7 日），USTR 于 2026 年 5 月 6 日启动了对两项对华 301 措施的第二次法定四年期审查，并通知国内产业措施可能终止。由于“收到了继续实施两项措施的请求”，措施“未终止，将继续有效，并可能进一步调整，包括法定四年期审查带来的调整”。

  • 收到的请求。据 Gateway Lines（2026 年 10 月 6 日）报道，针对 2018 年 7 月 6 日措施有 68 家生产商和 18 个协会请求继续，针对 2018 年 8 月 23 日措施有 57 家生产商和 19 个协会请求继续。
  • 税率不变。据 Sandler, Travis & Rosenberg（2026 年 10 月 8 日），税率按清单不同为 7.5% 至 25%，覆盖清单 1 至 4A 约 3,700 亿美元的自华进口。
  • 审查即将启动。ST&R 称将另行发布审查程序公告，并就关税的有效性及其经济影响（包括对消费者的影响）征求意见。

11 月 9 日排除到期

Gateway Lines 报道，目前剩余 178 项产品排除，若不延期将于 2026 年 11 月 9 日美东时间 23:59 到期；截至 10 月 6 日尚未发布延期公告。这些是此前更长清单的剩余部分：

Gateway Lines 写道：“除非 USTR 再次延期，11 月 10 日及以后入境的排除商品将按所属清单缴纳 301 关税。”其示例：一票 10 万美元的清单 3 商品，排除失效后多缴 25%，即 2.5 万美元；清单 4A 商品多缴 7.5%，即 7,500 美元。

我们的解读

按 301 关税延续到 2027 年做计划。审查可能带来调整，但公告中看不出会很快取消。中国制造商品的定价和毛利规划，应假设这些关税继续存在。

核查你的产品是否在用排除。排除项只有 178 项，很多卖家不受影响；但如果报关行一直在为你申报排除，11 月 9 日之后的货物关税可能大幅上升。现在就问清报关行。

把握入境时间。依赖排除的货物，11 月 9 日及之前入境仍可适用。海运需要数周，请核对到港和报关日期，而不是发货日期。

关注审查公告。意见征集期是进口商和行业协会说明关税影响的机会，请留意 USTR 和《联邦公报》的公告。

注意，301 关税只是美国对华商品关税的其中一层。可参考我们的《如何查找 HTS 编码和关税税率》和《中美休战延期与“30 换 30”清单》。本文为一般性信息，不构成法律意见。

进口商检查清单

  1. 确认每个 HTS 编码对应的 301 清单和税率。
  2. 询问报关行是否有报关单在使用 301 排除。
  3. 如有，梳理 11 月 9 日后入境的货物，重新核算到岸成本。
  4. 在 2027 年定价假设中计入 301 关税。
  5. 关注 USTR 的四年期审查公告和意见截止日期。
