How to Get UPC Barcodes for Amazon and Walmart: GS1 US Costs Explained

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-01 · 6 min read

Almost every new listing on Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop or Google Shopping asks for a product identifier, usually a UPC. Many new sellers buy the cheapest barcodes they can find online and only later discover problems with listings or brand matching. The official source in the US is GS1 US. This guide explains the options, what they cost and how to choose.

UPC, EAN, GTIN: what's the difference?

  • GTIN (Global Trade Item Number) is the umbrella term for product identification numbers managed by GS1.
  • UPC is the 12-digit GTIN format common in the US; EAN is the 13-digit format common elsewhere.
  • The barcode is just the printed image of the number.

Every distinct product variation (each size, color or pack count) normally needs its own GTIN, so count variations, not just product families, when you plan.

Your two GS1 US options

According to GS1 US's barcode pricing page, you can either license individual GTINs or license a GS1 Company Prefix that lets you create a block of numbers.

  • Single GS1 US GTIN: $30, with no annual renewal fee. GS1 US says it includes a free lifetime subscription to its Data Hub. It is not available for pharmaceutical products, variable-measure products, mixed cases or coupons.
  • GS1 Company Prefix: an initial fee plus an annual renewal, sized by how many items need barcodes.

Which option is right for you?

  • 1–9 products, no plans to grow fast: single GTINs at $30 each with no annual fee are simplest.
  • A growing brand with variations: a 100-item prefix ($750 plus $150 a year) usually makes more sense once you pass about 25 items, since 25 single GTINs would cost $750 too. Calculation: 25 × $30 = $750.
  • Factories with large catalogs: 1,000 or 10,000 capacity prefixes bring the cost per item down sharply.

Remember the renewal: GS1 US says that as long as you renew your membership each year, your product data stays linked to your company information. Budget the annual fee as a fixed cost of selling in the US.

Buy barcodes in the name of the company that owns the brand. Codes licensed to someone else, or resold from old prefixes, may not match your brand when retailers or marketplaces check GS1 data.

How to get your barcodes

  1. Count products and variations you plan to sell over the next two to three years.
  2. Choose single GTINs or a Company Prefix size on the GS1 US site.
  3. Provide your contact and company information and pay.
  4. Assign a GTIN to each product variation and record product details in GS1's data tools.
  5. Generate barcode images for your packaging and labels.
  6. Use the same GTINs consistently across Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop and Google.

Marketplace notes

Amazon, Walmart and Google Merchant Center all ask for product identifiers when you create listings, and each has its own rules and exemption processes for some product types (for example handmade, private-label or bundle items). Rules change, so check Seller Central, Walmart Seller Center and Merchant Center before you list. See our guides on Walmart Marketplace approval requirements and Google free listings.

Common mistakes

  • Running out of numbers. A 10-item prefix fills up fast once sizes and colors are counted; upgrading later means managing two sets of numbers.
  • Reusing GTINs. Don't reassign a GTIN from a discontinued product to a new one; buyers and retailers may still have the old item on file.
  • Letting the prefix lapse. Missing the annual renewal can break the link between your products and your company data.

Barcode checklist

  1. Count variations, not just products.
  2. License GTINs from GS1 US in your brand-owning company's name.
  3. Keep a master list of GTIN, SKU, product name and variation.
  4. Put the barcode on retail packaging and FBA/WFS labels where required.
  5. Renew your prefix every year if you use one.

如何为 Amazon、Walmart 获取 UPC 条码：GS1 US 费用详解

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-01 · 约 6 分钟阅读

在 Amazon、Walmart、TikTok Shop 或 Google Shopping 上新几乎都要填商品编码，通常是 UPC。很多新卖家在网上买最便宜的条码，后来才发现 Listing 或品牌匹配出问题。美国的官方来源是 GS1 US。本文讲清楚有哪些选择、各要多少钱、该怎么选。

UPC、EAN、GTIN 有什么区别

  • GTIN（全球贸易项目代码）是 GS1 管理的商品识别码的统称。
  • UPC 是美国常用的 12 位 GTIN；EAN 是其他地区常用的 13 位格式。
  • 条码只是这个号码的印刷图形。

每个不同的商品变体（每个尺码、颜色或包装数量）通常都需要单独的 GTIN，所以规划时要按变体数量算，而不只是按款式算。

GS1 US 的两种选择

据 GS1 US 条码价格页面，你可以单个购买 GTIN，或者购买厂商识别代码（GS1 Company Prefix），自行生成一批号码。

  • 单个 GS1 US GTIN：30 美元，无年费。GS1 US 称附赠其 Data Hub 终身订阅。不适用于药品、变量计量商品、混装箱或优惠券。
  • 厂商识别代码：首次费用加年续费，按需要条码的商品数量分档。

怎么选

  • 1–9 个商品、短期不会快速扩品：单个 GTIN 每个 30 美元、无年费，最简单。
  • 有变体、在成长的品牌：商品超过约 25 个时，100 容量的代码（750 美元加每年 150 美元）通常更划算，因为 25 个单个 GTIN 也要 750 美元。计算：25 × 30 = 750 美元。
  • 产品线大的工厂：1,000 或 10,000 容量的代码能大幅降低单个成本。

别忘了续费：GS1 US 表示，只要每年续费，你的商品数据就会一直与公司信息绑定。把年费当作在美国销售的固定成本。

用拥有品牌的公司名义购买条码。授权给别人的号码或从旧代码转卖的号码，在零售商或平台核对 GS1 数据时可能与你的品牌对不上。

获取条码的步骤

  1. 统计未来两三年计划销售的商品和变体数量。
  2. 在 GS1 US 网站选择单个 GTIN 或相应容量的厂商识别代码。
  3. 填写联系人和公司信息并付款。
  4. 为每个商品变体分配 GTIN，并在 GS1 的数据工具中录入商品信息。
  5. 为包装和标签生成条码图片。
  6. 在 Amazon、Walmart、TikTok Shop 和 Google 上统一使用同一套 GTIN。

平台注意事项

Amazon、Walmart 和 Google Merchant Center 上新时都会要求商品编码，并各自对部分商品类型（如手工、自有品牌或组合装）有规则和豁免流程。规则会变，上新前请分别查看 Seller Central、Walmart Seller Center 和 Merchant Center。可参考我们的Walmart 入驻审核要求和Google 免费商品展示指南。

常见误区

  • 号码不够用。算上尺码和颜色，10 容量的代码很快用完，日后升级就要管理两套号码。
  • 重复使用 GTIN。不要把停产商品的 GTIN 换给新品，买家和零售商可能仍存有旧商品记录。
  • 忘记续费。错过年续费可能导致商品与公司数据的关联中断。

条码清单

  1. 按变体而不是按款式计算数量。
  2. 以拥有品牌的公司名义向 GS1 US 购买 GTIN。
  3. 维护一份 GTIN、SKU、商品名和变体的总表。
  4. 按要求在零售包装和 FBA/WFS 标签上印条码。
  5. 使用厂商识别代码的，每年按时续费。
