US–China Truce Extended to Jan 10, 2027, and the "30-for-30" Tariff List

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-02 · 6 min read

Two pieces of US–China trade news landed in the last ten days. The tariff truce between the two countries has been extended to January 10, 2027, and on September 27 the White House published the first lists of products that could get reduced tariffs under a new "30-for-30" framework. For Chinese factories selling into the US, the list is worth reading line by line: it is full of everyday e-commerce categories.

What was announced

1. The truce runs to January 10, 2027

Bloomberg and Trading Economics reported on September 23–24, 2026 that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the US and China agreed to extend their trade truce to January 10, 2027. According to IndexBox, the truce had been due to expire on November 10, 2026, and includes the US tariff linked to fentanyl at a reduced 10% and China's suspension of retaliatory tariffs. Reports describe this as a two-month extension that buys time for talks rather than a broader settlement.

2. The "30-for-30" lists

On September 27, 2026, the White House released the lists for the new US–China Board of Trade. According to the terms of reference, the two sides agreed to consider lists of imported goods "totaling roughly $30 billion on each side, with a view toward providing reduced tariff treatment to those goods in a reciprocal manner." Values were based on 2024 bilateral trade, and the lists will be reviewed no more often than once a year.

The key point for sellers: no tariff has been cut yet. The document says future reductions "will be determined and implemented in accordance with each side's domestic legal processes."

Which Chinese products are on the US list

The U.S. Import List contains 77 HTSUS tariff lines. Grouped by chapter, they are dominated by consumer goods that sell on Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop:

Several lines are "ex-outs," meaning only part of the tariff line is covered. Check the exact description before assuming your product qualifies.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Stability through the holidays. The extension means the current tariff framework should hold through Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Christmas, which helps Q4 pricing.
  • A possible, not guaranteed, saving. If your products are on the list, a future reduction could improve landed cost in 2027. Don't price for it until a rate change is officially published.
  • January 10 is a date to plan around. If talks stall, conditions could change after the truce ends. Avoid committing to large Q1 shipments without a plan B.
  • Classification matters more than ever. Whether you benefit depends on the exact HTS code of each product. See our guide on finding your HTS code and duty rate.

What to do now

  1. Check your HTS codes against the 77 lines on the list, including the ex-out descriptions.
  2. Model two scenarios for Q1 2027: current duties continue, and duties change after January 10.
  3. Time inbound shipments so stock for January is cleared before you need it, with buffer for delays.
  4. Talk to your customs broker about how a future reduction would be applied and documented.
  5. Watch official sources (White House, USTR, Federal Register, CBP) rather than social media rumors.
Tip: Save a copy of the official list with today's date. If rates change later, you will want to show exactly which of your products were covered.

中美休战延至 2027 年 1 月 10 日，“30 换 30”降税清单有哪些商品

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-02 · 约 6 分钟阅读

过去十天，中美贸易有两条重要消息。两国关税休战期延长至 2027 年 1 月 10 日；9 月 27 日，白宫又公布了新“30 换 30”框架下可能获得关税减免的首批商品清单。对向美国出口的国内工厂来说，这份清单值得逐行细看：里面大量是电商常见类目。

公布了什么

1. 休战期延至 2027 年 1 月 10 日

据 彭博社和 Trading Economics 2026 年 9 月 23–24 日报道，美国财政部长贝森特表示，中美同意将贸易休战延长至 2027 年 1 月 10 日。据 IndexBox 报道，休战原定 2026 年 11 月 10 日到期，内容包括美方与芬太尼相关的关税降至 10%、中方暂停反制关税等。报道普遍认为，这次延长两个月是为谈判争取时间，而不是达成更全面的协议。

2. “30 换 30”清单

2026 年 9 月 27 日，白宫发布了新成立的“美中贸易委员会”相关清单。根据职权范围文件，双方同意考虑“各方总额约 300 亿美元”的进口商品清单，“以期以对等方式给予这些商品关税减免”。金额以 2024 年双边贸易数据为准，清单原则上每年最多调整一次。

对卖家最关键的一点：目前还没有任何关税被下调。文件明确写道，未来的降税将“按照各自国内法律程序确定和实施”。

美方清单上有哪些中国商品

《U.S. Import List》共有 77 个 HTSUS 税号。按章节归类后可以看到，大部分是亚马逊、沃尔玛和 TikTok Shop 上热卖的消费品：

类别清单中的例子
玩具、游戏、运动、节庆（25）9503 项下玩具（不含带射频、Wi-Fi、以太网或蓝牙功能的产品）、扑克牌、台球用品、充气球、网球用品、鱼钩鱼线、玻璃或木制圣诞装饰

婴童用品、枕头、睡袋（9）高脚椅、婴儿摇椅、儿童安全座椅、游戏围栏、睡袋、非棉质枕头和靠垫

部分税号标注了“ex-out”，表示只覆盖该税号下的部分商品。判断自己的产品是否在内之前，务必核对完整描述。

我们的解读

  • 节日季前后关税相对稳定：延期意味着现行关税框架大概率会持续到黑五、网一和圣诞，有利于 Q4 定价。
  • 可能降税，但不保证：如果你的产品在清单上，2027 年到岸成本有望改善，但在官方正式公布新税率之前，不要按降税后的成本定价。
  • 1 月 10 日是要提前规划的节点：如果谈判不顺，休战结束后情况可能变化。一季度大批量发货要准备好备选方案。
  • 归类比以往更重要：能否受益取决于每个产品的准确 HTS 编码。可参考我们的HTS 编码与关税查询指南。

现在可以做什么

  1. 核对 HTS 编码：把你的产品和清单上的 77 个税号逐一比对，包括 ex-out 描述。
  2. 做两种情景测算：2027 年一季度维持现行关税，以及 1 月 10 日后关税发生变化。
  3. 安排好头程时间：1 月要卖的货提前清关入仓，并为延误留出余量。
  4. 和报关行沟通：了解未来如果降税，会如何适用和申报。
  5. 关注官方渠道：以白宫、USTR、联邦公报和 CBP 为准，不要轻信社交媒体传言。
小提示：把今天的官方清单下载存档。将来税率变化时，你需要准确证明哪些产品在清单内。
