Shopify Q3 2026: GMV Up 30% to $119B, B2B Up 74%, AI Orders Up 3.3x

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-06 · 5 min read

Brand websites are not losing ground to marketplaces. On November 3, 2026, Shopify reported that merchants on its platform sold $119 billion of goods in the third quarter, up about 30% from a year earlier, with B2B sales growing even faster and AI-driven orders more than tripling. For brands deciding how much to invest in their own store versus Amazon or Walmart, here is what the numbers show.

The headline numbers

According to Shopify's Q3 2026 press release (Nov 3, 2026):

  • GMV (gross merchandise volume) was $119.1 billion, compared with $92.0 billion a year earlier, an increase of 29.5% (calculation from the release figures).
  • Revenue grew 32% to $3.74 billion; merchant solutions revenue reached $2.91 billion and subscription revenue $836 million.
  • Free cash flow margin was 22%, up from 18%.
  • Q4 outlook: revenue growth "at a low-thirties percentage rate."

President Harley Finkelstein said: "We powered $119 billion in GMV, while continuing to build commerce into every surface, every agent, and increasingly, everywhere."

Where the growth came from

On the earnings call, as transcribed by Investing.com (Nov 3, 2026), Shopify said international GMV grew 32%, B2B GMV grew 74% and offline (point-of-sale) GMV grew 30%. Merchant solutions revenue rose 35% and subscription revenue 20%. sec-api.io's filing analysis (Nov 4, 2026) reports Shopify Payments processed $81.4 billion, or 68% of GMV, up from 65% a year earlier.

AI shopping is showing up in the numbers

Finkelstein said on the call that "AI traffic is up 2.6x year-over-year. Orders are growing even faster at 3.3x." He added that AI channels send shoppers to product pages at 3x the rate of organic search, with 64% higher conversion, and that Shopify is "powering product discovery across all major AI channels, from ChatGPT to Microsoft to Muse." Management said it is not trying to pick winners among AI agents and wants merchants to sell through all of them.

What it doesn't tell us

Shopify's figures cover all merchants on its platform worldwide, not just US brands, and they include very large merchants. A small brand's own store will not automatically grow 30%. Traffic still has to be earned through ads, content, email and search, which is why many brands keep marketplaces as their main volume channel while building their own store.

What it means for brands (our analysis)

Your own store is a growth channel, not just a brand page. With GMV growing about 30%, independent stores are taking share. A Shopify store also gives you customer data and repeat-purchase control that marketplaces don't.

B2B is the sleeper opportunity. 74% B2B growth suggests more brands are selling wholesale online. Factories and brands with distributor or retail customers can move those orders onto a B2B storefront.

AI channels reward clean product data. If AI-driven orders are tripling, structured catalogs matter. The same feed that powers Google free listings feeds many AI shopping surfaces.

Marketplaces and DTC work together. Most brands don't have to choose. Tools such as Amazon's multichannel tools make it easier to run both from one inventory.

Checklist for brands

  1. Compare your own-store growth with marketplace growth over the last four quarters.
  2. Clean up product titles, attributes and GTINs so AI and search channels can read them.
  3. Test a B2B or wholesale price list if you sell to retailers or distributors.
  4. Track AI referral traffic separately in your analytics.
  5. Keep inventory synced across your store and marketplaces before Black Friday.

Shopify 2026 三季报：GMV 增 30% 至 1,190 亿美元，B2B 增 74%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-06 · 约 5 分钟阅读

品牌独立站并没有输给平台。2026 年 11 月 3 日，Shopify 公布第三季度平台商家销售额 1,190 亿美元，同比增长约 30%，其中 B2B 增长更快，AI 带来的订单增长超过三倍。对正在权衡独立站和 Amazon、Walmart 投入比例的品牌来说，这组数据很有参考价值。

核心数据

据 Shopify 2026 年第三季度新闻稿（2026 年 11 月 3 日）：

  • GMV（商品交易总额）1,191 亿美元，去年同期 920 亿美元，增长 29.5%（按新闻稿数据计算）。
  • 营收增长 32% 至 37.4 亿美元，其中商家解决方案营收 29.1 亿美元，订阅营收 8.36 亿美元。
  • 自由现金流利润率 22%，去年同期 18%。
  • Q4 展望：营收增速"在 30% 出头"。

Shopify 总裁 Harley Finkelstein 表示："我们本季度支撑了 1,190 亿美元 GMV，同时继续把商业能力嵌入每一个场景、每一个 AI 代理，并且越来越无处不在。"

增长从哪里来

据 Investing.com 整理的财报电话会文字稿（2026 年 11 月 3 日），Shopify 国际 GMV 增长 32%，B2B GMV 增长 74%，线下（POS）GMV 增长 30%；商家解决方案营收增长 35%，订阅营收增长 20%。据 sec-api.io 的财报分析（2026 年 11 月 4 日），Shopify Payments 处理了 814 亿美元交易，占 GMV 的 68%，去年同期为 65%。

AI 购物开始体现在数据里

Finkelstein 在电话会上表示："AI 带来的流量同比增长 2.6 倍，订单增长更快，达 3.3 倍。"他还说，AI 渠道把购物者导向商品页的比率是自然搜索的 3 倍，转化率高 64%，Shopify 正在"为从 ChatGPT 到 Microsoft 再到 Muse 的所有主要 AI 渠道提供商品发现能力"。管理层表示不会押注某一个 AI 代理，而是希望商家能通过所有代理销售。

数据没告诉我们的

Shopify 的数据覆盖全球所有平台商家，并不只是美国品牌，还包括很多超大商家。小品牌的独立站不会自动增长 30%，流量仍要靠广告、内容、邮件和搜索去争取。这也是为什么很多品牌一边以平台为主要销量渠道，一边逐步建设独立站。

我们的解读

独立站是增长渠道，不只是品牌展示页。GMV 增长约 30%，说明独立站在抢份额。Shopify 店铺还能给你平台给不了的客户数据和复购掌控力。

B2B 是被低估的机会。B2B 增长 74%，说明越来越多品牌在线上做批发。有经销商或零售客户的工厂和品牌，可以把这些订单搬到 B2B 店铺上。

AI 渠道看重干净的商品数据。AI 带来的订单在翻倍增长，结构化的商品目录变得关键。支撑 Google 免费商品展示的那份 Feed，也在为很多 AI 购物场景供数。

平台和独立站可以协同。大多数品牌不必二选一，亚马逊多渠道工具等工具让你可以用一份库存同时运营两边。

品牌行动清单

  1. 对比过去四个季度独立站与平台的增速。
  2. 整理商品标题、属性和 GTIN，让 AI 和搜索渠道都能读懂。
  3. 如果有零售商或经销商客户，试一份 B2B 批发价目表。
  4. 在分析工具中单独追踪 AI 来源流量。
  5. 黑五前确保独立站与各平台库存同步。
