Amazon Seller Liability Insurance: Requirements and How to Comply

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-27 · 6 min read

Many new Amazon sellers only learn about the insurance rule when a warning appears in Seller Central. Amazon requires commercial liability insurance once your sales pass a set level, and the policy has to meet specific terms, not just "any business insurance." Here is what the rule says, how to buy a policy that passes, and how to upload it without delays.

When you need it

According to Simply Business (Oct 17, 2025) and Jungle Scout, Amazon requires sellers to obtain commercial liability insurance within 30 days after exceeding $10,000 in gross proceeds in a single month on Amazon.com. Amazon can also ask for insurance at other times. Both sources note the requirements may change, so always check the current wording in Seller Central's insurance help page.

Tip: $10,000 in one month arrives quickly during Q4 or a successful launch. Get quotes before you hit it, so you are not buying under a 30-day deadline in December.

What the policy must include

The two sources above describe the same core requirements:

RequirementWhat Amazon asks for
TypeCommercial general liability (excess or umbrella liability can also be used), written on an occurrence basis
LimitsAt least $1,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate
What it coversYour business operations, all products you sell, completed operations and bodily injury, including product liability
Additional insuredAmazon.com Services LLC and its affiliates (and assignees)
Insurer ratingA- or better from S&P or A.M. Best
DeductibleLimited (the sources cite no more than $10,000), and any deductible must be shown on the certificate
NoticeThe insurer must give notice before cancellation or changes (Jungle Scout cites 30 days)

Source: Simply Business (Oct 17, 2025); Jungle Scout (Mar 13, 2024). Details such as deductible rules have changed over time; confirm them on the Seller Central page before you buy.

What it costs

Price depends on your product category, sales volume, claims history and where your company is based. Jungle Scout cites typical costs of around $500–$1,000 per year, with some policies from around $300 per year and pay-as-you-sell models starting at $26 per month. Treat these as rough reference points: children's products, electronics with batteries, supplements and items that touch the skin or food usually cost more to insure.

Special points for Chinese sellers

  • Coverage territory. Make sure the policy covers claims brought in the United States. A domestic Chinese policy often does not.
  • Named insured. The insured company name should match the legal entity on your Amazon account exactly, in English.
  • All products listed. If you add new categories later, check that the policy still covers them, especially higher-risk products.
  • Insurer rating. Ask the broker to confirm the insurer's S&P or A.M. Best rating in writing.

Step-by-step: get compliant

  1. Collect your data: legal entity name and address, product categories, annual sales estimate, and where products are made.
  2. Request 2–3 quotes from brokers who already insure Amazon sellers, and send them Amazon's requirements.
  3. Check the certificate of insurance (COI) line by line: limits, occurrence basis, additional insured wording, deductible and policy dates.
  4. Upload the COI in Seller Central's insurance section, with the policy number, provider and expiry date.
  5. Set a renewal reminder 30–45 days before expiry and upload the new certificate as soon as you have it.
  6. Keep records of supplier test reports and certificates. Insurers and Amazon may ask for them if there is a claim.

Common reasons a certificate is rejected

  • Additional insured missing or written with the wrong entity name.
  • Limits below $1,000,000 or written on a claims-made basis.
  • Deductible not shown or too high.
  • Expired policy or dates that don't match the upload.
  • Insured name different from the Amazon account's legal entity.

亚马逊卖家产品责任险：要求、费用与合规步骤

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-27 · 约 6 分钟阅读

很多新卖家是在后台弹出警告时，才第一次听说亚马逊的保险要求。销售额超过一定门槛后，亚马逊要求卖家购买商业责任险，而且保单必须满足具体条款，并不是随便买一份“公司保险”就行。下面讲清楚规则是什么、怎么买到能通过审核的保单，以及如何顺利上传。

什么时候必须买

根据 Simply Business（2025 年 10 月 17 日）和 Jungle Scout 的整理，在亚马逊美国站单月销售总额超过 1 万美元后，卖家需在 30 天内购买商业责任险；亚马逊在其他情况下也可能要求卖家提供保险。两家来源都提示规则可能调整，请以卖家后台保险帮助页面的最新表述为准。

小提示：旺季或新品爆单时，单月 1 万美元很快就会达到。建议提前询价，别等到 12 月在 30 天期限内匆忙购买。

保单必须满足哪些条件

上述两家来源列出的核心要求一致：

项目亚马逊要求
险种商业综合责任险（也可用超额责任险或伞式责任险），按“事故发生制”（occurrence basis）承保
保额每次事故及累计赔偿限额均不低于 100 万美元
保障范围经营活动、所售全部产品、完工责任和人身伤害，包括产品责任
附加被保险人Amazon.com Services LLC 及其关联公司（和受让人）
保险公司评级标普（S&P）或 A.M. Best 评级 A- 及以上
免赔额有上限（来源称不超过 1 万美元），且必须写在保险凭证上
通知保单取消或变更前保险公司需提前通知（Jungle Scout 称 30 天）

来源：Simply Business（2025 年 10 月 17 日）；Jungle Scout（2024 年 3 月 13 日）。免赔额等细节过去有过调整，购买前请在卖家后台核实。

大概要花多少钱

保费取决于产品类目、销售规模、理赔记录和公司所在地。Jungle Scout 给出的参考是：一般每年约 500–1,000 美元，部分保单低至每年 300 美元左右，按销售额计费的方案每月 26 美元起。这些只是粗略参考：儿童用品、带电池的电子产品、保健品以及接触皮肤或食品的产品，保费通常更高。

国内卖家要特别注意

  • 承保地域：确认保单覆盖在美国发生的索赔。国内保险公司的普通保单往往不覆盖。
  • 被保险人名称：保单上的公司英文名称必须和亚马逊账户的法律主体完全一致。
  • 覆盖所有在售产品：后续新增类目时，检查保单是否仍然覆盖，尤其是高风险产品。
  • 保险公司评级：让经纪人书面确认承保公司的标普或 A.M. Best 评级。

合规步骤

  1. 整理资料：公司法律名称和地址、产品类目、预计年销售额、产品生产地。
  2. 找 2–3 家询价：优先选择有亚马逊卖家承保经验的经纪人，把亚马逊的要求直接发给对方。
  3. 逐项核对保险凭证（COI）：保额、事故发生制、附加被保险人写法、免赔额和保单日期。
  4. 上传 COI：在卖家后台保险板块上传，并填写保单号、保险公司和到期日。
  5. 设置续保提醒：到期前 30–45 天开始续保，拿到新凭证后立即上传。
  6. 保存资料：供应商检测报告和认证证书要留档，一旦发生索赔，保险公司和亚马逊都可能要求提供。

保险凭证被拒的常见原因

  • 没有写附加被保险人，或主体名称写错；
  • 保额低于 100 万美元，或按“索赔发生制”（claims-made）承保；
  • 免赔额未注明或过高；
  • 保单已过期，或日期与上传信息不符；
  • 被保险人名称与亚马逊账户主体不一致。
