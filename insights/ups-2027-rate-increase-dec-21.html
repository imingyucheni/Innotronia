UPS 2027 Rate Increase: 5.9% From Dec 21 and a 50 lb Handling Minimum

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-26 · 5 min read

UPS has set its 2027 price increase, and it arrives before the holidays are even over. UPS daily rates for Ground, Air and International services will rise by an average net 5.9% on December 21, 2026, two weeks ahead of FedEx. For sellers who ship with UPS, the headline number is only part of the story: the rules for heavy packages and some ZIP codes are changing too.

What UPS announced

  • 5.9% average on daily rates. UPS will raise daily rates for ground, air and international services by an average of 5.9% from December 21, according to the UPS shipping rates page as reported by Supply Chain Dive (Oct 26, 2026). UPS said the increase "helps to support ongoing expansion and capability enhancements" and that more detailed rate previews will come later.
  • Small business rates. UPS small business rates for ground, air and international rise 4.5%, and a 5% domestic and international fuel surcharge applies on December 21, Supply Chain Dive reports.
  • Heavier Additional Handling minimum. The minimum billable weight for packages that need Additional Handling rises from 40 to 50 lb, according to Supply Chain Dive and Gooding Supply Chain Advisors (Oct 23, 2026).
  • ZIP and zone changes. UPS is changing which ZIP codes carry area surcharges and which ZIP codes map to each zone.
  • New origin-based fee. From December 21, a per-package surcharge will apply to Ground and Ground Saver shipments from certain origin ZIP codes for some customers. UPS said details would come in early November.
  • Fuel tables. Gooding reports that fuel surcharge tables for US Ground, Ground Saver and domestic Air change on November 2, 2026.

How it compares with FedEx

FedEx announced a 5.9% average increase effective January 4, 2027 (see our FedEx 2027 rate increase article). Gooding notes that FedEx's own tables showed Ground packages of 1–5 lb rising 6.5% on average, above the headline. UPS has not yet published service-level details, so a lane-by-lane comparison isn't possible yet.

The 50 lb Additional Handling rule in practice

If a package triggers Additional Handling (for example because of its size or packaging), UPS bills it at no less than the minimum weight. Calculation from the published rule: a 30 lb package that needs Additional Handling is billed at 40 lb today and at 50 lb from December 21, which is 25% more billable weight, as Gooding points out. Brands shipping long or bulky items such as furniture parts, sports gear or large home goods will feel this most.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Don't budget on 5.9%. As Paul Yaussy of Loop told Supply Chain Dive, "Don't plan around 5.9%," because it is an average on base rates. Depending on your weights and zones, your increase can be higher than the average, and surcharges sit on top.

The timing is awkward. The increase lands during the last week of holiday shipping, so any December reorder or Christmas-week shipment may already price at 2027 rates.

Packaging is now a pricing lever. If you can keep items out of Additional Handling by changing carton size or packaging, the new 50 lb floor makes that worth more than before.

Compare carriers now. With both UPS and FedEx at 5.9%, regional and last-mile carriers and USPS (whose temporary surcharges are scheduled to end January 17, 2027) may win more lanes. Re-quote before your 2027 contracts or marketplace shipping templates lock in.

Checklist before December 21

  1. Pull your last 90 days of UPS shipments by weight, zone and service.
  2. Flag SKUs that trigger Additional Handling and test smaller packaging.
  3. Watch for UPS's detailed rate previews and the origin-ZIP surcharge list in November.
  4. Get quotes from at least one alternative carrier for your top lanes.
  5. Update shipping charges and marketplace shipping templates for 2027.
  6. Re-check margins on heavy and low-priced SKUs.

UPS 2027 年涨价：12 月 21 日起平均涨 5.9%，额外处理最低 50 磅

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-26 · 约 5 分钟阅读

UPS 公布了 2027 年涨价方案，而且假日季还没结束就生效。2026 年 12 月 21 日起，UPS Ground、Air 和国际服务的日常费率平均净涨 5.9%，比 FedEx 早两周。对用 UPS 发货的卖家来说，5.9% 只是表面数字：超重包裹的计费规则和部分邮编的附加费也在变。

UPS 公布了什么

  • 日常费率平均涨 5.9%。据 Supply Chain Dive（2026 年 10 月 26 日）援引 UPS 运费页面，12 月 21 日起 UPS 陆运、空运和国际服务的日常费率平均上涨 5.9%。UPS 表示涨价"有助于支持持续扩张和能力提升"，更详细的费率预览将稍后公布。
  • 小企业费率。据 Supply Chain Dive，UPS 小企业费率（陆运、空运、国际）上涨 4.5%，并于 12 月 21 日起适用 5% 的国内和国际燃油附加费。
  • Additional Handling 最低计费重量提高。据 Supply Chain Dive 和 Gooding Supply Chain Advisors（2026 年 10 月 23 日），需要额外处理（Additional Handling）的包裹，最低计费重量从 40 磅提高到 50 磅。
  • 邮编和分区调整。UPS 将调整适用偏远地区附加费的邮编，以及邮编与运费分区的对应关系。
  • 新的按发货地收费。12 月 21 日起，部分客户从特定发货邮编寄出的 Ground 和 Ground Saver 包裹将按件收取附加费，UPS 称细节将于 11 月初公布。
  • 燃油附加费表。据 Gooding，美国 Ground、Ground Saver 和国内空运的燃油附加费表将于 2026 年 11 月 2 日调整。

与 FedEx 对比

FedEx 此前宣布 2027 年 1 月 4 日起平均涨价 5.9%（见我们的FedEx 2027 年涨价解读）。Gooding 指出，FedEx 自己的费率表显示 1–5 磅 Ground 包裹平均涨 6.5%，高于整体平均。UPS 尚未公布分服务明细，目前还无法逐条线路对比。

50 磅新规怎么算

包裹一旦触发 Additional Handling（比如尺寸或包装原因），UPS 会按不低于最低计费重量收费。按公布规则计算：一个需要额外处理的 30 磅包裹，现在按 40 磅计费，12 月 21 日起按 50 磅计费，计费重量多出 25%（Gooding 也指出了这一点）。家具配件、运动器材、大件家居等长条或大体积商品受影响最大。

我们的解读

别按 5.9% 做预算。Loop 的 Paul Yaussy 对 Supply Chain Dive 说："别按 5.9% 做计划"，因为这只是基础费率的平均值。视重量和分区不同，你的实际涨幅可能高于平均值，附加费还要另算。

时间点很尴尬。涨价落在假日季最后一周，12 月补货或圣诞周发出的包裹可能已经按 2027 年价格计费。

包装成了定价工具。如果能通过调整纸箱尺寸或包装让商品不触发 Additional Handling，50 磅新门槛下这样做比以前更划算。

现在就比价。UPS 和 FedEx 都涨 5.9%，区域和尾程承运商以及 USPS（其临时附加费计划于 2027 年 1 月 17 日结束）可能拿下更多线路。在 2027 年合同和平台运费模板定下来之前重新询价。

12 月 21 日前的行动清单

  1. 导出过去 90 天 UPS 发货数据，按重量、分区和服务分类。
  2. 找出触发 Additional Handling 的 SKU，测试更小的包装。
  3. 11 月留意 UPS 详细费率预览和按发货邮编收费名单。
  4. 主要线路至少再找一家承运商报价。
  5. 更新 2027 年运费设置和各平台运费模板。
  6. 重新核算重货和低价 SKU 的利润。
