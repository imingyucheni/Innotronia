UPS Q3 2026: Fewer Packages, Higher Prices, and a Normal Peak Ahead

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-29 · 5 min read

UPS is heading into the holidays shipping fewer packages but earning more on each one. On October 27, 2026, UPS reported third-quarter results that beat expectations and raised its full-year outlook, while confirming it expects a normal peak-season surge despite carrying far less Amazon volume. For sellers, the numbers explain why parcel prices keep rising.

The Q3 numbers

According to UPS's Q3 2026 earnings release (Oct 27, 2026):

  • Revenue: $22.9 billion consolidated, up 6.7% year over year.
  • US Domestic: revenue of $14.9 billion, up 4.5%, driven by a 6.1% increase in revenue per piece. Average daily volume was 15.6 million pieces, down about 3.3% from 16.2 million a year earlier.
  • International: revenue up 7.1%, with revenue per piece up 15.0%.
  • Supply Chain Solutions: revenue up 18.2%, led by forwarding and logistics, including healthcare.
  • Earnings: diluted EPS of $1.37, or $1.69 on an adjusted basis.
  • Outlook raised: full-year 2026 revenue of about $92.6 billion and adjusted EPS of about $7.28. For Q4, UPS expects revenue of about $25.7 billion, per STAT Times (Oct 27, 2026).

The release does not name Amazon but says planned volume reductions from "our largest customer" were completed in the second quarter of 2026. CEO Carol Tomé said, "Given the strength we see in the business, we are raising our full-year financial guidance."

What UPS said about peak season

On the earnings call, as reported by Supply Chain Dive (Oct 27, 2026):

  • This is UPS's first peak season since cutting its Amazon volume in half.
  • Just over 100 US customers generate about 80% of the annual peak volume spike, Tomé said.
  • Based on those customers' forecasts, UPS expects average daily volume to rise about 25% from Q3 to Q4, in line with last year's peak, though Q4 US daily volume should dip slightly year over year.
  • UPS plans to hire 100,000 seasonal workers.
  • Small and medium-sized businesses made up 35.1% of Q3 US volume, up about 240 basis points year over year.
  • About half of the Q3 revenue-per-piece gain came from base rates and customer mix; higher fuel surcharges made up the rest.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

UPS is chasing small and mid-size shippers. With less Amazon volume, SMB shippers are a bigger and growing share of UPS's business. That can mean better attention and sales offers for growing brands, but prices are moving up, not down.

Rate increases are working for UPS. Revenue per piece rose 6.1% while volume fell. Combined with the 5.9% general rate increase on December 21, expect UPS to keep prioritizing price over volume in 2027.

Fuel is a big part of the bill. UPS said fuel accounted for about half of its revenue-per-piece gain. With the USPS weekly fuel surcharge also starting in January, fuel will be a larger line in every carrier's cost.

Peak capacity should be fine, but plan early. A 25% rise from Q3 to Q4 is in line with last year, and UPS is hiring 100,000 seasonal workers. Still, peak surcharges apply and cutoffs are firm, so don't wait until mid-December to ship.

Checklist for the rest of peak

  1. Confirm your UPS peak surcharges and any account-specific caps.
  2. Ship holiday inventory to warehouses and FBA/WFS early.
  3. Compare UPS with USPS and regional carriers for light parcels before 2027 rates start.
  4. If you're a growing SMB shipper, ask your UPS rep about contract terms before January.
  5. Add a fuel line to your 2027 shipping budget for every carrier.

UPS 2026 年三季报：件数下降、单价上涨，旺季照常

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-29 · 约 5 分钟阅读

UPS 正以"件数更少、单件更赚钱"的状态进入假日季。2026 年 10 月 27 日，UPS 公布第三季度业绩，超出预期并上调全年指引，同时确认尽管亚马逊业务量大幅减少，旺季增幅仍将与往年持平。对卖家来说，这组数据解释了为什么快递价格还在涨。

第三季度数据

据 UPS 2026 年第三季度财报新闻稿（2026 年 10 月 27 日）：

  • 营收：合并营收 229 亿美元，同比增长 6.7%。
  • 美国国内：营收 149 亿美元，增长 4.5%，主要来自单件收入增长 6.1%。日均包裹量 1,560 万件，比去年同期的 1,620 万件下降约 3.3%。
  • 国际：营收增长 7.1%，单件收入增长 15.0%。
  • 供应链解决方案：营收增长 18.2%，主要由货代和物流（含医疗）带动。
  • 利润：摊薄每股收益 1.37 美元，调整后 1.69 美元。
  • 上调指引：2026 全年营收约 926 亿美元，调整后每股收益约 7.28 美元。据 STAT Times（2026 年 10 月 27 日），UPS 预计第四季度营收约 257 亿美元。

新闻稿没有点名亚马逊，但提到来自"最大客户"的计划性减量已于 2026 年第二季度完成。CEO Carol Tomé 表示："鉴于业务的强劲表现，我们上调全年财务指引。"

UPS 对旺季的说法

据 Supply Chain Dive（2026 年 10 月 27 日）对财报电话会的报道：

  • 这是 UPS 把亚马逊业务量砍半后的第一个旺季。
  • Tomé 表示，约 100 多家美国客户贡献了每年旺季增量的约 80%。
  • 根据这些客户的预测，UPS 预计第四季度日均量比第三季度增长约 25%，与去年旺季相当，但第四季度美国日均量同比会略有下降。
  • UPS 计划招聘 10 万名季节性员工。
  • 中小企业客户占第三季度美国业务量的 35.1%，同比提高约 240 个基点。
  • 第三季度单件收入的增长约一半来自基础费率和客户结构，另一半来自燃油附加费上涨。

我们的解读

UPS 在争取中小卖家。亚马逊量减少后，中小企业在 UPS 业务中的占比越来越大。成长型品牌可能获得更多关注和销售报价，但价格方向是向上，不是向下。

涨价策略对 UPS 有效。件数下降，单件收入却涨了 6.1%。再加上 12 月 21 日平均 5.9% 的涨价，预计 UPS 2027 年会继续"保价不保量"。

燃油占了大头。UPS 表示单件收入增长约一半来自燃油。加上 1 月起的 USPS 按周燃油附加费，燃油费在各家承运商账单里的占比都会更高。

旺季运力应该够，但要早发。Q3 到 Q4 增长 25% 与去年持平，UPS 也在招 10 万季节工。但旺季附加费照收、截止时间不变，别拖到 12 月中旬才发货。

旺季剩余时间的行动清单

  1. 确认你的 UPS 旺季附加费及账户专属上限。
  2. 尽早把假日库存发到海外仓和 FBA/WFS。
  3. 2027 年新价生效前，轻小件对比 UPS、USPS 和区域承运商。
  4. 成长型中小卖家可在 1 月前与 UPS 销售代表谈合同条款。
  5. 2027 年运费预算中为每家承运商加入燃油费一项。
