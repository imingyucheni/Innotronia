Amazon Sponsored Products Bidding Strategies and Placement Adjustments Explained

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-30 · 6 min read

Two settings quietly decide how much you pay for every Sponsored Products click: the campaign's bidding strategy and its placement adjustments. Many sellers leave the defaults and then wonder why top-of-search clicks cost far more than their keyword bid. Here is how the settings work, how they combine, and how to choose them.

The three bidding strategies

  • Dynamic bids – down only. Amazon may lower your bid in real time when a click seems less likely to convert. It never raises it. This is the most conservative option.
  • Dynamic bids – up and down. Amazon may raise or lower your bid. According to Amazon Ads' guide to dynamic bidding, "we will increase or decrease your bids by up to 100% for all placements based on performance." Amazon's example: a $1.00 bid can go up to a maximum of $2.00.
  • Fixed bids. Amazon uses your exact bid (plus any placement adjustment) and does not change it based on conversion likelihood.
Note: Some older third-party guides say up-and-down can raise bids by 100% for top of search but only 50% elsewhere. Amazon's current guide says up to 100% for all placements. Check Amazon's help pages for the latest rules, as they can change.

Placement adjustments

Sponsored Products ads show in three placements: top of search (first row of results), rest of search and product pages. You can raise your bid for each placement by a percentage. In January 2024, Amazon Ads announced that advertisers can adjust bids for rest of search by up to 900%, the same as the other placements, and that the control works with fixed and dynamic bidding for both auto and manual campaigns.

Which strategy to use when

SituationSuggested strategyWhy
New campaign, little dataDown onlyLimits overspending while you learn which terms convert
Proven exact-match keywords with good ACoSUp and downLets Amazon bid harder on clicks likely to convert
Branded keywords, defending your own listingsFixed or up and downKeeps you visible on searches for your brand
Tight budget or thin marginDown onlyBids never go above your bid plus any placement adjustment
Testing placementsFixedMakes placement results easier to compare

These are starting points from our experience, not Amazon rules. Your data should decide.

How to set placement adjustments with data

  1. Open the placement report for the campaign and look at at least 2–4 weeks of data.
  2. Compare ACoS and conversion rate by placement. Top of search often converts better but costs more per click.
  3. Raise the adjustment in small steps (for example 10–25%) only where ACoS is below your target.
  4. Lower or remove adjustments where ACoS is above target, and consider lowering the base bid.
  5. Change one thing at a time and wait for new data before the next change.
  6. Watch budget pacing. If a campaign runs out of budget early in the day, your adjustments may be too high.

Common mistakes

  • Combining high placement adjustments with up-and-down bidding on new, unproven keywords.
  • Judging a placement on a few days of data.
  • Forgetting that a high base bid multiplies through every adjustment.
  • Changing strategy, bids and adjustments all at once, then not knowing what worked.

亚马逊商品推广竞价策略与广告位调整详解

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-30 · 约 6 分钟阅读

每一次 Sponsored Products（商品推广）点击花多少钱，其实由两个设置悄悄决定：广告活动的竞价策略和广告位调整。很多卖家用默认设置，然后疑惑为什么搜索顶部的点击费远高于关键词出价。下面讲清楚这两个设置怎么运作、如何叠加，以及怎么选。

三种竞价策略

  • 动态竞价 – 仅降低：当点击转化可能性较低时，亚马逊会实时降低出价，但绝不上调。这是最保守的选项。
  • 动态竞价 – 提高和降低：亚马逊可以上调或下调出价。根据 Amazon Ads 动态竞价官方指南，“我们会根据表现将出价在所有广告位上最多提高或降低 100%”。官方示例：1 美元的出价最高可以涨到 2 美元。
  • 固定竞价：亚马逊按你设置的出价（加上广告位调整）竞价，不会根据转化可能性调整。
注意：一些较早的第三方资料说，“提高和降低”在搜索顶部最多上调 100%，其他位置只有 50%。亚马逊目前的官方指南写的是所有广告位最多 100%。规则可能调整，请以亚马逊帮助页面为准。

广告位调整

商品推广广告有三个展示位置：搜索结果顶部（首行）、搜索结果其余位置和商品详情页。你可以按百分比分别提高每个位置的出价。2024 年 1 月，Amazon Ads 宣布“搜索结果其余位置”的出价调整上限也提高到 900%，与另外两个位置相同，并且适用于自动和手动广告的固定竞价和动态竞价。

不同情况怎么选

情况建议策略原因
新广告活动、数据少仅降低在摸清哪些词能转化之前，控制超支
已验证、ACoS 良好的精准词提高和降低让亚马逊在高转化点击上出更高价
品牌词、防守自家 Listing固定或提高和降低保证自家品牌搜索中的曝光
预算紧、利润薄仅降低出价不会超过基础出价加广告位调整
测试广告位效果固定更容易比较各广告位的表现

以上是基于我们经验的起点建议，并非亚马逊规则，最终以你的数据为准。

用数据设置广告位调整

  1. 打开广告位报告，至少看 2–4 周的数据。
  2. 按广告位比较 ACoS 和转化率：搜索顶部转化通常更好，但单次点击更贵。
  3. 小步提高调整比例（例如每次 10%–25%），只在 ACoS 低于目标的广告位上调。
  4. ACoS 高于目标的广告位降低或取消调整，必要时同时降低基础出价。
  5. 每次只改一个变量，等有新数据再做下一步调整。
  6. 关注预算消耗：如果广告活动一早就花完预算，说明调整可能设得过高。

常见错误

  • 对未经验证的新关键词同时使用高广告位调整和“提高和降低”策略；
  • 只看几天数据就判断广告位好坏；
  • 忽视基础出价过高会在每一层调整中被放大；
  • 策略、出价和调整一次全改，最后不知道哪个起了作用。
