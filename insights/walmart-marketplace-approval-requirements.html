Walmart Marketplace Approval Requirements: Documents and Checklist

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-26 · 6 min read

Walmart Marketplace is not open to everyone: every seller goes through an application and business verification, and incomplete or inconsistent documents are the most common reason for delays. The good news is that the requirements are published. Prepare them properly and approval is usually straightforward.

Walmart's published requirements

According to Walmart's official beginner's guide, sellers applying to the US marketplace should have:

  • A business tax ID or business license number (an SSN is not accepted).
  • Supporting documents that verify your business name and address.
  • A history of marketplace or e-commerce success.
  • Products with GTIN/UPC GS1 company prefix numbers.
  • A catalog that complies with Walmart's prohibited-products policy.
  • Fulfillment through Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) or another B2C US warehouse with returns capability.

Walmart states there are no setup or monthly fees; instead you pay a referral fee on each sale, which Walmart says ranges from 6% to 15% depending on category and price.

Documents for business verification

Walmart's Marketplace Learn guide to business verification (updated June 15, 2026) lists what you upload during onboarding:

ItemWhat Walmart asks forCommon mistake
Photo IDFront and back of the ID; clear and well-lit; PNG, JPG or PDFCropped or blurry photos
Business documentBusiness license or registration certificate showing company name, address and dateName or address differs from the application
Proof of addressFor example a utility bill addressed to the business or applicant, or a letter of authorizationOld or personal documents that don't match
Identity detailsEntered exactly as on your ID, including residential addressAbbreviations or different spellings
Company detailsCountry of incorporation, registration or license number, entity type, business phone, primary contactDetails differ from the registration document

For some countries of incorporation, Walmart may also ask for identity information for the company's legal representative or director. Chinese sellers should have the legal representative's details ready before starting.

A step-by-step checklist

  1. Decide which entity applies. A US LLC or corporation generally provides a W-9, while a foreign company such as a Chinese one generally provides a W-8BEN-E. Use the entity that will receive payouts and own the brand.
  2. Make every document match. Company name, address and registration number should be identical across the license, proof of address, tax form and bank account.
  3. Prepare proof of track record. Have store links or sales reports from Amazon, your own site or other marketplaces ready.
  4. Check your UPCs. Walmart asks for GS1 company prefix numbers, so buy barcodes from GS1 in the brand owner's name rather than using resold codes.
  5. Plan fulfillment. Decide between WFS and your own US warehouse or 3PL. Be ready to explain shipping times and how returns are handled in the US.
  6. Review restricted categories. Some categories need extra approval or documents; check before you list.
  7. After approval, complete tax and payment setup, list a few top SKUs first and watch performance metrics from day one.
Tip: Most delays come from mismatched details, not missing ones. Before you submit, put every document side by side and compare the company name and address letter by letter.

New-seller savings: worth planning around

Walmart's New-Seller Savings 2026 program runs from Feb 2, 2026 to Jan 31, 2027 for sellers who go live after Feb 1, 2026 and opt in after launch. Walmart lists referral-fee discounts of 20% on the first $50K of GMV, 30% on $50K–$500K and 40% above $500K (up to $72K in total), plus credits for WFS, search engine marketing and Walmart Connect ads. After approval: protect the account

Walmart tracks seller performance on metrics including cancellation rate, on-time delivery, refund rate, valid tracking rate and seller response rate. Thresholds are published in Seller Center and may change, so check them there. Upload valid tracking for every order, set realistic handling times, keep inventory in sync and answer customer messages quickly.

沃尔玛美国站入驻要求：审核资料与准备清单

Atronia Innovations · 2026-09-26 · 约 6 分钟阅读

沃尔玛美国站（Walmart Marketplace）不是谁都能直接开店：每个卖家都要提交入驻申请并完成企业验证，而资料不完整、前后不一致是审核卡住最常见的原因。好在沃尔玛的入驻要求是公开的，提前准备到位，通过审核通常并不难。

沃尔玛官方公布的入驻要求

根据沃尔玛官方新手指南，申请美国站的卖家需要具备：

  • 企业税号或营业执照号（不接受个人 SSN）；
  • 能证明公司名称和地址的证明文件；
  • 在其他平台或独立站有过成功的电商经营记录；
  • 产品拥有 GS1 公司前缀的 GTIN/UPC 条码；
  • 产品目录符合沃尔玛禁售商品政策；
  • 履约能力：使用沃尔玛官方仓 WFS，或其他具备退货处理能力的美国本土 B2C 仓库。

沃尔玛表示平台不收开店费和月租，只在成交后按类目收取佣金，官方说明佣金率在 6%–15% 之间，视类目和售价而定。

企业验证需要哪些资料

沃尔玛 Marketplace Learn 企业验证指南（2026 年 6 月 15 日更新）列出了入驻时需要上传的资料：

资料官方要求常见错误
身份证件照片证件正反面，清晰明亮；PNG、JPG 或 PDF 格式照片裁切不全或模糊
企业证明文件营业执照或注册证书，需显示公司名称、地址和日期名称、地址与申请表不一致
地址证明如寄给公司或申请人的水电账单，或授权书用了过期或与公司不符的个人文件
身份信息完全按证件填写，包括居住地址缩写、拼写不一致
公司信息注册国家/地区、注册号或营业执照号、公司类型、公司电话、主要联系人与注册文件上的信息不一致

对于部分注册国家，沃尔玛可能还会要求提供公司法定代表人或董事的身份信息。国内公司申请前，建议先把法人资料准备好。

入驻准备清单

  1. 确定用哪个主体申请：美国 LLC/公司一般提交 W-9，中国等境外公司一般提交 W-8BEN-E。选用将来收款、持有品牌的那个主体。
  2. 所有资料保持一致：营业执照、地址证明、税表和收款账户上的公司名称、地址、注册号必须完全一致。
  3. 准备经营记录：把亚马逊、独立站或其他平台的店铺链接、销售报表准备好。
  4. 检查 UPC：沃尔玛要求使用 GS1 公司前缀条码，建议以品牌方名义直接向 GS1 购买条码，不要用转卖的码。
  5. 规划履约：在 WFS 和自有美国仓/第三方海外仓之间做好选择，能说明配送时效和美国本土退货处理方式。
  6. 核对限制类目：部分类目需要额外审核或资质，上架前先确认。
  7. 审核通过后：完成税务和收款设置，先上几款主力 SKU，从第一天起关注绩效指标。
小提示：大部分审核延误不是因为缺资料，而是资料对不上。提交前把所有文件摆在一起，公司名称和地址逐字核对。

新卖家优惠：值得纳入规划

入驻之后：守住店铺

沃尔玛会考核卖家绩效，指标包括取消率、准时送达率、退款率、有效追踪率和卖家回复率。具体标准以卖家后台公布为准，并可能调整。每单都要上传有效物流单号，处理时效设置要实际，库存保持同步，买家消息及时回复。
