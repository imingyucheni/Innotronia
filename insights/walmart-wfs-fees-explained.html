Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) Fees Explained, With Examples

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-03 · 6 min read

Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) is Walmart's answer to Amazon FBA: you send inventory to Walmart's warehouses, and Walmart picks, packs, ships and handles customer service and returns. The fee structure is simpler than many sellers expect, but a few surcharges and the peak-season storage rule can change your margin. Here is how the fees work, with worked examples.

Fulfillment fees

According to Walmart's WFS fees page (updated May 27, 2026), the standard fulfillment fee is charged per unit by shipping weight:

  • 1 lb or less: $3.45
  • 2 lb: $4.95
  • 3 lb: $5.45
  • 4–20 lb: $5.75 plus $0.40 for each lb over 4 lb

Surcharges are added on top:

SurchargeAmount per unit
Apparel$0.50
Hazardous materials$0.50
Item price under $10$1.00
Oversize 1$3.00
Oversize 2$20.00

Big and bulky items have a separate schedule, which Walmart lists as $155 plus $0.80 per lb over 90 lb for items up to 500 lb. Check the page for heavier tiers and exact size definitions.

Storage fees

PeriodFee
January–September$0.75 per cubic foot per month
October–December, stored up to 30 days$0.75 per cubic foot per month
October–December, stored more than 30 days$0.75 + $1.50 per cubic foot per month
Stored 366–450 days (from June 30, 2026)$2.25 per cubic foot per month
Stored more than 450 days (from June 30, 2026)$7.50 per cubic foot per month

Source: Walmart Marketplace Learn, May 27, 2026. Walmart has no setup or monthly fee for selling on its marketplace, according to its seller guide; WFS fees are charged only when you use the service.

Worked examples

Using the published rates (calculation only; your referral fee and other costs come on top):

  • Phone case, 0.5 lb, sells for $14.99: $3.45 fulfillment. No price surcharge because it is above $10.
  • T-shirt, 0.8 lb, sells for $9.99: $3.45 + $0.50 apparel + $1.00 under-$10 = $4.95. The under-$10 surcharge alone adds about 10% of the price.
  • Kitchen appliance, 7 lb: $5.75 + 3 × $0.40 = $6.95.
  • Storage over peak: a carton of 2 cubic feet that sits from October 1 to November 30 pays $0.75 × 2 for October, then ($0.75 + $1.50) × 2 for November, about $6.00 in total for the two months.
Tip: If a product sells for just under $10, test pricing it at $10 or above, or bundle two units. Removing the $1.00 surcharge can matter more than a small price change.

Common WFS fee mistakes

  • Using product weight instead of shipping weight. Packaging adds weight and can push an item into the next tier.
  • Ignoring the under-$10 surcharge. It applies per unit, so it hits low-price, high-volume items hardest.
  • Overstocking for Q4. Units stored more than 30 days in October–December pay three times the base storage rate.
  • Forgetting aged stock. From June 30, 2026, units older than 450 days pay $7.50 per cubic foot per month, ten times the January–September rate.
  • Comparing only fulfillment fees. Include inbound freight, prep and returns when comparing WFS with other options.

WFS vs your own 3PL

WFS makes sense when Walmart is a meaningful channel, your products are small and standard-size, and you want fast-delivery badges without managing carriers. A third-party warehouse can be better when you sell across many channels, have oversized or slow-moving items, or need custom packaging. Many sellers use both: WFS for best sellers, a 3PL for everything else.

Checklist before you send inventory

  1. Weigh and measure each SKU in its final packaging and compare against Walmart's size tiers.
  2. Calculate the total fee per unit, including surcharges, and add it to your margin sheet.
  3. Price above $10 where possible, or bundle low-price items.
  4. Send only the stock you expect to sell within the peak window to avoid the October–December over-30-day storage surcharge.
  5. Review aged inventory monthly and remove or discount units approaching 366 days.

沃尔玛 WFS 费用详解：配送费、仓储费与计算示例

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-03 · 约 6 分钟阅读

沃尔玛官方仓（WFS）相当于沃尔玛版的 FBA：你把库存送进沃尔玛仓库，由沃尔玛负责拣货、打包、配送、客服和退货。它的收费结构比很多卖家想象的简单，但几项附加费和旺季仓储规则会明显影响利润。下面讲清楚收费方式，并附实际计算示例。

配送费

根据沃尔玛 WFS 费用页面（2026 年 5 月 27 日更新），标准件按运输重量逐件收取配送费：

  • 1 磅及以下：3.45 美元
  • 2 磅：4.95 美元
  • 3 磅：5.45 美元
  • 4–20 磅：5.75 美元，超过 4 磅的部分每磅加 0.40 美元

另有附加费：

附加费每件金额
服装0.50 美元
危险品0.50 美元
售价低于 10 美元1.00 美元
超尺寸 1 级3.00 美元
超尺寸 2 级20.00 美元

大件商品另有收费标准，沃尔玛列出的 500 磅以内为 155 美元，超过 90 磅部分每磅加 0.80 美元。更重档位和具体尺寸定义请以官方页面为准。

仓储费

时间段费用
1–9 月每立方英尺每月 0.75 美元
10–12 月，存放不超过 30 天每立方英尺每月 0.75 美元
10–12 月，存放超过 30 天每立方英尺每月 0.75 + 1.50 美元
存放 366–450 天（2026 年 6 月 30 日起）每立方英尺每月 2.25 美元
存放超过 450 天（2026 年 6 月 30 日起）每立方英尺每月 7.50 美元

来源：Walmart Marketplace Learn，2026 年 5 月 27 日。根据沃尔玛卖家指南，在沃尔玛平台开店没有开店费和月租；WFS 费用只在使用该服务时收取。

计算示例

按官方公布的费率计算（仅为配送和仓储费，佣金等其他成本另计）：

  • 手机壳，0.5 磅，售价 14.99 美元：配送费 3.45 美元。售价高于 10 美元，不收低价附加费。
  • T 恤，0.8 磅，售价 9.99 美元：3.45 + 0.50（服装）+ 1.00（低于 10 美元）= 4.95 美元。仅低价附加费就约占售价的 10%。
  • 厨房小家电，7 磅：5.75 + 3 × 0.40 = 6.95 美元。
  • 旺季仓储：一箱 2 立方英尺的货从 10 月 1 日放到 11 月 30 日，10 月按 0.75 × 2，11 月按 (0.75 + 1.50) × 2 计算，两个月合计约 6.00 美元。
小提示：售价略低于 10 美元的产品，可以测试定价到 10 美元以上，或做成两件装。省掉 1 美元附加费，往往比小幅调价更有效。

WFS 费用常见误区

  • 用产品重量而不是运输重量：包装会增加重量，可能让商品跳到下一个计费档位。
  • 忽视低于 10 美元附加费：它按件收取，对低价走量的产品影响最大。
  • Q4 备货过多：10–12 月存放超过 30 天的库存，仓储费是基础费率的三倍。
  • 忘记清理老库存：2026 年 6 月 30 日起，库龄超过 450 天的库存每立方英尺每月 7.50 美元，是 1–9 月费率的十倍。
  • 只比较配送费：对比 WFS 和其他方案时，要算上头程运费、贴标备货和退货成本。

WFS 还是自有第三方仓？

如果沃尔玛是你的重要渠道、产品小而标准、又想拿到快速配送标识而不想自己管物流，WFS 很合适。如果你多渠道销售、产品超大或动销慢，或者需要定制包装，第三方仓可能更好。很多卖家两者结合：爆款放 WFS，其他走第三方仓。

发货入仓前的检查清单

  1. 按最终包装称重量尺寸，对照沃尔玛的尺寸分级。
  2. 算出含附加费的单件总费用，写进利润表。
  3. 尽量定价在 10 美元以上，低价品做组合装。
  4. 只发旺季能卖完的量，避免 10–12 月存放超 30 天的附加仓储费。
  5. 每月检查库龄，接近 366 天的库存及时移除或打折清理。
