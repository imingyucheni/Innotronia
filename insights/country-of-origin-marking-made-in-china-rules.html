"Made in China" Labels: US Country-of-Origin Marking Rules for Sellers

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-08 · 6 min read

"Made in China" on a product or box is not just a label; it is a US legal requirement, and getting it wrong can cost an extra 10% duty or force you to re-label goods at the port. Many factories selling to US marketplaces also print US brand names and addresses on packaging, which triggers extra rules. This guide explains the country-of-origin marking rules in US customs regulations (19 CFR Part 134) in plain language.

This is a general summary, not legal advice. Complex cases, such as products assembled in several countries, may need a CBP ruling or a customs broker's advice.

The basic rule

Under 19 CFR 134.11, unless an exception applies, "every article of foreign origin (or its container) imported into the United States shall be marked in a conspicuous place as legibly, indelibly, and permanently as the nature of the article (or container) will permit," showing the ultimate purchaser in the US "the English name of the country of origin."

  • Country of origin means the country of manufacture, production or growth. Work done in another country must cause a "substantial transformation" to change the origin (19 CFR 134.1).
  • Ultimate purchaser is generally the last person in the US who receives the article in the form in which it was imported. For most marketplace goods, that's the consumer.

The cost of getting it wrong

According to 19 CFR 134.2, articles not properly marked "shall be subject to additional duties of 10 percent of the final appraised value" unless exported or destroyed under customs supervision before liquidation. The duty applies both for failing to show the English country name and for missing words needed "to prevent deception or mistake."

Shipment value (appraised)Extra marking duty (10%)
$10,000$1,000
$25,000$2,500
$50,000$5,000

Calculation: appraised value × 10%, per 19 CFR 134.2. This is on top of normal duties, and goods may also be held until they are re-marked.

How to mark: method and permanence

19 CFR 134.41 says marking is "best met by marking worked into the article at the time of manufacture," for example die-sunk or molded on metal, glazed on ceramics, or printed on paper articles. The marking should stay on "until it reaches the ultimate purchaser unless it is deliberately removed," must "survive normal distribution and store handling," and the purchaser "must be able to find the marking easily and read it without strain."

The most common trap for private-label brands: a box printed with "Designed in California" or a Los Angeles address. Under 134.46, that needs "Made in China" (or the actual origin) close by and in comparable size.

Practical checklist for factories and brands

  1. Confirm the true country of origin, especially for products with parts from several countries.
  2. Mold, print or sew "Made in China" (or the right country) into the product where practical.
  3. Check packaging for any US names, cities or "USA" wording and add origin marking next to them.
  4. Use the full English country name; avoid unclear abbreviations.
  5. Make sure labels survive shipping and handling; avoid easily removed stickers where permanent marking is possible.
  6. Keep photos of markings on file for each SKU.
  7. Share marking requirements with your factory in the purchase order.

"Made in China"怎么标：美国原产地标识规则卖家指南

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-08 · 约 6 分钟阅读

商品或包装上的"Made in China"不只是一个标签，而是美国的法定要求。弄错了，可能被加征 10% 的关税，或在港口被要求重新贴标。很多面向美国平台的工厂还会在包装上印美国品牌名和地址，这又会触发额外规定。本文用通俗的话讲清楚美国海关法规（19 CFR 第 134 部分）的原产地标识规则。

本文为一般性介绍，不构成法律意见。多国组装等复杂情况，可能需要申请 CBP 预裁定或咨询报关行。

基本规则

根据 19 CFR 134.11，除非有例外，"每件进口到美国的外国原产商品（或其容器）都必须在显眼位置，以商品（或容器）性质所允许的清晰、不易褪色且持久的方式进行标识"，向美国最终购买者表明"原产国的英文名称"。

  • 原产国指商品的制造、生产或种植国。在其他国家进行的加工必须构成"实质性改变"，才能改变原产地（19 CFR 134.1）。
  • 最终购买者一般指在美国最后一个以进口时形态收到商品的人。对大多数平台商品来说，就是消费者。

弄错的代价

根据 19 CFR 134.2，未按要求标识的商品"须加征最终完税价格 10% 的额外关税"，除非在清算前在海关监管下退运或销毁。没有标明英文国名、或缺少"防止欺骗或误解"所需文字，都会被加征。

货物完税价格额外标识关税（10%）
10,000 美元1,000 美元
25,000 美元2,500 美元
50,000 美元5,000 美元

计算：完税价格 × 10%，依据 19 CFR 134.2。这是在正常关税之外另加的，货物还可能被扣留直到重新标识。

如何标识：方式与持久性

19 CFR 134.41 指出，标识"最好在生产时直接做进商品里"，例如金属件冲压或模压、陶瓷烧釉、纸制品印刷。标识应保留"直到到达最终购买者手中，除非被故意去除"，必须"经得起正常流通和门店搬运"，购买者"必须能轻松找到并毫不费力地看清"。

自有品牌最常踩的坑：包装上印着"Designed in California"或洛杉矶地址。按 134.46，必须在附近以相当字号标注"Made in China"（或实际原产国）。

工厂与品牌实操清单

  1. 确认真实原产国，尤其是零部件来自多个国家的产品。
  2. 条件允许时，把"Made in China"（或正确国名）模压、印刷或缝制在产品上。
  3. 检查包装上是否有美国名称、城市或"USA"字样，并在旁边加注原产地。
  4. 使用完整英文国名，避免含糊的缩写。
  5. 确保标签经得起运输搬运；能做永久标识的，避免用易撕贴纸。
  6. 为每个 SKU 保存标识照片存档。
  7. 在采购订单中把标识要求写给工厂。

标识只是合规的一部分，也可参考我们的如何查找 HTS 编码和CPSC 电子申报指南。
