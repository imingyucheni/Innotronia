Best Buy Launches on TikTok Shop: What It Means for Sellers

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-04 · 5 min read

Best Buy, one of America's biggest electronics retailers, is opening a store on TikTok Shop in late October 2026. It is another sign that TikTok Shop has moved from a place for impulse buys to a mainstream channel where large brands compete for the same creators and shoppers as independent sellers. Here is what was announced and what it means if you sell on TikTok Shop.

What Best Buy announced

  • Timing and range. In a September 22, 2026 announcement, Best Buy said it will launch on TikTok Shop in late October with nearly 10,000 products, including headphones, cameras, small appliances, laptops and accessories, from brands such as Oura, Bose, Dyson and Microsoft.
  • How it works. Shoppers can buy through creator videos, Best Buy's shop page, the Shop tab and in-app product pages. Best Buy fulfills orders, and eligible purchases can be returned in store or by mail.
  • Creators and ads. Best Buy says creators in its program can promote the range with affiliate links, and advertisers can create shoppable content with measurement.
  • Why. Jonathan Greer, Best Buy's vice president of e-commerce, said: "Social media isn't just influencing what people buy anymore. It's shaping how they discover products."

Retail Dive notes that Ulta Beauty, Sephora, Crocs, Pacsun and Tarte Cosmetics are already selling on TikTok Shop, and cites Emarketer data that TikTok Shop made up nearly one-fifth of social commerce in 2025.

How big TikTok Shop has become

According to Momentum Works and Tabcut data reported by Tubefilter on August 6, 2026, TikTok Shop's global GMV reached $50.3 billion in the first half of 2026, up 92% year over year, with a full-year projection of $123.5 billion. US GMV was $11.8 billion, and the US overtook Indonesia as the platform's largest market, as Net Influencer also reported.

Some details are not public yet: the exact launch date, whether Best Buy will run TikTok Shop ads at scale, and how its prices will compare with its own website. Watch Best Buy and TikTok Shop announcements as the launch rolls out.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Electronics and small appliances get more crowded. If you sell headphones, chargers, kitchen gadgets or accessories, expect a large retailer with famous brands, in-store returns and big creator budgets in your category from late October, right as holiday demand peaks.
  • Creator rates may rise. Retailers running affiliate programs compete for the same creators. Lock in creator partnerships and commissions for November now.
  • The Shop tab matters as much as video. With over half of GMV coming from the Shop tab, titles, images, prices and reviews matter like on a marketplace, not just viral content.
  • Differentiate where Best Buy can't. Niche products, bundles, private-label accessories and value pricing are harder for a big-box retailer to match.
  • Trust signals count. Shoppers will compare your returns and delivery promise with Best Buy's. Clear return policies and fast shipping help.

What to do before late October

  1. Check which of your SKUs overlap with Best Buy's categories and brands.
  2. Confirm creator partners and sample shipments for November content.
  3. Optimize Shop tab listings: keyword-rich titles, clear main images, competitive prices.
  4. Build bundles or exclusive variants that are hard to price-match.
  5. Make sure inventory and delivery times can handle the BFCM window.
Tip: Watch Best Buy's TikTok Shop pricing on overlapping items during the first week of launch, and adjust your offers before Black Friday rather than during it.

百思买入驻 TikTok Shop：对卖家意味着什么

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-04 · 约 5 分钟阅读

美国最大的电子产品零售商之一百思买（Best Buy）将于 2026 年 10 月下旬入驻 TikTok Shop。这再次说明，TikTok Shop 已经从冲动消费的地方，变成大品牌和独立卖家争夺同一批达人和买家的主流渠道。下面整理官方公布的信息，以及对 TikTok Shop 卖家的影响。

百思买公布了什么

  • 时间和品类：百思买在 2026 年 9 月 22 日的公告中表示，将于 10 月下旬上线 TikTok Shop，提供近 1 万款商品，包括耳机、相机、小家电、笔记本电脑和配件，品牌包括 Oura、Bose、戴森和微软等。
  • 购物方式：买家可以通过达人视频、百思买店铺页、商城（Shop tab）和商品详情页下单，由百思买发货，符合条件的订单可到门店或邮寄退货。
  • 达人和广告：百思买表示，其达人计划成员可通过联盟链接推广商品，广告主也可以制作可购物内容并追踪效果。
  • 原因：百思买电商副总裁 Jonathan Greer 表示：“社交媒体不只是在影响人们买什么，更在塑造人们如何发现产品。”

Retail Dive 指出，Ulta Beauty、丝芙兰、Crocs、Pacsun 和 Tarte 等品牌已经在 TikTok Shop 销售，并援引 Emarketer 数据称，2025 年 TikTok Shop 约占社交电商的五分之一。

TikTok Shop 已经有多大

据 Tubefilter 2026 年 8 月 6 日援引 Momentum Works 和 Tabcut 的数据，2026 年上半年 TikTok Shop 全球 GMV 达 503 亿美元，同比增长 92%，全年预计 1,235 亿美元。美国 GMV 为 118 亿美元，美国已超过印尼成为平台最大市场，Net Influencer 也报道了这一数据。

部分细节尚未公开：具体上线日期、百思买是否会大规模投放 TikTok Shop 广告，以及价格是否与其官网一致。上线前后请留意百思买和 TikTok Shop 的官方公告。

我们的解读

  • 3C 和小家电类目更拥挤：如果你卖耳机、充电器、厨房小工具或配件，10 月下旬起，你的类目会出现一个拥有知名品牌、门店退货和大笔达人预算的大零售商，而且正赶上节日需求高峰。
  • 达人费用可能上涨：做联盟推广的零售商会和你争夺同一批达人。现在就把 11 月的达人合作和佣金谈好。
  • 商城和视频一样重要：超过一半的 GMV 来自商城，标题、图片、价格和评价的重要性和传统平台一样，不能只靠爆款视频。
  • 做百思买做不了的差异化：小众产品、组合装、自有品牌配件和高性价比，是大型零售商很难跟进的。
  • 信任感很关键：买家会拿你的退货和配送承诺与百思买比较，清晰的退货政策和快速发货很加分。

10 月下旬前要做的事

  1. 检查哪些 SKU 与百思买的类目和品牌重叠。
  2. 确认 11 月内容的达人合作和寄样安排。
  3. 优化商城 Listing：关键词标题、清晰主图、有竞争力的价格。
  4. 打造难以比价的组合装或专属款。
  5. 确保库存和配送时效能撑住黑五网一。
小提示：上线第一周盯一下百思买在 TikTok Shop 上重叠商品的价格，在黑五之前而不是黑五当天调整你的优惠。
