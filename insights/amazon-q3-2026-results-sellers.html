Amazon Q3 2026: Seller Services Up 9%, Ads Up 11%, Retail Up 2%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-30 · 5 min read

Amazon's latest results show where the money is coming from: seller fees and ads keep growing faster than Amazon's own retail sales. On October 29, 2026, Amazon reported third-quarter net sales of $201.9 billion, up 12%, with third-party seller services up 9% and advertising up 11%. Here is what the numbers say about the marketplace heading into Q4.

The headline numbers

According to Amazon's Q3 2026 earnings release (Oct 29, 2026):

  • Net sales rose 12% to $201.9 billion from $180.2 billion (13% excluding currency effects).
  • Operating income was $28.1 billion, up from $17.4 billion; Amazon called its 13.9% operating margin its highest ever.
  • Net income was $20.7 billion, or $1.89 per diluted share, down from $21.2 billion, mainly because Q3 2025 included $9.5 billion of pre-tax gains on Anthropic investments.
  • Segments: North America sales up 6% to $112.9 billion; International up 5% to $43.1 billion; AWS up 39% to $46.0 billion.
  • Prime Day timing: Prime Day ran in Q2 for most large countries this year versus Q3 last year. Amazon says growth "would have been approximately 400 basis points higher" without that shift.

Seller fees and ads vs Amazon's own retail

The revenue breakdown in the release shows third-party seller services, which mainly reflect commissions plus fulfillment and shipping fees paid by sellers, at $46.5 billion, up 9%. Advertising services reached $19.6 billion, up 11%. Amazon's own online store sales grew just 2% to $68.5 billion.

Ad growth was the slowest in years: TheDesk (Oct 2026) notes that Amazon's ad revenue had regularly grown more than 20% in earlier quarters, and Amazon partly blamed the Prime Day timing. The company expects advertising growth to return to the "high teens" in Q4.

Q4 outlook and other highlights

  • Q4 guidance: net sales of $240.0–247.0 billion (up 12–16%) and operating income of $28.5–33.5 billion.
  • Delivery speed: Amazon reported its fastest Q3 speeds ever for Prime members, with more than 1.5 billion items arriving same-day or overnight in the quarter.
  • Seller tools: the release highlights Seller Assistant workflows for restocking and pricing, and multichannel tools in Seller Central for managing listings, orders and profitability across channels.
  • AI shopping: Amazon says Alexa for Shopping was used by more than 400 million customers in the last 12 months.
  • Holiday staffing: plans to hire more than 300,000 seasonal and other US operations workers.

CEO Andy Jassy said "broader selection and speedier delivery are helping us capture more retail market segment share with improving margins."

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Third-party sellers are a growth engine. Seller services grew more than four times faster than Amazon's own online store sales. The marketplace is where Amazon is adding selection, which also means more competition for every listing.

Ad costs may rise in Q4. If advertising growth returns to the high teens as Amazon expects, competition for clicks around Black Friday and Cyber Monday will be intense. Set budgets and bid caps per campaign and watch ACoS daily.

Speed sets expectations. With 1.5 billion same-day or overnight items in a quarter, shoppers expect fast delivery. FBA and well-placed inventory matter more than ever in Q4.

Use the new tools, carefully. Seller Assistant and multichannel tools can save time, but check automated pricing and restock decisions against your margins.

Q4 checklist

  1. Confirm FBA inventory for Black Friday week is received.
  2. Set Q4 ad budgets with daily caps and clear ACoS targets.
  3. Review fees on your top SKUs; seller fees are a growing share of Amazon's revenue.
  4. Check delivery speed badges on your listings and fix slow offers.
  5. Test Seller Assistant suggestions on a few SKUs before applying them widely.

亚马逊 2026 年三季报：卖家服务增 9%、广告增 11%、自营仅增 2%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-30 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊最新财报说明了钱从哪里来：卖家服务费和广告的增速，持续快于亚马逊自营零售。2026 年 10 月 29 日，亚马逊公布第三季度净销售额 2,019 亿美元，增长 12%，其中第三方卖家服务增长 9%，广告增长 11%。下面看看这些数字对 Q4 平台走势意味着什么。

核心数据

据亚马逊 2026 年第三季度财报（2026 年 10 月 29 日）：

  • 净销售额从 1,802 亿美元增长 12% 至 2,019 亿美元（剔除汇率影响增长 13%）。
  • 营业利润 281 亿美元，去年同期为 174 亿美元；亚马逊称 13.9% 的营业利润率创历史新高。
  • 净利润 207 亿美元，摊薄每股 1.89 美元，低于去年同期的 212 亿美元，主要因为 2025 年第三季度含有 95 亿美元 Anthropic 投资税前收益。
  • 分部：北美销售额增长 6% 至 1,129 亿美元；国际增长 5% 至 431 亿美元；AWS 增长 39% 至 460 亿美元。
  • Prime Day 时间：今年多数大市场的 Prime Day 在第二季度，去年在第三季度。亚马逊称若剔除这一因素，增速"约高 400 个基点"。

卖家费用和广告 vs 自营零售

财报的营收拆分显示，第三方卖家服务（主要是卖家支付的佣金及配送、运输费用）为 465 亿美元，增长 9%；广告服务 196 亿美元，增长 11%；亚马逊自营线上商店仅增长 2%，为 685 亿美元。

广告增速是多年来最慢的：据 TheDesk（2026 年 10 月），亚马逊广告收入此前多个季度增速都超过 20%，公司部分归因于 Prime Day 时间调整，并预计第四季度广告增速将回到"十几个百分点的高位"。

Q4 展望和其他亮点

  • Q4 指引：净销售额 2,400–2,470 亿美元（增长 12–16%），营业利润 285–335 亿美元。
  • 配送速度：亚马逊称 Prime 会员第三季度配送速度创同期最快，当季超过 15 亿件商品当日或次日送达。
  • 卖家工具：财报提到 Seller Assistant 自动处理补货和定价，以及 Seller Central 多渠道管理工具，可跨渠道管理 Listing、订单和利润。
  • AI 购物：亚马逊称过去 12 个月有超过 4 亿顾客使用 Alexa 购物功能。
  • 假日招聘：计划在美国运营网络招聘超过 30 万人。

CEO Andy Jassy 表示："更丰富的选品和更快的配送正帮助我们在利润率提升的同时获得更多零售市场份额。"

我们的解读

第三方卖家是增长引擎。卖家服务的增速是自营线上商店的四倍多。亚马逊正通过平台扩充选品，这也意味着每个 Listing 面对的竞争更多。

Q4 广告成本可能上升。如果广告增速如亚马逊预期回到高位，黑五网一期间的点击竞争会非常激烈。按广告活动设置预算和出价上限，每天盯 ACoS。

速度决定预期。一个季度 15 亿件当日/次日达，消费者对配送速度的期待已经被拉高。Q4 的 FBA 和库存布局比以往更重要。

新工具要谨慎用。Seller Assistant 和多渠道工具能省时间，但自动定价和补货建议要对照利润核查。

Q4 清单

  1. 确认黑五周的 FBA 库存已签收上架。
  2. 设置带每日上限和明确 ACoS 目标的 Q4 广告预算。
  3. 复核主力 SKU 的各项费用——卖家费用在亚马逊营收中的占比在提高。
  4. 检查 Listing 的配送时效标签，优化慢速报价。
  5. 先在少量 SKU 上测试 Seller Assistant 建议，再大范围应用。
