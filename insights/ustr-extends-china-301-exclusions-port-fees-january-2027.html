USTR Extends 178 China Tariff Exclusions and Ship-Fee Pause to January 2027

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-09 · 5 min read

Two China trade deadlines that importers have been watching all fall were pushed back on the day they were due. On November 9, 2026, the US Trade Representative extended all 178 remaining Section 301 tariff exclusions for Chinese products and kept port fees on Chinese-built and Chinese-operated ships suspended, both into January 2027. This is a follow-up to our Section 301 exclusions article (Oct 18) and our port-fee proposal article (Oct 31).

Decision 1: 178 tariff exclusions extended to January 9, 2027

According to USTR's notice (FR Doc. 2026-22925, filed Nov 9, 2026), the Trade Representative "determined that it is appropriate to further extend all 178 current exclusions," citing "ongoing discussions with China."

  • New end date: the extension covers goods entered on or after 12:01 a.m. ET on November 10, 2026 and before 11:59 p.m. ET on January 9, 2027.
  • What's covered: 164 general exclusions plus 14 exclusions for certain solar manufacturing equipment, under HTSUS headings 9903.88.69 and 9903.88.70.
  • Scope: each exclusion is limited to the 10-digit HTSUS numbers and product descriptions in the relevant US notes, and is available for any product that meets the description.
  • Implementation: US Customs and Border Protection will issue entry guidance.

The notice traces how the list shrank: in December 2023 USTR reviewed 352 reinstated and 77 COVID-related exclusions, extended 164 of them in May 2024, and added 14 solar exclusions in September 2024.

TechTimes (Nov 9, 2026) notes that, unless USTR acts again, entries after January 9 pay the full Section 301 duty. Caixin Global (Nov 9, 2026) also reported the extension of the 178 categories.

Decision 2: China ship fees stay suspended to January 10, 2027

In a separate notice (FR Doc. 2026-22867), USTR finalized its October proposal: the maritime action is "further suspended through 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on January 9, 2027." During the suspension, no party owes the port fees in Annexes I, II and III or the Annex V.A duties on ship-to-shore cranes and other cargo-handling equipment.

USTR said it received about 60 unique comments. Supporters said a further suspension "would avoid increased shipping costs and prevent commercial disruptions"; opponents argued the fees are needed to encourage investment in US shipbuilding.

What it means for importers (our analysis)

No change at the border on November 10. If your products use one of the 178 exclusions, keep claiming it; the duty savings continue for two more months.

Another short window. Two months is brief. If you import excluded goods, decide whether to bring forward shipments that can clear before January 9, while weighing storage costs.

Ocean costs avoid a shock, for now. With port fees suspended, carriers have no new US port-fee charges to pass on through early January. The question returns ahead of Chinese New Year shipping.

Watch early January. Both measures now share one deadline. Expect the next decision in the first days of January 2027, tied to US–China talks.

Checklist for China importers

  1. Confirm whether any of your HTS codes fall under the 178 exclusions; see how to find your HTS code.
  2. Ask your broker to keep filing the exclusion headings correctly through January 9.
  3. Model costs with and without the exclusion for Q1 2027 shipments.
  4. Check ocean quotes for any surcharge language tied to port fees.
  5. Set a reminder for early January to check USTR's next notice.

USTR 将 178 项对华关税排除和船舶港口费暂停延至 2027 年 1 月

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-09 · 约 5 分钟阅读

进口商整个秋天都在盯的两个对华贸易截止日，在到期当天被推后了。2026 年 11 月 9 日，美国贸易代表办公室（USTR）宣布延长全部 178 项对华 301 关税排除，并继续暂停对中国建造和运营船舶的港口费，两项都延至 2027 年 1 月。本文是我们301 关税排除文章（10 月 18 日）和港口费延期提议文章（10 月 31 日）的后续。

决定一：178 项关税排除延至 2027 年 1 月 9 日

据 USTR 公告（FR Doc. 2026-22925，2026 年 11 月 9 日提交），贸易代表"认为进一步延长全部 178 项现行排除是适当的"，理由是"与中国的讨论仍在进行"。

  • 新截止日：适用于美东时间 2026 年 11 月 10 日 0:01 起、2027 年 1 月 9 日 23:59 前报关的货物。
  • 覆盖范围：164 项一般排除加 14 项光伏制造设备排除，对应 HTSUS 税号 9903.88.69 和 9903.88.70。
  • 适用规则：每项排除以相关美国注释中的 10 位 HTSUS 编码和商品描述为准，符合描述的商品均可适用。
  • 执行：美国海关与边境保护局（CBP）将发布报关指引。

公告回顾了清单的缩减过程：2023 年 12 月 USTR 审议了 352 项恢复排除和 77 项新冠相关排除，2024 年 5 月延长其中 164 项，2024 年 9 月又新增 14 项光伏排除。

TechTimes（2026 年 11 月 9 日）指出，除非 USTR 再次行动，1 月 9 日之后报关的货物将按全额 301 关税缴纳。财新国际（2026 年 11 月 9 日）也报道了 178 类商品排除获延期。

决定二：中国船舶港口费继续暂停至 2027 年 1 月 10 日

在另一份公告（FR Doc. 2026-22867）中，USTR 确认了 10 月的提议：海运相关措施"进一步暂停至美东时间 2027 年 1 月 9 日 23:59"。暂停期间，任何一方都无需缴纳附件 I、II、III 中的港口费，以及附件 V.A 中针对岸桥和其他装卸设备的关税。

USTR 称共收到约 60 份不同意见。支持者认为继续暂停"可避免运费上涨、防止商业中断"；反对者则认为收费有助于推动美国造船业投资。

我们的解读

11 月 10 日口岸不会有变化。如果你的产品适用这 178 项排除之一，继续申报即可，关税优惠再延两个月。

又是一个短窗口。两个月很短。进口排除类商品的，可以考虑把能在 1 月 9 日前清关的货提前出运，同时权衡仓储成本。

海运成本暂时躲过冲击。港口费继续暂停，船公司在 1 月初之前没有新的美国港口费可转嫁。这个问题会在春节出货前再次出现。

盯紧 1 月初。两项措施现在是同一个截止日，预计下一次决定会在 2027 年 1 月初，并与中美谈判挂钩。

对华进口商清单

  1. 确认你的 HTS 编码是否属于这 178 项排除，参见如何查找 HTS 编码。
  2. 请报关行在 1 月 9 日前继续正确申报排除税号。
  3. 按有无排除两种情况测算 2027 年第一季度到货成本。
  4. 检查海运报价中与港口费相关的附加费条款。
  5. 设好 1 月初提醒，查看 USTR 下一份公告。
