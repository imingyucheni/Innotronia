Amazon Adds Prime Delivery to Merchant Websites and Cuts MCF Fees up to 25%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-01 · 5 min read

Amazon wants to fulfill more of your orders, not just the ones placed on Amazon. On September 24, 2026, it announced that merchants using Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) can offer Prime delivery on their own websites at no extra cost, plus a new pricing program that cuts fulfillment fees by 15–25% for six months. For brands that sell on Shopify, other marketplaces or social channels, this changes the math on where to keep inventory.

What Amazon announced

  • Prime delivery on your own site. According to Amazon's announcement, new and existing MCF merchants selling in the US can add "fast, free Prime delivery" to their websites at no cost beyond standard MCF fees. The Prime badge appears on product pages, Amazon verifies the shopper's Prime membership at checkout, and the order confirmation comes from the merchant.
  • How to turn it on. Merchants can enable it through the Amazon MCF and Buy with Prime app for Shopify. Amazon says Selling Partner API integrations through partners including Fulfil, LingXing and WebBee are coming, and that setup is about 70% faster on average than standalone Buy with Prime.
  • MCF Preferred Pricing Program. FBA sellers can save 15–25% on fulfillment fees for orders from websites, other marketplaces and social channels during their first six months. Amazon says the program combines MCF discounts with FBA credits per unit shipped, has no contract, uses one-click enrollment in Seller Central, and gives deeper discounts at higher volume.
  • Merchant-owned service. MarketScale (Sept 26, 2026) notes that, unlike Buy with Prime, this MCF route lets merchants keep their own checkout, returns and customer service.

Amazon also shared early results:

One early user, Skinnies Instant Lifts, told Amazon it saw "a nearly 10% increase in sales" after enabling Prime delivery. The Metaverse Street Journal also reported Amazon's figures that US sellers using both MCF and FBA see 19% lower out-of-stock rates. These are Amazon's own figures and have not been independently verified.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • One inventory pool, more channels. If your stock already sits in FBA, you can fulfill website and marketplace orders from it with a Prime promise, instead of keeping a separate 3PL stock for each channel.
  • The badge can lift conversion. Shoppers know Prime delivery. A trusted delivery promise on a smaller brand's site can matter more than on Amazon itself.
  • Read the six-month window carefully. The 15–25% savings apply to the first six months. Model your costs both with and without the discount before moving all channels.
  • Check marketplace rules. Some marketplaces have their own rules on packaging or carrier branding for orders fulfilled by a third party. Confirm before routing their orders through MCF.
  • Concentration risk. Putting every channel's inventory with one provider is convenient but adds dependency. Keep a backup plan for peak season and account issues.

Checklist: should you try it?

  1. List your non-Amazon order volume by channel and average order size.
  2. Compare current fulfillment cost per order with MCF fees, with and without the Preferred Pricing discount.
  3. Check delivery speed your customers get today vs a Prime promise.
  4. Test on your own site first with a few top SKUs through the Shopify app.
  5. Track conversion and returns for 4–6 weeks before expanding.
  6. Plan inventory so Amazon and off-Amazon demand don't run your FBA stock out before Black Friday.
Tip: Enroll in the pricing program when you are ready to ship real volume. If the savings window starts at enrollment, starting before your channels are connected wastes part of it. Check the exact terms in Seller Central.

亚马逊开放独立站 Prime 配送，多渠道配送费最高省 25%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-01 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊想帮你配送更多订单，而不只是亚马逊站内的订单。2026 年 9 月 24 日，亚马逊宣布：使用多渠道配送（MCF）的商家，可以在自己的网站上免费提供 Prime 配送；同时推出新的定价计划，前六个月配送费可节省 15%–25%。对同时在 Shopify、其他平台或社交渠道销售的品牌来说，库存放在哪里的算法变了。

亚马逊公布了什么

  • 独立站可用 Prime 配送：根据亚马逊官方公告，在美国销售的新老 MCF 商家，都可以在自己的网站上提供“快速、免费的 Prime 配送”，除标准 MCF 费用外不再额外收费。商品页会显示 Prime 标识，结账时由亚马逊验证会员身份，订单确认邮件仍由商家发出。
  • 如何开通：可通过 Shopify 上的 Amazon MCF and Buy with Prime 应用开通。亚马逊表示，Fulfil、领星（LingXing）、WebBee 等合作伙伴的 SP-API 对接即将上线，开通速度平均比单独使用 Buy with Prime 快约 70%。
  • MCF 优惠定价计划：FBA 卖家在前六个月内，来自独立站、其他平台和社交渠道的订单配送费可节省 15%–25%。亚马逊称该计划结合了 MCF 折扣和按件发放的 FBA 返点，无需签约，在卖家后台一键报名，发货量越大折扣越深。
  • 商家保留服务主导权：MarketScale（2026 年 9 月 26 日）指出，与 Buy with Prime 不同，这种 MCF 方式让商家保留自己的结账页、退货和客服流程。

亚马逊还公布了早期数据：

早期使用者 Skinnies Instant Lifts 告诉亚马逊，开通 Prime 配送后“销售额增长近 10%”。The Metaverse Street Journal 也报道了亚马逊的数据：同时使用 MCF 和 FBA 的美国卖家断货率低 19%。以上均为亚马逊自己公布的数据，尚未经过独立核实。

我们的解读

  • 一个库存池，多个渠道：如果你的货已经在 FBA，可以直接用它履行独立站和其他平台的订单，并承诺 Prime 时效，不用再为每个渠道单独放一份第三方仓库存。
  • Prime 标识能提升转化：买家熟悉 Prime 配送。对中小品牌独立站来说，一个可信的配送承诺可能比在亚马逊站内更有价值。
  • 注意“前六个月”：15%–25% 的优惠只在前六个月有效。把所有渠道迁过来之前，分别测算有折扣和没折扣两种情况的成本。
  • 核对其他平台规则：部分平台对第三方配送订单的包装或承运商标识有要求，用 MCF 发这些平台的订单前先确认。
  • 集中风险：所有渠道的库存都放在一家服务商很方便，但依赖也更大。为旺季和账号问题准备好备用方案。

要不要试？行动清单

  1. 统计站外订单量：按渠道列出订单数和平均客单价。
  2. 对比单均配送成本：现有方案 vs MCF 费用（分别算有、无优惠定价折扣）。
  3. 对比配送时效：现在买家收到货要多久，和 Prime 承诺比如何。
  4. 先在独立站小范围测试：通过 Shopify 应用挑几个主力 SKU 试点。
  5. 跟踪 4–6 周：关注转化率和退货率，再决定是否扩大。
  6. 规划库存：避免站内外需求叠加，导致 FBA 在黑五前断货。
小提示：等到准备好真正放量时再报名定价计划。如果优惠期从报名时开始计算，渠道还没接好就报名，会白白浪费一部分优惠期。具体条款以卖家后台为准。
