Choosing an agency

What Your E-commerce Agency Report Should Show: ACOS, TACoS and More

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-06 · 6 min read

A good e-commerce agency should make it easy to see whether your money is working. A bad report buries you in screenshots and vanity numbers. Whether you already work with an agency or are choosing one, here are the numbers a monthly report should include, how they are calculated, and the questions to ask when something looks off.

Start with the official definitions

Amazon Ads defines its two core efficiency metrics clearly. According to its ACOS guide:

  • ACOS (advertising cost of sales) = ad spend ÷ ad revenue × 100. Amazon's example: spend $50 and earn $100, and ACOS is 50%.
  • ROAS (return on ad spend) = ad revenue ÷ ad spend. Amazon calls ROAS "the inverse of Amazon ACOS"; in the same example ROAS is 2.

Amazon's ROAS guide adds that the two "are measuring the same results," just in different formats. If your agency reports ROAS and your team thinks in ACOS, convert: ACOS = 1 ÷ ROAS.

The KPIs a monthly report should include

KPIHow it's calculatedWhy it matters

Ad spendTotal paid to the ad platformYour direct marketing cost
Ad-attributed salesSales the ad platform credits to adsShows what ads are directly driving
ACOSAd spend ÷ ad sales × 100 (Amazon Ads)Efficiency of ad spend
ROASAd sales ÷ ad spend (Amazon Ads)Same as ACOS, inverted

A worked example

MonthAd spendAd salesTotal salesACOSTACoS
Month 1$5,000$20,000$40,00025%12.5%
Month 2$6,000$22,000$55,00027.3%10.9%

Calculation: illustrative numbers using the formulas above. ACOS got slightly worse in Month 2, but TACoS improved because total sales grew faster than ad spend. Judging only by ACOS would miss that the ads helped the whole business grow. Ask for both numbers every month, side by side.

Your break-even ACOS equals your profit margin before ad costs. If you make 30% before ads, an ACOS above 30% loses money on ad-attributed sales. Ask your agency to show ACOS against this line for each main product.

Questions to ask about any report

  • Which attribution window and source? Ad platforms credit sales differently; numbers from different tools won't match exactly.
  • Is ad spend net of credits or promotions? Make sure costs aren't understated.
  • What changed and why? A good report explains the main actions taken and their results, not just the numbers.
  • What's next month's plan? Budgets, tests, new products and expected outcomes.
  • How do fees affect the picture? If your agency charges a share of sales, check how that sales figure is defined.

Red flags

  • Only showing impressions, clicks or ROAS, never total sales or profit.
  • Changing which metrics appear from month to month.
  • No access to the raw data or the ad account itself. You should always own the accounts; see how to give an agency safe account access.
  • Explaining every bad month with "the algorithm" and no specific actions.
  • Reporting sales without returns or refunds when your fees or decisions depend on net revenue.

电商代运营月报该看什么：ACOS、TACoS 等关键指标

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-06 · 约 6 分钟阅读

好的电商代运营应该让你一眼看出钱花得值不值；差的报告则用截图和面子数据把你淹没。不论你已经在合作还是正在挑选服务商，本文告诉你月报里应该有哪些数字、怎么计算，以及发现异常时该问什么。

先看官方定义

Amazon Ads 对两个核心效率指标有明确定义。据其 ACOS 指南：

  • ACOS（广告销售成本比）= 广告花费 ÷ 广告销售额 × 100。亚马逊的例子：花 50 美元、带来 100 美元销售，ACOS 为 50%。
  • ROAS（广告支出回报率）= 广告销售额 ÷ 广告花费。亚马逊称 ROAS 是"ACOS 的倒数"，同一例子中 ROAS 为 2。

亚马逊 ROAS 指南补充说，两者"衡量的是同一结果"，只是表达形式不同。如果代运营报 ROAS 而你习惯看 ACOS，换算即可：ACOS = 1 ÷ ROAS。

月报应包含的 KPI

KPI计算方式为什么重要
总销售额周期内全部销售，含广告和自然单生意真正依赖的数字
广告花费付给广告平台的总额直接营销成本
广告归因销售额广告平台归因给广告的销售看广告直接带来了多少
ACOS广告花费 ÷ 广告销售额 × 100（Amazon Ads）广告花费效率
ROAS广告销售额 ÷ 广告花费（Amazon Ads）与 ACOS 互为倒数
TACoS（行业术语）广告花费 ÷ 总销售额 × 100看广告是在带动整体增长，还是只在"买"销量
自然单占比（总销售额 − 广告销售额）÷ 总销售额健康的增长通常会逐步提高这一比例
贡献利润销售额 − 产品成本 − 平台费用 − 广告费 − 运费增长是否赚钱
库存健康可售天数、断货、库龄断货的商品广告卖不出去

来源：ACOS 和 ROAS 公式来自 Amazon Ads；其他为通用计算方法。代运营应在报告中写明每个指标的口径。

计算示例

月份广告花费广告销售额总销售额ACOSTACoS
第 1 月5,000 美元20,000 美元40,000 美元25%12.5%
第 2 月6,000 美元22,000 美元55,000 美元27.3%10.9%

计算说明：用上述公式套用示例数字。第 2 月 ACOS 略有变差，但 TACoS 改善了，因为总销售额增长快于广告花费。只看 ACOS，就会忽略广告其实带动了整盘生意的增长。每个月都要求两个指标并排展示。

保本 ACOS 等于广告前利润率。广告前利润率 30% 时，ACOS 超过 30% 的广告单就是亏钱的。请代运营按主力商品展示 ACOS 与保本线的对比。

看报告时该问的问题

  • 归因窗口和数据来源是什么？不同广告平台归因方式不同，不同工具的数据不会完全一致。
  • 广告花费是否扣除了抵扣额或促销？确保成本没有被低估。
  • 变了什么、为什么？好的报告会解释主要动作和结果，而不只是罗列数字。
  • 下个月计划是什么？预算、测试、新品和预期结果。
  • 费用如何影响结果？如果代运营按销售额抽成，要看清这个销售额的口径。

危险信号

  • 只给曝光、点击或 ROAS，从不给总销售额或利润。
  • 每个月展示的指标都在变。
  • 你看不到原始数据或广告账户本身。账户应始终归你所有，参见如何安全地给代运营开通权限。
  • 每个差月份都归咎于"算法"，却说不出具体做了什么。
  • 在费用或决策依赖净销售额时，报告的销售额却不扣退货退款。
