Amazon FBA Costs Explained: Every Fee Type and the New-Seller Credits

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-07 · 6 min read

Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) is how most sellers get the Prime badge, but its costs are spread across several fee types that many new sellers only discover on their first statements. This guide walks through every FBA cost component Amazon lists, the new-seller credits currently on offer, and how to estimate your real cost per unit before you ship inventory.

What FBA is and why sellers use it

According to Amazon's FBA page, FBA "lets you outsource order fulfillment to Amazon and offer customers free, two-day shipping through Prime." Amazon also claims shipping with FBA "costs 70% less per unit than comparable premium options offered by other major US carriers," based on its own comparison. Amazon stores your inventory, picks, packs and ships orders, and handles customer service and returns for FBA orders.

The FBA cost components

Amazon says "costs for FBA depend on the products you sell and the exact services you use." The main components:

CostHow Amazon describes itWhat drives it
Fulfillment feePer unit, "based on the product's price, weight, and dimensions"Size tier, shipping weight, price
Monthly storage"Charged monthly based on the space your inventory occupies," using daily average volume in cubic feetCubic feet, time of year
Aged inventory"Charged monthly for all items stored in a fulfillment center for more than 181 days"Slow sellers, overstock
Returns processing"Charged on orders when Amazon provides a customer with free return shipping"Category, return rate
Inbound placement serviceApplies to "standard and large bulky sized products"How you split inbound shipments
Referral fee (all sellers)A percentage of the sale price by categoryCategory, price

Source: sell.amazon.com FBA and pricing pages (accessed Nov 7, 2026). Exact rates are in Seller Central help pages and change periodically; use Amazon's Revenue Calculator for specific products.

FBA fees come on top of Amazon's referral fees and plan fees. Per Amazon's pricing page:

For a side-by-side with Walmart, see Amazon vs Walmart referral fees; for Walmart's fulfillment equivalent, see Walmart WFS fees.

New-seller FBA credits

Amazon's FBA page lists incentives for new Professional sellers:

  • $100 off shipments using the Amazon Partnered Carrier program.
  • A $200 credit toward fulfillment costs with Amazon Global Logistics.
  • A $400 credit for inbound placement costs.
  • Free storage and customer returns through the FBA New Selection program.

Eligibility rules and time limits apply; check the current terms in Seller Central before relying on them.

Storage costs grow quietly. Send inventory in smaller, more frequent shipments for slow sellers, and watch the 181-day mark so items don't trigger aged inventory charges.

How to estimate your real cost per unit

  1. Measure and weigh the packaged product accurately; size tier drives the fulfillment fee.
  2. Run the product through the Revenue Calculator to get the fulfillment and referral fees.
  3. Estimate monthly storage from cubic feet and expected weeks of cover.
  4. Add inbound shipping and placement costs for your shipment plan.
  5. Add an allowance for returns processing in categories with free returns.
  6. Subtract everything from your selling price to get contribution before ads.

Ways to lower FBA costs

  • Right-size packaging. Dropping a size tier or a weight bracket can save on every unit.
  • Keep inventory lean. Match stock to sell-through to limit storage and aged inventory charges.
  • Plan Q4 early. Storage costs depend on time of year; avoid overstocking late in the season.
  • Use the credits. New sellers should plan first shipments to use the available credits.
  • Review returns. High return rates add processing costs; fix listing and quality issues behind them.
  • Recheck after fee updates. Amazon revises FBA fees periodically, so rerun the calculator for top SKUs whenever new rates are announced.

亚马逊 FBA 费用详解：各项费用构成与新卖家补贴

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-07 · 约 6 分钟阅读

大多数卖家靠亚马逊物流（FBA）拿到 Prime 标识，但 FBA 的成本分散在好几种费用里，很多新卖家要等看到第一份结算单才发现。本文逐项梳理亚马逊列出的 FBA 费用构成、目前的新卖家补贴，以及发货前如何估算真实的单件成本。

FBA 是什么、卖家为什么用

据 亚马逊 FBA 官方页面，FBA"让你把订单履约外包给亚马逊，并通过 Prime 为顾客提供免费两日达"。亚马逊还称，按其自身比较，使用 FBA 的"单件成本比其他美国主要承运商的同类高端服务低 70%"。亚马逊负责存储库存、拣货、打包、发货，并处理 FBA 订单的客服和退货。

FBA 费用构成

亚马逊表示"FBA 成本取决于你销售的商品和使用的具体服务"。主要构成：

费用亚马逊的描述影响因素
配送费按件收取，"基于商品价格、重量和尺寸"尺寸分段、发货重量、售价
月度仓储费"按库存占用的空间每月收取"，以日均立方英尺计算体积、季节
库龄附加费"对在运营中心存放超过 181 天的所有商品每月收取"滞销品、库存过多
退货处理费"在亚马逊为顾客提供免费退货运输的订单上收取"品类、退货率
入库配置服务费适用于"标准尺寸和大件商品"入库货件的拆分方式
销售佣金（所有卖家）按品类收取售价的一定比例品类、售价

来源：sell.amazon.com FBA 页面和费用页面（2026 年 11 月 7 日访问）。具体费率见卖家后台帮助页面且会定期调整，单个商品请用亚马逊收入计算器测算。

销售佣金和销售计划

FBA 费用之外，还有亚马逊销售佣金和计划费用。据亚马逊费用页面：

项目亚马逊公布费率
专业计划每月 39.99 美元
个人计划每售出一件 0.99 美元
家居厨房佣金15%
玩具游戏佣金15%
消费电子佣金8%
最低佣金（多数品类）0.30 美元

与 Walmart 的对比见亚马逊与 Walmart 佣金对比；Walmart 的仓配服务费用见Walmart WFS 费用。

新卖家 FBA 补贴

亚马逊 FBA 页面列出了面向新专业卖家的激励：

  • 使用亚马逊合作承运人发货立减 100 美元。
  • 使用 Amazon Global Logistics 可获 200 美元配送费抵扣。
  • 入库配置费抵扣 400 美元。
  • 通过 FBA 新品计划（New Selection）享受免费仓储和顾客退货。

补贴有资格条件和时限，使用前请在卖家后台核实最新条款。

仓储费会悄悄变多。慢销品分小批、多频次补货，并留意 181 天节点，避免产生库龄附加费。

如何估算真实单件成本

  1. 准确测量和称重带包装的商品，尺寸分段决定配送费。
  2. 用收入计算器测算配送费和佣金。
  3. 按体积和预计可售周数估算月度仓储费。
  4. 加上头程运费和入库配置费用。
  5. 免费退货品类要预留退货处理费。
  6. 用售价减去以上全部，得到广告前的贡献利润。

降低 FBA 成本的方法

  • 优化包装尺寸。降一个尺寸分段或重量档，每件都能省钱。
  • 保持库存精简。按动销备货，减少仓储费和库龄附加费。
  • 提前规划 Q4。仓储费随季节变化，旺季后期别压太多货。
  • 用好补贴。新卖家规划首批货件时把可用补贴算进去。
  • 关注退货。高退货率会增加处理费，从 Listing 和质量上找原因。
  • 费率调整后重算。亚马逊会定期调整 FBA 费用，每次公布新费率后，用计算器重算主力 SKU。
