eBay and Etsy Q3 2026: eBay US Volume Up 27%, Etsy Take Rate Hits 26.2%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-01 · 5 min read

Amazon and Walmart get most of the attention, but eBay and Etsy are still sizable US channels, and both reported third-quarter results on October 28, 2026. eBay's US sales volume jumped 27%, helped by its Depop acquisition, while Etsy returned to buyer growth for the first time in two years. Here is what the numbers show for sellers looking beyond the two biggest marketplaces.

eBay: strong US growth, boosted by Depop

According to eBay's Q3 2026 earnings release (Oct 28, 2026):

  • GMV (gross merchandise volume) was $23.2 billion, up 15% as reported and 16% on an FX-neutral basis.
  • US GMV rose 27% to $12.5 billion; international GMV grew 4% to $10.6 billion.
  • Revenue was $3.2 billion, up 14%.
  • Active buyers reached 147 million, up 10%, but only 1% excluding the Depop and Tise acquisitions.
  • eBay Live is generating GMV "at a multibillion-dollar annualized run rate," with growth "well into the triple digits," and the AI-powered Magical Listing tool expanded to all new and reactivated listers in the US and five other markets.
  • Q4 guidance: GMV of $23.7–24.1 billion, up 12–14% FX-neutral.

Value Added Resource (Oct 28, 2026) points out that Depop, acquired July 30, 2026, added nearly three percentage points to currency-adjusted GMV growth, and that GMV growth excluding acquisitions slowed from 14% in Q2 to 13% in Q3. It also reports that eBay is cutting about 9% of its workforce, with $130–150 million of restructuring costs expected, and that some sellers have complained about the forced rollout of Magical Listing.

Etsy: modest growth, higher take rate

According to Etsy's Q3 2026 shareholder letter (Oct 28, 2026):

  • GMS (gross merchandise sales) for the Etsy marketplace was $2.6 billion, up 8.8% (9.1% currency-neutral).
  • Revenue rose 10.9% to $692 million.
  • Take rate rose 50 basis points to 26.2%, "primarily driven by further enhancements to Etsy Ads."
  • Active buyers were about 88 million, up 1%, the first year-over-year growth in two years.
  • Q4 outlook: GMS of $3.46–3.52 billion, up 5–7%.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

eBay's US marketplace is growing again. Even excluding acquisitions, 13% GMV growth is solid. Value Added Resource reports that eBay's focus categories grew 24% and that global consumer-to-consumer GMV grew more than 20%, so recommerce and enthusiast categories are where the momentum is. Brands with refurbished, open-box or collectible-adjacent products should take a fresh look at the US marketplace.

Etsy is more expensive than it looks. A 26.2% take rate means sellers, on average, pay over a quarter of sales to Etsy in fees and ads. Price accordingly, and track ad spend closely.

Live selling is spreading. eBay Live joins TikTok Shop in showing that live video drives sales. If you already sell live on TikTok, eBay Live may be worth testing.

Diversification still matters. Compare these growth rates with Amazon's Q3 results: Amazon's third-party seller services grew 9%. A second or third channel spreads risk, but only if you can manage inventory and listings across them.

Seller checklist

  1. Check whether your products fit eBay's recommerce and enthusiast categories.
  2. Calculate your all-in fee rate on each marketplace, including ads.
  3. Test eBay Live or Etsy Ads on a small set of products before scaling.
  4. Review AI-generated listing details for accuracy before publishing.
  5. Use a single inventory system to avoid overselling across channels.

eBay 与 Etsy 2026 三季报：eBay 美国交易额增 27%，Etsy 抽佣率达 26.2%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-01 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊和 Walmart 吸引了大部分目光，但 eBay 和 Etsy 仍是不小的美国渠道。两家都在 2026 年 10 月 28 日发布了第三季度业绩：受收购 Depop 带动，eBay 美国交易额大涨 27%；Etsy 则两年来首次实现买家数增长。下面看看这些数据对想在两大平台之外拓展渠道的卖家意味着什么。

eBay：美国增长强劲，Depop 助推

据 eBay 2026 年第三季度财报（2026 年 10 月 28 日）：

  • GMV（商品交易总额）232 亿美元，按报告口径增长 15%，剔除汇率影响增长 16%。
  • 美国 GMV 增长 27% 至 125 亿美元；国际 GMV 增长 4% 至 106 亿美元。
  • 营收 32 亿美元，增长 14%。
  • 活跃买家 1.47 亿，增长 10%；剔除 Depop 和 Tise 两项收购后仅增长 1%。
  • eBay Live 的 GMV 已达"数十亿美元年化规模"，同比增长"远超三位数"；AI 驱动的 Magical Listing 已向美国等六个市场的所有新卖家和回流卖家开放。
  • Q4 指引：GMV 237–241 亿美元，剔除汇率影响增长 12–14%。

Value Added Resource（2026 年 10 月 28 日）指出，2026 年 7 月 30 日收购的 Depop 为剔除汇率后的 GMV 增长贡献了近 3 个百分点；剔除收购后，GMV 增速从第二季度的 14% 放缓至第三季度的 13%。该文还报道 eBay 正裁员约 9%，预计重组成本 1.3–1.5 亿美元，并有卖家抱怨 Magical Listing 被强制推广。

Etsy：温和增长，抽佣率上升

据 Etsy 2026 年第三季度致股东信（2026 年 10 月 28 日）：

  • GMS（商品销售总额，仅 Etsy 主平台）26 亿美元，增长 8.8%（剔除汇率 9.1%）。
  • 营收增长 10.9% 至 6.92 亿美元。
  • 抽佣率（take rate）提高 50 个基点至 26.2%，"主要来自 Etsy Ads 的进一步优化"。
  • 活跃买家约 8,800 万，增长 1%，两年来首次同比增长。
  • Q4 展望：GMS 34.6–35.2 亿美元，增长 5–7%。

我们的解读

eBay 美国站重回增长。剔除收购后 13% 的 GMV 增速也算稳健。据 Value Added Resource，eBay 重点品类增长 24%，全球个人对个人（C2C）GMV 增长超过 20%，二手循环和爱好者品类是增长所在。有翻新、开箱或收藏相关产品的品牌，值得重新评估 eBay 美国站。

Etsy 比看起来更贵。26.2% 的抽佣率意味着卖家平均要把超过四分之一的销售额以佣金和广告形式交给 Etsy。定价时要算进去，并密切跟踪广告花费。

直播带货在扩散。eBay Live 和 TikTok Shop 一样证明了直播能带货。已在 TikTok 做直播的卖家，可以试试 eBay Live。

多渠道布局仍然重要。对比亚马逊三季报：亚马逊第三方卖家服务增长 9%。第二、第三渠道能分散风险，但前提是你能跨平台管理库存和 Listing。

卖家清单

  1. 看看你的产品是否契合 eBay 的二手循环和爱好者品类。
  2. 计算各平台含广告在内的综合费率。
  3. 先在少量商品上测试 eBay Live 或 Etsy Ads，再扩大。
  4. 发布前核对 AI 生成的 Listing 信息是否准确。
  5. 用统一的库存系统，避免多渠道超卖。
