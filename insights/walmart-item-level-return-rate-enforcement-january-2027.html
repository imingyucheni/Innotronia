Walmart Will Enforce Return Rate by Item Starting January 2027

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-13 · 5 min read

Walmart is about to judge returns item by item, not just account by account. On November 6, 2026, Walmart Marketplace posted a release note saying that from January 2027, individual items with high seller-accountable return rates may be warned or temporarily unpublished, even if the seller's overall Return Rate is within the standard. With holiday returns arriving in January, the timing matters.

What Walmart announced

In its release note “Prepare for item-level Return Rate enforcement” (Nov 6, 2026), Walmart says it "will provide Return Rate metrics at the item level in addition to your overall Return Rate." It explains that this "allows Walmart to take targeted action rather than automatically affecting your entire catalog," and states: "Beginning in January 2027, items with high rates of seller-accountable returns may receive a warning or be temporarily unpublished, even if your overall Return Rate meets the Seller Performance Standard."

Walmart's Return Rate guide, updated the same day, adds that "Item-level actions apply only to items flagged for high returns," while "broader or repeated performance issues may result in additional action under Seller Performance Standards." The change was also reported by Seller Essentials on November 10, which notes that a Walmart email to sellers on November 9 described the timing only as "in the next few months."

The rules in one table

The account-level standard itself is unchanged. Walmart first announced the Return Rate metric in its January 2026 seller newsletter, defining it as the share of delivered items returned "due to a seller-related issue over the past 60 days." Here is how the rules now fit together, according to Walmart's guide:

RuleWhat Walmart says
Overall Return Rate, new inventory6% or below
Overall Return Rate, Resold inventory9% or below
Measurement windowItems delivered in the past 60 days
What countsSeller-accountable returns: defective, damaged, incorrect item, arrived late
Item-level action (from January 2027)Warning or temporary unpublishing of the flagged item
Item-level thresholdNot published by Walmart
WFS itemsWalmart says most metrics, including Return Rate, are met for you

Note what is missing: Walmart has not said what item-level rate counts as "high." Treat any specific number you see elsewhere as a guess until Walmart publishes one.

Why returns deserve attention now

Returns are a bigger issue online than in stores. According to the NRF and Happy Returns 2025 Retail Returns Landscape (Oct 15, 2025), retailers expected 15.8% of 2025 sales ($849.9 billion) to be returned, compared with 16.9% in 2024. They estimated that 19.3% of online sales would be returned and expected 17% of holiday sales to come back.

Most of those returns are not the seller's fault (for example, "changed my mind"), and Walmart counts only seller-accountable reasons. But January is when holiday returns land, which is exactly when the new item-level enforcement begins.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • A good account average no longer protects every SKU. One problem item can be unpublished while the rest of your catalog is fine. Large catalogs with a few weak products are most exposed.
  • Bestsellers carry the most risk. If a top seller is unpublished during January, the revenue loss is far larger than the return cost itself.
  • WFS sellers have less to worry about. Walmart's guide says that with Walmart Fulfillment Services, most metrics including Return Rate are met for you. Seller-fulfilled items need the most work.
  • Listings are part of returns control. "Incorrect item" and "not as described" returns often start with wrong photos, sizes or UPCs.

Checklist before January

  1. In Seller Center, go to Returns and select View returns insight to see each item's Return Rate, units returned and top return reasons.
  2. Sort by units returned and fix the top 10 items first: photos, size charts, specs and compatibility notes.
  3. Check UPC-to-product matching for any "incorrect item" returns.
  4. Upgrade packaging for items with "damaged" returns and review carriers with repeated handling problems.
  5. Review lag times and transit settings so "arrived late" returns don't pile up during peak.
  6. Remember that returns older than 60 days stop counting, so fixes made now take time to show in your rate.
  7. Confirm your return settings match the Walmart returns policy and our seller performance standards guide.

沃尔玛 2027 年 1 月起按单品考核退货率：高退货商品或被下架

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-13 · 约 5 分钟阅读

沃尔玛即将按单品考核退货，而不再只看账户整体。2026 年 11 月 6 日，沃尔玛 Marketplace 发布更新说明：从 2027 年 1 月起，卖家责任退货率高的单个商品可能会被警告或临时下架，即使卖家的整体退货率达标也不例外。假日季的退货恰好集中在 1 月，这个时间点很关键。

沃尔玛宣布了什么

在 更新说明《Prepare for item-level Return Rate enforcement》（2026 年 11 月 6 日） 中，沃尔玛表示除了整体退货率，还将提供单品层面的退货率数据，以便"有针对性地采取措施，而不是自动影响你的整个商品目录"。原文明确写道：从 2027 年 1 月起，卖家责任退货率高的商品可能收到警告或被临时下架，即使整体退货率符合卖家绩效标准。

沃尔玛同日更新的 退货率指南 补充说明：单品层面的处理只针对被标记为高退货的商品；但范围更广或反复出现的绩效问题，仍可能按卖家绩效标准受到进一步处理。Seller Essentials 11 月 10 日 也报道了这一变化，并提到沃尔玛 11 月 9 日发给卖家的邮件只说"未来几个月内"实施。

一张表看懂规则

账户层面的标准本身没有变。沃尔玛在 2026 年 1 月卖家通讯 中首次公布退货率指标，定义为过去 60 天内因卖家原因被退回的已送达商品占比。根据沃尔玛指南，现在的规则如下：

规则沃尔玛官方说法
账户整体退货率（全新商品）不高于 6%
账户整体退货率（Resold 二手 / 翻新商品）不高于 9%
统计周期过去 60 天内送达的商品
计入范围卖家责任退货：有缺陷、破损、发错货、送达延迟
单品层面处理（2027 年 1 月起）对被标记的商品发出警告或临时下架
单品"高退货率"阈值沃尔玛尚未公布
WFS 商品沃尔玛表示大部分指标（含退货率）视为达标

需要注意：沃尔玛还没有说明单品退货率多高才算"高"。在官方公布之前，看到的任何具体数字都只是猜测。

为什么现在要重视退货

线上退货问题比线下更突出。根据 NRF 与 Happy Returns《2025 零售退货报告》（2025 年 10 月 15 日），零售商预计 2025 年销售额的 15.8%（8,499 亿美元）会被退回，2024 年为 16.9%；线上销售的退货比例预计为 19.3%，假日季销售预计有 17% 被退回。

这些退货大部分并非卖家责任（例如"买家改变主意"），而沃尔玛只统计卖家责任退货。但 1 月正是假日退货集中到来的时候，也正是单品考核开始执行的时候。

我们的解读

  • 账户平均值好看，不代表每个 SKU 都安全。一个问题商品可能被下架，而其他商品一切正常。SKU 多、但有几款质量偏弱的卖家风险最大。
  • 爆款风险最高。如果主力款在 1 月被下架，损失的销售额远大于退货本身的成本。
  • WFS 卖家压力较小。沃尔玛指南表示，使用沃尔玛仓配（WFS）时，包括退货率在内的大部分指标视为达标；自发货商品最需要优化。
  • listing 质量就是退货管控的一部分。"发错货""与描述不符"类退货，很多源于图片、尺码或 UPC 不准确。

1 月前的行动清单

  1. 在 Seller Center 进入 Returns，点击 View returns insight，查看每个商品的退货率、退货件数和主要退货原因。
  2. 按退货件数排序，先优化前 10 个商品：图片、尺码表、参数和兼容性说明。
  3. 针对"发错货"类退货，核对 UPC 与商品是否对应正确。
  4. "破损"类退货多的商品，升级包装，并排查反复出问题的承运商。
  5. 检查备货时间（lag time）和运输时效设置，避免旺季"送达延迟"退货堆积。
  6. 超过 60 天的退货不再计入，所以现在做的改进需要一段时间才能体现在数据上。
  7. 对照 沃尔玛退货政策 和 沃尔玛卖家绩效标准指南 检查退货设置。
