Wayfair Q3 2026: US Revenue Up 11.4% in a Flat Home-Goods Market

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-07 · 5 min read

Home goods have been one of the hardest categories in US e-commerce since the pandemic boom ended. Wayfair's latest results suggest demand is finding its way back, at least to the biggest players. On November 4, 2026, Wayfair reported its fastest quarterly growth since the pandemic, with US revenue up 11.4% while the overall category stayed roughly flat. For furniture and home-goods factories selling in the US, it is a useful signal.

The Q3 numbers

According to Wayfair's Q3 2026 earnings release (Nov 4, 2026):

  • Total net revenue was $3.42 billion, up 9.7% from $3.12 billion.
  • US net revenue was $3.04 billion, up 11.4%; international fell 2.3% to $380 million.
  • Orders delivered rose 12.2% to 11.0 million; active customers grew 4.7% to 22.2 million.
  • Average order value slipped to $311 from $317.
  • Repeat customers placed 80.0% of orders; mobile accounted for 64.4% of orders.
  • Adjusted EBITDA margin was 6.9%, which Wayfair called "our best Q3 result since 2020."

CEO Niraj Shah said "Q3 was our strongest quarter of growth in the post-pandemic period," adding: "With U.S. revenue growing double digits against a roughly flat category here in Q3, we've done exactly that." Revenue came in about 1.2% above analyst estimates of roughly $3.38 billion, according to 24/7 Wall St. (Nov 4, 2026).

What drove it

Digital Commerce 360 (Nov 6, 2026), reporting on the earnings call, highlights several drivers:

  • AI shopping: Wayfair works with Google, OpenAI and Meta and shares "tens of millions of products" with those platforms. Traffic referred from AI tools more than doubled from the prior quarter but is still about 1% of total traffic.
  • Lower ad dependence: advertising was 9.9% of revenue, its lowest since 2021, while direct-visit revenue grew more than 15%.
  • Creators and loyalty: creator-driven sales grew more than 70% compared with fall 2025's Way Day, and Wayfair Rewards ($29 a year) continues to grow.
  • Physical stores: three large-format US stores are open, a fourth in Denver is scheduled for November 20, and more are planned for 2027.

What it means for home-goods sellers (our analysis)

Demand is shifting to scale players, not growing overall. Wayfair says the category was roughly flat. Growth comes from taking share, so smaller brands need sharper value and better listings rather than expecting a rising tide.

Smaller baskets, more orders. Orders grew faster than revenue as average order value fell. Shoppers are buying, but often lower-priced pieces. Factories should make sure entry-price items are well represented.

Logistics is the battleground. Furniture is bulky and expensive to ship. Whether you sell through Wayfair, Amazon or Walmart, reliable large-parcel and freight delivery wins; see our guides on heavy and bulky shipping on Amazon and Walmart and the LTL freight class guide.

Repeat buyers matter. With 80% of orders from repeat customers, quality and reviews compound over time. A product that disappoints loses more than one sale.

AI discovery is early but real. At about 1% of traffic, AI referrals are small, but doubling quarter over quarter. Clean product data (dimensions, materials, assembly) helps across every channel.

Checklist for furniture and home-goods brands

  1. Review your price ladder: include strong entry-price options.
  2. Complete dimensions, materials, weight capacity and assembly details on every listing.
  3. Check delivery promises and damage rates for bulky items.
  4. Compare channel performance across Wayfair, Amazon and Walmart quarterly.
  5. Prepare holiday inventory for large items early, given longer freight lead times.

Wayfair 2026 三季报：家居大盘持平，美国营收逆势增 11.4%

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-07 · 约 5 分钟阅读

疫情红利结束后，家居一直是美国电商最难做的品类之一。Wayfair 最新财报显示需求正在回流，至少流向了头部玩家。2026 年 11 月 4 日，Wayfair 公布疫情以来最快的季度增长：美国营收增长 11.4%，而整体品类基本持平。对在美国销售的家具家居工厂来说，这是个值得关注的信号。

第三季度数据

据 Wayfair 2026 年第三季度财报（2026 年 11 月 4 日）：

  • 总净营收 34.2 亿美元，从 31.2 亿美元增长 9.7%。
  • 美国净营收 30.4 亿美元，增长 11.4%；国际下降 2.3% 至 3.8 亿美元。
  • 交付订单增长 12.2% 至 1,100 万单；活跃客户增长 4.7% 至 2,220 万。
  • 平均订单金额从 317 美元降至 311 美元。
  • 老客户贡献 80.0% 的订单；移动端占订单的 64.4%。
  • 调整后 EBITDA 利润率 6.9%，Wayfair 称这是"2020 年以来最好的第三季度"。

CEO Niraj Shah 表示："第三季度是我们疫情后增长最强的一个季度"，并说："在美国品类整体基本持平的情况下，我们的美国营收实现了两位数增长。"据 24/7 Wall St.（2026 年 11 月 4 日），营收比分析师约 33.8 亿美元的预期高出约 1.2%。

增长从哪里来

据 Digital Commerce 360（2026 年 11 月 6 日）对财报电话会的报道：

  • AI 购物：Wayfair 与 Google、OpenAI、Meta 合作，向这些平台提供"数千万件商品"。来自 AI 工具的流量环比翻倍以上，但仍只占总流量约 1%。
  • 降低广告依赖：广告占营收 9.9%，为 2021 年以来最低；直接访问带来的营收增长超过 15%。
  • 达人与会员：达人带来的销售比 2025 年秋季 Way Day 增长超过 70%，年费 29 美元的 Wayfair Rewards 会员持续增长。
  • 线下门店：美国已开三家大型门店，丹佛店计划 11 月 20 日开业，2027 年还将开更多。

我们的解读

需求在向头部集中，而不是整体增长。Wayfair 说品类基本持平，增长来自抢份额。中小品牌要靠更好的性价比和 Listing，而不是指望大盘上涨。

客单变小、单量变多。订单增速快于营收，平均订单金额下降。消费者在买，但更多是低价位商品。工厂要确保入门价位的产品足够有竞争力。

物流是主战场。家具体积大、运费高。无论在 Wayfair、Amazon 还是 Walmart，稳定的大件和货运配送都是胜负手，可参考我们的亚马逊与 Walmart 大件物流和LTL 货运等级指南。

复购很关键。80% 的订单来自老客户，质量和评价的影响会不断累积，一件让人失望的商品损失的不只是一单。

AI 导流还早，但在增长。AI 来源流量只占约 1%，但环比翻倍。完整的商品数据（尺寸、材质、承重、安装方式）在所有渠道都有用。

家具家居品牌清单

  1. 梳理价格带，确保有强有力的入门款。
  2. 每个 Listing 补全尺寸、材质、承重和安装信息。
  3. 检查大件商品的配送承诺和破损率。
  4. 每季度对比 Wayfair、Amazon 和 Walmart 各渠道表现。
  5. 大件货运周期长，提前备好假日季库存。
