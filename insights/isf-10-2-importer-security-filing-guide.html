ISF 10+2 Explained: Importer Security Filing Rules for Ocean Shipments to the US

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-11 · 6 min read

If you ship ocean containers to the US, someone must file an Importer Security Filing (ISF) with US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) before the goods are even loaded in China. A late or wrong ISF can mean holds, exams and a bond claim. Here is what the rule requires, who is responsible, and how to stay compliant, based on the regulation itself.

What ISF is and when it applies

ISF, often called "10+2," is set out in 19 CFR Part 149. Under § 149.2, it applies to cargo arriving in the US by vessel, except bulk cargo, and it must be submitted in English through a CBP-approved electronic system. The "10" are data elements the importer provides; the "+2" (vessel stow plan and container status messages) come from the ocean carrier. ISF does not apply to air freight, express parcels or truck shipments.

Compliance has been required since January 26, 2010, according to § 149.2(g).

Who is the "ISF Importer"

Section 149.1 defines the ISF Importer as "the party causing goods to arrive within the limits of a port in the United States by vessel." For goods entering the US, that is normally the owner, purchaser or consignee, or an agent such as a licensed customs broker. In practice, many Chinese sellers ask their freight forwarder or US broker to file. You can delegate the filing, but your company remains responsible for the data, so give your agent accurate information early.

The 10 data elements and their deadlines

According to § 149.2(b) and § 149.3(a), the importer must provide these elements:

#Data elementDeadline
1Seller24 hours before loading at the foreign port
2Buyer24 hours before loading
3Importer of record number / FTZ applicant ID24 hours before loading
4Consignee number(s)24 hours before loading
5Manufacturer (or supplier)*24 hours before loading
6Ship-to party*24 hours before loading
7Country of origin*24 hours before loading
8Commodity HTSUS number (6-digit minimum)*24 hours before loading
9Container stuffing locationAs early as possible, no later than 24 hours before US arrival
10Consolidator (stuffer)As early as possible, no later than 24 hours before US arrival

* Under § 149.3(a), the manufacturer, country of origin and HTSUS number must be linked to each other at the line-item level. The HTSUS number must be provided to at least six digits; it can be used for entry only if given at 10 digits by the importer of record or its licensed broker.

Four elements (manufacturer, ship-to party, country of origin and HTSUS number) may first be filed as "best available data" under § 149.2(f), but must be updated no later than 24 hours before the vessel arrives in the US.

Updates, withdrawals and penalties

  • Update: if any information changes or more accurate data becomes available before the goods arrive, § 149.2(d) requires you to update the filing.
  • Withdraw: if the goods will no longer be imported into the US, § 149.2(e) requires you to withdraw the ISF.
  • Bond liability: the basic importation bond conditions in 19 CFR 113.62(j) state that if the principal defaults on ISF obligations, liquidated damages of $5,000 for each violation may be claimed.
The $5,000 figure is the bond amount set in the regulation. How CBP enforces it in any specific case can vary; check with your licensed customs broker.

Practical checklist for sellers

  1. Get an importer of record number and a bond. The number is usually an IRS/EIN number. Section 149.5(b) requires the ISF Importer to hold a bond, such as a basic importation and entry bond or a dedicated ISF bond; arrange it before your first shipment.
  2. Agree who files. Put it in writing with your forwarder or broker, including how they will receive data and confirm acceptance.
  3. Collect data from the factory early. Get the full manufacturer name and address, stuffing location and consolidator before booking, not after the container is loaded.
  4. Classify first. Confirm HTS codes before shipment; see how to find your HTS code. Make sure country of origin matches your product marking (country-of-origin marking rules).
  5. Use the house bill of lading level. Section 149.3(a) requires data at the house bill level, so for consolidated (LCL) cargo each house bill needs its own correct data.
  6. Keep FBA/3PL addresses correct. The ship-to party is the first place that will physically receive the goods after customs release, often a deconsolidation warehouse or 3PL, not the final Amazon FC.
  7. Update before arrival. If your supplier, stuffing location or HTS code changes, update the ISF before the vessel arrives.

Common mistakes

  • Listing the trading company as manufacturer when the regulation asks for the party that last manufactures or assembles, or the supplier of finished goods.
  • Filing after loading because the forwarder only received the packing list at the port.
  • Reusing an old ISF template after changing factories.
  • Forgetting to withdraw a filing when a shipment is cancelled.

ISF 10+2 申报详解：海运进口美国必须知道的规则

Atronia Innovations · 2026-11-11 · 约 6 分钟阅读

只要你走海运整柜或拼箱发往美国，就必须有人在货物在中国装船之前，向美国海关与边境保护局（CBP）提交进口商安全申报（ISF）。ISF 迟报或报错，可能导致扣货、查验甚至保证金索赔。本文根据法规原文，讲清楚要求是什么、谁负责，以及卖家如何合规。

ISF 是什么，什么时候需要

ISF 俗称"10+2"，规定在 美国联邦法规 19 CFR 第 149 部分。根据第 149.2 条，凡经船舶运抵美国的货物（散货除外）都需要申报，并须以英文通过 CBP 认可的电子系统提交。"10"是进口方提供的 10 项数据；"+2"（船舶积载图和集装箱状态信息）由船公司提供。空运、快递小包和陆运不适用 ISF。

根据第 149.2(g) 条，该规定自 2010 年 1 月 26 日起强制执行。

谁是"ISF 申报人"

第 149.1 条把 ISF 申报人定义为"使货物经船舶运抵美国港口范围内的一方"。对于进入美国的货物，通常是货主、买方或收货人，也可以是持牌报关行等代理人。实际操作中，很多中国卖家委托货代或美国报关行代为申报。申报可以委托，但数据责任仍在你的公司，所以要尽早给代理提供准确信息。

10 项申报要素及截止时间

根据第 149.2(b) 条和第 149.3(a) 条，需申报以下要素：

#申报要素截止时间
1卖方（Seller）境外港装船前 24 小时
2买方（Buyer）装船前 24 小时
3进口商登记号（IOR No.）/ FTZ 申请人编号装船前 24 小时
4收货人编号（Consignee No.）装船前 24 小时
5制造商（或供应商）*装船前 24 小时
6交货地 / 收货方（Ship-to party）*装船前 24 小时
7原产国*装船前 24 小时
8HTSUS 商品编码（至少 6 位）*装船前 24 小时
9装箱地点尽早，最迟抵达美国港口前 24 小时
10拼箱 / 装箱人（Consolidator）尽早，最迟抵达美国港口前 24 小时

* 根据第 149.3(a) 条，制造商、原产国和 HTSUS 编码必须在逐项商品层面一一对应。HTSUS 编码至少报到 6 位；只有进口商或其持牌报关行报到 10 位，才能同时用于正式报关。

根据第 149.2(f) 条，制造商、交货地、原产国和 HTSUS 编码这 4 项可以先按"现有最佳数据"申报，但最迟须在船舶抵达美国前 24 小时更新。

更新、撤回与处罚

  • 更新：货物抵达前如有信息变化或获得更准确数据，第 149.2(d) 条要求更新申报。
  • 撤回：货物不再进口美国的，第 149.2(e) 条要求撤回 ISF。
  • 保证金责任：19 CFR 113.62(j) 的进口保证金条款规定，申报人违反 ISF 义务的，每项违规可被索赔 5,000 美元的违约金。
5,000 美元是法规中写明的保证金违约金额度；CBP 在具体个案中如何执行可能有所不同，请向你的持牌报关行确认。

卖家实操清单

  1. 首票前办好进口商登记号和保证金。登记号通常用 IRS/EIN 号；第 149.5(b) 条要求 ISF 申报人持有保证金（Bond），例如进口保证金或专门的 ISF 保证金。
  2. 书面约定谁来申报。与货代或报关行写清数据如何传递、如何确认 CBP 已接收。
  3. 提前向工厂收集资料。制造商全称和地址、装箱地点、装箱人，要在订舱前拿到，而不是装柜之后。
  4. 先归类再出货。出货前确认 HTS 编码，参考 如何查找 HTS 编码；原产国要与产品标识一致（见 原产地标识规则）。
  5. 按分提单（House B/L）申报。第 149.3(a) 条要求按分提单层面申报，拼箱货每个分提单都要有各自准确的数据。
  6. 写对 FBA / 海外仓地址。Ship-to party 是清关放行后第一个实际收货的地点，往往是拆柜仓或第三方海外仓，而不是最终的亚马逊仓库。
  7. 到港前完成更新。供应商、装箱地点或 HTS 编码有变，一定在船抵美前更新 ISF。

常见错误

  • 把贸易公司填成制造商，而法规要求的是最后生产或组装的一方，或成品供应商。
  • 货代到了港口才拿到装箱单，导致装船后才申报。
  • 换了工厂却沿用旧的 ISF 模板。
  • 订单取消后忘记撤回申报。
