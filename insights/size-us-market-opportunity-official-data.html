How to Size Your US Market Opportunity Using Official Data

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-08 · 6 min read

Before you invest in a US launch, you need a realistic view of how big the opportunity is. Many sellers rely on a single tool's sales estimate or a competitor's review count. A better approach combines official market data with marketplace signals, from the top down and the bottom up. This guide shows how, starting with the US government's own e-commerce figures.

Start with the official big picture

The U.S. Census Bureau's quarterly report (released August 18, 2026) estimates US retail e-commerce sales of $340.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026, seasonally adjusted, up 12.2% from a year earlier, while total retail sales grew 6.7%. E-commerce accounted for 17.1% of total retail sales.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Table 1, Aug 18, 2026. The next release, for Q3 2026, is scheduled for November 19, 2026. Note that Census figures cover employer retail firms and are not adjusted for price changes.

Two things stand out. First, e-commerce grew almost twice as fast as total retail in Q2 2026 (12.2% vs 6.7%). Second, seasonality is strong: on a not-seasonally-adjusted basis, Census puts e-commerce at 18.3% of retail sales in Q4 2025 ($365.2 billion), compared with 15.8% in Q3 2025. The holiday quarter is where online sales concentrate.

Step 1: narrow from the market to your category

The national total tells you the market is large and growing, but you need a category view. Useful steps:

  • Use the Census Bureau's annual retail and e-commerce data by kind of business for broad category sizes (see the Census e-commerce page).
  • Check industry reports and trade associations for your specific category.
  • Note whether your category sells mainly through marketplaces, brand websites or physical stores; this changes which channel to start with.

Step 2: measure demand bottom-up

  1. Search demand. Use marketplace search tools (such as Amazon's Product Opportunity Explorer in Seller Central) and keyword tools to see monthly search volume for your main keywords.
  2. Competitor sales signals. Look at best-seller ranks, review counts and review growth for the top 20 products. Treat third-party sales estimates as rough ranges.
  3. Price bands. Map where most sales happen by price; a crowded $15–$25 band needs a different plan from an open $60+ band.
  4. Seasonality. Check how demand changes over the year; many categories earn a large share of revenue in Q4.

Step 3: turn it into a sales target

Multiply realistic monthly units by your price for each channel, then subtract referral fees, fulfillment, ads and returns to see if the target is profitable. Start with a conservative share of the demand you measured; new brands rarely capture top-seller volumes in the first months.

Tip: Build three scenarios (conservative, base, strong) and order inventory for the conservative case, with a reorder plan ready if sales beat it.

Common mistakes

  • Using the national e-commerce total as if it were your market.
  • Trusting one tool's sales estimate without cross-checking.
  • Ignoring seasonality and planning a launch just after a category's peak.
  • Forgetting that growth figures from Census are not adjusted for inflation.
  • Sizing the market but not the competition: a large market with entrenched leaders can be harder than a smaller, open niche.

A simple worksheet

  1. Write down the top 10 keywords and their monthly search volume.
  2. List the top 20 competitors with price, rating, reviews and estimated sales range.
  3. Mark the price band and features where you can be different.
  4. Estimate conservative, base and strong monthly units.
  5. Calculate profit per unit after all fees and ad costs, and check that the base case still makes money.

如何用官方数据测算你的美国市场机会

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-08 · 约 6 分钟阅读

在投入资源进入美国市场之前，你需要对机会大小有一个现实的判断。很多卖家只看某个工具的销量估算，或者竞品的评论数。更好的做法是把官方市场数据和平台信号结合起来，自上而下、自下而上两头测算。本文从美国政府公布的电商数据讲起，教你一步步做。

先看官方大盘

根据美国人口普查局季度报告（2026 年 8 月 18 日发布），2026 年第二季度美国零售电商销售额（季节调整后）为 3,402 亿美元，同比增长 12.2%，同期零售总额增长 6.7%。电商占零售总额的 17.1%。

季度（季节调整后）电商占零售比例电商同比增长
2025 年 Q216.3%+5.0%
2025 年 Q316.4%+5.3%
2025 年 Q416.7%+5.9%
2026 年 Q117.0%+10.1%
2026 年 Q2（初步）17.1%+12.2%

来源：美国人口普查局表 1，2026 年 8 月 18 日。2026 年第三季度数据预计 11 月 19 日发布。注意：普查局数据仅覆盖有雇员的零售企业，且未剔除价格因素。

有两点值得注意：第一，2026 年第二季度电商增速几乎是零售总额的两倍（12.2% 对 6.7%）；第二，季节性很强：按未季节调整口径，普查局数据显示 2025 年第四季度电商占零售 18.3%（3,652 亿美元），而第三季度为 15.8%。节日季是线上销售最集中的季度。

第一步：从大盘收窄到你的类目

全国总数说明市场大且在增长，但你需要类目层面的判断：

  • 参考普查局按行业划分的年度零售和电商数据，了解大类规模（见普查局电商统计页面）。
  • 查阅你所在类目的行业报告和行业协会数据。
  • 判断你的类目主要通过平台、品牌官网还是线下门店销售，这决定从哪个渠道切入。

第二步：自下而上测算需求

  1. 搜索需求：用平台搜索工具（如亚马逊卖家后台的 Product Opportunity Explorer）和关键词工具，查看核心关键词的月搜索量。
  2. 竞品销量信号：看前 20 名产品的排名、评论数和评论增长速度。第三方销量估算只能当作大致区间。
  3. 价格带：看销量主要集中在哪个价格段；拥挤的 15–25 美元区间和空白的 60 美元以上区间，打法完全不同。
  4. 季节性：看全年需求变化，很多类目的收入很大一部分来自第四季度。

第三步：换算成销售目标

用每个渠道合理的月销量乘以售价，再扣除佣金、履约、广告和退货，看目标是否赚钱。起步时按测得需求的保守比例估算，新品牌在头几个月很少能做到头部卖家的销量。

小提示：做保守、基准、乐观三种情景，按保守情景备货，并准备好补货计划，以防卖得比预期好。

常见错误

  • 把全国电商总额当成自己的市场规模。
  • 只信一个工具的销量估算，不做交叉验证。
  • 忽视季节性，在类目旺季刚过时上新。
  • 忘记普查局的增长数据没有剔除通胀。
  • 只测市场不看竞争：头部稳固的大市场，可能比开放的小细分更难做。

简单测算表

  1. 写下前 10 个关键词及其月搜索量。
  2. 列出前 20 个竞品的价格、评分、评论数和销量区间。
  3. 标出你能做出差异的价格带和功能点。
  4. 估算保守、基准、乐观三种月销量。
  5. 计算扣除所有费用和广告后的单件利润，确认基准情景下仍然赚钱。
