How to Ship Lithium Battery Products With USPS: Rules for Sellers

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-31 · 6 min read

Phones, earbuds, power tools, toys, e-bike parts and power banks: a huge share of products sold online contain lithium batteries. They can be mailed in the US, but USPS sets strict limits on battery size, quantity, transport mode and markings. Get it wrong and packages can be refused, returned or worse. This guide summarizes the USPS domestic rules in Publication 52, section 349.

This is a summary for sellers, not a substitute for the official rules. Always check the current USPS Publication 52, section 349 and your carrier's requirements.

Know your battery type

  • Lithium-ion (including lithium polymer) are rechargeable, the kind in phones, laptops, power banks and most consumer electronics. USPS limits them by watt-hours (Wh = amp-hours × volts).
  • Lithium metal batteries are non-rechargeable, such as coin cells in watches or some single-use batteries. USPS limits them by lithium content in grams.

Check the Wh rating printed on your battery. A 10,000 mAh power bank at 3.7 V is 37 Wh (10 Ah × 3.7 V), under the 100 Wh limit. Calculation: Wh = Ah × V.

Air vs ground

Publication 52 talks about "surface" and "air" transportation. The key rule: batteries shipped on their own (spares, replacement batteries, power banks sold as standalone batteries) must go by surface transportation only, except lithium-ion mailed within Alaska. Products with batteries installed, or batteries packed with the device they power, can go by surface or air within the limits above.

In practice, sellers of standalone batteries use a ground service. USPS Ground Advantage is USPS's ground parcel service; see our USPS Ground Advantage guide. Confirm with USPS or your label provider which services move by surface for your lanes.

Markings and packaging

  • Lithium battery mark. When required, a DOT-approved lithium battery mark goes flat on the address side, showing the right UN number: UN3481 for lithium-ion in or with equipment, UN3480 for lithium-ion alone, UN3091 for lithium metal in or with equipment, UN3090 for lithium metal alone.
  • Surface-only text. Standalone lithium-ion packages must show "Surface Mail Only, Lithium-ion Batteries - Forbidden for Transportation Aboard Passenger Aircraft" (or the "Secondary Lithium Batteries" wording). Lithium metal has matching wording.
  • Used or defective devices. Used, damaged or defective electronic devices must be marked "Restricted Electronic Device" and "Surface Transportation Only," and are prohibited from air transportation.
  • Packaging basics. Rigid outer packaging, complete delivery and return addresses, protection against short circuits and accidental activation, and cushioning to prevent movement.

International shipping

Internationally, USPS is far stricter: only small consumer lithium batteries installed in the equipment they operate are generally mailable, and standalone batteries are prohibited. Section 349 refers you to Publication 52 section 622 and the International Mail Manual for details. Many sellers use courier or specialist services for battery products going abroad.

Seller checklist

  1. Identify each product's battery type and its Wh rating or lithium content.
  2. Confirm it is under 100 Wh per battery (lithium-ion) or 2 g (lithium metal).
  3. Decide whether the battery is installed, packed with equipment, or sold alone.
  4. For standalone batteries, use a ground service, keep packages at 5 lb or less and keep batteries in sealed original packaging.
  5. Apply the correct lithium battery mark and surface-only wording when required.
  6. Never ship damaged, defective or recalled batteries, including customer returns that look damaged.
  7. Train your warehouse or 3PL on these rules and audit a sample of packages.

如何用 USPS 寄送带锂电池的商品：卖家规则指南

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-31 · 约 6 分钟阅读

手机、耳机、电动工具、玩具、电动车配件、充电宝……网上卖的商品里，带锂电池的占了很大比例。它们可以在美国境内邮寄，但 USPS 对电池容量、数量、运输方式和标识都有严格规定。弄错了，包裹可能被拒收、退回，甚至更糟。本文梳理 USPS Publication 52 第 349 节的美国境内规则。

本文是面向卖家的摘要，不能替代官方规定。请以最新的 USPS Publication 52 第 349 节和承运商要求为准。

先分清电池类型

  • 锂离子电池（含锂聚合物）可充电，常见于手机、笔记本、充电宝和大多数消费电子。USPS 按瓦时（Wh = 安时 × 伏特）限制。
  • 锂金属电池不可充电，如手表纽扣电池或部分一次性电池。USPS 按锂含量（克）限制。

看电池上标注的 Wh。一个 10,000 mAh、3.7 V 的充电宝是 37 Wh（10 Ah × 3.7 V），低于 100 Wh 上限。计算：Wh = Ah × V。

航空还是地面

Publication 52 用的是"地面运输"和"航空运输"。关键规则：单独寄送的电池（备用电池、替换电池、作为独立电池销售的充电宝）只能走地面运输，阿拉斯加州内寄送的锂离子电池除外。电池装在产品里、或随所供电的设备一起包装的，在上述限制内地面、航空都可以。

实际操作中，卖独立电池的卖家会用地面服务。USPS Ground Advantage 是 USPS 的地面包裹服务，详见我们的USPS Ground Advantage 指南。具体线路哪些服务走地面，请向 USPS 或面单服务商确认。

标识和包装

  • 锂电池标识。需要时，在地址面平贴美国交通部（DOT）认可的锂电池标识，注明对应 UN 编号：设备内或随设备的锂离子为 UN3481，单独锂离子为 UN3480，设备内或随设备的锂金属为 UN3091，单独锂金属为 UN3090。
  • 仅限地面字样。单独寄送的锂离子电池包裹须标注 "Surface Mail Only, Lithium-ion Batteries - Forbidden for Transportation Aboard Passenger Aircraft"（或 "Secondary Lithium Batteries" 表述），锂金属有对应表述。
  • 二手或故障设备。二手、损坏或有缺陷的电子设备须标注 "Restricted Electronic Device" 和 "Surface Transportation Only"，且禁止航空运输。
  • 包装要求。坚固的外包装、完整的收寄件地址、防短路和防意外启动保护，以及防止晃动的缓冲。

国际寄送

国际邮件规则严格得多：一般只允许寄送安装在设备内的小型消费类锂电池，单独电池禁止寄送。第 349 节要求参见 Publication 52 第 622 节和《国际邮件手册》。很多卖家会用快递或专线渠道寄送带电产品出境。

卖家清单

  1. 确认每个产品的电池类型及其 Wh 或锂含量。
  2. 确认锂离子电池每块不超过 100 Wh，锂金属电池每块不超过 2 克。
  3. 区分电池是装在设备内、随设备包装，还是单独销售。
  4. 单独电池走地面服务，包裹不超过 5 磅，电池保留密封原包装。
  5. 按要求贴锂电池标识和仅限地面字样。
  6. 绝不寄送损坏、有缺陷或被召回的电池，包括看起来有损坏的退货。
  7. 培训仓库或第三方仓，并抽查包裹是否合规。
