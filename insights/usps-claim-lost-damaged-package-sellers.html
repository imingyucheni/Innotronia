How to File a USPS Claim for Lost or Damaged Packages

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-17 · 6 min read

Every shipper loses or damages some packages, and peak season makes it more likely. If you ship with USPS, a claim can recover the value of a lost or damaged order, but only if you file within the right window and with the right evidence. This guide walks through USPS's official claim rules, deadlines and a simple process to keep your claims organized.

Is the package covered?

USPS pays claims on insured items. Its claims page notes that insurance may be included with the service or purchased separately, and that "USPS may not legally pay compensation for uninsured lost or damaged articles." For example, USPS says Ground Advantage includes $100 of insurance for both outbound and return shipments, with extra coverage up to $5,000 available to buy. If you ship higher-value items, buy additional insurance when you create the label.

USPS also states that it "does not pay a claim higher than an item's actual value," so insuring an item for more than it is worth does not raise the payout.

When to file

The filing window depends on whether the package is lost or damaged, and on the service:

SituationFile afterFile before
Damaged item or missing contents (any service)Right away60 days from mailing date
Lost: Priority Mail Express7 days60 days
Lost: Priority Mail, Ground Advantage, Insured Mail, COD, Registered Mail15 days60 days
Lost: APO/FPO Priority Mail Express Military21 days180 days
Lost: APO/FPO/DPO insured or registered mail45 days1 year

Source: USPS, File a Claim (accessed October 17, 2026). Day counts run from the mailing date. Missing the 60-day deadline is an easily avoidable way to lose a claim, so track it per package.

Evidence USPS asks for

  • Tracking or label number (USPS notes these are 13 to 34 characters).
  • Proof of insurance: the original mailing receipt, outer packaging showing an insured label, or a printed online label record.
  • Proof of value: a sales receipt, paid invoice, credit card statement or printed online transaction record, among others.
  • Proof of damage: clear photos and a repair estimate from a reputable dealer. USPS says to keep the original packaging and everything in it until the claim is settled; you may be asked to bring the package to a Post Office for inspection.

Either the sender or the recipient can file, and USPS says the person filing must have the original mailing receipt. For sellers, the cleanest approach is usually to file yourself as the sender, using your label record and order invoice.

How the process works

  1. File online through a USPS.com account, the fastest route; or request a paper Domestic Claim form by phone and mail it with your evidence.
  2. Wait for a decision. USPS says decisions usually arrive within 5 to 10 days, and approved claims are paid within 7 to 10 business days.
  3. Appeal if needed. You can appeal within 30 days of a partial or full denial, addressing the reasons given and adding new documentation. A final appeal must be filed within 30 days of the appeal denial.

A simple claims routine for sellers

  1. Insure high-value orders above the included coverage when you buy the label.
  2. Keep evidence by default: save label records and order invoices; photograph high-value items and packing before shipping.
  3. Run a weekly tracking check for packages with no scan updates, and diarize the "file after" date for each.
  4. Ask customers for photos and to keep the packaging when they report damage.
  5. File before day 60, and log each claim with its status and payout.
  6. Review patterns monthly: repeated damage on one SKU usually means packaging needs to change.

Note that marketplace programs have their own rules: for example, orders shipped with marketplace-purchased labels may come with separate protections. See our article on Ship with Walmart in ShipStation for one example.

USPS 丢件与破损索赔指南：卖家怎么申报

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-17 · 约 6 分钟阅读

只要发货，就难免有包裹丢失或破损，旺季概率更高。如果你用 USPS 发货，提交索赔可以追回丢件或破损订单的货值——前提是在正确的时间窗内、带齐正确的证据。本文梳理 USPS 官方索赔规则、截止时间，以及一套便于管理索赔的简单流程。

包裹有没有保险？

USPS 只对有保险的邮件赔付。其索赔页面说明，保险可能随服务附带，也可以单独购买，并指出“USPS 依法不能对未投保的丢失或损坏物品进行赔偿”。例如，USPS 称 Ground Advantage 的去程和退货都包含 100 美元保险，可额外购买最高 5,000 美元保额。发高价值商品时，请在打单时加购保险。

USPS 还规定“赔付金额不会超过物品的实际价值”，所以超额投保并不会提高赔付。

什么时候申报

申报时间窗取决于包裹是丢失还是破损，以及所用服务：

情形最早申报最晚申报
破损或内件缺失（任何服务）立即邮寄日起 60 天
丢失：Priority Mail Express7 天60 天
丢失：Priority Mail、Ground Advantage、保价邮件、代收货款、挂号信15 天60 天
丢失：APO/FPO 军邮 Priority Mail Express21 天180 天
丢失：APO/FPO/DPO 保价或挂号邮件45 天1 年

来源：USPS“提交索赔”页面（2026 年 10 月 17 日访问）。天数从邮寄日起算。错过 60 天截止是完全可以避免的索赔失败原因，请按包裹逐一跟踪。

USPS 要求的证据

  • 追踪号或面单号（USPS 说明为 13–34 位字符）。
  • 投保证明：原始邮寄收据、带保价标签的外包装，或打印的线上面单记录。
  • 价值证明：销售收据、已付发票、信用卡账单或打印的线上交易记录等。
  • 破损证明：清晰照片及正规维修商的维修报价。USPS 要求在索赔结案前保留原包装和所有内件，并可能要求将包裹带到邮局查验。

寄件人或收件人都可以申报，USPS 要求申报人持有原始邮寄收据。对卖家来说，通常最简单的方式是作为寄件人自己申报，用面单记录和订单发票作为证据。

流程怎么走

  1. 线上申报：通过 USPS.com 账户提交，最快；也可以电话索取纸质国内索赔表，填好后连同证据邮寄。
  2. 等待结果：USPS 称通常 5–10 天给出决定，获批后 7–10 个工作日内付款。
  3. 必要时申诉：收到部分或全部拒赔后 30 天内可申诉，针对拒赔理由补充新材料。终审申诉须在申诉被驳回后 30 天内提交。

卖家的简易索赔流程

  1. 高价值订单加保：超过附带保额的订单，打单时加购保险。
  2. 默认留存证据：保存面单记录和订单发票；高价值商品发货前拍照留存商品和打包情况。
  3. 每周查一次物流：找出长时间无扫描更新的包裹，记下各自的“最早可申报”日期。
  4. 请顾客拍照：顾客反馈破损时，请其拍照并保留包装。
  5. 60 天内提交：每单索赔登记状态和赔付金额。
  6. 每月复盘：同一 SKU 反复破损，通常说明包装需要改进。

注意，各平台的物流项目有各自规则：例如通过平台购买的面单可能附带单独的保障。可参考我们的《Ship with Walmart 接入 ShipStation》一文。
