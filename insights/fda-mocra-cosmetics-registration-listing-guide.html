FDA Cosmetics Registration and Product Listing Under MoCRA: A Seller Guide

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-26 · 6 min read

If you make or sell skincare, makeup, hair care or fragrance in the US, FDA rules changed a lot under the Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act of 2022 (MoCRA). Facilities now have to register with the FDA, products have to be listed, and serious adverse events must be reported. This guide explains who has to do what, with a focus on Chinese factories and brands selling through Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop.

This is a general overview, not legal advice. Check FDA's pages or a regulatory consultant for your specific products.

Two separate obligations: facilities and products

  • Facility registration. "Manufacturers and processors must register their facilities with the FDA and renew their registration every two years," according to FDA's registration and listing page (current as of Sept 9, 2026). This applies to the factory, wherever it is, if it makes or processes cosmetics for the US market. Foreign facilities also name a US agent in their registration.
  • Product listing. "A responsible person must list each marketed cosmetic product with the FDA, including product ingredients," and update the listing every year. The responsible person is the manufacturer, packer or distributor whose name appears on the label, which is often the brand, not the factory.

Both are filed electronically, mainly through FDA's free Cosmetics Direct portal, which FDA describes as a Structured Product Labeling (SPL) authoring tool.

How many companies have done it

FDA publishes running totals. As of June 30, 2026, it reported the following:

FDA cosmetics data (as of June 30, 2026)Count
Unique, active facility registrations16,398
Unique, active product listings1,298,361

Source: FDA, Registration & Listing of Cosmetic Product Facilities and Products. Your competitors are already in the system; buyers and marketplaces increasingly expect you to be too.

Other MoCRA duties for the responsible person

  • Serious adverse event reports must go to FDA within 15 business days, with a copy of the label, according to FDA's MoCRA overview (current as of Aug 10, 2026). New medical information received within a year must also be sent within 15 business days.
  • Safety substantiation records must be kept. FDA notes that animal testing is not a requirement for marketing a cosmetic product.
  • Records access and recalls. FDA can access certain safety records and can order a mandatory recall if a company refuses a voluntary one and a product may cause serious harm.
  • Fragrance allergen labeling will be set by a future FDA regulation, so watch for updates.

The small business exemption

MoCRA exempts certain small businesses from facility registration and product listing. FDA's small business form defines this as a business whose average annual US cosmetic sales over the previous three years are under $1,000,000, adjusted for inflation, per FDA's small business exemption form.

The exemption does not apply to products that are injected, intended for internal use, or that regularly come into contact with the mucous membrane of the eye, according to FDA. A brand with low sales that sells mascara or eyeliner, for example, still needs to register and list. Also note that products regulated as drugs (sunscreens, for example) follow FDA's drug rules instead.

Who does what: factory vs brand

TaskUsually done by
Register the manufacturing facility (renew every 2 years)The factory (with its US agent if outside the US)
List each product and its ingredients (update yearly)The responsible person named on the label (often the brand)
Report serious adverse events within 15 business daysThe responsible person

Product listings reference the facility's registration number, so brands need that number from their factory before listing.

Step-by-step checklist

  1. Confirm each product is a cosmetic (not a drug) under FDA definitions.
  2. Work out whether you qualify for the small business exemption, and whether any product falls outside it.
  3. Ask your factory for its FDA facility registration number, or help it register.
  4. Decide who is the responsible person on the label and make sure the label contact details are correct.
  5. Create a Cosmetics Direct account and list each product with its full ingredient list.
  6. Set calendar reminders: annual listing updates and two-year facility renewals.
  7. Set up a process to log complaints and report serious adverse events within 15 business days.
  8. Keep safety substantiation files ready in case FDA or a marketplace asks.
Keep your FDA registration and listing numbers handy. Marketplaces and retail buyers often ask for compliance documents when you launch beauty products or when a listing is flagged.

FDA 化妆品工厂注册与产品备案（MoCRA）卖家指南

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-26 · 约 6 分钟阅读

只要在美国生产或销售护肤、彩妆、洗护或香水产品，就绕不开 2022 年《化妆品监管现代化法案》（MoCRA）。现在工厂要在 FDA 注册，产品要逐个备案（listing），严重不良事件必须上报。本文讲清楚谁要做什么，重点面向通过 Amazon、Walmart、TikTok Shop 销售的中国工厂和品牌。

本文为一般性介绍，不构成法律意见。具体产品请以 FDA 官网或专业法规顾问意见为准。

两项独立义务：工厂注册 + 产品备案

  • 工厂注册。据 FDA 注册与备案页面（2026 年 9 月 9 日更新），"生产商和加工商必须在 FDA 注册其工厂，并每两年续期一次"。只要为美国市场生产或加工化妆品，无论工厂在哪个国家都要注册。境外工厂注册时还需指定美国代理人（US agent）。
  • 产品备案。"责任人必须在 FDA 为每个上市的化妆品备案，包括产品成分"，并每年更新。责任人（responsible person）指标签上署名的生产商、包装商或经销商——通常是品牌方，而不是工厂。

两者都通过 FDA 免费的 Cosmetics Direct 系统在线提交，FDA 将其描述为结构化产品标签（SPL）编写工具。

已经有多少企业完成

FDA 会公布累计数据，截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日：

FDA 化妆品数据（截至 2026 年 6 月 30 日）数量
有效工厂注册（去重）16,398
有效产品备案（去重）1,298,361

来源：FDA 化妆品工厂注册与产品备案页面。你的竞争对手大多已经在系统里，采购商和平台也越来越默认你应该完成备案。

责任人的其他 MoCRA 义务

  • 严重不良事件须在 15 个工作日内连同标签副本上报 FDA，据 FDA MoCRA 概述页（2026 年 8 月 10 日更新）。一年内收到的新医疗信息也须在 15 个工作日内补报。
  • 安全性证明记录必须保存。FDA 特别说明，化妆品上市不要求动物实验。
  • 记录调取与召回。FDA 可调取部分安全记录；如企业拒绝自愿召回且产品可能造成严重危害，FDA 可下令强制召回。
  • 香料过敏原标识将由 FDA 后续法规规定，需持续关注。

小企业豁免

MoCRA 对部分小企业豁免工厂注册和产品备案。据 FDA 小企业豁免表格，小企业指过去三年在美国的化妆品年均销售额低于 100 万美元（按通胀调整）的企业。

但据 FDA，注射类、内用类，以及经常接触眼部黏膜的产品不适用豁免。比如销售额不高、但卖睫毛膏或眼线笔的品牌，仍然要注册和备案。另外，按药品监管的产品（如防晒霜）适用 FDA 药品规则，不走化妆品这套流程。

工厂和品牌各自负责什么

事项通常由谁负责

产品备案时需要填写工厂的注册号，所以品牌方要先从工厂拿到这个号码。

操作清单

  1. 确认每个产品按 FDA 定义属于化妆品而非药品。
  2. 判断自己是否符合小企业豁免，以及是否有产品不在豁免范围内。
  3. 向工厂索要 FDA 工厂注册号，或协助工厂注册。
  4. 明确标签上的责任人，并核对标签联系方式。
  5. 开通 Cosmetics Direct 账号，为每个产品提交完整成分表。
  6. 设置提醒：产品备案每年更新，工厂注册每两年续期。
  7. 建立投诉记录流程，确保严重不良事件 15 个工作日内上报。
  8. 准备好安全性证明文件，以备 FDA 或平台要求。
把 FDA 注册号和备案号放在手边。上架美妆个护产品或 Listing 被审核时，平台和零售采购商经常要求提供合规文件。
