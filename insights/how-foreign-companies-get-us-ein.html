How a Foreign Company Gets a US EIN: IRS Rules for Sellers

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-19 · 6 min read

Sooner or later, most foreign companies selling into the US are asked for an EIN, the US federal tax ID for businesses. A US subsidiary needs one; a Chinese parent company may need one for tax-treaty paperwork, some marketplace programs or banking. The good news: the IRS issues EINs for free. The catch: the fast online route is only for businesses based in the US. This guide explains the official options for foreign applicants.

What an EIN is

According to the IRS, "An EIN is a federal tax ID number for businesses, tax-exempt organizations and other entities." It is free when you get it directly from the IRS. Be cautious of websites that charge to "apply" for you; they submit the same form.

Common reasons a cross-border seller needs one include:

  • Setting up a US company (LLC or corporation), which needs its own EIN.
  • Completing withholding or tax-treaty forms for a foreign entity. The IRS says foreign entities that need an EIN to claim a tax treaty exemption on Form W-8BEN must submit Form SS-4.
  • Marketplace, payment or banking onboarding that asks for a US tax ID. Requirements vary by platform and program, so check each one.

Filling in Form SS-4 as a foreign entity

The IRS gives specific instructions for foreign entities that need an EIN for treaty or withholding purposes:

  • Where the form asks for an SSN or ITIN, write "N/A" unless the applicant already has one.
  • For question 10 (reason for applying), check "other" and write something like "For W-8BEN Purposes Only" or "For Tax Treaty Purposes Only."
  • If questions 11 through 17 do not apply because there is no US tax return filing requirement, mark them "N/A."

If you are forming a US company instead, the company applies as a US entity, and the questions about business activity and filing requirements apply normally. Get advice on the entity choice first.

After you get the EIN

  • Keep the IRS confirmation letter. Banks and platforms often ask for it.
  • Answer IRS letters. The IRS says that if a foreign entity with no filing requirement later receives a letter asking for a return, it should respond immediately that it has no requirement to file; not responding may lead to a procedural assessment of tax.
  • Don't apply twice. If the entity later becomes liable to file, the IRS says it "should not apply for a new EIN" and should use the one first issued.
  • Match names exactly. Use the same legal name and address on marketplace, bank and tax forms to avoid verification problems.

Checklist

  1. Decide which entity needs the EIN: the foreign parent, a US subsidiary, or both.
  2. Prepare the legal name, address, responsible party details and reason for applying.
  3. Use the phone line or fax if your principal place of business is outside the US.
  4. Store the EIN confirmation letter securely and share it only with trusted parties.
  5. Talk to a cross-border tax adviser about filing obligations before you start selling.

海外公司如何申请美国 EIN 税号：IRS 官方规则

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-19 · 约 6 分钟阅读

向美国销售的海外公司，迟早会被要求提供 EIN——美国联邦企业税号。美国子公司必须有；中国母公司也可能因税收协定文件、部分平台项目或银行开户需要它。好消息是 IRS 免费签发 EIN；但最快的线上申请只适用于主要营业地在美国的企业。本文讲清海外申请人可用的官方途径。

EIN 是什么

根据 IRS的说明，“EIN 是适用于企业、免税组织及其他实体的联邦税号”。直接向 IRS 申请是免费的。对收费“代办申请”的网站要谨慎，它们提交的是同一张表。

跨境卖家常见的需要 EIN 的情形：

  • 设立美国公司（LLC 或公司），公司需要自己的 EIN。
  • 为海外实体填写预扣税或税收协定表格。IRS 表示，需要 EIN 以便在 W-8BEN 表上申请税收协定豁免的海外实体，须提交 SS-4 表。
  • 平台、支付或银行开户时要求提供美国税号。各平台和项目要求不同，请逐一确认。

四种申请方式

海外实体如何填写 SS-4 表

IRS 对需要 EIN 用于税收协定或预扣税目的的海外实体有具体说明：

  • 表中要求填写 SSN 或 ITIN 处，若申请人没有，填写“N/A”。
  • 第 10 题（申请原因）勾选“other”，并注明“For W-8BEN Purposes Only”或“For Tax Treaty Purposes Only”。
  • 若因没有美国报税义务而第 11–17 题不适用，标注“N/A”。

如果是设立美国公司，则由该公司作为美国实体申请，经营活动和报税相关问题正常填写。先就实体选择咨询专业意见。

拿到 EIN 之后

  • 保存 IRS 确认函。银行和平台经常要求提供。
  • 回复 IRS 来信。IRS 表示，没有报税义务的海外实体如收到要求报税的信函，应立即回复说明无报税义务；不回复可能导致程序性税款评定。
  • 不要重复申请。IRS 表示，若该实体日后有了报税义务，“不应申请新的 EIN”，应继续使用最初签发的号码。
  • 名称保持一致。平台、银行和税务表格上使用完全一致的法定名称和地址，避免验证出问题。

检查清单

  1. 确定哪个实体需要 EIN：海外母公司、美国子公司，或两者都要。
  2. 准备法定名称、地址、负责人信息和申请原因。
  3. 主要营业地在美国境外的，使用电话或传真申请。
  4. 妥善保存 EIN 确认函，只与可信方共享。
  5. 开卖前就报税义务咨询跨境税务顾问。
