Walmart Marketplace Referral Fees: Rates, Tiers and Examples

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-15 · 6 min read

Walmart Marketplace has no setup or monthly subscription fee; instead it takes a referral fee on every sale. The rate depends on the category you choose during item setup, and for many categories it also depends on the price. Get the category or price band wrong and your margin math is off. This guide explains how Walmart's referral fees work, with the official rates and worked examples.

How the referral fee works

According to Walmart's referral fee page (accessed October 15, 2026):

  • Charged only when you sell. "These fees are only deducted once you complete a sale," and Walmart states "zero setup, monthly, or hidden fees."
  • Based on the total sales price. "Total sales price includes item price as well as shipping and handling, gift wrap and other charges."
  • Set by category and product type. "Referral fees vary based on the category and product type you choose during item setup."

Fulfillment costs are separate: if you use Walmart Fulfillment Services, see our WFS fees guide.

Two kinds of tiers: price bands and portions

Walmart uses two different tier structures, and they work differently:

  • Price bands (Apparel, Beauty, Baby, Grocery): the whole sale gets one rate based on the total price. A $14.99 shirt pays 5%; the same shirt at $20.99 pays 15% of the entire price.
  • Portions (Electronics Accessories, Furniture, Jewelry, Watches and others): each part of the price is charged at its own rate, like tax brackets. The effective rate falls as the price rises.

Here is how the effective referral fee looks for some sample prices:

Calculation: apparel at $12 = 5% ($0.60), $18 = 10% ($1.80), $30 = 15% ($4.50). Electronics accessories at $150 = 15% × $100 + 8% × $50 = $19 (12.7%). Furniture at $500 = 15% × $200 + 10% × $300 = $60 (12%). Jewelry at $400 = 20% × $250 + 5% × $150 = $57.50 (14.4%).

How to use this in pricing

Watch the band edges

In price-band categories, crossing a threshold changes the fee on the whole price. An apparel item at $15.00 pays $0.75; at $15.50 it pays $1.55. Make sure a small price increase, or adding shipping charges (which count toward the total price), doesn't push you into a higher band and cancel out the gain.

Choose the correct category

The fee follows the category and product type you choose at item setup. Choose the one that accurately describes the product; Walmart can review and correct categories, and the wrong one can affect fees and how shoppers find your item.

Bundle to change the math

For low-priced items in band categories, a bundle at a higher price may pay a higher rate. Run the numbers both ways, including fulfillment costs, before you decide.

Build a fee line into every SKU

Your SKU margin sheet should show price, referral fee, fulfillment cost, ad cost and returns. Recalculate whenever you change price or join a deal event.

Checklist

  1. Confirm the category and product type of each listing in Seller Center.
  2. Look up the current rate and tier type for each category.
  3. Flag SKUs priced near a band threshold (for example $10, $15 or $20).
  4. Include shipping charges in the total price when calculating the fee.
  5. Recheck fees before deal events and price changes.

沃尔玛 Marketplace 佣金详解：费率、分档与计算示例

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-15 · 约 6 分钟阅读

沃尔玛 Marketplace 不收开店费和月租，而是在每笔成交时收取佣金（referral fee）。费率取决于上架时选择的类目，很多类目还和售价挂钩。类目或价格档选错，毛利测算就会出错。本文结合官方费率和计算示例，讲清楚沃尔玛佣金怎么收。

佣金怎么收

根据沃尔玛佣金费率页面（2026 年 10 月 15 日访问）：

  • 成交才收。“只有在完成销售后才会扣除这些费用”，且沃尔玛称“没有开店费、月费或隐藏费用”。
  • 按总售价计算。“总售价包括商品价格以及运费和手续费、礼品包装和其他费用。”
  • 由类目和产品类型决定。“佣金因上架时选择的类目和产品类型而异。”

履约费用另算：如果使用沃尔玛官方仓（WFS），请参阅我们的《WFS 费用详解》。

两种分档方式：价格档与分段计费

沃尔玛有两种不同的分档结构，计算方式不一样：

  • 价格档（服装、美妆、母婴、食品杂货）：按总价落在哪一档，整单适用一个费率。14.99 美元的 T 恤付 5%；同一件卖 20.99 美元，则整单付 15%。
  • 分段计费（电子配件、家具、珠宝、手表等）：售价的每一段按各自费率计算，类似累进税率，售价越高，实际费率越低。

以下是几个示例价格下的实际佣金比例：

计算方法：服装 12 美元 = 5%（0.60 美元），18 美元 = 10%（1.80 美元），30 美元 = 15%（4.50 美元）。电子配件 150 美元 = 15% × 100 + 8% × 50 = 19 美元（12.7%）。家具 500 美元 = 15% × 200 + 10% × 300 = 60 美元（12%）。珠宝 400 美元 = 20% × 250 + 5% × 150 = 57.50 美元（14.4%）。

定价时怎么用

盯紧价格档边界

在价格档类目中，越过门槛会改变整单的费率。一件服装卖 15.00 美元付 0.75 美元佣金；卖 15.50 美元则付 1.55 美元。注意小幅涨价，或加收运费（运费计入总售价），都可能把你推到更高档，反而得不偿失。

选对类目

佣金跟随上架时选择的类目和产品类型。请选择准确描述产品的类目；沃尔玛可能审核并更正类目，选错既影响费用，也影响买家搜索。

用组合装调整账算

价格档类目里的低价商品，做成高价组合装可能适用更高费率。把履约成本一起算进去，两种方案都算一遍再决定。

每个 SKU 都要有佣金这一行

SKU 毛利表应包括售价、佣金、履约成本、广告成本和退货。每次调价或参加促销活动都要重新计算。

检查清单

  1. 在卖家后台核对每个 Listing 的类目和产品类型。
  2. 查清每个类目的当前费率和分档方式。
  3. 标出售价接近档位门槛（如 10、15、20 美元）的 SKU。
  4. 计算佣金时把运费计入总售价。
  5. 参加促销和调价前重新核算佣金。
