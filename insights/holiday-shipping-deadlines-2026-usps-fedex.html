2026 Holiday Shipping Deadlines: USPS, FedEx and UPS Ship-By Dates

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-23 · 5 min read

Every December, some shoppers wait too long and some sellers promise too much. Knowing each carrier's last recommended ship date lets you set clear "order by" messages, plan staffing and avoid a wave of late-delivery complaints. This guide collects the 2026 holiday ship-by dates published for USPS and FedEx, with notes on UPS, and a checklist to plan around them.

USPS: 2026 recommended send-by dates

From the USPS 2026 holiday shipping dates page, for expected delivery before December 25:

USPS Ground AdvantageThu, Dec 1710-day or longer service; check by ZIP
First-Class MailThu, Dec 17Thu, Dec 17
Priority MailFri, Dec 18Fri, Dec 18
Priority Mail ExpressSat, Dec 19Sat, Dec 19

USPS international dates: First-Class Package International and Priority Mail International by Wednesday, December 9; Priority Mail Express International by Wednesday, December 16. Military (APO/FPO/DPO) Ground Advantage is as early as Wednesday, November 4. USPS notes that actual delivery may vary based on origin, destination, acceptance time and other conditions.

UPS: what we know

As of late September, Value Added Resource reported that UPS had not published 2026 ship-by dates, that delivery is available for all services on Christmas Eve, that there is no Ground pickup on December 24, and that UPS is closed for regular pickup and delivery on Christmas Day. ShipStation's October 21 update lists UPS 3 Day Select by December 21, 2nd Day Air by December 22 and Next Day Air by December 23, and directs UPS Ground shippers to UPS's calculate-time-and-cost tool. Confirm UPS dates with UPS before you rely on them.

How to use these dates

  1. Work backwards from your handling time. If you need two business days to pick and pack, your own "order by" date for Ground Advantage is about two business days before December 17.
  2. Add a buffer. These are recommendations, not guarantees. Many sellers set customer cutoffs a day or two earlier.
  3. Show clear messages. Put "Order by [date] for delivery before Christmas" on listings, your website and order confirmations.
  4. Offer an upgrade after the cutoff. After the ground deadline, offer Priority Mail or FedEx Express options at checkout for last-minute buyers.
  5. Plan pickups. Book extra carrier pickups for the busiest weeks, and remember there is no UPS Ground pickup on December 24.
  6. Know your marketplace rules. Marketplace delivery promises and FBA inventory deadlines are separate from carrier dates; check each platform.

For choosing between USPS services, see our Priority Mail vs Ground Advantage guide.

Don't forget peak pricing

Holiday shipping also costs more. USPS's temporary holiday prices for Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express and Parcel Select run from October 4, 2026 to January 17, 2027, and UPS and FedEx apply their own peak surcharges through the season. If you upgrade late orders to faster services, check that the extra cost is covered by your shipping charge or margin. Our 2026 peak season surcharges article has the details.

2026 假日季发货截止日期：USPS、FedEx 与 UPS

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-23 · 约 5 分钟阅读

每年 12 月，总有消费者下单太晚、卖家承诺太满。掌握各承运商的建议最晚发货日，才能设定清晰的“截单”提示、安排人手，避免集中出现延迟投诉。本文汇总 USPS 和 FedEx 公布的 2026 年假日季发货截止日期，附 UPS 相关信息和规划清单。

USPS：2026 年建议最晚寄件日

来自 USPS 2026 年假日发货日期页面，以 12 月 25 日前送达为目标：

服务美国本土 48 州阿拉斯加、夏威夷、波多黎各及属地
USPS Ground Advantage12 月 17 日（周四）时效 10 天以上，按邮编查询
First-Class Mail12 月 17 日（周四）12 月 17 日（周四）
Priority Mail12 月 18 日（周五）12 月 18 日（周五）
Priority Mail Express12 月 19 日（周六）12 月 19 日（周六）

USPS 国际件：First-Class Package International 和 Priority Mail International 截至 12 月 9 日（周三）；Priority Mail Express International 截至 12 月 16 日（周三）。军邮（APO/FPO/DPO）Ground Advantage 最早 11 月 4 日（周三）截止。USPS 提醒实际送达时间受始发地、目的地、交寄时间等因素影响。

UPS：目前已知信息

据 Value Added Resource 9 月下旬报道，UPS 当时尚未公布 2026 年发货截止日期；平安夜所有服务均可派送，12 月 24 日无 Ground 揽收，圣诞节当天暂停常规揽收和派送。ShipStation 10 月 21 日更新列出 UPS 3 Day Select 截至 12 月 21 日、2nd Day Air 截至 12 月 22 日、Next Day Air 截至 12 月 23 日，UPS Ground 则需用 UPS 时效与费用计算工具查询。使用前请向 UPS 确认。

如何使用这些日期

  1. 从备货时间倒推。如果拣货打包需要两个工作日，你自己的 Ground Advantage“截单日”大约是 12 月 17 日前两个工作日。
  2. 预留缓冲。这些是建议日期，不是保证。很多卖家把对客户的截单时间再提前一到两天。
  3. 展示清晰提示。在 Listing、独立站和订单确认中注明“[日期]前下单，圣诞节前送达”。
  4. 截单后提供升级选项。过了陆运截止日，在结账时为最后时刻的买家提供 Priority Mail 或 FedEx 快递选项。
  5. 安排揽收。为最忙的几周预约额外揽收，并记住 12 月 24 日 UPS 没有 Ground 揽收。
  6. 了解平台规则。平台的配送承诺和 FBA 入仓截止日与承运商日期是两回事，请分别核对。

USPS 服务选择可参考我们的《Priority Mail 与 Ground Advantage 怎么选》。

别忘了旺季价格

假日季发货也更贵。USPS 对 Ground Advantage、Priority Mail、Priority Mail Express 和 Parcel Select 的假日临时价格从 2026 年 10 月 4 日持续到 2027 年 1 月 17 日，UPS 和 FedEx 整个旺季也有各自的高峰附加费。如果为晚下单的订单升级到更快的服务，要确认运费收入或毛利能覆盖额外成本。详见我们的《2026 旺季附加费》。
