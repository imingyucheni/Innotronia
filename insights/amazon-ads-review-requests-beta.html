Amazon Ads Review Requests: Paid Review Invitations Head to Beta

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-19 · 5 min read

Amazon is turning review requests into an ad product. On September 29, 2026, Amazon Ads announced Review Requests, a paid campaign type that invites recent buyers to rate or review a product, and said it opens in beta in the US in late October 2026. For brands launching new products, reviews are often the biggest hurdle, so this matters. Here is how it works, what Amazon has and has not disclosed, and how to think about it.

What Amazon announced

According to Amazon Ads' announcement (Sept 29, 2026):

  • What it does. "This new advertising solution reaches customers who recently purchased your product" and invites them to leave a rating or written review.
  • Launch. "Review Requests launches in open beta in the United States in late October 2026." Amazon says additional marketplaces and Amazon properties are planned for future releases.
  • Eligibility. Sellers and vendors with an advertising console account in good standing and no active policy violations. Products qualify while they have "fewer than 1,000 total ratings and reviews," and campaigns pause automatically at that threshold.
  • Management. Campaigns run in Amazon Ads Agent with "the same bidding and budget controls" as other Amazon Ads campaigns, and through the Amazon Ads Unified API.

PPC Land (Oct 12, 2026) points out that the 3× claim is a company-supplied before-and-after comparison with no control group, and that sample size, baseline, category mix and spend were not disclosed. It also notes that placements are described only as "Amazon's highest-traffic pages" and that it is unclear whether resulting reviews will be labelled. EcomCrew (Oct 1, 2026) adds that Amazon has not published a minimum bid, pricing benchmark or expected invitation volume.

How it differs from what you already have

  • Request a Review button. Free, sent one order at a time from Seller Central. Review Requests is a paid ad campaign shown on Amazon, with bids and budgets.
  • Vine. Vine gives free product to selected reviewers. Review Requests targets customers who already bought the product.
  • No guaranteed review. As EcomCrew notes, you pay to reach verified buyers, not for a positive rating: customers can leave one star, five stars or nothing.

That last point matters legally too. The FTC's rule on reviews bans paying for reviews that express a particular sentiment; see our FTC fake reviews rule guide. An ad that invites all recent buyers to review, with no condition on what they say, is a different thing, and it is Amazon's own product.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

Useful for launches, if the price is right. New ASINs with few reviews struggle to convert. If the cost per additional review is reasonable, Review Requests could shorten that phase. Until pricing is visible, treat it as an experiment.

Measure incrementality. Compare weekly new reviews and ratings before and during the campaign, and against similar products not in the test. Watch average star rating too: more reviews from a product with quality issues can lower your rating.

Fix the product page first. An invitation won't help if buyers are unhappy. Check return reasons and negative reviews before spending.

Keep using free tools. The Request a Review button and Vine still work. Use paid requests on top, not instead.

Checklist for the beta

  1. List ASINs with fewer than 1,000 ratings and reviews that sell steadily.
  2. Check your ads account is in good standing with no policy violations.
  3. Record each ASIN's weekly review and rating pace for 4 weeks before testing.
  4. Start with a small budget and track cost per additional review and star rating.
  5. Keep the free Request a Review routine running.

亚马逊广告推出 Review Requests：付费邀评即将公测

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-19 · 约 5 分钟阅读

亚马逊要把“邀评”做成广告产品了。2026 年 9 月 29 日，亚马逊广告宣布推出 Review Requests：一种付费广告活动，邀请近期买家为商品打分或写评论，并称将于 2026 年 10 月下旬在美国开启公开测试。对上新品牌来说，评论往往是最大的门槛，所以这件事值得关注。下面梳理它怎么运作、亚马逊公布了什么、没公布什么，以及该如何看待。

亚马逊公布了什么

根据亚马逊广告公告（2026 年 9 月 29 日）：

  • 作用。“这一新的广告方案触达近期购买过你产品的顾客”，邀请他们留下评分或文字评论。
  • 上线。“Review Requests 将于 2026 年 10 月下旬在美国开启公开测试。”亚马逊称后续将扩展到更多站点和亚马逊旗下场景。
  • 资格。广告账户状态良好、无未解决违规的卖家和供应商。商品在“评分和评论总数少于 1,000”时符合条件，达到门槛后活动自动暂停。
  • 管理方式。在 Amazon Ads Agent 中使用与其他亚马逊广告“相同的出价和预算控制”，也可通过 Amazon Ads Unified API 管理。

PPC Land（2026 年 10 月 12 日）指出，“3 倍”是公司自己提供的前后对比数据，没有对照组，样本量、基数、品类结构和花费都未披露；广告位只说是“亚马逊流量最高的页面”，也不清楚由此产生的评论是否会被标注。EcomCrew（2026 年 10 月 1 日）补充称，亚马逊没有公布最低出价、价格参考或预期邀请量。

与现有工具的区别

  • “请求评论”按钮。免费，在卖家后台逐单发送。Review Requests 是在亚马逊站内展示的付费广告，有出价和预算。
  • Vine 计划。Vine 是把免费产品送给受邀评论者；Review Requests 针对的是已经购买的顾客。
  • 不保证评论。正如 EcomCrew 所说，你付费是为了触达真实买家，而不是买好评：顾客可以给一星、五星，也可以不评。

这一点在法律上也很重要。FTC 规则禁止为特定倾向的评论付费，参见我们的《FTC 虚假评论规则》。邀请所有近期买家评论、对内容不设任何条件的广告，与此性质不同，而且这是亚马逊自己的产品。

我们的解读

对新品有用，前提是价格合适。评论少的新 ASIN 转化难。如果每多获得一条评论的成本合理，Review Requests 能缩短这一阶段。在价格公开之前，把它当作实验。

衡量增量效果。对比测试前后及与未参与测试的同类产品的每周新增评论和评分，同时关注平均星级：如果产品有质量问题，评论越多星级可能越低。

先把产品页做好。买家不满意，邀请也没用。投放前先检查退货原因和差评。

免费工具继续用。“请求评论”按钮和 Vine 依然有效，付费邀评是叠加，不是替代。

测试期检查清单

  1. 列出评分和评论少于 1,000、销量稳定的 ASIN。
  2. 确认广告账户状态良好、无违规。
  3. 测试前记录每个 ASIN 连续 4 周的每周评论和评分增速。
  4. 小预算起步，跟踪每条增量评论的成本和星级变化。
  5. 继续执行免费的“请求评论”流程。
