Ship with Walmart Comes to ShipStation: Discounted Labels and Lost-Package Protection

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-08 · 5 min read

Walmart Marketplace sellers who ship their own orders can now buy Walmart's discounted shipping labels without leaving ShipStation, one of the most widely used multichannel shipping tools. The launch, announced on October 6, 2026, comes with lost-package protection and a subscription discount, just as holiday volumes start to climb. Here is what it includes and what to check before switching.

What was announced

According to ShipStation's October 6, 2026 press release and product blog:

  • Walmart-negotiated rates in ShipStation. Walmart Marketplace sellers can buy eligible "Ship with Walmart" labels inside ShipStation, using rates Walmart has negotiated with domestic carriers, alongside their existing carrier accounts.
  • US domestic only. The program currently supports US domestic shipments.
  • Lost-package protection. Eligible orders shipped on time can receive up to $100 for lost-item claims, covering lost-in-transit and after-delivery claims. Sellers can also request a review of negative feedback for approved claims.
  • Subscription discount. Walmart sellers new to ShipStation who activate Ship with Walmart get 20% off their ShipStation subscription for one year; terms apply.
  • Simplified settings. ShipStation says recommended carrier and service options are based on the seller's delivery promise, helping on-time delivery and valid tracking metrics.

Matt Norman, ShipStation's vice president of strategic business development, said: "Sellers get Walmart-negotiated rates and seller protections, including up to $100 of order protection." PYMNTS notes that ShipStation lets sellers manage Walmart orders alongside Amazon, TikTok Shop, Shopify and eBay orders in the same tool.

What to watch out for

EcomCrew points out several limits. Walmart describes its protection as a discretionary benefit rather than insurance. It covers lost orders, not packages that arrive damaged. And labels bought with your own negotiated carrier account ("bring your own account") are excluded, so you may have to choose between your own rates and Walmart's protection. ShipStation's blog advertises rates "up to 50%" below market, but that is a company claim; compare real rates on your own shipments.

Why Walmart is doing this

PYMNTS cites Walmart CFO John David Rainey saying in April that marketplace sales were growing about 20%. More sellers shipping their own orders means more risk of late or lost deliveries, which hurts the customer experience. Putting Walmart's rates and protections inside the tools sellers already use is a low-friction way to raise delivery standards, alongside programs such as WFS.

What it means for sellers (our analysis)

  • Easier Walmart fulfillment for multichannel sellers. If you already run Amazon and TikTok Shop orders through ShipStation, adding Walmart labels with seller protections removes a separate workflow.
  • Performance metrics matter more in Q4. On-time shipping and valid tracking affect your Walmart account health and badges. Labels that support these metrics are useful in peak season.
  • $100 is a cap, not full value. For higher-value orders, the protection covers only part of a loss; keep other coverage in place.
  • Rate shopping is still essential. Regional carriers or your own contracts may beat Walmart's rates on some lanes.

Checklist before you switch

  1. Pick 20–30 recent Walmart orders across weights and zones and compare Ship with Walmart rates with your current labels.
  2. Check your average order value against the $100 protection cap.
  3. Decide which orders should use Walmart labels and which should stay on your own accounts.
  4. Confirm handling times so orders ship on time and qualify for protection.
  5. Read Walmart's current seller protection policy in Seller Center.
Tip: Set ShipStation automation rules by order weight and destination, so each order uses the cheapest label that still meets your delivery promise.

Ship with Walmart 接入 ShipStation：折扣面单与丢件保障

Atronia Innovations · 2026-10-08 · 约 5 分钟阅读

自发货的沃尔玛美国站卖家，现在可以直接在 ShipStation（最常用的多渠道发货工具之一）里购买沃尔玛的折扣面单。这项于 2026 年 10 月 6 日宣布的服务附带丢件保障和订阅折扣，正赶上节日订单量开始攀升。下面整理服务内容，以及切换前要核对的事项。

公布了什么

根据 ShipStation 的 2026 年 10 月 6 日新闻稿和产品博客：

  • 在 ShipStation 中使用沃尔玛协议运价：沃尔玛卖家可在 ShipStation 内购买符合条件的 “Ship with Walmart” 面单，使用沃尔玛与美国国内承运商谈下的运价，并可与自己的承运商账户并行使用。
  • 仅限美国国内：目前只支持美国国内件。
  • 丢件保障：按时发货的符合条件订单，丢件理赔最高 100 美元，包括运输途中丢失和签收后丢失；理赔获批后还可申请复核差评。
  • 订阅折扣：首次使用 ShipStation 的沃尔玛卖家开通该服务，可享一年 ShipStation 订阅 8 折，具体以条款为准。
  • 简化设置：ShipStation 称会根据卖家的配送承诺推荐承运商和服务，帮助提升准时送达率和有效追踪率。

ShipStation 战略业务发展副总裁 Matt Norman 表示：“卖家可以获得沃尔玛协议运价和卖家保障，包括最高 100 美元的订单保障。”PYMNTS 指出，ShipStation 允许卖家在同一工具中同时处理沃尔玛、亚马逊、TikTok Shop、Shopify 和 eBay 的订单。

需要注意的地方

EcomCrew 指出了几处限制：沃尔玛把这项保障定位为“酌情提供的福利，而不是保险”；只保丢件，不保破损；使用自己协议运价的“自带账户”面单不在保障范围内，因此卖家可能要在自有运价和沃尔玛保障之间做选择。ShipStation 博客宣传运价“最多比市场价低 50%”，这是公司自己的说法，请用你自己的订单实测比价。

沃尔玛为什么这么做

PYMNTS 援引沃尔玛首席财务官 John David Rainey 4 月的说法，称第三方平台销售增长约 20%。自发货的卖家越多，延误和丢件的风险就越大，会影响买家体验。把沃尔玛的运价和保障放进卖家已经在用的工具里，是一种阻力很小的提升配送标准的方式，与 WFS 等项目相互配合。

我们的解读

  • 多渠道卖家发沃尔玛单更方便：如果你已经用 ShipStation 处理亚马逊和 TikTok Shop 订单，沃尔玛面单加上卖家保障，可以省掉一套单独流程。
  • Q4 绩效指标更关键：按时发货和有效追踪影响沃尔玛店铺健康度和标识，旺季能帮助达标的面单很有价值。
  • 100 美元是上限，不是全额：高客单订单只能覆盖部分损失，原有的保障措施要保留。
  • 仍然要比价：在部分线路上，区域快递或你自己的合同价可能比沃尔玛运价更便宜。

切换前的检查清单

  1. 挑 20–30 个不同重量和分区的近期沃尔玛订单，对比 Ship with Walmart 运价与现有面单。
  2. 对照 100 美元保障上限，看看你的平均客单价。
  3. 决定哪些订单用沃尔玛面单，哪些继续用自有账户。
  4. 确认处理时效，保证按时发货以符合保障条件。
  5. 到沃尔玛卖家中心阅读最新的卖家保障政策。
小提示：在 ShipStation 里按订单重量和目的地设置自动化规则，让每个订单都用满足配送承诺的最便宜面单。
