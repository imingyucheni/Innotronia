Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Atronia Innovations Inc. ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through innotronia.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you send a shipping quote request or an e-commerce consultation form, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name, company or brand name, email address, and phone number or WeChat ID
  • Your service preferences (for example: service type, parcel size, shipping volume, plan, platforms, SKU count, business goals)
  • Any message you write to us

Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider may record standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type and the time of your visit) in server logs for security and reliability. Your browser also stores your language preference locally on your own device. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare quotes or proposals
  • To provide, manage and improve our services if you become a client
  • To send you a confirmation that we received your message
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or abuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

3. Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run this website. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • FormSubmit — delivers the contents of our website forms to our inbox
  • Google Workspace — our business email
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Calendly and Zoom — if you book a call, Calendly collects the details you enter in its booking form and creates a Zoom meeting. Calendly's booking widget loads only when you open it and may set its own cookies.

4. How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and, if we work together, for the duration of our business relationship and any period required by law, tax or accounting rules. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see below).

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have shared with us. Depending on where you live — for example California (CCPA/CPRA) or the European Economic Area (GDPR) — you may have additional rights, such as the right to know what we hold about you or to object to certain processing. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To make a request, send us a message through the contact form and mention "privacy request".

6. Security

Our website is served over encrypted HTTPS connections, and we limit access to inquiry information to the people who need it to respond to you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. International visitors

We have offices in Los Angeles (United States) and Shenzhen (China), and our team and service providers may process information in the United States, China or other countries. By contacting us, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in these locations.

8. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us any time through the contact form on our website.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 24 日

Atronia Innovations Inc.（"我们"）尊重您的隐私。本政策说明我们通过 innotronia.com 收集哪些信息、如何使用，以及您拥有的选择权。

1. 我们收集的信息

您主动提供的信息。当您提交运费报价或电商咨询表单时，我们会收集您选择填写的内容，例如：

  • 姓名、公司或品牌名称、邮箱地址、电话或微信号
  • 服务需求（例如：服务类型、包裹尺寸、发货量、方案、平台、SKU 数量、经营目标）
  • 您给我们的留言

自动收集的信息。网站托管服务商可能会在服务器日志中记录标准技术信息（如 IP 地址、浏览器类型和访问时间），用于安全与稳定运行。您的浏览器也会在您自己的设备上保存语言偏好。我们不使用广告或跨站追踪 Cookie。

2. 我们如何使用您的信息

  • 回复您的咨询，并准备报价或方案
  • 如您成为客户，用于提供、管理和改进我们的服务
  • 向您发送"已收到咨询"的确认邮件
  • 履行法律义务，防范欺诈或滥用

我们不会出售您的个人信息，也不会将其提供给第三方用于其自身的营销。

3. 服务提供商

我们使用少数可信赖的服务商来运行本网站，它们仅为向我们提供服务而处理信息：

  • FormSubmit —— 将网站表单内容转发到我们的邮箱
  • Google Workspace —— 我们的企业邮箱
  • GitHub Pages —— 网站托管
  • Calendly 与 Zoom —— 如您预约通话，Calendly 会收集您在其预约表单中填写的信息并创建 Zoom 会议。Calendly 预约窗口仅在您打开时加载，并可能设置其自身的 Cookie。

4. 信息保存期限

我们会在回复您所需的期间内保存咨询信息；如双方开展合作，则在合作期间以及法律、税务或会计规定要求的期限内保存。您可以随时要求我们删除（见下文）。

5. 您的选择与权利

您可以要求查阅、更正或删除您提供给我们的个人信息。根据您所在地区（例如美国加州 CCPA/CPRA 或欧洲经济区 GDPR），您可能还享有其他权利，例如了解我们持有哪些关于您的信息，或反对某些处理。我们不会因您行使这些权利而区别对待您。

如需提出请求，请通过联系表单给我们留言，并注明"隐私请求"。

6. 信息安全

本网站通过加密的 HTTPS 连接提供服务，且仅限需要回复您的人员查看咨询信息。任何传输或存储方式都无法做到绝对安全，但我们会采取合理措施保护您的信息。

7. 国际访客

我们在美国洛杉矶和中国深圳设有办公室，我们的团队及服务商可能在美国、中国或其他国家处理信息。联系我们即表示您了解您的信息可能会被传输至这些地区并在当地处理。

8. 儿童

本网站及服务面向企业，不针对 16 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的信息。

9. 政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本政策，页面顶部的生效日期即为最近一次更新时间。

10. 联系我们

对本政策有任何疑问，欢迎随时通过网站上的联系表单与我们联系。
